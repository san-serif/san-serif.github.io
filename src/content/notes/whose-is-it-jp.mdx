の links two nouns the way English uses "'s" or "of," and it chains as many times as needed in a row without ever needing a second connector.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="ハワイのマウイ島[とう]です。" romaji="Hawai no Mauitou desu." english="It's Maui Island in Hawaii." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="田中[たなか]さんの五歳[ごさい]の子供[こども]です。" romaji="Tanaka-san no gosai no kodomo desu." english="It's Tanaka's five-year-old child." />

<Hint>の chains freely: ハワイのマウイ島のホテルの朝ご飯 stacks four nouns into one phrase, "the breakfast of the hotel of Maui Island of Hawaii," with no extra connector needed at each step. English needs "of" repeated or a string of possessives; Japanese just keeps reusing の.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="黒[くろ]いのがいいです。" romaji="Kuroi no ga ii desu." english="I'd like the black one." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="ピザは、大[おお]きいのと小[ちい]さいのとどっちがいいですか。" romaji="Piza wa, ookii no to chiisai no to dochira ga ii desu ka." english="For pizza, which is better, the big one or the small one?" />

<Hint>This is a completely different job: の standing in for a noun you don't want to repeat, "the one." 黒いの means "the black one," letting an adjective carry the whole phrase without ever naming what it's describing. Context supplies the missing noun, the same way English "I'll take the black one" doesn't need to repeat "shirt" or "bag."</Hint>
