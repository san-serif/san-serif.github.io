You open the door and the surprise party lights up: 啊！ At the end of an exclamation, 啊 lets the reaction ring out—surprise, relief, delight, or frustration. The situation and the speaker’s voice tell you which one.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="啊！这是什么？" traditional="啊！這是什麼？" pinyin="ā! zhè shì shénme?" jyutping="aa1! ze2 si6 sam6 mo1?" english="Ah! What is this?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她很大方。" traditional="她很大方。" pinyin="tā hěn dàfang." jyutping="taa1 han2 daai6 fong1." english="She's very generous." />
