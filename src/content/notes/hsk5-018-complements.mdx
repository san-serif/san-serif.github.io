Your friend gives directions so clearly that you can find the café on your own. Later, you hear she won a prize and jumped for joy. Both use 得 after a verb or adjective, but the phrase after 得 tells you whether it describes the manner, the degree, or the result.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="老师讲得很清楚，我们一下子就听懂了。" traditional="老師講得很清楚，我們一下子就聽懂了。" pinyin="lǎoshī jiǎng de hěn qīngchu, wǒmen yíxiàzi jiù tīngdǒng le." jyutping="lou5 si1 gong2 dak1 han2 cing1 co2, ngo5 mun4 jat1 haa5 zi2 zau6 teng1 dung2 liu5." english="The teacher explained it clearly, so we understood right away." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她听到自己得奖，高兴得跳了起来。" traditional="她聽到自己得獎，高興得跳了起來。" pinyin="tā tīngdào zìjǐ déjiǎng, gāoxìng de tiào le qǐlai." jyutping="taa1 teng1 dou3 zi6 gei2 dak1 zoeng2, gou1 hing3 dak1 tiu3 liu5 hei2 loi4." english="When she heard she’d won a prize, she was so happy she jumped up." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我昨晚忙得连晚饭都没吃。" traditional="我昨晚忙得連晚飯都沒吃。" pinyin="wǒ zuówǎn máng de lián wǎnfàn dōu méi chī." jyutping="ngo5 zok6 maan5 mong4 dak1 lin4 maan5 faan6 dou1 mut6 hek3." english="I was so busy last night that I didn’t even eat dinner." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="风大得把窗户都吹开了，快关上吧。" traditional="風大得把窗戶都吹開了，快關上吧。" pinyin="fēng dà de bǎ chuānghu dōu chuī kāi le, kuài guānshàng ba." jyutping="fung1 daai6 dak1 baa2 coeng1 wu6 dou1 ceoi1 hoi1 liu5, faai3 gwaan1 soeng5 baa6." english="The wind was so strong it blew the window open; close it quickly." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这道题把大家难坏了，最后还是老师一步一步讲清楚的。" traditional="這道題把大家難壞了，最後還是老師一步一步講清楚的。" pinyin="zhè dào tí bǎ dàjiā nán huài le, zuìhòu háishi lǎoshī yí bù yí bù jiǎng qīngchu de." jyutping="ze5 dou6 tai4 baa2 daai6 gaa1 naan4 waai6 liu5, zeoi3 hau6 waan4 si6 lou5 si1 jat1 bou6 jat1 bou6 gong2 cing1 co2 dik1." english="This problem had everyone stumped; in the end, the teacher explained it step by step." />

<Hint type="hint">After a verb, 得 can describe how well something is done: 讲得清楚. With an adjective or feeling, it can introduce an outcome or strong degree: 忙得没吃饭, 高兴得跳起来, 难得坏了. Keep the complement after the word it describes.</Hint>
