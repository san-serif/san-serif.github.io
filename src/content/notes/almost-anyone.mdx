You nearly leave your phone on a café table. A friend catches it, and you say anyone might make that mistake. -(으)ㄹ 뻔하다 describes the event that narrowly did not happen; 아무…(이)나 opens the choice to anyone or anything.

### A Near Miss

Use -ㄹ 뻔하다 after a vowel or ㄹ and -을 뻔하다 after another consonant. A past near miss usually ends in 뻔했어요: 넘어질 뻔했어요. The event was close to happening but did not actually occur.

<SentenceBlock formal="카페에 휴대폰을 두고 올 뻔했습니다." formalRom="kapee hyudaeponeul dugo ol ppeonhaetseumnida." polite="카페에 휴대폰을 두고 올 뻔했어요." politeRom="kapee hyudaeponeul dugo ol ppeonhaesseoyo." casual="카페에 휴대폰을 두고 올 뻔했어." casualRom="kapee hyudaeponeul dugo ol ppeonhaesseo." english="I almost left my phone at the café." />

<SentenceBlock formal="계단에서 넘어질 뻔했습니다." formalRom="gyedaneseo neomeojil ppeonhaetseumnida." polite="계단에서 넘어질 뻔했어요." politeRom="gyedaneseo neomeojil ppeonhaesseoyo." casual="계단에서 넘어질 뻔했어." casualRom="gyedaneseo neomeojil ppeonhaesseo." english="I nearly fell on the stairs." />

### Any Person or Any Option

Use 아무 + noun + 이나 after a consonant and 나 after a vowel: 아무 책이나, 아무 음식이나. 아무나 means anyone, 아무거나 anything, and 아무 데나 anywhere. The choice is unrestricted within the relevant context.

<SentenceBlock formal="그런 실수는 아무나 할 수 있습니다." formalRom="geureon silsuneun amuna hal su itseumnida." polite="그런 실수는 아무나 할 수 있어요." politeRom="geureon silsuneun amuna hal su isseoyo." casual="그런 실수는 아무나 할 수 있어." casualRom="geureon silsuneun amuna hal su isseo." english="Anyone can make that sort of mistake." />

<SentenceBlock formal="여기 있는 책은 아무 책이나 빌려도 됩니다." formalRom="yeogi inneun chaegeun amu chaegina billyeodo doemnida." polite="여기 있는 책은 아무 책이나 빌려도 돼요." politeRom="yeogi inneun chaegeun amu chaegina billyeodo dwaeyo." casual="여기 있는 책은 아무 책이나 빌려도 돼." casualRom="yeogi inneun chaegeun amu chaegina billyeodo dwae." english="You can borrow any of the books here." />

<Hint type="hint">아무나 can appear with an affirmative expression. 아무도 normally needs a negative: 아무도 안 왔어요, “Nobody came.” “Anyone” and “nobody” use different partners.</Hint>
