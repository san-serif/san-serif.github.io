At the shop, you buy a dozen eggs; at home, a bag of rice and a roll of paper. Chinese measure words keep track of what kind of thing you’re counting: 打 is a dozen, 袋 a bag, 卷 a roll, and 棵 a plant. Learn each one beside a familiar noun.
