You want to explain why someone’s work deserves respect. Name the feature that matters, then connect an effort with its outcome. These patterns make an evaluation easier to follow in writing or a presentation.

### Name the Feature Behind the Judgement

Use a statement-shaped modifier before 점에서: action verbs -ㄴ다는/-는다는, adjectives -다는, nouns -(이)라는. The phrase means in the respect that or on the grounds that. It singles out the feature supporting what follows.

### Connect Effort With Its Outcome

Use the completed-action modifier -(으)ㄴ + 결과: 조사한 결과, 노력한 결과. The following clause states the result or discovery. Nouns such as 조사 can also take 결과 directly: 조사 결과.

<SentenceBlock formal="꾸준히 연습한 결과 대회에서 상을 받았습니다." formalRom="kkujunhi yeonseuphan gyeolgwa daehoeeseo sangeul badatseumnida." polite="꾸준히 연습한 결과 대회에서 상을 받았어요." politeRom="kkujunhi yeonseuphan gyeolgwa daehoeeseo sangeul badasseoyo." casual="꾸준히 연습한 결과 대회에서 상을 받았어." casualRom="kkujunhi yeonseuphan gyeolgwa daehoeeseo sangeul badasseo." english="As a result of practising steadily, I won a prize in the contest." />

<SentenceBlock formal="자료를 조사한 결과 새로운 사실을 알게 됐습니다." formalRom="jaryoreul josahan gyeolgwa saeroun sasireul alge dwaetseumnida." polite="자료를 조사한 결과 새로운 사실을 알게 됐어요." politeRom="jaryoreul josahan gyeolgwa saeroun sasireul alge dwaesseoyo." casual="자료를 조사한 결과 새로운 사실을 알게 됐어." casualRom="jaryoreul josahan gyeolgwa saeroun sasireul alge dwaesseo." english="After researching the material, I learned a new fact." />

<Hint type="hint">Use 점에서 to give the criterion for your evaluation. Use 결과 to connect the process with an outcome. A feature and a consequence are different kinds of support for an argument.</Hint>
