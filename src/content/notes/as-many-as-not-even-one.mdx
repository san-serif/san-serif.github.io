Beyond its basic "also" meaning, も attached right after a number expresses surprise at how much or how many, "as many as." Attached after a question word like 誰, どこ, or 何, paired with a negative verb, it means "not even one," a total, sweeping negative.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="10時間[じゅうじかん]も寝[ね]ました。" romaji="Juujikan mo nemashita." english="I slept for a whopping ten hours." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="５千円[ごせんえん]も払[はら]いました。" romaji="Gosen'en mo haraimashita." english="I paid as much as 5,000 yen." />

<Hint>Xも after a number always signals the speaker finds that number surprisingly high, 10時間も寝ました implies "that's a lot of sleep." Compare しか, which you already know pairs with a negative for "only," も here works almost like its opposite, emphasizing a large or excessive amount.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="誰[だれ]も来[き]ません。" romaji="Dare mo kimasen." english="Nobody is coming." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="何[なに]もしていません。" romaji="Nani mo shite imasen." english="I'm not doing anything." />

<Hint>誰も, どこも, 何も, いつも, all pair with a negative verb to sweep the whole category into the negative, "no one at all," "nowhere at all," "nothing at all." Without the negative, these same words shift meaning entirely, いつも without a negative just means "always."</Hint>
