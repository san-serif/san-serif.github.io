You tried making food for friends over the holiday. Looking back, you can share an observation about the day, or explain how people responded to what you did. -던데요 remembers a scene; -았/었더니 connects an action with its observed result.

### Recall a Scene for the Listener

Attach -던데요 to a verb or adjective stem to share a remembered observation. With nouns use -(이)던데요. Depending on context, it can carry surprise, background for a reply, or a gentle contrast with what the listener said.

<SentenceBlock polite="그 식당 음식이 정말 맛있던데요." politeRom="geu sikdang eumsigi jeongmal masitdeondeyo." casual="그 식당 음식이 정말 맛있던데." casualRom="geu sikdang eumsigi jeongmal masitdeonde." english="I found the food at that restaurant really tasty." />

<SentenceBlock polite="아까 지수가 급하게 나가던데요." politeRom="akka jisuga geuphage nagadeondeyo." casual="아까 지수가 급하게 나가던데." casualRom="akka jisuga geuphage nagadeonde." english="I saw Jisu leaving in a hurry earlier." />

### Do Something, Observe the Result

Use the past form plus 더니: 만들었더니, 물어봤더니, 전화했더니. In this pattern the first clause commonly reports your own action, and the next gives a reaction, result, or discovery that followed.

<SentenceBlock polite="친구들에게 잡채를 만들어 줬더니 정말 좋아했어요." politeRom="chingudeurege japchaereul mandeureo jwotdeoni jeongmal joahaesseoyo." casual="친구들에게 잡채를 만들어 줬더니 정말 좋아했어." casualRom="chingudeurege japchaereul mandeureo jwotdeoni jeongmal joahaesseo." english="I made japchae for my friends, and they really liked it." />

<SentenceBlock polite="사무실에 전화했더니 금요일까지 신청하라고 했어요." politeRom="samusire jeonhwahaetdeoni geumyoilkkaji sincheongharago haesseoyo." casual="사무실에 전화했더니 금요일까지 신청하라고 했어." casualRom="samusire jeonhwahaetdeoni geumyoilkkaji sincheongharago haesseo." english="I called the office, and they told me to apply by Friday." />

<Hint type="hint">[-더라고요](/notes/keep-at-it-i-found) directly shares a remembered discovery. -던데요 often offers that memory as background or a response for the listener to take up. Their uses overlap; context and intonation matter.</Hint>
