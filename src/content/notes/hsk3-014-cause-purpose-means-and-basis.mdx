The recipe says two teaspoons, so that’s what goes in the bowl. 按照 names the plan, instruction, or rule you’re following; 按 can set a measure or standard. Put that guide up front, then say what you do by it.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们按照地图走，十分钟后就到了湖边。" traditional="我們按照地圖走，十分鐘後就到了湖邊。" pinyin="wǒmen ànzhào dìtú zǒu, shí fēnzhōng hòu jiù dào le húbiān." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 on3 ziu3 dei6 tou4 zau2, sap6 fan1 zung1 hau6 zau6 dou3 liu5 wu4 bin1." english="We followed the map and reached the lakeside ten minutes later." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="请按照说明先插上电源，再按绿色按钮。" traditional="請按照說明先插上電源，再按綠色按鈕。" pinyin="qǐng ànzhào shuōmíng xiān chā shàng diànyuán, zài àn lǜsè ànniǔ." jyutping="cing2 on3 ziu3 syut3 ming4 sin1 caa1 soeng5 din6 jyun4, zoi3 on3 luk6 sik1 on3 nau2." english="Follow the instructions: plug in the power first, then press the green button." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这批鸡蛋按打出售，买得多还可以送货。" traditional="這批雞蛋按打出售，買得多還可以送貨。" pinyin="zhè pī jīdàn àn dǎ chūshòu, mǎi de duō hái kěyǐ sònghuò." jyutping="ze5 pai1 gai1 daan2 on3 daa1 ceot1 sau6, maai5 dak1 do1 waan4 ho2 ji5 sung3 fo3." english="These eggs are sold by the dozen, and larger orders can be delivered." />

<Hint type="hint">按照 often takes a plan, rule, or instructions. 按 can also mean “per” or “according to a unit,” as in 按打 “by the dozen.”</Hint>
