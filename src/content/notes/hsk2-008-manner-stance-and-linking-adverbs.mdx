Someone spills your tea. Was it on purpose, almost full, or perhaps just an accident? 故意 marks intention, 差不多 gives an approximate amount or point, and 好像/也许 let you make a guess. Pick the adverb that matches how sure you are and what you mean to say.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他故意不告诉我。" traditional="他故意不告訴我。" pinyin="tā gùyì bù gàosù wǒ。" jyutping="taa1 gu3 ji3 bat1 gou3 sou3 ngo5。" english="He deliberately didn’t tell me." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她差不多和你一样高。" traditional="她差不多和你一樣高。" pinyin="tā chàbuduō hé nǐ yīyàng gāo。" jyutping="taa1 caa1 bat1 do1 wo4 nei5 jat1 joeng6 gou1。" english="She's almost the same height as you." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我必须去睡觉了！我明天早上有课。" traditional="我必須去睡覺了！我明天早上有課。" pinyin="wǒ bìxū qù shuìjiào le！ wǒ míngtiān zǎoshang yǒu kè。" jyutping="ngo5 bit1 seoi1 heoi3 seoi6 gaau3 liu5！ ngo5 ming4 tin1 zou2 soeng6 jau5 fo3。" english="I have to get to sleep! I have class tomorrow morning." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="不好意思，你好像打错电话了。" traditional="不好意思，你好像打錯電話了。" pinyin="bùhǎoyìsi， nǐ hǎoxiàng dǎ cuò diànhuà le。" jyutping="bat1 hou2 ji3 si3， nei5 hou2 zoeng6 daa2 co3 din6 waa2 liu5。" english="Sorry, but I think you've got the wrong number." />
