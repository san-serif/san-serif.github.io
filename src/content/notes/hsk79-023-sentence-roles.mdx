In “brush your teeth,” teeth receive the action. But a verb-object phrase can also name an activity or the means used to do it. Read the verb and object together; their pairing can tell you more than “who did what to what.”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="为了方便取出来，她把钱存了个活期。" traditional="為了方便取出來，她把錢存了個活期。" pinyin="wèile fāngbiàn qǔ chūlai, tā bǎ qián cún le ge huóqī." jyutping="wai6 liu5 fong1 bin6 ceoi2 ceot1 loi4, taa1 baa2 cin2 cyun4 liu5 go3 wut6 kei4." english="She put the money in a demand deposit so she can withdraw it easily." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="爷爷每天早上写毛笔，练了一辈子书法。" traditional="爺爺每天早上寫毛筆，練了一輩子書法。" pinyin="yéye měitiān zǎoshang xiě máobǐ, liàn le yíbèizi shūfǎ." jyutping="je4 je4 mui5 tin1 zou2 soeng6 se2 mou4 bat1, lin6 liu5 jat1 bui3 zi2 syu1 faat3." english="Grandpa practices calligraphy with a brush every morning; he’s done it all his life." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="天太干了，记得给花园浇水。" traditional="天太乾了，記得給花園澆水。" pinyin="tiān tài gān le, jìde gěi huāyuán jiāo shuǐ." jyutping="tin1 taai3 gon1 liu5, gei3 dak1 kap1 faa1 jyun4 ziu1 seoi2." english="It’s so dry; remember to water the garden." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="公司正在招人才，还在为新项目拉赞助。" traditional="公司正在招人才，還在為新項目拉贊助。" pinyin="gōngsī zhèngzài zhāo réncái, hái zài wèi xīn xiàngmù lā zànzhù." jyutping="gung1 si1 zing3 zoi6 ziu1 jan4 coi4, waan4 zoi6 wai6 san1 hong6 muk6 laai1 zaan3 zo6." english="The company is recruiting talent and seeking sponsors for its new project." />

<Hint type="hint">存活期 names a way of depositing, 写毛笔 names the tool, 浇水 names the material used, and 招人才 names the goal. Don’t automatically translate every noun after a verb as the thing receiving the action.</Hint>
