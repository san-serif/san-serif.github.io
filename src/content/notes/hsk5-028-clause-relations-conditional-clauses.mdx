“We won’t make the last train unless we leave now.” 除非……才/否则…… sets the one condition that changes the outcome. State the exception first; then tell the listener what depends on it.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="除非你想去，否则你不必去这个派对。" traditional="除非你想去，否則你不必去這個派對。" pinyin="chúfēi nǐ xiǎng qù， fǒuzé nǐ búbì qù zhège pàiduì。" jyutping="ceoi4 fei1 nei5 soeng2 heoi3， fau2 zak1 nei5 bat1 bit1 heoi3 ze2 go3 paai3 deoi3。" english="You don’t have to go to the party unless you want to." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="立刻起床，否则你上学会迟到。" traditional="立刻起床，否則你上學會遲到。" pinyin="lìkè qǐchuáng， fǒuzé nǐ shàngxué huì chídào。" jyutping="laap6 hak1 hei2 cong4， fau2 zak1 nei5 soeng5 hok6 wui2 ci4 dou3。" english="Get up at once, or you’ll be late for school." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="慢慢来，不然你可能会出错的。" traditional="慢慢來，不然你可能會出錯的。" pinyin="mànmànlái， bùrán nǐ kěnéng huì chūcuò de。" jyutping="maan6 maan6 loi4， bat1 jin4 nei5 ho2 nang4 wui2 ceot1 co3 dik1。" english="Take your time, or you may make some mistakes." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我才是那个想和你在一起的人！" traditional="我才是那個想和你在一起的人！" pinyin="wǒ cái shì nà ge xiǎng hé nǐ zài yìqǐ de rén！" jyutping="ngo5 coi4 si6 naa5 go3 soeng2 wo4 nei5 zoi6 jat1 hei2 dik1 jan4！" english="I'm the one who wants to be with you." />
