きっかけ is the event, reason, or encounter that gives rise to a new action. を契機[けいき]に has a similar “trigger / turning point” meaning, but sounds formal and often appears in reports about a wider change.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="友人[ゆうじん]の勧[すす]めをきっかけに、日本語[にほんご]を習[なら]い始[はじ]めました。" romaji="Yuujin no susume o kikkake ni, nihongo o narai-hajimemashita." english="A friend's recommendation got me started learning Japanese." />

きっかけ can name the motivating event with をきっかけに, or ask for the story behind a new habit: 日本語を始めたきっかけは何ですか, “What got you started with Japanese?”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="震災[しんさい]を契機[けいき]に、建物[たてもの]の安全基準[あんぜんきじゅん]が見直[みなお]されました。" romaji="Shinsai o keiki ni, tatemono no anzen kijun ga minaosaremashita." english="The earthquake prompted a review of building safety standards." />

を契機に presents an event as the turning point that prompted a larger response or new phase. It is common in news, policy, and formal explanations; in everyday conversation, きっかけに is usually more natural.

<Hint type="insight">When someone asks why you took up a hobby, きっかけ is the natural word for the story: 友達に誘われたのがきっかけです, “A friend invited me, and that's what started it.” Use を契機に for a formal account of a broader shift.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Both point to a trigger. きっかけ is flexible and conversational; を契機に frames a specific event as a turning point, often for a policy, organization, or society.</Hint>
