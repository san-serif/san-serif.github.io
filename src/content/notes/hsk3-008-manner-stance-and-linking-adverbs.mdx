The team handles the work themselves and helps one another finish it. 亲自 puts the person at the center of the action; 互相/相互 shows people acting toward each other. Add 尽量 for “as much as possible,” or 大概/恐怕 when you’re estimating or voicing a concern.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我从来没有亲自去过那里。" traditional="我從來沒有親自去過那裡。" pinyin="wǒ cónglái méiyǒu qīnzì qù guò nàli。" jyutping="ngo5 cung4 loi4 mut6 jau5 can1 zi6 heoi3 gwo3 naa5 lei5。" english="I’ve never been there myself." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他们互相帮忙做功课。" traditional="他們互相幫忙做功課。" pinyin="tāmen hùxiāng bāngmáng zuò gōngkè。" jyutping="taa1 mun4 wu6 soeng1 bong1 mong4 zou6 gung1 fo3。" english="They help each other with their homework." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="尽量用好你的时间。" traditional="盡量用好你的時間。" pinyin="jǐnliàng yòng hǎo nǐ de shíjiān。" jyutping="zeon2 loeng6 jung6 hou2 nei5 dik1 si4 gaan3。" english="Try to make the best use of your time." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他们相互认识。" traditional="他們相互認識。" pinyin="tāmen xiānghù rènshi。" jyutping="taa1 mun4 soeng1 wu6 jing6 sik1。" english="They know each other." />
