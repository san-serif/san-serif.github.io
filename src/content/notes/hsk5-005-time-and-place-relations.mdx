The days get shorter; the café switches to its winter menu. 随着 links one changing circumstance with another development: 随着天气变冷, “as the weather gets colder.” The next clause shows what is changing alongside it.







<CSentenceBlock simplified="吃饭时，我们喝了一点白酒。" traditional="吃飯時，我們喝了一點白酒。" pinyin="chīfàn shí， wǒmen hē le yīdiǎn báijiǔ。" jyutping="sik6 faan6 si4， ngo5 mun4 ho1 liu5 jat1 dim2 baak6 zau2。" english="We had a little baijiu with dinner." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="大自然在我们的生活中扮演着重要的角色。" traditional="大自然在我們的生活中扮演著重要的角色。" pinyin="dàzìrán zài wǒmen de shēnghuó zhōng bànyǎn zhuózhòng yào de juésè。" jyutping="daai6 zi6 jin4 zoi6 ngo5 mun4 dik1 sang1 wut6 zung1 baan6 jin2 zoek6 zung6 jiu3 dik1 gok3 sik1。" english="Nature plays an important role in our lives." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他被一大群记者包围着。" traditional="他被一大群記者包圍著。" pinyin="tā bèi yī dà qún jìzhě bāowéi zhe。" jyutping="taa1 bei6 jat1 daai6 kwan4 gei3 ze2 baau1 wai4 zoek3。" english="He was surrounded by a large group of reporters." />
