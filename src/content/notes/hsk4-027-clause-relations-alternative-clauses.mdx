It’s raining, so you and a friend are deciding how to get to the cinema. Use 或者 to lay out possible choices in a statement. When you ask someone to choose between options, use 还是 instead.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们可以坐公交车，或者打车去电影院。" traditional="我們可以坐公交車，或者搭車去電影院。" pinyin="wǒmen kěyǐ zuò gōngjiāochē, huòzhě dǎ chē qù diànyǐngyuàn." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 ho2 ji5 co5 gung1 gaau1 ce1, waak6 ze2 daa2 ce1 heoi3 din6 jing2 jyun2." english="We can take the bus or a taxi to the cinema." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="周末你可以在家休息，或者跟朋友出去走走。" traditional="週末你可以在家休息，或者跟朋友出去走走。" pinyin="zhōumò nǐ kěyǐ zài jiā xiūxi, huòzhě gēn péngyou chūqù zǒuzou." jyutping="zau1 mut6 nei5 ho2 ji5 zoi6 gaa1 jau1 sik1, waak6 ze2 gan1 pang4 jau5 ceot1 heoi3 zau2 zau2." english="At the weekend, you can rest at home or go out with a friend." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="报名可以在网上填，或者到办公室拿表格。" traditional="報名可以在網上填，或者到辦公室拿表格。" pinyin="bàomíng kěyǐ zài wǎngshàng tián, huòzhě dào bàngōngshì ná biǎogé." jyutping="bou3 meng2 ho2 ji5 zoi6 mong5 soeng6 tin4, waak6 ze2 dou3 baan6 gung1 sat1 naa4 biu2 gaak3." english="You can register online or pick up a form at the office." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这道菜可以放香菜，或者加一点儿辣椒。" traditional="這道菜可以放香菜，或者加一點兒辣椒。" pinyin="zhè dào cài kěyǐ fàng xiāngcài, huòzhě jiā yìdiǎnr làjiāo." jyutping="ze5 dou6 coi3 ho2 ji5 fong3 hoeng1 coi3, waak6 ze2 gaa1 jat1 dim2 ji4 laat6 ziu1." english="You can add cilantro to this dish or put in a little chili." />

<Hint type="hint">或者 introduces an option inside a statement or suggestion. For a direct question such as “Tea or coffee?”, place 还是 between the choices instead.</Hint>
