<JapaneseLessonHead />

You have just met a classmate. Start with はじめまして, say your name, and finish with よろしくお願いします. These are useful complete phrases even before you know every part of their grammar.

### Your first exchange

<JSentenceBlock furigana="こんにちは。" romaji="Konnichiwa." english="Hello." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="はじめまして。アンナです。" romaji="Hajimemashite. Anna desu." english="Nice to meet you. I'm Anna." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="よろしくお願[ねが]いします。" romaji="Yoroshiku onegai shimasu." english="I look forward to getting to know you." />

<Hint type="insight">よろしくお願いします has no single English equivalent. After an introduction it closes the exchange warmly; in other settings it can ask for someone's cooperation. Learn the whole phrase first.</Hint>

### Say something about yourself

Put the topic first, mark it with は, and finish a polite noun statement with です. The particle は is written with the character for *ha* but pronounced *wa* here.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="私[わたし]は学生[がくせい]です。" romaji="Watashi wa gakusei desu." english="I'm a student." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="田中[たなか]さんは先生[せんせい]です。" romaji="Tanaka-san wa sensei desu." english="Tanaka is a teacher." />

Add か at the end to ask a polite yes-or-no question. In conversation, once the person is clear, you can leave the topic out.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="学生[がくせい]ですか。" romaji="Gakusei desu ka." english="Are you a student?" />

<Hint type="hint">です identifies or describes with a noun here; it does not mean “there is.” The next lesson uses ある and いる for things and people that exist or are somewhere.</Hint>

### Greetings change with the moment

<JSentenceBlock furigana="おはようございます。" romaji="Ohayou gozaimasu." english="Good morning. (polite)" />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="こんばんは。" romaji="Konbanwa." english="Good evening." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="また明日[あした]。" romaji="Mata ashita." english="See you tomorrow." />

<Hint type="hint">さようなら is a goodbye, but it can sound more final than a casual “see you.” またね or また明日 often fits everyday partings better.</Hint>
