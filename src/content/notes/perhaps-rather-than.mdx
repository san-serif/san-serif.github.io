You are thinking about your next few years. “Perhaps I’ll work abroad” keeps the future open; “rather than choosing quickly” explains the approach you prefer. Both help a plan stay thoughtful without sounding settled.

### A Tentative View or Expectation

Verb or adjective stem + -지 않을까 싶다 offers a cautious thought, expectation, or intention: 필요하지 않을까 싶어요. Although the form contains a negative question, it often suggests a positive possibility: “I suspect it may…”.

### Prefer One Approach Over Another

Use verb stem + -기보다는 before the preferred action or description: 서두르기보다는 기다리다. It can express a preference or correct how something should be characterised. Nouns use -(이)라기보다는 when contrasting descriptions.

<SentenceBlock formal="빨리 결정하기보다는 충분히 생각하고 싶습니다." formalRom="ppalli gyeoljeonghagibodaneun chungbunhi saenggakhago sipseumnida." polite="빨리 결정하기보다는 충분히 생각하고 싶어요." politeRom="ppalli gyeoljeonghagibodaneun chungbunhi saenggakhago sipeoyo." casual="빨리 결정하기보다는 충분히 생각하고 싶어." casualRom="ppalli gyeoljeonghagibodaneun chungbunhi saenggakhago sipeo." english="Rather than deciding quickly, I want to think it through." />

<SentenceBlock formal="혼자 준비하기보다는 경험이 있는 사람에게 물어볼 것입니다." formalRom="honja junbihagibodaneun gyeongheomi inneun saramege mureobol geosimnida." polite="혼자 준비하기보다는 경험이 있는 사람에게 물어볼 거예요." politeRom="honja junbihagibodaneun gyeongheomi inneun saramege mureobol geoyeyo." casual="혼자 준비하기보다는 경험이 있는 사람에게 물어볼 거야." casualRom="honja junbihagibodaneun gyeongheomi inneun saramege mureobol geoya." english="Rather than preparing alone, I’ll ask someone with experience." />

<Hint type="hint">[-는 대신에](/notes/getting-better-instead) can replace one action with another. -기보다는 often presents the second as the better choice or the more accurate description, rather than simply recording a substitution.</Hint>
