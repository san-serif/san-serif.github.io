You try on a coat, check the tag, and wince: 太贵了! Degree words such as 很 and 太 go before the adjective they affect. 很 can make a description sound neutral; 太 often carries your reaction, especially with 了 at the end.

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这件外套太贵了，我先不买。" traditional="這件外套太貴了，我先不買。" pinyin="Zhè jiàn wàitào tài guì le, wǒ xiān bù mǎi." jyutping="ze5 gin6 ngoi6 tou3 taai3 gwai3 liu5, ngo5 sin1 bat1 maai5." english="This coat is too expensive, so I won’t buy it for now." />

太 comes before the adjective. With 了 at the end, 太贵了 sounds like a strong reaction: “too expensive!”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天很热，孩子们都在家里。" traditional="今天很熱，孩子們都在家裡。" pinyin="Jīntiān hěn rè, háizimen dōu zài jiālǐ." jyutping="gam1 tin1 han2 jit6, haai4 zi2 mun4 dou1 zoi6 gaa1 leoi5." english="It’s hot today, so the children are all at home." />

很 often makes an adjective sound like a neutral description. 都 comes before the place phrase and means the whole group is at home.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她非常喜欢游泳，一星期去三次。" traditional="她非常喜歡游泳，一星期去三次。" pinyin="Tā fēicháng xǐhuan yóuyǒng, yì xīngqī qù sān cì." jyutping="taa1 fei1 soeng4 hei2 fun1 jau4 wing5, jat1 sing1 kei4 heoi3 saam1 ci3." english="She really likes swimming and goes three times a week." />

非常 strengthens 喜欢. It goes before the verb phrase, just as 很 and 太 go before adjectives.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你真快！我还没准备好呢。" traditional="你真快！我還沒準備好呢。" pinyin="Nǐ zhēn kuài! Wǒ hái méi zhǔnbèi hǎo ne." jyutping="nei5 zan1 faai3! ngo5 waan4 mut6 zeon2 bei6 hou2 ne1." english="You’re so quick! I’m still not ready." />

<Hint type="hint">很 often makes an adjective predicate sound complete; it does not always carry the strong English meaning “very.” 都 normally comes before the verb or adjective it scopes over.</Hint>
