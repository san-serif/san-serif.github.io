前に (before) attaches after a verb's plain dictionary form, regardless of when the sentence itself is set. あとで (after) attaches after a verb's plain past た-form. Both can also follow a noun with の.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="新聞[しんぶん]を読[よ]む前[まえ]に着替[きが]えます。" romaji="Shinbun o yomu mae ni kigaemasu." english="I get dressed before reading the newspaper." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="花[はな]に水[みず]をやった後[あと]で犬[いぬ]の散歩[さんぽ]をします。" romaji="Hana ni mizu o yatta ato de inu no sanpo o shimasu." english="I water the flowers, then walk the dog." />

<Hint>前に always takes the dictionary form even in a past-tense sentence, 食べる前に行きました, "I went before eating," never 食べた前に. あとで works the opposite way, always the past た-form, even when the whole sentence describes a habit or the future, 食べた後で寝ます.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="会社[かいしゃ]に行[い]く前[まえ]に天気予報[てんきよほう]を見[み]ます。" romaji="Kaisha ni iku mae ni tenki yohou o mimasu." english="I check the weather forecast before going to the office." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="会社[かいしゃ]に着[つ]いた後[あと]でメールをチェックします。" romaji="Kaisha ni tsuita ato de meeru o chekku shimasu." english="I check my email after arriving at the office." />

<Hint>Attached to a noun instead of a verb, both just need の between them, 授業の前に, パーティーの後で, "before class," "after the party," a much simpler pattern than the verb forms above.</Hint>
