You’re arranging to meet a friend and need to check the day. Chinese often answers with the weekday right after the subject, without 是. Add 星期几 to ask which day, then use the same pattern to give the answer.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天星期几？" traditional="今天星期幾？" pinyin="jīntiān xīngqī jǐ?" jyutping="gam1 tin1 sing1 kei4 gei2?" english="What day is it today?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天星期二。" traditional="今天星期二。" pinyin="jīntiān xīngqī'èr." jyutping="gam1 tin1 sing1 kei4 ji6." english="Today is Tuesday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们星期五见面吧。" traditional="我們星期五見面吧。" pinyin="wǒmen xīngqīwǔ jiànmiàn ba." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 sing1 kei4 ng5 gin3 min6 baa6." english="Let’s meet on Friday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="星期天我不上班。" traditional="星期天我不上班。" pinyin="xīngqītiān wǒ bú shàngbān." jyutping="sing1 kei4 tin1 ngo5 bat1 soeng5 baan1." english="I don’t work on Sundays." />

<Hint type="hint">In 今天星期二, 星期二 is the predicate: it tells you what day today is. When a weekday comes before the subject, as in 星期天我不上班, it sets the time for the whole sentence.</Hint>
