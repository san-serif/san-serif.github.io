After a long debate about where to open the shop, you land on the essentials: 归根到底，租金和人流量才是关键 (“when all is said and done, rent and foot traffic are what matter”). 可不是 is an emphatic “exactly,” and 没说的 praises something as beyond doubt or unquestionably good.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="归根到底，这是钱的问题。" traditional="歸根到底，這是錢的問題。" pinyin="guīgēndàodǐ, zhè shì qián de wèntí." jyutping="gwai1 gan1 dou3 dai2, ze2 si6 cin4 dik1 man6 tai4." english="In the final analysis, this is a question of money." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他这次测验不合格可不是开玩笑的。" traditional="他這次測驗不合格可不是開玩笑的。" pinyin="tā zhè cì cèyàn bù hégé kěbushì kāiwánxiào de。" jyutping="taa1 ze2 ci3 cak1 jim6 bat1 hap6 gaak3 ho2 bat1 si6 hoi1 waan4 siu3 dik1。" english="His failing the test is no laughing matter." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他对朋友真是没说的。" traditional="他對朋友真是沒說的。" pinyin="tā duì péngyou zhēnshi méishuōde." jyutping="taa1 deoi3 pang4 jau5 zan1 si6 mut6 syut3 dik1." english="He is really excellent to his friends (nothing to complain about)." />
