“Will he make it to dinner?” Your friend asks. You can make the whole question “whether he can come today” the subject of your answer. Chinese can also put the affected thing first, or stack details before a noun to help the listener pick out exactly which one you mean.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他今天能不能来，谁也不知道。" traditional="他今天能不能來，誰也不知道。" pinyin="tā jīntiān néng bu néng lái, shéi yě bù zhīdào." jyutping="taa1 gam1 tin1 nang4 bat1 nang4 loi4, seoi4 jaa5 bat1 zi1 dou3." english="Whether he can come today, nobody knows." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这本书已经翻译成英文了。" traditional="這本書已經翻譯成英文了。" pinyin="zhè běn shū yǐjīng fānyì chéng Yīngwén le." jyutping="ze5 bun2 syu1 ji5 ging1 faan1 jik6 sing4 jing1 man4 liu5." english="This book has already been translated into English." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="奶奶昨天寄来的那封信已经被我读完了。" traditional="奶奶昨天寄來的那封信已經被我讀完了。" pinyin="nǎinai zuótiān jì lái de nà fēng xìn yǐjīng bèi wǒ dú wán le." jyutping="naai4 naai4 zok6 tin1 gei3 loi4 dik1 naa5 fung1 seon3 ji5 ging1 bei6 ngo5 duk6 jyun4 liu5." english="The letter Grandma sent yesterday has already been read by me." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我刚买的那件红色外套已经洗好了。" traditional="我剛買的那件紅色外套已經洗好了。" pinyin="wǒ gāng mǎi de nà jiàn hóngsè wàitào yǐjīng xǐ hǎo le." jyutping="ngo5 gong1 maai5 dik1 naa5 gin6 hung4 sik1 ngoi6 tou3 ji5 ging1 sai2 hou2 liu5." english="The red coat I just bought has already been washed." />

<Hint type="hint">A full idea can serve as the subject, as in 他今天能不能来. With a patient subject, the thing affected by the action comes first, as with 这本书. Before a noun, each detail narrows down which item you mean: 我刚买的那件红色外套.</Hint>
