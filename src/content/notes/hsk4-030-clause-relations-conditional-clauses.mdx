Rain, sun, or a last-minute change of plan: the team is still going. 不管……都…… and 无论……都…… keep the result steady across different possible conditions. Put the changing situation first, then say what stays true.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="不管他是上学还是做生意，我都不会反对。" traditional="不管他是上學還是做生意，我都不會反對。" pinyin="bùguǎn tā shì shàngxué háishi zuò shēngyì， wǒ dōu bú huì fǎnduì。" jyutping="bat1 gun2 taa1 si6 soeng5 hok6 waan4 si6 zou6 saang1 ji3， ngo5 dou1 bat1 wui5 faan2 deoi3。" english="Whether he studies or goes into business, I won’t object." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="无论你想去还是不想去，你也要去。" traditional="無論你想去還是不想去，你也要去。" pinyin="wúlùn nǐ xiǎng qù háishi bùxiǎng qù， nǐ yě yào qù。" jyutping="mou4 leon6 nei5 soeng2 heoi3 waan4 si6 bat1 soeng2 heoi3， nei5 jaa5 jiu3 heoi3。" english="You have to go there whether you like it or not." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们都很好。" traditional="我們都很好。" pinyin="wǒmen dōu hěn hǎo." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 dou1 han2 hou2." english="We're all very well." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我也不是。" traditional="我也不是。" pinyin="wǒ yě bùshì。" jyutping="ngo5 jaa5 bat1 si6。" english="Nor am I." />
