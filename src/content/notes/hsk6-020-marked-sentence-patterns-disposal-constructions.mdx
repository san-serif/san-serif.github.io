In these causative 把 sentences, a cause or person brings about a change. With a nonhuman cause, the object is affected (大雨把草地淋湿了, “the heavy rain soaked the grass”). With a person as subject, the object may be the person who is made to act or change (老师把学生教会了, “the teacher taught the students until they knew it”).

<CSentenceBlock simplified="昨晚的大雨把野餐的草地淋湿了，大家只好把东西搬进屋里。" traditional="昨晚的大雨把野餐的草地淋濕了，大家只好把東西搬進屋裡。" pinyin="zuówǎn de dàyǔ bǎ yěcān de cǎodì línshī le, dàjiā zhǐhǎo bǎ dōngxi bān jìn wūli." jyutping="zok6 maan5 dik1 daai6 jyu5 baa2 je5 caan1 dik1 cou2 dei6 lam4 sap1 liu5, daai6 gaa1 zi2 hou2 baa2 dung1 sai1 bun1 zeon3 uk1 leoi5." english="Last night’s heavy rain soaked the picnic lawn, so everyone had to carry the things indoors." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="突然响起的警报把正在开会的人都吓了一跳。" traditional="突然響起的警報把正在開會的人都嚇了一跳。" pinyin="tūrán xiǎngqǐ de jǐngbào bǎ zhèngzài kāihuì de rén dōu xià le yí tiào." jyutping="dat6 jin4 hoeng2 hei2 dik1 ging2 bou3 baa2 zing3 zoi6 hoi1 wui6 dik1 jan4 dou1 haak3 liu5 jat1 tiu3." english="The alarm that suddenly went off startled everyone in the meeting." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="教练把队员们练得更有耐力，也教会了他们新的配合方法。" traditional="教練把隊員們練得更有耐力，也教會了他們新的配合方法。" pinyin="jiàoliàn bǎ duìyuánmen liàn de gèng yǒu nàilì, yě jiāohuì le tāmen xīn de pèihé fāngfǎ." jyutping="gaau3 lin6 baa2 deoi6 jyun4 mun4 lin6 dak1 gang3 jau5 noi6 lik6, jaa5 gaau3 wui6 liu5 taa1 mun4 san1 dik1 pui3 hap6 fong1 faat3." english="The coach trained the team to have more stamina and taught them a new way to coordinate." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这个笑话把客人们逗笑了，连服务员也忍不住笑起来。" traditional="這個笑話把客人們逗笑了，連服務員也忍不住笑起來。" pinyin="zhège xiàohua bǎ kèrénmen dòuxiào le, lián fúwùyuán yě rěnbuzhù xiào qǐlái." jyutping="ze5 go3 siu3 waa6 baa2 haak3 jan4 mun4 dau6 siu3 liu5, lin4 fuk6 mou6 jyun4 jaa5 jan2 bat1 zyu6 siu3 hei2 loi4." english="The joke made the guests laugh; even the server couldn’t help laughing." />

<Hint type="hint">The verb phrase needs to show a result or a clear effect on the object. Compare the inanimate cause 大雨 with the coach as the person who brings about a change in the team.</Hint>
