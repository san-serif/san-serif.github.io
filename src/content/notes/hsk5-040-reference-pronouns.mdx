The two teammates trust 彼此: each trusts the other. 如此 points back to the situation or describes how something is. One is mutual; the other reaches back to an idea already in the conversation.

### A Mutual Relationship

<CSentenceBlock simplified="虽然我们性格不同，但彼此都很尊重对方。" traditional="雖然我們性格不同，但彼此都很尊重對方。" pinyin="Suīrán wǒmen xìnggé bùtóng, dàn bǐcǐ dōu hěn zūnzhòng duìfāng." jyutping="seoi1 jin4 ngo5 mun4 sing3 gaak3 bat1 tung4, daan6 bei2 ci2 dou1 han2 zeon1 zung6 deoi3 fong1." english="Although our personalities differ, we both respect each other." />

彼此 sits before the shared action, 尊重. It tells us the respect goes both ways.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他们认识多年，工作上一直彼此信任。" traditional="他們認識多年，工作上一直彼此信任。" pinyin="Tāmen rènshi duōnián, gōngzuò shàng yìzhí bǐcǐ xìnrèn." jyutping="taa1 mun4 jing6 sik1 do1 nin4, gung1 zok3 soeng6 jat1 zik6 bei2 ci2 seon3 jam6." english="They’ve known each other for years and have always trusted each other at work." />

### Referring Back

<CSentenceBlock simplified="事情没有你想的如此复杂，我们可以一步一步解决。" traditional="事情沒有你想的如此複雜，我們可以一步一步解決。" pinyin="Shìqing méiyǒu nǐ xiǎng de rúcǐ fùzá, wǒmen kěyǐ yí bù yí bù jiějué." jyutping="si6 cing4 mut6 jau5 nei5 soeng2 dik1 jyu4 ci2 fuk1 zaap6, ngo5 mun4 ho2 ji5 jat1 bou6 jat1 bou6 gaai2 kyut3." english="Things aren’t as complicated as you think; we can solve them one step at a time." />

如此 points back to the situation being discussed. Here 如此复杂 means “this complicated” or “as complicated as that.”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你如此认真地准备，面试一定会顺利。" traditional="你如此認真地準備，面試一定會順利。" pinyin="Nǐ rúcǐ rènzhēn de zhǔnbèi, miànshì yídìng huì shùnlì." jyutping="nei5 jyu4 ci2 jing6 zan1 dik1 zeon2 bei6, min6 si6 jat1 ding6 wui5 seon6 lei6." english="You’ve prepared so carefully; the interview should go well." />

如此 can also come before an adjective or manner phrase to mean “so” or “in this way.” In conversation, people often choose 这么 or 那么 instead.

<Hint type="hint">Use 彼此 when the action is mutual: 彼此帮助, 彼此了解. Use 如此 to point back to a situation or degree: 如此重要, 如此安排.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">彼此 usually needs people on both sides of the relationship. It can stand before a verb, but not every English “each other” sentence uses it in the same position.</Hint>
