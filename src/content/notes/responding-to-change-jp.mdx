These formal patterns describe developments and how something changes in response. にしたがって links two changes or says something proceeds in line with a standard; に応じて means “according to / in response to”; に伴って marks a related change occurring alongside another; つつある describes a transition currently underway.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="人口[じんこう]が減[へ]るにしたがって、空[あ]き家[や]が増[ふ]えています。" romaji="Jinkou ga heru ni shitagatte, akiya ga fuete imasu." english="As the population declines, vacant houses are increasing." />

With one change leading alongside another, にしたがって is close to the N3 につれて, but sounds more formal and can also mean “in accordance with” a rule or plan. It is common in reports about gradual trends.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="利用者[りようしゃ]の希望[きぼう]に応[おう]じて、時間[じかん]を変更[へんこう]できます。" romaji="Riyousha no kibou ni oujite, jikan o henkou dekimasu." english="We can change the time according to the user's preference." />

に応じて focuses on adapting an action or response to a need, condition, or level: 要望に応じて, “in response to a request”; 能力に応じて, “according to ability.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="制度[せいど]の変更[へんこう]に伴[ともな]って、申請方法[しんせいほうほう]も変[か]わりました。" romaji="Seido no henkou ni tomonatte, shinsei houhou mo kawarimashita." english="Along with the system change, the application method changed too." />

に伴って describes a related development that comes along with a change or event. It is common in announcements and formal explanations.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="現在[げんざい]、オンライン申請[しんせい]が広[ひろ]まりつつあります。" romaji="Genzai, onrain shinsei ga hiromari tsutsu arimasu." english="Online applications are currently becoming more widespread." />

Verb stem + つつある presents a change as actively in progress. It is more formal and report-like than ～ているところだ, and commonly describes broad social or institutional developments.

<Hint type="insight">Use に応じて when describing flexible services, pricing, or support tailored to different needs. Use に伴って for a notice about what changes as a result of a broader event. A report may use つつある to show that a transition is still under way.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">The focus differs: にしたがって = linked trend or standard; に応じて = adjustment to a condition; に伴って = related change alongside an event; つつある = change in progress. These forms are common in writing and formal speech.</Hint>
