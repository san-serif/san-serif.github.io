Dinner is on the stove, your playlist is on, and you’re trying to finish homework: 我一边听音乐，一边做作业. 一边…一边… holds two actions side by side while they happen together. If the actions happen one after the other, choose a sequence pattern instead.

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="妈妈做饭，我看书。" traditional="媽媽做飯，我看書。" pinyin="Māma zuò fàn, wǒ kàn shū." jyutping="maa4 maa1 zou6 faan6, ngo5 hon3 syu1." english="Mum cooks while I read." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我一边听音乐，一边做作业。" traditional="我一邊聽音樂，一邊做作業。" pinyin="Wǒ yìbiān tīng yīnyuè, yìbiān zuò zuòyè." jyutping="ngo5 jat1 bin1 teng1 jam1 ngok6, jat1 bin1 zou6 zok3 jip6." english="I listen to music while doing homework." />

<Hint type="hint">一边 is repeated before both actions. The shorter pattern can omit a linking word when the relationship is obvious.</Hint>
