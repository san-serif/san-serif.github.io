The forecast said rain, and sure enough, the picnic gets rained out: 果然. 其实 can reset the story with what’s really going on; 根本 makes a negative firm, and 只好 says the other options have run out. These words show how the speaker is interpreting the moment.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们昨天试了一下，果然很好。" traditional="我們昨天試了一下，果然很好。" pinyin="wǒmen zuótiān shì le yíxià， guǒrán hěn hǎo。" jyutping="ngo5 mun4 zok6 tin1 si3 liu5 jat1 haa6， gwo2 jin4 han2 hou2。" english="We tried it yesterday, and it turned out to be very good." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你住在我们家，当然能少花点钱。" traditional="你住在我們家，當然能少花點錢。" pinyin="nǐ zhù zài wǒmen jiā， dāngrán néng shǎo huā diǎn qián。" jyutping="nei5 zyu6 zoi6 ngo5 mun4 gaa1， dong1 jin4 nang4 siu2 faa1 dim2 cin2。" english="If you stay with us, of course you’ll spend less." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我就想知道到底出了什么事。" traditional="我就想知道到底出了什麼事。" pinyin="wǒ jiù xiǎng zhīdào dàodǐ chū le shénme shì。" jyutping="ngo5 zau6 soeng2 zi1 dou3 dou3 dai2 ceot1 liu5 sam6 mo1 si6。" english="I just want to know what actually happened." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="反正已经太晚了。" traditional="反正已經太晚了。" pinyin="fǎnzhèng yǐjīng tài wǎn le。" jyutping="faan2 zing3 ji5 ging1 taai3 maan5 liu5。" english="It’s too late anyway." />
