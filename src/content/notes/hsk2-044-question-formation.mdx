You recognize someone at the café, but you’re not quite sure. 是不是 lets you check directly: “Is that your teacher?” For a softer check when you think you already know the answer, put 吧 at the end.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="那位是不是你的老师？" traditional="那位是不是你的老師？" pinyin="nà wèi shì bu shì nǐ de lǎoshī?" jyutping="naa5 wai2 si6 bat1 si6 nei5 dik1 lou5 si1?" english="Is that your teacher?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你是不是也住在附近？" traditional="你是不是也住在附近？" pinyin="nǐ shì bu shì yě zhù zài fùjìn?" jyutping="nei5 si6 bat1 si6 jaa5 zyu6 zoi6 fu6 gan6?" english="Do you live nearby too?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你今天也来上课吧？" traditional="你今天也來上課吧？" pinyin="nǐ jīntiān yě lái shàngkè ba?" jyutping="nei5 gam1 tin1 jaa5 loi4 soeng5 fo3 baa6?" english="You’re coming to class today too, right?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们现在出发吧？" traditional="我們現在出發吧？" pinyin="wǒmen xiànzài chūfā ba?" jyutping="ngo5 mun4 jin6 zoi6 ceot1 faat3 baa6?" english="Let’s leave now, shall we?" />

<Hint type="hint">是不是 is part of the question itself and can appear before the point you’re checking: 你是不是累了？ 吧 comes at the end and makes the question sound like a confirmation or suggestion.</Hint>
