You hear the news and 便赶来了—then you hurried over. 便 links an earlier event to what followed, with a more formal feel than 就. Elsewhere in the story, 不禁 marks a reaction, 仿佛 an impression, 亲眼 direct observation, and 特意 deliberate effort.
