てから attaches to a verb's て-form and says "after doing this, then that." It's built from the same て-form connector you already know, plus から, marking a firm sequence rather than just a loose list of actions.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="拓也[たくや]はニュースを見[み]てから会社[かいしゃ]に行[い]きます。" romaji="Takuya wa nyuusu o mitekara kaisha ni ikimasu." english="Takuya watches the news, then goes to the office." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="コーヒーを入[い]れてからメールを読[よ]みます。" romaji="Koohii o iretekara meeru o yomimasu." english="I make coffee, then read my email." />

<Hint>Plain て just links actions loosely, "I did this and that," without insisting on order. てから locks the sequence in place: the first action has to finish before the second one starts, which matters when the order itself is the point.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="予約[よやく]してから行[い]った方[ほう]がいいですよ。" romaji="Yoyaku shitekara itta hou ga ii desu yo." english="You should make a reservation before you go." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="初[はじ]めて日本[にほん]のアニメを見[み]てからずっと、アニメが好[す]きです。" romaji="Hajimete nihon no anime o mitekara zutto, anime ga suki desu." english="Ever since I first watched Japanese anime, I've loved it." />

<Hint>てからずっと adds "ever since," stretching the sequence from that first action all the way to now, a common pattern for describing how a habit or feeling got started.</Hint>
