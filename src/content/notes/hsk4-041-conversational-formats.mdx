The train leaves at six, and you are catching it. 说什么也得赶上 or 怎么着也得赶上 makes the determination clear: whatever happens, this has to get done. Put the emphatic phrase before 也得, then name the action.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="说什么也得先把问题解决。" traditional="說什麼也得先把問題解決。" pinyin="shuō shénme yě děi xiān bǎ wèntí jiějué." jyutping="syut3 sam6 mo1 jaa5 dak1 sin1 baa2 man6 tai4 gaai2 kyut3" english="Whatever anyone says, we still have to solve the problem first." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="不管遇到什么困难，这件事怎么着也得做完。" traditional="不管遇到什麼困難，這件事怎麼著也得做完。" pinyin="bùguǎn yùdào shénme kùnnan, zhè jiàn shì zěnmezhe yě děi zuòwán." jyutping="bat1 gun2 jyu6 dou3 sam6 mo1 kwan4 naan4, ze2 gin6 si6 zam2 zoek6 jaa5 dak1 zou6 jyun4" english="Whatever difficulties come up, we still have to see this through." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这项工作说什么也得今天完成。" traditional="這項工作說什麼也得今天完成。" pinyin="zhè xiàng gōngzuò shuō shénme yě děi jīntiān wánchéng." jyutping="ze2 hong6 gung1 zok3 syut3 sam6 mo1 jaa5 dak1 gam1 tin1 jyun4 sing4" english="This work has to be finished today, no matter what." />

<Hint type="hint">Here 得 is pronounced děi and means “have to.” It signals necessity; it is not the result complement 得 pronounced dé.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">The two lead-ins sound emphatic. Use them when you want to show resolve, rather than for a small, routine request.</Hint>
