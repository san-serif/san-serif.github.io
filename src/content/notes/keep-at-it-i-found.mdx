You try a pottery class and find it unexpectedly calming. That is one experience you can report. If a friend keeps practising, they may discover their own benefits over time. Korean distinguishes the remembered discovery from the expected result of continuing.

### Continue and See What Happens

Verb stem + -다 보면 describes a result or discovery that comes during continued or repeated activity: 연습하다 보면, 듣다 보면. The result often has a general or future reading. -다 보니까 can describe a result you have already reached through that process.

<SentenceBlock polite="계속 연습하다 보면 익숙해질 거예요." politeRom="gyesok yeonseuphada bomyeon iksukhaejil geoyeyo." casual="계속 연습하다 보면 익숙해질 거야." casualRom="gyesok yeonseuphada bomyeon iksukhaejil geoya." english="If you keep practising, you’ll get used to it." />

<SentenceBlock polite="책을 읽다 보면 시간이 금방 가요." politeRom="chaegeul ikda bomyeon sigani geumbang gayo." casual="책을 읽다 보면 시간이 금방 가." casualRom="chaegeul ikda bomyeon sigani geumbang ga." english="When I get absorbed in reading, time passes quickly." />

### Report Your Own Discovery

Attach -더라고요 to a verb or adjective stem to recall something you directly observed or experienced. Nouns take -(이)더라고요. It shares the discovery with the listener rather than simply stating a general fact.

<SentenceBlock polite="직접 해 보니까 생각보다 재미있더라고요." politeRom="jikjeop hae bonikka saenggakboda jaemiitdeoragoyo." casual="직접 해 보니까 생각보다 재미있더라고." casualRom="jikjeop hae bonikka saenggakboda jaemiitdeorago." english="When I tried it myself, I found it more fun than I expected." />

<SentenceBlock polite="그 카페는 아침에도 사람이 많더라고요." politeRom="geu kapeneun achimedo sarami manteoragoyo." casual="그 카페는 아침에도 사람이 많더라고." casualRom="geu kapeneun achimedo sarami manteorago." english="I found that café was busy even in the morning." />

<Hint type="hint">Use -더라고요 for a remembered observation, rather than as a routine substitute for “I did.” Reporting your own deliberate action normally needs the ordinary past; describing a feeling you discovered through it can suit -더라고요.</Hint>
