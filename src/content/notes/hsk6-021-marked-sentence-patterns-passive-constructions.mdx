The door swings open in the wind: 门被风给吹开了. In a 被 sentence, name the affected thing first, then say what happened. 给 can appear after the doer for emphasis in a spoken line; the affected person or thing remains the sentence’s focus.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="门被风给吹开了。" traditional="門被風給吹開了。" pinyin="mén bèi fēng gěi chuī kāi le." jyutping="mun4 bei6 fung1 kap1 ceoi1 hoi1 liu5" english="The wind blew the door open." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我的自行车被人给偷走了。" traditional="我的自行車被人給偷走了。" pinyin="wǒ de zìxíngchē bèi rén gěi tōu zǒu le." jyutping="ngo5 dik1 zi6 hang4 ce1 bei6 jan4 kap1 tau1 zau2 liu5" english="Someone stole my bicycle." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="材料已经被老师检查过了。" traditional="材料已經被老師檢查過了。" pinyin="cáiliào yǐjīng bèi lǎoshī jiǎnchá guo le." jyutping="coi4 liu6 ji5 ging1 bei6 lou5 si1 gim2 caa4 gwo3 liu5" english="The materials have already been checked by the teacher." />

<Hint type="hint">The subject before 被 is the affected participant, not the person doing the action. If the doer is included, it follows 被; 给 may appear after that doer in an emphatic spoken version.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">In conversation, 被 often appears when something goes wrong or when the result matters more than who caused it.</Hint>
