You wanted to cook like your friend. After sharing a kitchen for a year, you ended up learning. 처럼 supplies the model; -게 되다 describes the change that brought you to a new situation.

### Name the Model

Attach 처럼 directly to a noun: 친구처럼, 요리사처럼. It means like or in the manner of. The comparison can concern appearance, behaviour, or a way of doing something.

<SentenceBlock formal="저도 언니처럼 요리를 잘하고 싶습니다." formalRom="jeodo eonnicheoreom yorireul jalhago sipseumnida." polite="저도 언니처럼 요리를 잘하고 싶어요." politeRom="jeodo eonnicheoreom yorireul jalhago sipeoyo." casual="나도 언니처럼 요리를 잘하고 싶어." casualRom="nado eonnicheoreom yorireul jalhago sipeo." english="I want to cook well like my older sister." />

<SentenceBlock formal="이 카페는 집처럼 편안합니다." formalRom="i kapeneun jipcheoreom pyeonanhamnida." polite="이 카페는 집처럼 편안해요." politeRom="i kapeneun jipcheoreom pyeonanhaeyo." casual="이 카페는 집처럼 편안해." casualRom="i kapeneun jipcheoreom pyeonanhae." english="This café is as comfortable as home." />

### A Situation Changes

Verb stem + -게 되다 describes coming to do something, often because of circumstances, arrangements, or a gradual change. The ending on 되다 carries tense: 배우게 됐어요, 일하게 될 거예요. It can describe a welcome development as well as an unexpected one.

<SentenceBlock formal="친구 덕분에 요리를 배우게 됐습니다." formalRom="chingu deokbune yorireul baeuge dwaetseumnida." polite="친구 덕분에 요리를 배우게 됐어요." politeRom="chingu deokbune yorireul baeuge dwaesseoyo." casual="친구 덕분에 요리를 배우게 됐어." casualRom="chingu deokbune yorireul baeuge dwaesseo." english="Thanks to my friend, I came to learn cooking." />

<SentenceBlock formal="다음 달부터 서울에서 일하게 됐습니다." formalRom="daeum dalbuteo seoureseo ilhage dwaetseumnida." polite="다음 달부터 서울에서 일하게 됐어요." politeRom="daeum dalbuteo seoureseo ilhage dwaesseoyo." casual="다음 달부터 서울에서 일하게 됐어." casualRom="daeum dalbuteo seoureseo ilhage dwaesseo." english="It has been arranged that I will work in Seoul from next month." />

<Hint type="hint">[배우기로 했어요](/notes/decided) foregrounds a decision to learn. 배우게 됐어요 foregrounds the new situation you arrived at. The person may still have made choices along the way.</Hint>
