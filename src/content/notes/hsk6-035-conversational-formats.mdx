These patterns often carry frustration. One calls out bad timing, one shows the result of someone’s action, and one criticizes a choice to leave an obvious option unused.

### Not Earlier, Not Later

早不X，晚不X…偏偏… frames a complaint: the action could have happened at another time, but it happened at the most inconvenient one.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="早不说，晚不说，偏偏现在说。" traditional="早不說，晚不說，偏偏現在說。" pinyin="Zǎo bù shuō, wǎn bù shuō, piānpiān xiànzài shuō." jyutping="zou2 bat1 syut3, maan5 bat1 syut3, pin1 pin1 jin6 zoi6 syut3." english="You didn’t say it earlier or later; you had to say it now." />


### Look What You Did

看/瞧你把…X得 draws attention to a result caused by someone’s action; X得 describes how the person or thing ended up.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你看你把孩子吓得不敢说话了。" traditional="你看你把孩子嚇得不敢說話了。" pinyin="Nǐ kàn nǐ bǎ háizi xià de bù gǎn shuōhuà le." jyutping="nei5 hon3 nei5 baa2 haai4 zi2 haak3 dak1 bat1 gam2 syut3 waa6 liu5." english="Look, you scared the child so much they can’t speak." />


### Leaving the Obvious Thing Undone

放着X不Y criticizes someone for leaving X alone while doing Y instead.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="放着好好的饭不吃，偏要吃零食。" traditional="放著好好的飯不吃，偏要吃零食。" pinyin="Fàngzhe hǎohāo de fàn bù chī, piān yào chī língshí." jyutping="fong3 zoek6 hou2 hou2 dik1 faan6 bat1 sik6, pin1 jiu3 sik6 ling4 sik6." english="There’s a perfectly good meal, but you leave it and insist on eating snacks." />

<Hint type="hint">Read the frame as a whole before translating each word. The repeated words are part of the pattern, not accidental repetition.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">These forms are conversational: speakers use them to show annoyance, resignation, or emphasis. Tone of voice helps you tell playful teasing from real criticism.</Hint>
