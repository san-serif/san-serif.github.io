These patterns describe how action is directed between people and goals. に向かって marks movement toward a physical destination or target; に向けて often focuses on preparing or directing effort toward a future goal. に応えて means responding to a request, need, or expectation. XをYに任せる means entrusting X to Y, leaving the responsibility or decision with them.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="大会[たいかい]に向[む]けて、毎日[まいにち]練習[れんしゅう]しています。" romaji="Taikai ni mukete, mainichi renshuu shite imasu." english="I'm practicing every day in preparation for the tournament." />

に向けて points to an intended goal or audience and often appears with preparation, effort, or a message. に向かって can also mean moving in a physical direction: 駅に向かって歩く, “walk toward the station.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="参加者[さんかしゃ]の要望[ようぼう]に応[こた]えて、開始時間[かいしじかん]を早[はや]めました。" romaji="Sankasha no youbou ni kotaete, kaishi jikan o hayamemashita." english="In response to the participants' request, we moved the start time earlier." />

に応えて responds positively or actively to a request, demand, or expectation. It often implies that an action is taken to meet what others asked for.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="細[こま]かい作業[さぎょう]は経験者[けいけんしゃ]に任[まか]せましょう。" romaji="Komakai sagyou wa keikensha ni makasemashou." english="Let's leave the detailed work to someone experienced." />

XをYに任せる means to entrust X to Y or leave X up to Y. The person after に receives responsibility or discretion. It can sound trusting, but can also imply that the speaker is handing off a burden.

<Hint type="insight">A company may launch a new service に向けて a campaign, then revise it to meet feedback に応えて. In a team, a manager can assign a task to a colleague with 任せる. The particles show whether something is a goal, a response, or entrusted responsibility.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Goal or intended audience: に向けて. Physical direction: に向かって. Respond to a request: に応えて. Entrust a task or decision: XをYに任せる.</Hint>
