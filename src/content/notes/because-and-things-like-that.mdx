A friend asks how your film club started and what members do. You can give a conversational reason with -아/어 가지고, then list typical activities with -ㄴ다거나. The list gives examples rather than every possibility.

### A Spoken Reason

The -아/어 form plus 가지고 often supplies a reason in conversation: 좋아해 가지고, 바빠 가지고. It is frequently shortened to -아/어 갖고. For careful formal writing, a connector such as -아서/어서 is usually a better fit.

<SentenceBlock polite="영화를 좋아해 가지고 동호회에 가입했어요." politeRom="yeonghwareul joahae gajigo donghohoee gaiphaesseoyo." casual="영화를 좋아해 가지고 동호회에 가입했어." casualRom="yeonghwareul joahae gajigo donghohoee gaiphaesseo." english="I joined the club because I like films." />

<SentenceBlock polite="일이 많아 가지고 어제 못 갔어요." politeRom="iri mana gajigo eoje mot gasseoyo." casual="일이 많아 가지고 어제 못 갔어." casualRom="iri mana gajigo eoje mot gasseo." english="I couldn’t go yesterday because I had a lot of work." />

### List Representative Activities

Present action verbs take -ㄴ다거나 after a vowel and -는다거나 after a consonant: 본다거나, 먹는다거나. ㄹ drops: 놀다 → 논다거나. Adjectives take -다거나 and nouns -(이)라거나. The last item can use an ordinary ending.

<SentenceBlock polite="주말에는 영화를 본다거나 책을 읽어요." politeRom="jumareneun yeonghwareul bondageona chaegeul ilgeoyo." casual="주말에는 영화를 본다거나 책을 읽어." casualRom="jumareneun yeonghwareul bondageona chaegeul ilgeo." english="On weekends I do things like watch films or read books." />

<SentenceBlock polite="모이면 요리를 한다거나 같이 밥을 먹어요." politeRom="moimyeon yorireul handageona gachi babeul meogeoyo." casual="모이면 요리를 한다거나 같이 밥을 먹어." casualRom="moimyeon yorireul handageona gachi babeul meogeo." english="When we meet, we do things like cook or eat together." />

<Hint type="hint">[-거나](/notes/or) can simply connect alternatives. -ㄴ다거나 is useful when naming examples of the sorts of things involved; the list need not be exhaustive.</Hint>
