This group covers necessity and rules in formal Japanese. を余儀なくされる means someone is forced into a change; なくしては～ない says something cannot happen without a necessary condition; ずにはすまない and ずにはおかない describe actions that cannot be avoided or will certainly be brought about. んがため expresses a purpose in literary style. べからず is a written prohibition, while に当たらない and には及ばない say something is unnecessary or does not warrant a response. ても差し支えない grants permission. These patterns differ in who has the choice and how formal the statement sounds.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="悪天候[あくてんこう]のため、計画[けいかく]の変更[へんこう]を余儀[よぎ]なくされました。" romaji="Akutenkou no tame, keikaku no henkou o yogi naku saremashita." english="Because of bad weather, we were forced to change the plan." />

を余儀なくされる is passive: circumstances leave no alternative. It commonly appears in reports about changes, delays, or losses. ざるを得ない, covered in the N2 lesson, instead describes the speaker's own unavoidable action.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="この問題[もんだい]は、全員[ぜんいん]の協力[きょうりょく]なくしては解決[かいけつ]できません。" romaji="Kono mondai wa, zen'in no kyouryoku nakushite wa kaiketsu dekimasen." english="Without everyone's cooperation, this problem cannot be solved." />

なくしては～ない makes a prerequisite explicit. ずにはすまない describes an action one cannot get away without doing, often due to responsibility; ずにはおかない predicts a result that is bound to happen or a reaction that cannot be suppressed. する changes to せず in these forms.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="資料[しりょう]は事前[じぜん]に提出[ていしゅつ]しても差[さ]し支[つか]えありません。" romaji="Shiryou wa jizen ni teishutsu shite mo sashitsukae arimasen." english="You may submit the materials in advance." />

ても差し支えない is a formal permission: “there is no problem if you do.” には及ばない means there is no need to go that far, while には当たらない says an action or judgment is not warranted. べからず is a stern written “must not.”

<Hint type="insight">Official reports often use を余儀なくされる to describe an external constraint without naming a person at fault. In a personal apology, ずにはすまない can show that the speaker recognizes an obligation to act. Choose the form that matches whether the focus is external pressure, personal responsibility, or permission.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Forced by circumstances: を余儀なくされる. Essential prerequisite: なくしては～ない. Cannot avoid doing: ずにはすまない. Bound to cause a result: ずにはおかない. Formal permission: ても差し支えない. Written prohibition: べからず.</Hint>
