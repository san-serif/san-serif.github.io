You can state a reason and result in sequence without a linker, or make the relationship explicit with 因为……所以……. Because the words already mark the connection, you usually do not need to add another cause or result phrase.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我是因为太忙，所以才没有回信给你的。" traditional="我是因為太忙，所以才沒有回信給你的。" pinyin="wǒ shì yīnwèi tài máng， suǒyǐ cái méiyǒu huíxìn gěi nǐ de。" jyutping="ngo5 si6 jan1 wai6 taai3 mong4， so2 ji5 coi4 mut6 jau5 wui4 seon3 kap1 nei5 dik1。" english="I was too busy, so I didn’t write back to you." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天下雨了，所以我没去学校。" traditional="今天下雨了，所以我沒去學校。" pinyin="jīntiān xiàyǔ le， suǒyǐ wǒ méi qù xuéxiào。" jyutping="gam1 tin1 haa6 jyu5 zo2， so2 ji5 ngo5 mut6 heoi3 hok6 haau6。" english="It rained today, so I didn’t go to school." />
