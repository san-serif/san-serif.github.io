Not every note is a grammar rule. Some of the most useful pieces of Korean are small, fixed words that soften a question or carry gratitude, the kind of vocabulary that makes a sentence sound like a person said it instead of a textbook.

<SentenceBlock formal="혹시 친구에게 전화해도 되겠습니까?" formalRom="hoksi chinguege jeonhwahaedo doegesseumnikka?" polite="혹시 친구에게 전화해도 될까요?" politeRom="hoksi chinguege jeonhwahaedo doelkkayo?" casual="혹시 친구에게 전화해도 돼?" casualRom="hoksi chinguege jeonhwahaedo dwae?" english="Would it be okay if I called my friend?" />

<Hint>에게 ("to") is doing real grammatical work here, marking who's receiving the call, and it gets its own note later. Until then, just spot it on sight: [person] + 에게 means the action is directed at them.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="덕분에 이메일에 답장했습니다." formalRom="deokbune imeire dapjanghaetseumnida." polite="덕분에 이메일에 답장했어요." politeRom="deokbune imeire dapjanghaesseoyo." casual="덕분에 이메일에 답장했어." casualRom="deokbune imeire dapjanghaesseo." english="Thanks to you, I replied to the email." />

<SentenceBlock formal="혹시나 늦을까 봐 먼저 문자를 보냈습니다." formalRom="hoksina neujeulkka bwa meonjeo munjareul bonaetseumnida." polite="혹시나 늦을까 봐 먼저 문자를 보냈어요." politeRom="hoksina neujeulkka bwa meonjeo munjareul bonaesseoyo." casual="혹시나 늦을까 봐 먼저 문자를 보냈어." casualRom="hoksina neujeulkka bwa meonjeo munjareul bonaesseo." english="I sent a text ahead in case I might be late." />

<SentenceBlock formal="덕분에 잘 지냈습니다." formalRom="deokbune jal jinaetseumnida." polite="덕분에 잘 지냈어요." politeRom="deokbune jal jinaesseoyo." casual="덕분에 잘 지냈어." casualRom="deokbune jal jinaesseo." english="Thanks to you, I've been doing well." />

<SentenceBlock formal="혹시 시간 있습니까?" formalRom="hoksi sigan isseumnikka?" polite="혹시 시간 있어요?" politeRom="hoksi sigan isseoyo?" casual="혹시 시간 있어?" casualRom="hoksi sigan isseo?" english="Do you have time, by any chance?" />

<Hint>혹시 doesn't map onto one English word. Dropping it in front of a question softens the ask, closer to how English tacks on "by any chance" before something that might be a little presumptuous. Leave it off and the same question just sounds blunter, not wrong, just more direct.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="각각 이름이 다릅니다." formalRom="gakgak ireumi dareumnida." polite="각각 이름이 달라요." politeRom="gakgak ireumi dallayo." casual="각각 이름이 달라." casualRom="gakgak ireumi dalla." english="Each one's name is different." />
