“It’s not the blue folder, but the green one.” “The new café is both quiet and cozy.” 不是……而是…… replaces the mistaken idea; 既……又…… gives two details that are true at once. Listen for whether the speaker is correcting or adding.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她不是星期天来找我，而是星期一来找我。" traditional="她不是星期天來找我，而是星期一來找我。" pinyin="tā bú shì Xīngqītiān lái zhǎo wǒ， érshì Xīngqīyī lái zhǎo wǒ。" jyutping="taa1 bat1 si6 sing1 kei4 tin1 loi4 zaau2 ngo5， ji4 si6 sing1 kei4 jat1 loi4 zaau2 ngo5。" english="She came to see me on Monday, not Sunday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们既不知道，也没听说过这件事。" traditional="我們既不知道，也沒聽說過這件事。" pinyin="wǒmen jì bùzhī dào， yě méi tīngshuō guò zhè jiàn shì。" jyutping="ngo5 mun4 gei3 bat1 zi1 dou6， jaa5 mut6 ting1 syut3 gwo3 ze2 gin6 si6。" english="We neither knew nor had heard about this." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他今天又来了。" traditional="他今天又來了。" pinyin="tā jīntiān yòu lái le。" jyutping="taa1 gam1 tin1 jau6 loi4 liu5。" english="He came again today." />
