The same event can be told from different seats in the room: who benefits, who receives something, and who has to deal with the result. 为 and 向 point to people in different roles; 把 foregrounds the affected object, while 被 brings the affected person or thing to the front. 叫 and 让 can put someone in charge of the next action.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="会不会是因为昨天没写作业，被留下来了？" traditional="會不會是因為昨天沒寫作業，被留下來了？" pinyin="huì bu huì shì yīnwèi zuótiān méi xiě zuòyè， bèi liúxiàlai le？" jyutping="wui5 bat1 wui5 si6 jan1 wai6 zok3 tin1 mut6 se2 zok3 jip6， bei6 lau4 haa6 loi4 liu5？" english="Were you kept behind because you didn’t do your homework yesterday?" />
