The notice at the clinic says 需预约, “an appointment is required.” 需 is a compact, formal “need,” often followed by a noun or action in written instructions. In conversation, people are more likely to say 需要.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="做生意需要本钱。" traditional="做生意需要本錢。" pinyin="zuòshēngyi xūyào běnqián." jyutping="zou6 saang1 ji3 seoi1 jiu3 bun2 cin4." english="Doing business requires capital." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这项工程需要巨大的财力支持。" traditional="這項工程需要巨大的財力支持。" pinyin="zhèxiàng gōngchéng xūyào jùdà de cáilì zhīchí." jyutping="ze2 hong6 gung1 cing4 seoi1 jiu3 geoi6 daai6 dik1 coi4 lik6 zi1 ci4." english="This project requires huge financial support." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="红花还需绿树叶衬托。" traditional="紅花還需綠樹葉襯托。" pinyin="hóng huā hái xū lǜ shùyè chèntuō。" jyutping="hung4 faa1 waan4 seoi1 luk6 syu6 jip6 can3 tok3。" english="Red flowers still need green leaves to set them off." />
