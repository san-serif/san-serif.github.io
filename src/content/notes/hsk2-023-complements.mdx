A complement after the verb tells you what the action achieves or how it happens. 看完 says the reading reached its end; 走出去 gives a direction; 说得清楚 describes how clearly someone speaks. The verb and its complement work as one unit.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我看完这本书以后，就把它还给图书馆了。" traditional="我看完這本書以後，就把它還給圖書館了。" pinyin="wǒ kàn wán zhè běn shū yǐhòu, jiù bǎ tā huán gěi túshūguǎn le." jyutping="ngo5 hon3 jyun4 ze5 bun2 syu1 ji5 hau6, zau6 baa2 taa1 waan4 kap1 tou4 syu1 gun2 liu5." english="After finishing the book, I returned it to the library." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="下课以后，学生们从教室里走出来，去操场上活动。" traditional="下課以後，學生們從教室裡走出來，去操場上活動。" pinyin="xiàkè yǐhòu, xuéshengmen cóng jiàoshì lǐ zǒu chūlái, qù cāochǎng shàng huódòng." jyutping="haa6 fo3 ji5 hau6, hok6 saang1 mun4 cung4 gaau3 sat1 leoi5 zau2 ceot1 loi4, heoi3 cou1 coeng4 soeng6 wut6 dung6." english="After class, the students walked out of the classroom and went to exercise on the field." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="大家都准备好了，现在可以出发了。" traditional="大家都準備好了，現在可以出發了。" pinyin="dàjiā dōu zhǔnbèi hǎo le, xiànzài kěyǐ chūfā le." jyutping="daai6 gaa1 dou1 zeon2 bei6 hou2 liu5, jin6 zoi6 ho2 ji5 ceot1 faat3 liu5." english="Everyone is ready, so we can set off now." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她说得很清楚，我一下子就听懂了。" traditional="她說得很清楚，我一下子就聽懂了。" pinyin="tā shuō de hěn qīngchu, wǒ yíxiàzi jiù tīngdǒng le." jyutping="taa1 syut3 dak1 han2 cing1 co2, ngo5 jat1 haa5 zi2 zau6 teng1 dung2 liu5." english="She spoke very clearly, so I understood right away." />

<Hint type="hint">Result complements such as 完、好、懂 say what became true after an action. Directional complements such as 出来 show movement. For a manner description, use 得 between the verb and the describing phrase: 说得很清楚.</Hint>
