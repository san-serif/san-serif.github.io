The queue is long; by the time you reach the counter, the last pastry is gone. 才 can stress that something happened only after a wait or condition. 刚好/恰好 mean “just at the right time,” while 偏 adds the feeling that an event went against expectation or arrived at an inconvenient moment.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我才是那个想和你在一起的人！" traditional="我才是那個想和你在一起的人！" pinyin="wǒ cái shì nà ge xiǎng hé nǐ zài yìqǐ de rén！" jyutping="ngo5 coi4 si6 naa5 go3 soeng2 wo4 nei5 zoi6 jat1 hei2 dik1 jan4！" english="I'm the one who wants to be with you." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他回来的时候，我刚好写完了信。" traditional="他回來的時候，我剛好寫完了信。" pinyin="tā huílai de shíhou, wǒ gānghǎo xiěwán le xìn." jyutping="taa1 wui4 loi4 dik1 si4 hau6, ngo5 gong1 hou2 se2 jyun4 liu5 seon3" english="I had just finished writing the letter when he came back." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他恰好在我们需要帮助的时候来了。" traditional="他恰好在我們需要幫助的時候來了。" pinyin="tā qiàhǎo zài wǒmen xūyào bāngzhù de shíhou lái le." jyutping="taa1 hap1 hou2 zoi6 ngo5 mun4 seoi1 jiu3 bong1 zo6 dik1 si4 hau6 loi4 liu5" english="He arrived just when we needed help." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他偏要一个人去。" traditional="他偏要一個人去。" pinyin="tā piān yào yí ge rén qù." jyutping="taa1 pin1 jiu3 jat1 go3 jan4 heoi3" english="He insists on going alone." />
