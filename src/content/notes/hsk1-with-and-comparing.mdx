You and a friend head to school together: 我跟朋友去学校. Here 跟 brings your companion along. In 茶和咖啡, 和 simply joins two items. The word after the connector helps you see whether it’s linking a person to an action or one item to another.

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我跟朋友去学校。" traditional="我跟朋友去學校。" pinyin="Wǒ gēn péngyou qù xuéxiào." jyutping="ngo5 gan1 pang4 jau5 heoi3 hok6 haau6." english="I’m going to school with a friend." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我喜欢茶和咖啡。" traditional="我喜歡茶和咖啡。" pinyin="Wǒ xǐhuan chá hé kāfēi." jyutping="ngo5 hei2 fun1 caa4 wo4 gaa3 fe1." english="I like tea and coffee." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="老师跟学生一起看电影。" traditional="老師跟學生一起看電影。" pinyin="Lǎoshī gēn xuéshēng yìqǐ kàn diànyǐng." jyutping="lou5 si1 gan1 hok6 saang1 jat1 hei2 hon3 din6 jing2." english="The teacher watches a film together with the students." />
