You thought someone was quiet until you got to know them. Looking back, you remember the café where you used to meet. -줄 알다 reports what you believed; -던 attaches a remembered activity or state to a noun.

### What You Believed to Be True

Use -는 줄 알다 for a present action, -(으)ㄴ 줄 알다 for a completed action or present adjective, -(으)ㄹ 줄 알다 for an expected event, and -인 줄 알다 for a noun. In the past, 알았어요 often contrasts an earlier belief with what you now know.

<SentenceBlock formal="회의가 벌써 끝난 줄 알았습니다." formalRom="hoeuiga beolsseo kkeunnan jul aratseumnida." polite="회의가 벌써 끝난 줄 알았어요." politeRom="hoeuiga beolsseo kkeunnan jul arasseoyo." casual="회의가 벌써 끝난 줄 알았어." casualRom="hoeuiga beolsseo kkeunnan jul arasseo." english="I thought the meeting had already ended." />

### Attach a Remembered Experience to a Noun

Stem + -던 looks back at an ongoing, repeated, or unfinished action, or a past state: 자주 가던 카페, 읽던 책, 조용하던 거리. The recollection is central; -던 by itself does not simply mean every action happened once and finished.

<SentenceBlock formal="여기는 우리가 자주 만나던 카페입니다." formalRom="yeogineun uriga jaju mannadeon kapeimnida." polite="여기는 우리가 자주 만나던 카페예요." politeRom="yeogineun uriga jaju mannadeon kapeyeyo." casual="여기는 우리가 자주 만나던 카페야." casualRom="yeogineun uriga jaju mannadeon kapeya." english="This is the café where we used to meet often." />

<SentenceBlock formal="읽던 책을 가방에 넣었습니다." formalRom="ikdeon chaegeul gabange neoeotseumnida." polite="읽던 책을 가방에 넣었어요." politeRom="ikdeon chaegeul gabange neoeosseoyo." casual="읽던 책을 가방에 넣었어." casualRom="ikdeon chaegeul gabange neoeosseo." english="I put the book I had been reading into my bag." />

<Hint type="hint">Compare 읽은 책, “a book I read,” with 읽던 책, “the book I was reading.” Also distinguish belief from skill: 수영할 줄 알아요 can mean know how to swim; this page focuses on what someone takes to be true.</Hint>
