“The view was beautiful, and the sunset was especially beautiful.” 格外、极, and 极其 turn up the intensity of a quality. 共 does a different job: it gathers items into one total. Ask whether you’re turning up a description or counting the whole set.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他格外高兴。" traditional="他格外高興。" pinyin="tā géwài gāoxìng." jyutping="taa1 gaak3 ngoi6 gou1 hing3." english="He's especially happy." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这件事极其重要。" traditional="這件事極其重要。" pinyin="zhè jiàn shì jíqí zhòngyào。" jyutping="ze2 gin6 si6 gik6 kei4 zung6 jiu3。" english="This matter is extremely important." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="墙上挂着极漂亮的图画。" traditional="牆上掛著極漂亮的圖畫。" pinyin="qiáng shàng guà zhe jí piàoliang de túhuà。" jyutping="coeng4 soeng5 gwaa3 zoek3 gik6 piu3 loeng6 dik1 tou4 waa2。" english="An extremely beautiful picture hangs on the wall." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我想和你共进早餐。" traditional="我想和你共進早餐。" pinyin="wǒ xiǎng hé nǐ gòng jìn zǎocān。" jyutping="ngo5 soeng2 wo4 nei5 gung6 zeon3 zou2 caan1。" english="I'd like to have breakfast with you." />
