These patterns distinguish schedules, assumptions, and avoided actions. ことになっている describes a rule, arrangement, or schedule already established. つもりで means “with the intention of” or “as if,” depending on the context. ずに済む means an action can be avoided, often with a sense of relief. 思うように describes whether something goes as one hopes or expects.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="会議[かいぎ]は午後[ごご]三時[さんじ]に始[はじ]まることになっています。" romaji="Kaigi wa gogo sanji ni hajimaru koto ni natte imasu." english="The meeting is scheduled to begin at 3 p.m." />

ことになっている describes a decided arrangement or rule. It does not necessarily mean the speaker personally made the decision. For a personal decision, ことにしている or ことにした is more appropriate.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="忘[わす]れないように、メモを取[と]るつもりで話[はなし]を聞[き]きました。" romaji="Wasurenai you ni, memo o toru tsumori de hanashi o kikimashita." english="I listened intending to take notes so I wouldn't forget." />

つもりで can express intention, but it can also frame an action as if something were true: 親のつもりで助言する, “give advice as if I were a parent.” 思うように often appears with いかない to mean things do not go as planned. だけは can emphasize doing everything one can: できるだけはやってみた, “I did as much as I could.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="早[はや]めに予約[よやく]したので、長[なが]く待[ま]たずに済[す]みました。" romaji="Hayame ni yoyaku shita node, nagaku matazu ni sumimashita." english="Because I booked early, I didn't have to wait long." />

ずに済む describes avoiding an action or difficulty. It follows a negative verb stem; する becomes せずに. It often conveys relief that an undesirable action was unnecessary.

<Hint type="insight">A posted notice may say 事前予約が必要ということになっています, describing the current rule. A traveler might say 予約しておいたので並ばずに済んだ, highlighting the hassle they avoided.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Established schedule or rule: ことになっている. Intend to / act as if: つもりで. Avoid having to do something: ずに済む. As hoped: 思うように.</Hint>
