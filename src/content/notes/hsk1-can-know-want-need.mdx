Your friend asks you to cook tonight. 我会做饭 says you know how; 今天我不能做饭 says something gets in the way today. For dinner itself, 我想吃面 is a wish, while 我要吃面 sounds more decided. These words go before the action, but each gives your answer a different reason or strength.

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我会说中文。" traditional="我會說中文。" pinyin="Wǒ huì shuō Zhōngwén." jyutping="ngo5 wui5 syut3 zung1 man4." english="I can speak Chinese." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天我不能去。" traditional="今天我不能去。" pinyin="Jīntiān wǒ bù néng qù." jyutping="gam1 tin1 ngo5 bat1 nang4 heoi3." english="I can’t go today." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我想喝茶，也要吃饭。" traditional="我想喝茶，也要吃飯。" pinyin="Wǒ xiǎng hē chá, yě yào chī fàn." jyutping="ngo5 soeng2 hot3 caa4, jaa5 jiu3 sik6 faan6." english="I’d like tea, and I also need to eat." />

<Hint type="hint">A modal is followed by a verb: 我想去. Do not add 的 between the modal and the verb.</Hint>
