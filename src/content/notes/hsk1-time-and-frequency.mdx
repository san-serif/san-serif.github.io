Planning the morning? You can say what you’ll do first, what comes next, and what’s happening right now. These time words go before the action they place in sequence or describe.

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我先做完作业，再出去玩。" traditional="我先做完作業，再出去玩。" pinyin="Wǒ xiān zuò wán zuòyè, zài chūqù wán." jyutping="ngo5 sin1 zou6 jyun4 zok3 jip6, zoi3 ceot1 heoi3 waan2." english="I’ll finish my homework first, then go out to play." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我有时在家吃早饭。" traditional="我有時在家吃早飯。" pinyin="Wǒ yǒushí zài jiā chī zǎofàn." jyutping="ngo5 jau5 si4 zoi6 gaa1 hek3 zou2 faan6." english="Sometimes I eat breakfast at home." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你等一下，我马上回来。" traditional="你等一下，我馬上回來。" pinyin="Nǐ děng yíxià, wǒ mǎshàng huílái." jyutping="nei5 dang2 jat1 haa5, ngo5 maa5 soeng6 wui4 loi4." english="Wait a moment; I’ll be right back." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="老师正在说话，请大家听。" traditional="老師正在說話，請大家聽。" pinyin="Lǎoshī zhèngzài shuōhuà, qǐng dàjiā tīng." jyutping="lou5 si1 zing3 zoi6 syut3 waa6, cing2 daai6 gaa1 teng1." english="The teacher is speaking now; everyone, please listen." />

<Hint type="hint">先…再… gives a common sequence. For “again” in an instruction, 再 often precedes the verb: 请再说一次.</Hint>
