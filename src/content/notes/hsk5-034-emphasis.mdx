You missed the last train and had to take an expensive taxi home. The next day, you tell your friend you’ll never stay out that late again. These forms make a strong negative point: a lasting change, an emphatic correction, or something you can’t manage no matter how you try.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="搬家以后，我再也没见过老邻居。" traditional="搬家以後，我再也沒見過老鄰居。" pinyin="bānjiā yǐhòu, wǒ zàiyě méi jiànguo lǎo línjū." jyutping="bun1 gaa1 ji5 hau6, ngo5 zoi3 jaa5 mut6 gin3 gwo3 lou5 leon4 geoi1." english="Since moving, I’ve never seen my old neighbor again." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="别担心，我可没忘记你的生日。" traditional="別擔心，我可沒忘記你的生日。" pinyin="bié dānxīn, wǒ kě méi wàngjì nǐ de shēngrì." jyutping="bit6 daam1 sam1, ngo5 ho2 mut6 mong4 gei3 nei5 dik1 saang1 jat6." english="Don’t worry; I really haven’t forgotten your birthday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这道题我怎么也想不出来，得请老师帮忙。" traditional="這道題我怎麼也想不出來，得請老師幫忙。" pinyin="zhè dào tí wǒ zěnme yě xiǎng bu chūlai, děi qǐng lǎoshī bāngmáng." jyutping="ze5 dou6 tai4 ngo5 zam2 mo1 jaa5 soeng2 bat1 ceot1 loi4, dak1 cing2 lou5 si1 bong1 mong4." english="I can’t figure out this problem no matter how hard I try; I’ll have to ask the teacher for help." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="外面下着大雨，我们怎么都不想出门。" traditional="外面下著大雨，我們怎麼都不想出門。" pinyin="wàimiàn xiàzhe dà yǔ, wǒmen zěnme dōu bù xiǎng chūmén." jyutping="ngoi6 min6 haa6 zoek6 daai6 jyu5, ngo5 mun4 zam2 mo1 dou1 bat1 soeng2 ceot1 mun4." english="It’s pouring outside, and none of us wants to go out." />

<Hint type="hint">再也不/没 describes something that will not happen again. 可 adds emphasis or corrects an assumption. 怎么也/都不/没 says that an action or result remains impossible across all attempts.</Hint>
