You used to live near a good friend, but now you rarely meet. Looking back can simply mark a past situation as separate from now, or turn into regret about what you should have done.

### A Past Situation Set Apart From Now

Build -았었/었었- from the past form plus another 었: 살았었어요, 배웠었어요, 했었어요. It often presents a former situation that no longer holds, or a past episode viewed as separate from the present. Ordinary -았/었- remains enough for many past statements.

<SentenceBlock formal="예전에는 부산에 살았었습니다." formalRom="yejeoneneun busane sarasseotseumnida." polite="예전에는 부산에 살았었어요." politeRom="yejeoneneun busane sarasseosseoyo." casual="예전에는 부산에 살았었어." casualRom="yejeoneneun busane sarasseosseo." english="I used to live in Busan." />

<SentenceBlock formal="어렸을 때는 매운 음식을 못 먹었었습니다." formalRom="eoryeosseul ttaeneun maeun eumsigeul mot meogeosseotseumnida." polite="어렸을 때는 매운 음식을 못 먹었었어요." politeRom="eoryeosseul ttaeneun maeun eumsigeul mot meogeosseosseoyo." casual="어렸을 때는 매운 음식을 못 먹었었어." casualRom="eoryeosseul ttaeneun maeun eumsigeul mot meogeosseosseo." english="When I was young, I couldn’t eat spicy food." />

### Regret a Choice

Use -ㄹ걸 그랬다 after a vowel or ㄹ and -을걸 그랬다 after another consonant: 갈걸 그랬어요, 먹을걸 그랬어요. For regretting something you did, use -지 말걸 그랬어요. The usual subject is the speaker looking back on their own choice.

<SentenceBlock formal="친구에게 먼저 연락할걸 그랬습니다." formalRom="chinguege meonjeo yeollakhalgeol geuraetseumnida." polite="친구에게 먼저 연락할걸 그랬어요." politeRom="chinguege meonjeo yeollakhalgeol geuraesseoyo." casual="친구에게 먼저 연락할걸 그랬어." casualRom="chinguege meonjeo yeollakhalgeol geuraesseo." english="I should have contacted my friend first." />

<SentenceBlock formal="그런 말을 하지 말걸 그랬습니다." formalRom="geureon mareul haji malgeol geuraetseumnida." polite="그런 말을 하지 말걸 그랬어요." politeRom="geureon mareul haji malgeol geuraesseoyo." casual="그런 말을 하지 말걸 그랬어." casualRom="geureon mareul haji malgeol geuraesseo." english="I shouldn’t have said that." />

<Hint type="hint">Write 연락할걸 as one word in this regret ending, with a space before 그랬어요. It is a missed better choice, rather than an obligation that still lies ahead.</Hint>
