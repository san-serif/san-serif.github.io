You want to take part in a Korean speech contest. Explaining the goal and deciding your next step are different jobs: -기 위해서 supplies the purpose; -아/어야겠다 announces what you now feel you should do.

### Explain the Purpose

Attach -기 위해서 to an action verb stem: 배우기 위해서, 참가하기 위해서. With nouns use 을/를 위해서: 건강을 위해서. 위해 without 서 is also common. It expresses a deliberate purpose or benefit.

<SentenceBlock formal="대회에 참가하기 위해서 매일 연습합니다." formalRom="daehoee chamgahagi wihaeseo maeil yeonseuphamnida." polite="대회에 참가하기 위해서 매일 연습해요." politeRom="daehoee chamgahagi wihaeseo maeil yeonseuphaeyo." casual="대회에 참가하기 위해서 매일 연습해." casualRom="daehoee chamgahagi wihaeseo maeil yeonseuphae." english="I practise every day to take part in the contest." />

<SentenceBlock formal="건강을 위해서 아침마다 걷습니다." formalRom="geongangeul wihaeseo achimmada geotseumnida." polite="건강을 위해서 아침마다 걸어요." politeRom="geongangeul wihaeseo achimmada georeoyo." casual="건강을 위해서 아침마다 걸어." casualRom="geongangeul wihaeseo achimmada georeo." english="I walk every morning for my health." />

### Arrive at a Resolution

Use the -아/어 form plus 야겠다: 가야겠다, 먹어야겠다, 해야겠다. It often presents a resolution formed from the situation: “I’d better…” or “I should…”. Add 요 when speaking politely.

<SentenceBlock formal="오늘부터 발음을 더 연습해야겠습니다." formalRom="oneulbuteo bareumeul deo yeonseuphaeyagetseumnida." polite="오늘부터 발음을 더 연습해야겠어요." politeRom="oneulbuteo bareumeul deo yeonseuphaeyagesseoyo." casual="오늘부터 발음을 더 연습해야겠어." casualRom="oneulbuteo bareumeul deo yeonseuphaeyagesseo." english="I’d better practise pronunciation more from today." />

<SentenceBlock formal="신청 날짜를 알아봐야겠습니다." formalRom="sincheong naljjareul arabwayagetseumnida." polite="신청 날짜를 알아봐야겠어요." politeRom="sincheong naljjareul arabwayagesseoyo." casual="신청 날짜를 알아봐야겠어." casualRom="sincheong naljjareul arabwayagesseo." english="I should check the application date." />

<Hint type="hint">[해야 돼요](/notes/have-to) states a requirement. 해야겠어요 often shows you recognising the need and resolving to act. It is useful when a new fact changes your plan.</Hint>
