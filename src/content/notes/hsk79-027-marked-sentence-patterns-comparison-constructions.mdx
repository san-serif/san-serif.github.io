The new model is lighter than last year’s; the formal report calls its performance superior. 比起……（来） frames one option against another; 形容词+于 makes a compact written comparison; 比……还…… can say one thing even exceeds a benchmark.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="比起这幅画来，那幅画的色彩鲜艳多了。" traditional="比起這幅畫來，那幅畫的色彩鮮艷多了。" pinyin="bǐqǐ zhè fú huà lái, nà fú huà de sècǎi xiānyàn duō le." jyutping="bei2 hei2 ze3 fuk1 waa2 loi4, naa5 fuk1 waa2 dik1 sik1 coi2 sin1 jim6 do1 liu5" english="Compared with this painting, that one has much brighter colors." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他们没来这里，是吗？" traditional="他們沒來這裡，是嗎？" pinyin="tāmen méi lái zhèlǐ， shì ma？" jyutping="taa1 mun4 mut6 loi4 ze5 leoi5， si6 maa3？" english="They didn't come here, did they?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="学校坐落于车站附近，步行五分钟就能到。" traditional="學校坐落於車站附近，步行五分鐘就能到。" pinyin="xuéxiào zuòluò yú chēzhàn fùjìn, bùxíng wǔ fēnzhōng jiù néng dào." jyutping="hok6 haau6 co5 lok6 jyu1 ce1 zaam6 fu6 gan6, bou6 hang4 ng5 fan1 zung1 zau6 nang4 dou3" english="The school is located near the station, a five-minute walk away." />
