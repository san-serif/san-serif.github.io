Attach に (for a な-adjective or noun) or the く-form (for an い-adjective) to する or なる and you get "to make X" or "to become X." する marks a deliberate change someone causes; なる marks a change that just happens on its own.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="10年前[じゅうねんまえ]より会社[かいしゃ]が大[おお]きくなりました。" romaji="Juu nen mae yori kaisha ga ookiku narimashita." english="The company has gotten bigger than it was ten years ago." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="ロボットで家事[かじ]を楽[らく]にしたいです。" romaji="Robotto de kaji o raku ni shitai desu." english="I want to use robots to make housework easier." />

<Hint>大きくなる just reports a change, the company grew, with no one named as the cause. 楽にする names a deliberate actor making the change happen, "I want robots to make housework easier," the same distinction that shapes 自動詞・他動詞 pairs like 開く and 開ける.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="暖[あたた]かくなりましたね。" romaji="Atatakaku narimashita ne." english="It's gotten warmer, hasn't it?" />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="あまり手紙[てがみ]を書[か]かなくなりました。" romaji="Amari tegami o kakanaku narimashita." english="I've stopped writing letters much." />

<Hint>Attached to a verb's negative ない-form instead of an adjective, なくなる means "stopped doing" or "no longer does," 書かなくなる, "I've come to not write anymore," describing a habit that gradually faded rather than a single decision to quit.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="ロビーで話[はな]す時[とき]は日本語[にほんご]にしてください。" romaji="Robii de hanasu toki wa nihongo ni shite kudasai." english="When talking in the lobby, please make it Japanese." />

<Hint>してください built on する turns this into an instruction, "please make it X," commonly used for house rules or requests, テレビの音を大きくしないでください, "please don't turn the TV volume up."</Hint>
