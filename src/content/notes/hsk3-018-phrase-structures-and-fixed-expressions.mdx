The sky is dark, the wind is picking up, and the walk home looks like a wet one. 看起来 and 看上去 introduce an impression from what you can observe. 有的是 is a different kind of reassurance: there are plenty available.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你看起来很累。你应该休息一两个小时。" traditional="你看起來很累。你應該休息一兩個小時。" pinyin="nǐ kànqǐlai hěn lèi。 nǐ yīnggāi xiūxi yī liǎng ge xiǎoshí。" jyutping="nei5 hon3 hei2 loi4 han2 leoi6。 nei5 jing1 goi1 jau1 sik1 jat1 loeng5 go3 siu2 si4。" english="You look tired. You should rest for an hour or two." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你今天看上去很开心。" traditional="你今天看上去很開心。" pinyin="nǐ jīntiān kàn shàngqu hěn kāixīn。" jyutping="nei5 gam1 tin1 hon3 soeng5 heoi3 han2 hoi1 sam1。" english="You look very happy today." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他有的是钱。" traditional="他有的是錢。" pinyin="tā yǒu de shì qián。" jyutping="taa1 jau5 dik1 si6 cin4。" english="He has plenty of money." />
