You can’t find your phone. You look, search again, ask everyone, and still come up empty. These casual frames capture repeated attempts, something you can’t manage no matter how you try, or a feeling so strong that “very” hardly covers it.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我找了又找，还是没看见手机。" traditional="我找了又找，還是沒看見手機。" pinyin="wǒ zhǎo le yòu zhǎo, háishi méi kànjiàn shǒujī." jyutping="ngo5 zaau2 liu5 jau6 zaau2, waan4 si6 mut6 hon3 gin3 sau2 gei1." english="I searched and searched, but still couldn’t find my phone." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我问也问不出来，查也查不到，只好再打一次电话。" traditional="我問也問不出來，查也查不到，只好再打一次電話。" pinyin="wǒ wèn yě wèn bu chūlai, chá yě chá bu dào, zhǐhǎo zài dǎ yí cì diànhuà." jyutping="ngo5 man6 jaa5 man6 bat1 ceot1 loi4, caa4 jaa5 caa4 bat1 dou3, zi2 hou2 zoi3 daa2 jat1 ci3 din6 waa2." english="I couldn’t get an answer by asking or searching, so I had to call again." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他改了又改，改了又改，终于把报告交上去了。" traditional="他改了又改，改了又改，終於把報告交上去了。" pinyin="tā gǎi le yòu gǎi, gǎi le yòu gǎi, zhōngyú bǎ bàogào jiāo shàngqù le." jyutping="taa1 goi2 liu5 jau6 goi2, goi2 liu5 jau6 goi2, zung1 jyu1 baa2 bou3 gou3 gaau1 soeng5 heoi3 liu5." english="He revised it again and again, and finally turned in the report." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="听说她明天回来，他别提有多高兴了。" traditional="聽說她明天回來，他別提有多高興了。" pinyin="tīngshuō tā míngtiān huílai, tā bié tí yǒu duō gāoxìng le." jyutping="teng1 syut3 taa1 ming4 tin1 wui4 loi4, taa1 bit6 tai4 jau5 do1 gou1 hing3 liu5." english="He’s over the moon that she’s coming back tomorrow." />

<Hint type="hint">X了又Y describes doing something repeatedly. X也X不……，Y也Y不…… says repeated attempts don’t work. 别提有多X了 is an emphatic way to say a feeling or quality is extreme.</Hint>
