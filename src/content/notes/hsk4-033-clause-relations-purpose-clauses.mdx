You send the address so your friend can find the restaurant. 好让 introduces the intended result: give them the information 好让他们找到地方. In a shorter pattern, 好 can go straight before the action you want to make possible. The first action sets up the second.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你把酒店地址发给我，好让我直接打车过去。" traditional="你把酒店地址發給我，好讓我直接打車過去。" pinyin="nǐ bǎ jiǔdiàn dìzhǐ fā gěi wǒ, hǎo ràng wǒ zhíjiē dǎchē guòqu." jyutping="nei5 baa2 zau2 dim3 dei6 zi2 faat3 kap1 ngo5, hou2 joeng6 ngo5 zik6 zip3 daa2 ce1 gwo3 heoi3." english="Send me the hotel address so I can take a taxi straight there." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们早点出发，好赶上第一班火车。" traditional="我們早點出發，好趕上第一班火車。" pinyin="wǒmen zǎodiǎn chūfā, hǎo gǎnshàng dì-yī bān huǒchē." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 zou2 dim2 ceot1 faat3, hou2 gon2 soeng5 dai6 jat1 baan1 fo2 ce1." english="Let’s leave a little earlier so we can catch the first train." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她把窗户打开，好让房间透透气。" traditional="她把窗戶打開，好讓房間透透氣。" pinyin="tā bǎ chuānghu dǎkāi, hǎo ràng fángjiān tòutou qì." jyutping="taa1 baa2 coeng1 wu6 daa2 hoi1, hou2 joeng6 fong4 gaan1 tau3 tau3 hei3." english="She opened the window to let some fresh air into the room." />

<Hint type="hint">好 here is a purpose linker, “so that.” Do not confuse it with 好 as an adjective meaning “good” or a result complement meaning “finish/get ready.”</Hint>
