-지만 means "but," and it attaches straight to any stem, no vowel harmony, no irregular shifts, not even a consonant-versus-vowel split to check. It even sits happily after a past-tense marker: 아팠지만 (was sick, but...).

<SentenceBlock formal="고기는 안 좋아하지만 생선은 좋아합니다." formalRom="gogineun an joahajiman saengseoneun joahamnida." polite="고기는 안 좋아하지만 생선은 좋아해요." politeRom="gogineun an joahajiman saengseoneun joahaeyo." casual="고기는 안 좋아하지만 생선은 좋아해." casualRom="gogineun an joahajiman saengseoneun joahae." english="I don't like meat, but I like fish." />

<Hint>고기는...생선은... stacks two 는 markers back to back, the same contrastive move from Old News, New News. -지만 states the contrast grammatically; doubling up 는 on both halves underlines it.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="어제는 아팠지만 오늘은 괜찮습니다." formalRom="eojeneun apatjiman oneureun gwaenchanseumnida." polite="어제는 아팠지만 오늘은 괜찮아요." politeRom="eojeneun apatjiman oneureun gwaenchanayo." casual="어제는 아팠지만 오늘은 괜찮아." casualRom="eojeneun apatjiman oneureun gwaenchana." english="I was sick yesterday, but I'm okay today." />

<SentenceBlock formal="신발이 예쁘지만 비쌉니다." formalRom="sinbari yeppeujiman bissamnida." polite="신발이 예쁘지만 비싸요." politeRom="sinbari yeppeujiman bissayo." casual="신발이 예쁘지만 비싸." casualRom="sinbari yeppeujiman bissa." english="The shoes are pretty but expensive." />

<SentenceBlock formal="식당이 작지만 음식이 맛있습니다." formalRom="sikdangi jakjiman eumsigi masisseumnida." polite="식당이 작지만 음식이 맛있어요." politeRom="sikdangi jakjiman eumsigi masisseoyo." casual="식당이 작지만 음식이 맛있어." casualRom="sikdangi jakjiman eumsigi masisseo." english="The restaurant is small but the food is delicious." />

<SentenceBlock formal="토요일에는 가지만 일요일에는 안 갑니다." formalRom="toyoireneun gajiman iryoireneun an gamnida." polite="토요일에는 가지만 일요일에는 안 가요." politeRom="toyoireneun gajiman iryoireneun an gayo." casual="토요일에는 가지만 일요일에는 안 가." casualRom="toyoireneun gajiman iryoireneun an ga." english="I go on Saturday, but not on Sunday." />

<SentenceBlock formal="맵지만 맛있습니다." formalRom="maepjiman masisseumnida." polite="맵지만 맛있어요." politeRom="maepjiman masisseoyo." casual="맵지만 맛있어." casualRom="maepjiman masisseo." english="It's spicy but delicious." />

<Hint>That last one is worth noticing closely: 맵다 is ㅂ-irregular everywhere else, 매워요, 매운, but in front of -지만 it stays 맵지만, untouched. -지만 doesn't trigger any of the irregular shifts you've learned. It just attaches.</Hint>
