You’ve argued for the train, then remember the station is closed. 话又说回来 brings the conversation back to a point that changes the picture. X也不是，不X也不是 describes being stuck between choices; X也好/也罢，Y也好/也罢 accepts either one.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我在电视上看到过他，不过没有看到过他本人。" traditional="我在電視上看到過他，不過沒有看到過他本人。" pinyin="wǒ zài diànshì shàng kàndào guò tā， bùguò méiyǒu kàndào guò tā běnrén。" jyutping="ngo5 zoi6 din6 si6 soeng5 hon3 dou3 gwo3 taa1， bat1 gwo3 mut6 jau5 hon3 dou3 gwo3 taa1 bun2 jan4。" english="I have seen him on TV but not in the flesh." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你们的计划很好，可是我的更好。" traditional="你們的計劃很好，可是我的更好。" pinyin="nǐmen de jìhuà hěn hǎo， kěshì wǒ de gèng hǎo。" jyutping="nei5 mun4 dik1 gai3 waak6 han2 hou2， ho2 si6 ngo5 dik1 gang3 hou2。" english="Your plan is a good one, but mine is a better one." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="去也好，不去也好。" traditional="去也好，不去也好。" pinyin="qù yěhǎo, bù qù yěhǎo." jyutping="heoi3 jaa5 hou2, bat1 heoi3 jaa5 hou2." english="Going is fine, not going is also fine." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你喜欢哪个就买哪个，但不能两个都买。" traditional="你喜歡哪個就買哪個，但不能两個都買。" pinyin="nǐ xǐhuan nǎge jiù mǎi nǎge， dàn bùnéng liǎng ge dōu mǎi。" jyutping="nei5 hei2 fun1 naa5 go3 zau6 maai5 naa5 go3， daan6 bat1 nang4 loeng5 go3 dou1 maai5。" english="Buy whichever one you like, but you can’t buy both." />
