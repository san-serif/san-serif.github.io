Imagine you had a year free to live somewhere else. A hypothetical condition lets you explore the idea; a wish says what you would like to happen. Neither requires the plan to be settled.

### Imagine the Condition

Present action verbs take -ㄴ다면 after a vowel and -는다면 after most consonants: 간다면, 먹는다면. Drop ㄹ before ㄴ: 살다 → 산다면. Adjectives take -다면, and nouns -(이)라면. Compared with [-(으)면](/notes/if-you-have-time), this often makes an imagined premise more explicit; it need not be impossible.

<SentenceBlock formal="시간이 충분하다면 외국에서 살아 보고 싶습니다." formalRom="sigani chungbunhadamyeon oegugeseo sara bogo sipseumnida." polite="시간이 충분하다면 외국에서 살아 보고 싶어요." politeRom="sigani chungbunhadamyeon oegugeseo sara bogo sipeoyo." casual="시간이 충분하다면 외국에서 살아 보고 싶어." casualRom="sigani chungbunhadamyeon oegugeseo sara bogo sipeo." english="If I had enough time, I’d like to try living abroad." />

<SentenceBlock formal="다시 여행을 간다면 어디에 가고 싶습니까?" formalRom="dasi yeohaengeul gandamyeon eodie gago sipseumnikka?" polite="다시 여행을 간다면 어디에 가고 싶어요?" politeRom="dasi yeohaengeul gandamyeon eodie gago sipeoyo?" casual="다시 여행을 간다면 어디에 가고 싶어?" casualRom="dasi yeohaengeul gandamyeon eodie gago sipeo?" english="If you travelled again, where would you like to go?" />

### Say What You Hope Will Happen

Use -았/었으면 하다 for a hope: 만났으면 해요, 좋았으면 해요. Although the condition contains a past-shaped ending, the wish can concern the present or future. It overlaps with [-(으)면 좋겠어요](/notes/i-hope-so).

<SentenceBlock formal="언젠가 세계 일주를 했으면 합니다." formalRom="eonjenga segye iljureul haesseumyeon hamnida." polite="언젠가 세계 일주를 했으면 해요." politeRom="eonjenga segye iljureul haesseumyeon haeyo." casual="언젠가 세계 일주를 했으면 해." casualRom="eonjenga segye iljureul haesseumyeon hae." english="I hope to travel around the world someday." />

<SentenceBlock formal="우리 가족이 모두 건강했으면 합니다." formalRom="uri gajogi modu geonganghaesseumyeon hamnida." polite="우리 가족이 모두 건강했으면 해요." politeRom="uri gajogi modu geonganghaesseumyeon haeyo." casual="우리 가족이 모두 건강했으면 해." casualRom="uri gajogi modu geonganghaesseumyeon hae." english="I hope everyone in my family stays healthy." />

<Hint type="hint">For a noun hypothesis use 학생이라면, “if someone is a student,” rather than attaching the action-verb shape -는다면 to the noun. A past hypothesis uses -았/었다면: 시간이 있었다면, “if there had been time.”</Hint>
