極まりない and 極まる intensify an evaluation to “extremely” or “utterly,” often in formal criticism. の極み and の至り name the height of an emotion or state. に足る and に恥じない describe something worthy of attention or reputation; に足りない says it does not merit something. Adj限りだ expresses an intense feeling, while にもほどがある criticizes something as going too far. に難くない says something is easy to imagine or infer. に堪えない expresses an inability to bear something, or in set expressions an overwhelming feeling. These are strong evaluations, so the surrounding tone matters.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="説明[せつめい]を聞[き]かずに決[き]めるのは、軽率極[けいそつきわ]まりない。" romaji="Setsumei o kikazu ni kimeru no wa, keisotsu kiwamari nai." english="Making a decision without hearing the explanation is extremely rash." />

極まりない follows a な-adjective stem or noun to make a strong formal judgment. 極まる has a similar meaning and can follow a noun or adjective stem. It is emphatic and often appears in criticism or commentary.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="この記録[きろく]は、研究[けんきゅう]に足[た]る価値[かち]があります。" romaji="Kono kiroku wa, kenkyuu ni taru kachi ga arimasu." english="This record has value worthy of research." />

に足る means “worthy of” or “sufficient to merit,” and commonly appears with 信頼, 評価, or 注目. に足りない means “not worth” or “not enough to merit.” の極み and の至り are set, elevated ways to express the height of a feeling, as in 感激の極み or 光栄の至り.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="そんな言[い]い訳[わけ]にもほどがあります。" romaji="Sonna iiwake ni mo hodo ga arimasu." english="There is a limit to excuses like that." />

にもほどがある calls out excess, often with irritation. Adj限りだ expresses a strong feeling as the speaker's utmost state. に恥じない means “worthy of” a name or reputation, as in 名に恥じない働き, “work worthy of the name.”

<Hint type="insight">These patterns are not neutral praise words. に足る is measured and formal; に恥じない gives praise tied to a standard; 極まりない and にもほどがある can sound forceful or disapproving. Use them when that strength is intended.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Merits attention: に足る. Falls short of merit: に足りない. Lives up to a name: に恥じない. Maximum degree: の極み or Adj限りだ. Goes too far: にもほどがある.</Hint>
