These three short words connect an action to a person or a place in different ways. 给 marks who receives something, 对 points to the person being addressed or affected, and 离 measures distance from a reference point.

### Giving Something to a Recipient

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我把文件给你发过去了，你收到以后告诉我。" traditional="我把文件給你發過去了，你收到以後告訴我。" pinyin="Wǒ bǎ wénjiàn gěi nǐ fā guòqù le, nǐ shōudào yǐhòu gàosu wǒ." jyutping="ngo5 baa2 man4 gin6 kap1 nei5 faat3 gwo3 heoi3 liu5, nei5 sau1 dou3 ji5 hau6 gou3 sou3 ngo5." english="I sent the file over to you; let me know when you get it." />

给 answers “to whom?” The recipient comes after 给 and before the action.

### The Person Addressed or Affected

<CSentenceBlock simplified="老师对我们说，这次考试并不难。" traditional="老師對我們說，這次考試並不難。" pinyin="Lǎoshī duì wǒmen shuō, zhè cì kǎoshì bìng bù nán." jyutping="lou5 si1 deoi3 ngo5 mun4 syut3, ze5 ci3 haau2 si3 bing6 bat1 naan4." english="The teacher told us that this exam isn’t difficult." />

对 points toward the person receiving the words. It is not the same as 给 here: the teacher is speaking to us, not handing us an object.

### Distance from a Point

<CSentenceBlock simplified="火车站离这里还有十分钟的路程。" traditional="火車站離這裡還有十分鐘的路程。" pinyin="Huǒchēzhàn lí zhèlǐ hái yǒu shí fēnzhōng de lùchéng." jyutping="fo2 ce1 zaam6 lei4 ze5 leoi5 waan4 jau5 sap6 fan1 zung1 dik1 lou6 cing4." english="The train station is still a ten-minute trip from here." />

离 measures how much distance or time separates one point from another. In this sentence, it is the time left before departure.

<Hint type="hint">Ask a different question for each word: 给谁 (to whom?), 对谁 (toward whom?), 离哪儿 (how far or how long from what point?).</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">给 and 对 can both appear before a person, but the relationship changes: 给 marks a recipient, while 对 marks who an action, comment, or attitude is directed toward.</Hint>
