You see flowers outside a neighbour’s door and infer there is a celebration. Then you describe the room as pretty as a picture. 모양이다 makes an inference from clues; noun + 같이 makes a comparison.

### Put the Clues Together

Use -는 모양이다 for present actions, -(으)ㄴ 모양이다 for completed actions and present adjectives, and -인 모양이다 after nouns. 있다/없다 use -는: 있는 모양이에요. The clue can be something seen or heard, rather than a direct statement of certainty.

<SentenceBlock formal="문 앞에 꽃이 많습니다. 손님이 오는 모양입니다." formalRom="mun ape kkochi manseumnida. sonnimi oneun moyangimnida." polite="문 앞에 꽃이 많아요. 손님이 오는 모양이에요." politeRom="mun ape kkochi manayo. sonnimi oneun moyangieyo." casual="문 앞에 꽃이 많아. 손님이 오는 모양이야." casualRom="mun ape kkochi mana. sonnimi oneun moyangiya." english="There are lots of flowers by the door. It looks like guests are coming." />

<SentenceBlock formal="불이 꺼진 걸 보니 모두 나간 모양입니다." formalRom="buri kkeojin geol boni modu nagan moyangimnida." polite="불이 꺼진 걸 보니 모두 나간 모양이에요." politeRom="buri kkeojin geol boni modu nagan moyangieyo." casual="불이 꺼진 걸 보니 모두 나간 모양이야." casualRom="buri kkeojin geol boni modu nagan moyangiya." english="Judging by the lights being off, it seems everyone has gone out." />

### Like Something Else

The comparison particle 같이 attaches directly to a noun: 그림같이, 가족같이. It often overlaps with [처럼](/notes/like-you-ended-up), though fixed expressions can favour one form. The separate adverb 같이, meaning together, is written with a space.

<SentenceBlock formal="거실이 그림같이 아름답습니다." formalRom="geosiri geurimgachi areumdapseumnida." polite="거실이 그림같이 아름다워요." politeRom="geosiri geurimgachi areumdawoyo." casual="거실이 그림같이 아름다워." casualRom="geosiri geurimgachi areumdawo." english="The living room is as beautiful as a picture." />

<SentenceBlock formal="우리는 가족같이 지냅니다." formalRom="urineun gajokgachi jinaemnida." polite="우리는 가족같이 지내요." politeRom="urineun gajokgachi jinaeyo." casual="우리는 가족같이 지내." casualRom="urineun gajokgachi jinae." english="We live like a family." />

<Hint type="hint">가족같이 지내요 means live like a family. 가족과 같이 지내요 means live together with one’s family. The particle and spacing help show which job 같이 is doing.</Hint>
