The weather is getting cooler, so you change your weekend plans. -아/어지다 describes a change in a quality. 대신에 can replace one action with another or balance a drawback against a benefit.

### A Quality Changes

Adjective -아/어 form + 지다 means become that way: 좋아지다, 따뜻해지다, 조용해지다. The change may be gradual or quick. Tense belongs on 지다: 좋아졌어요, 좋아질 거예요.

<SentenceBlock formal="아침저녁으로 날씨가 시원해졌습니다." formalRom="achimjeonyeogeuro nalssiga siwonhaejyeotseumnida." polite="아침저녁으로 날씨가 시원해졌어요." politeRom="achimjeonyeogeuro nalssiga siwonhaejyeosseoyo." casual="아침저녁으로 날씨가 시원해졌어." casualRom="achimjeonyeogeuro nalssiga siwonhaejyeosseo." english="The mornings and evenings have become cooler." />

<SentenceBlock formal="운동을 하면 기분이 좋아집니다." formalRom="undongeul hamyeon gibuni joajimnida." polite="운동을 하면 기분이 좋아져요." politeRom="undongeul hamyeon gibuni joajyeoyo." casual="운동을 하면 기분이 좋아져." casualRom="undongeul hamyeon gibuni joajyeo." english="Exercise improves my mood." />

### A Replacement or a Trade-Off

Use -는 대신에 after an action verb and -(으)ㄴ 대신에 after an adjective. A noun takes 대신에 directly: 커피 대신에 차. With actions it often means instead of; with contrasting qualities it can mean in return for that drawback, there is a benefit.

<SentenceBlock formal="산에 가는 대신에 집에서 쉬었습니다." formalRom="sane ganeun daesine jibeseo swieotseumnida." polite="산에 가는 대신에 집에서 쉬었어요." politeRom="sane ganeun daesine jibeseo swieosseoyo." casual="산에 가는 대신에 집에서 쉬었어." casualRom="sane ganeun daesine jibeseo swieosseo." english="I rested at home instead of going to the mountains." />

<SentenceBlock formal="이 방은 작은 대신에 월세가 쌉니다." formalRom="i bangeun jageun daesine wolsega ssamnida." polite="이 방은 작은 대신에 월세가 싸요." politeRom="i bangeun jageun daesine wolsega ssayo." casual="이 방은 작은 대신에 월세가 싸." casualRom="i bangeun jageun daesine wolsega ssa." english="This room is small, but the rent is cheap." />

<Hint type="hint">For a changing adjective use -아/어지다: 조용해졌어요. For coming to do an action use [-게 되다](/notes/like-you-ended-up): 일하게 됐어요. Both describe change, but attach to different kinds of predicate in these uses.</Hint>
