You’re reading a formal announcement about a proposal. It says the committee approved it, then explains that the plan will now move ahead. In reports and official writing, 被……所…… and 为……所…… put the affected person or thing first and name who acted.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这项计划被委员会所批准，预计下个月开始实施。" traditional="這項計劃被委員會所批准，預計下個月開始實施。" pinyin="zhè xiàng jìhuà bèi wěiyuánhuì suǒ pīzhǔn, yùjì xià ge yuè kāishǐ shíshī." jyutping="ze5 hong6 gai3 waak6 bei6 wai2 jyun4 wui6 so2 pai1 zeon2, jyu6 gai3 haa6 go3 jyut6 hoi1 ci2 sat6 si1." english="The plan was approved by the committee and is expected to be implemented next month." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这个结论为学界所接受，后来成了研究的基础。" traditional="這個結論為學界所接受，後來成了研究的基礎。" pinyin="zhège jiélùn wéi xuéjiè suǒ jiēshòu, hòulái chéng le yánjiū de jīchǔ." jyutping="ze5 go3 git3 leon6 wai4 hok6 gaai3 so2 zip3 sau6, hau6 loi4 sing4 liu5 jin4 gau3 dik1 gei1 co2." english="This conclusion was accepted by the academic community and later became the basis for research." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="古桥为洪水所毁，当地居民正在筹款重建。" traditional="古橋為洪水所毀，當地居民正在籌款重建。" pinyin="gǔqiáo wéi hóngshuǐ suǒ huǐ, dāngdì jūmín zhèngzài chóukuǎn chóngjiàn." jyutping="gu2 kiu4 wai4 hung4 seoi2 so2 wai2, dong1 dei6 geoi1 man4 zing3 zoi6 cau4 fun2 cung4 gin3." english="The old bridge was destroyed by the flood, and local residents are raising funds to rebuild it." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他的工作为同事们所认可，负责人特地公开表扬了他。" traditional="他的工作為同事們所認可，負責人特地公開表揚了他。" pinyin="tā de gōngzuò wéi tóngshìmen suǒ rènkě, fùzérén tèdì gōngkāi biǎoyáng le tā." jyutping="taa1 dik1 gung1 zok3 wai4 tung4 si6 mun4 so2 jing6 ho2, fu6 zaak3 jan4 dak6 dei6 gung1 hoi1 biu2 joeng4 liu5 taa1." english="His work was recognized by his colleagues, and the supervisor praised him publicly." />

<Hint type="hint">These forms sound formal and are common in written reports. Use 被……所…… or 为……所…… around the doer and action; in everyday conversation, a shorter 被 sentence is usually more natural.</Hint>
