“If the weather holds, we’ll go hiking; if it turns, we’ll visit the museum instead.” A long sentence can contain more than one relationship. Keep each clause group clear so the listener can follow how the full thoughts fit together.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="虽然会议比计划晚了半小时开始，但是大家先听了客户的意见，然后一起调整了方案。" traditional="雖然會議比計劃晚了半小時開始，但是大家先聽了客戶的意見，然後一起調整了方案。" pinyin="suīrán huìyì bǐ jìhuà wǎn le bàn xiǎoshí kāishǐ, dànshì dàjiā xiān tīng le kèhù de yìjiàn, ránhòu yìqǐ tiáozhěng le fāng'àn." jyutping="seoi1 jin4 wui6 ji5 bei2 gai3 waak6 maan5 liu5 bun3 siu2 si4 hoi1 ci2, daan6 si6 daai6 gaa1 sin1 teng1 liu5 hak3 wu6 dik1 ji3 gin3, jin4 hau6 jat1 hei2 tiu4 zing2 liu5 fong1 on3." english="Although the meeting started half an hour late, everyone first listened to the client and then revised the proposal together." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="如果明天不下雨，我们就去郊外骑车；要是天气不好，就在家做饭看电影。" traditional="如果明天不下雨，我們就去郊外騎車；要是天氣不好，就在家做飯看電影。" pinyin="rúguǒ míngtiān bù xiàyǔ, wǒmen jiù qù jiāowài qíchē; yàoshi tiānqì bù hǎo, jiù zài jiā zuòfàn kàn diànyǐng." jyutping="jyu4 gwo2 ming4 tin1 bat1 haa6 jyu5, ngo5 mun4 zau6 heoi3 gaau1 ngoi6 ke4 ce1; jiu3 si6 tin1 hei3 bat1 hou2, zau6 zoi6 gaa1 zou6 faan6 hon3 din6 jing2." english="If it doesn’t rain tomorrow, we’ll cycle out of town; if the weather is bad, we’ll cook and watch a film at home." />
