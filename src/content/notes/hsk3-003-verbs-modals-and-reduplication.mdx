打开门, 看见朋友, 离开房间: each verb describes a small change in what’s happening. Some two-character verbs can split when an object comes after them. If another complement follows, keep the verb together; let the example show which shape fits.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她昨天看见一个高大的男人。" traditional="她昨天看見一個高大的男人。" pinyin="tā zuótiān kànjiàn yí ge gāodà de nánrén。" jyutping="taa1 zok6 tin1 hon3 gin3 jat1 go3 gou1 daai6 dik1 naam4 jan4。" english="She saw a tall man yesterday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们正要离开的时候下雨了。" traditional="我們正要離開的時候下雨了。" pinyin="wǒmen zhèngyào líkāi de shíhou xià yǔ le。" jyutping="ngo5 mun4 zing3 jiu3 lei4 hoi1 dik1 si4 hau6 haa6 jyu5 zo2。" english="We were just about to leave when it started to rain." />
