The schedule changed repeatedly in a short span: 频频调整. 再度 marks a return to something that happened before; 即 is a formal “that is” or “namely,” often used to clarify a point in writing.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他频频点头表示同意。" traditional="他頻頻點頭表示同意。" pinyin="tā pínpín diǎntóu biǎoshì tóngyì." jyutping="taa1 pan4 pan4 dim2 tau4 biu2 si6 tung4 ji3." english="He nodded repeatedly to show agreement." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他再度当选为班长。" traditional="他再度當選為班長。" pinyin="tā zàidù dāngxuǎn wéi bānzhǎng." jyutping="taa1 zoi3 dou6 dong1 syun2 wai4 baan1 zoeng2." english="He was re-elected as class monitor." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="人生即是一场梦。" traditional="人生即是一場夢。" pinyin="rénshēng jí shì yī chǎng mèng。" jyutping="jan4 sang1 zik1 si6 jat1 coeng4 mung6。" english="Life is a dream." />
