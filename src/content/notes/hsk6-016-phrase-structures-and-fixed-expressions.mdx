“So you’re saying the venue is closed. 算了, let’s go somewhere else.” 就是说 and 这就是说 restate or clarify a point; 算了 lets the speaker drop a plan or complaint. Depending on the moment, it can sound resigned, forgiving, or simply practical.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="要是卖完了，就算了。" traditional="要是賣完了，就算了。" pinyin="yàoshi mài wán le, jiù suàn le." jyutping="jiu3 si6 maai6 jyun4 liu5, zau6 syun3 liu5" english="If it is sold out, never mind." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这就是说，他很忙。" traditional="這就是說，他很忙。" pinyin="zhè jiù shì shuō, tā hěn máng." jyutping="ze3 zau6 si6 syut3, taa1 han2 mong4" english="In other words, he is busy." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天不能去，也就是说，我们得改天再去。" traditional="今天不能去，也就是說，我們得改天再去。" pinyin="jīntiān bù néng qù, yě jiùshì shuō, wǒmen děi gǎitiān zài qù." jyutping="gam1 tin1 bat1 nang4 heoi3, jaa5 zau6 si6 syut3, ngo5 mun4 dak1 goi2 tin1 zoi3 heoi3" english="We cannot go today; that is to say, we will have to go another day." />
