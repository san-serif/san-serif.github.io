These forms help connect a statement to its circumstances or evidence. Verb + 上で means “after doing” or “in doing,” depending on whether it marks sequence or a condition for an action. によると and によれば cite a source for reported information. なぜなら introduces an explanation, usually followed by から or ため. The distinction is useful in instructions, reports, and clear explanations.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="規則[きそく]を確認[かくにん]した上[うえ]で、申[もう]し込[こ]んでください。" romaji="Kisoku o kakunin shita ue de, moushikonde kudasai." english="Please apply after checking the rules." />

Verb past form + 上で can mean “after doing X, then Y,” with a deliberate sequence. Noun + の上で means “in terms of” or “from the standpoint of.” It often signals that the following action should be taken only after the first step is complete.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="公式発表[こうしきはっぴょう]によると、変更[へんこう]は来月[らいげつ]からだそうです。" romaji="Koushiki happyou ni yoru to, henkou wa raigetsu kara da sou desu." english="According to the official announcement, the change begins next month." />

によると and によれば introduce the source of information. They commonly pair with reported forms such as そうだ or ということだ. They cite where the information came from; they do not by themselves prove that the report is true.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="今日[きょう]は参加[さんか]できません。なぜなら、別[べつ]の予定[よてい]があるからです。" romaji="Kyou wa sanka dekimasen. Nazenara, betsu no yotei ga aru kara desu." english="I can't participate today. The reason is that I have another commitment." />

なぜなら puts the reason after the claim, often at the beginning of a new sentence. It usually pairs with から or ため at the end of the explanation, giving the sentence a clear, written structure.

<Hint type="insight">A procedural message can say 申請内容を確認した上で、結果をお知らせします, “we'll notify you of the result after checking the application.” A news summary can say 発表によると, then report what the source stated.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">First do X, then Y: Vた上で. Source of a report: によると / によれば. Explanation after a claim: なぜなら…から.</Hint>
