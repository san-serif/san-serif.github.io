The storm catches you outside, and 连人带行李都淋湿了 (“the person and all the luggage got soaked”). 连A带B bundles related things together; 时A时B shows alternation, while 自A自B can describe people acting independently. Watch how the pattern holds the parts together—or separates them.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="那是一个许多人连听也没有听过的小国。" traditional="那是一個許多人連聽也沒有聽過的小國。" pinyin="nà shì yí ge xǔduō rén lián tīng yě méiyǒu tīng guo de xiǎo guó。" jyutping="naa5 si6 jat1 go3 heoi2 do1 jan4 lin4 teng1 jaa5 mut6 jau5 teng1 gwo3 dik1 siu2 gwok3。" english="That’s a small country many people have never even heard of." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我必须带我的儿子到你的办公室吗？" traditional="我必須帶我的兒子到你的辦公室嗎？" pinyin="wǒ bìxū dài wǒ de érzi dào nǐ de bàngōngshì ma？" jyutping="ngo5 bit1 seoi1 daai3 ngo5 dik1 ji4 zi2 dou3 nei5 dik1 baan6 gung1 sat1 maa3？" english="Do I have to bring my son to your office?" />
