You’re choosing between two cafés. One is cheaper, but the other is just as close. Chinese has a few everyday ways to compare: 比 marks “more/less than,” 不如 says one option doesn’t measure up, and 跟……一样 means “the same as.”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家店比那家店便宜十块钱。" traditional="這家店比那家店便宜十塊錢。" pinyin="zhè jiā diàn bǐ nà jiā diàn piányi shí kuài qián." jyutping="ze5 gaa1 dim3 bei2 naa5 gaa1 dim3 pin4 ji4 sap6 faai3 cin2." english="This shop is ten yuan cheaper than that one." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天比昨天冷三度。" traditional="今天比昨天冷三度。" pinyin="jīntiān bǐ zuótiān lěng sān dù." jyutping="gam1 tin1 bei2 zok3 tin1 laang5 saam1 dou6." english="It’s three degrees colder today than yesterday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="坐地铁不如坐公交方便。" traditional="坐地鐵不如坐公交方便。" pinyin="zuò dìtiě bùrú zuò gōngjiāo fāngbiàn." jyutping="co5 dei6 tit3 bat1 jyu4 co5 gung1 gaau1 fong1 bin6." english="Taking the subway isn’t as convenient as taking the bus." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这个房间跟那个房间一样大。" traditional="這個房間跟那個房間一樣大。" pinyin="zhè ge fángjiān gēn nà ge fángjiān yíyàng dà." jyutping="ze5 go3 fong4 gaan1 gan1 naa5 go3 fong4 gaan1 jat1 joeng6 daai6." english="This room is the same size as that one." />

<Hint type="hint">In a 比 sentence, say the quality first and the amount of difference after it: 便宜十块 (“ten yuan cheaper”). Don’t add 很 before the adjective in this pattern.</Hint>
