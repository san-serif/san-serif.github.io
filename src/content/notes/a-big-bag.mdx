Every adjective you've used has been a predicate: 가방이 커요, "the bag is big." To describe a noun the way English does, "a big bag," attach -ㄴ (vowel-ending stems) or -은 (consonant-ending stems) straight to the dictionary stem instead: 크다 becomes 큰, 높다 becomes 높은. Note this attaches to the plain stem, not the conjugated -아요/-어요 form.

<SentenceBlock formal="서울은 한국에서 가장 큰 도시입니다." formalRom="seoureun hangugeseo gajang keun dosiimnida." polite="서울은 한국에서 가장 큰 도시예요." politeRom="seoureun hangugeseo gajang keun dosiyeyo." casual="서울은 한국에서 가장 큰 도시야." casualRom="seoureun hangugeseo gajang keun dosiya." english="Seoul is the largest city in Korea." />

<SentenceBlock formal="한라산은 높은 산입니다." formalRom="hallasaneun nopeun sanimnida." polite="한라산은 높은 산이에요." politeRom="hallasaneun nopeun sanieyo." casual="한라산은 높은 산이야." casualRom="hallasaneun nopeun saniya." english="Hallasan is a high mountain." />

<SentenceBlock formal="불고기는 맛있는 한국 음식입니다." formalRom="bulgogineun masinneun hanguk eumsigimnida." polite="불고기는 맛있는 한국 음식이에요." politeRom="bulgogineun masinneun hanguk eumsigieyo." casual="불고기는 맛있는 한국 음식이야." casualRom="bulgogineun masinneun hanguk eumsigiya." english="Bulgogi is a delicious Korean food." />

<Hint>있다 and 없다 break the pattern. They take -는, the same modifier ending verbs use, not -ㄴ/은, because Korean treats them as more verb-like than purely descriptive. 맛있는 (delicious) and 재미있는 (interesting) are both built on 있다, so they inherit this.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="안나 씨는 재미있는 영화를 보고 싶어 합니다." formalRom="anna ssineun jaemiinneun yeonghwareul bogo sipeo hamnida." polite="안나 씨는 재미있는 영화를 보고 싶어 해요." politeRom="anna ssineun jaemiinneun yeonghwareul bogo sipeo haeyo." casual="안나 씨는 재미있는 영화를 보고 싶어 해." casualRom="anna ssineun jaemiinneun yeonghwareul bogo sipeo hae." english="Anna wants to watch an interesting movie." />

<SentenceBlock formal="큰 가방 있습니까?" formalRom="keun gabang isseumnikka?" polite="큰 가방 있어요?" politeRom="keun gabang isseoyo?" casual="큰 가방 있어?" casualRom="keun gabang isseo?" english="Do you have a big bag?" />

<SentenceBlock formal="무거운 가방입니다." formalRom="mugeoun gabangimnida." polite="무거운 가방이에요." politeRom="mugeoun gabangieyo." casual="무거운 가방이야." casualRom="mugeoun gabangiya." english="It's a heavy bag." />

<SentenceBlock formal="매운 음식을 좋아합니다." formalRom="maeun eumsigeul joahamnida." polite="매운 음식을 좋아해요." politeRom="maeun eumsigeul joahaeyo." casual="매운 음식을 좋아해." casualRom="maeun eumsigeul joahae." english="I like spicy food." />

<Hint>The ㅂ-irregular adjectives from [무거워요](/notes/heavy-light) keep their irregularity here too: ㅂ becomes 우, which then contracts with -ㄴ into -운. 무겁다 becomes 무거운, not 무겁은. 맵다 becomes 매운, following the same shift.</Hint>
