A friend has one afternoon in your city. You recommend somewhere worth visiting, then explain what you noticed on your own visit. -(으)ㄹ 만하다 offers an evaluation; -던데 recalls an experience that supports the next thought.

### Worth Doing, Good Enough to Try

Use -ㄹ 만하다 after a vowel or ㄹ and -을 만하다 after another consonant: 볼 만하다, 읽을 만하다. It can mean worthwhile, reasonably good, or manageable, depending on the activity and context. Leave a space before 만하다.

<SentenceBlock formal="이 박물관은 한 번 가 볼 만합니다." formalRom="i bangmulgwaneun han beon ga bol manhamnida." polite="이 박물관은 한 번 가 볼 만해요." politeRom="i bangmulgwaneun han beon ga bol manhaeyo." casual="이 박물관은 한 번 가 볼 만해." casualRom="i bangmulgwaneun han beon ga bol manhae." english="This museum is worth visiting once." />

<SentenceBlock formal="이 책은 초급 학생도 읽을 만합니다." formalRom="i chaegeun chogeup haksaengdo ilgeul manhamnida." polite="이 책은 초급 학생도 읽을 만해요." politeRom="i chaegeun chogeup haksaengdo ilgeul manhaeyo." casual="이 책은 초급 학생도 읽을 만해." casualRom="i chaegeun chogeup haksaengdo ilgeul manhae." english="This book is manageable even for beginner students." />

### An Observation Leads to the Next Point

Attach -던데 to a verb or adjective stem, or -(이)던데 to a noun. You recall something you experienced, then offer a related thought, question, or contrast. [-던데요](/notes/i-noticed-when-i-did) can leave that observation as a conversational turn on its own.

<SentenceBlock polite="주말에는 사람이 많던데 아침에 갈까요?" politeRom="jumareneun sarami manteonde achime galkkayo?" casual="주말에는 사람이 많던데 아침에 갈까?" casualRom="jumareneun sarami manteonde achime galkka?" english="I found it busy on weekends; shall we go in the morning?" />

<SentenceBlock polite="전망이 좋던데 창가에 앉을래요?" politeRom="jeonmangi joteonde changgae anjeullaeyo?" casual="전망이 좋던데 창가에 앉을래?" casualRom="jeonmangi joteonde changgae anjeullae?" english="The view was good when I went; would you like a window seat?" />

<Hint type="hint">볼 만해요 can be a measured recommendation: “It is worth seeing.” It can also mean something is tolerable or reasonably good, so context and tone decide how enthusiastic the evaluation is.</Hint>
