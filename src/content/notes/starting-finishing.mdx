はじめる (to start) and おわる (to finish) attach directly to a verb's stem, the part right before ます, turning any verb into "start doing X" or "finish doing X."

<JSentenceBlock furigana="二歳[にさい]の時[とき]、話[はな]しはじめました。" romaji="Nisai no toki, hanashihajimemashita." english="I started talking at age two." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="十三歳[じゅうさんさい]の時[とき]、日本語[にほんご]を勉強[べんきょう]しはじめました。" romaji="Juusansai no toki, nihongo o benkyoushihajimemashita." english="I started studying Japanese at thirteen." />

<Hint>話す becomes 話しはじめる, 勉強する becomes 勉強しはじめる, the stem attaches directly with no extra connector needed, exactly the same mechanism that builds たい and たがる onto a verb.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="部屋[へや]は掃除[そうじ]し終[お]わった？" romaji="Heya wa souji shiowatta?" english="Did you finish cleaning the room?" />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="サンドイッチは作[つく]り終[お]わった？" romaji="Sandoicchi wa tsukuriowatta?" english="Did you finish making the sandwiches?" />

<Hint>終わる only works on verbs that describe something with a natural endpoint, cleaning a room, writing a report, eating a meal. It doesn't attach comfortably to a state or an ongoing condition with no clear finish line, the same restriction that shapes which verbs 〜てしまう prefers later on.</Hint>
