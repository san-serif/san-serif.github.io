这家人历来重视周日的家庭聚餐 (“this family has long valued Sunday lunch”). 历来 and 向来 look back over what has consistently been true; 尚 points to a situation that still remains. One pair follows a pattern through time, the other checks what is true now.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这是历来最大的地震。" traditional="這是歷來最大的地震。" pinyin="zhè shì lìlái zuì dà de dìzhèn." jyutping="ze2 si6 lik6 loi4 zeoi3 daai6 dik1 dei6 zan3." english="This is the largest earthquake on record." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我向来不迟到。" traditional="我向來不遲到。" pinyin="wǒ xiànglái bù chídào." jyutping="ngo5 hoeng3 loi4 bat1 ci4 dou3." english="I am never late." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他在工作中经验尚浅。" traditional="他在工作中經驗尚淺。" pinyin="tā zài gōngzuò zhōng jīngyàn shàng qiǎn。" jyutping="taa1 zoi6 gung1 zok3 zung1 ging1 jim6 soeng6 cin2。" english="He is still green at the job." />
