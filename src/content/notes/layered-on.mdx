에다가 is a beefed-up version of the plain 에 particle, adding a physical sense of depositing one thing onto or into another. Technically you could say 국에 소금을 넣어요 with just 에, but 에다가 is what you'll actually hear, in the kitchen and in casual speech generally.

<SentenceBlock formal="국에다가 소금을 넣습니다." formalRom="gugedaga sogeumeul neoseumnida." polite="국에다가 소금을 넣어요." politeRom="gugedaga sogeumeul neoeoyo." casual="국에다가 소금을 넣어." casualRom="gugedaga sogeumeul neoeo." english="I add salt to the soup." />

<SentenceBlock formal="커피에다가 설탕하고 우유를 넣습니다." formalRom="keopiedaga seoltanghago uyureul neoseumnida." polite="커피에다가 설탕하고 우유를 넣어요." politeRom="keopiedaga seoltanghago uyureul neoeoyo." casual="커피에다가 설탕하고 우유를 넣어." casualRom="keopiedaga seoltanghago uyureul neoeo." english="I add sugar and milk to coffee." />

<SentenceBlock formal="가방에다가 책을 넣습니다." formalRom="gabangedaga chaegeul neoseumnida." polite="가방에다가 책을 넣어요." politeRom="gabangedaga chaegeul neoeoyo." casual="가방에다가 책을 넣어." casualRom="gabangedaga chaegeul neoeo." english="I put books in the bag." />

<SentenceBlock formal="티셔츠에다가 청바지를 입습니다." formalRom="tisyeocheuedaga cheongbajireul ipseumnida." polite="티셔츠에다가 청바지를 입어요." politeRom="tisyeocheuedaga cheongbajireul ibeoyo." casual="티셔츠에다가 청바지를 입어." casualRom="tisyeocheuedaga cheongbajireul ibeo." english="I wear jeans with a t-shirt." />

<Hint>[입다 vs 신다](/notes/dressed-for-it) taught which verb goes with which item. 에다가 answers a different question: what's stacked on top of what. 스웨터에다가 코트를 입어요 isn't just "I wear a coat," it's specifically "over a sweater."</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="스웨터에다가 코트를 입습니다." formalRom="seuweteoedaga koteureul ipseumnida." polite="스웨터에다가 코트를 입어요." politeRom="seuweteoedaga koteureul ibeoyo." casual="스웨터에다가 코트를 입어." casualRom="seuweteoedaga koteureul ibeo." english="I wear a coat over a sweater." />

<SentenceBlock formal="날씨가 추워서 코트에다가 목도리를 합니다." formalRom="nalssiga chuwoseo koteuedaga mokdorireul hamnida." polite="날씨가 추워서 코트에다가 목도리를 해요." politeRom="nalssiga chuwoseo koteuedaga mokdorireul haeyo." casual="날씨가 추워서 코트에다가 목도리를 해." casualRom="nalssiga chuwoseo koteuedaga mokdorireul hae." english="It's cold, so I wear a scarf with a coat." />

<SentenceBlock formal="초밥에다가 간장을 찍습니다." formalRom="chobabedaga ganjangeul jjikseumnida." polite="초밥에다가 간장을 찍어요." politeRom="chobabedaga ganjangeul jjigeoyo." casual="초밥에다가 간장을 찍어." casualRom="chobabedaga ganjangeul jjigeo." english="I dip sushi in soy sauce." />

<Hint>찍다 (to dip) is doing a very different job here than in 사진을 찍어요 (to take a photo) from [-(으)ㄴ 후에](/notes/then-what). Same verb, both describing a quick, precise touch, wildly different context.</Hint>
