At the end of a busy day, you might say the food was delicious, explain that you read the menu twice, and mention that you waited ten minutes. Chinese uses different complements to say how strongly you feel, how many times an action happened, and how long it lasted. When the action has an object, place that object before the repeated-action phrase: 这本书我看了两遍。
