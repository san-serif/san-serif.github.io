大家这说那说了半天，终于定下了计划. A这A那 can suggest scattered actions or details; 左A右B pairs sides or directions; 不怎么 softens a negative description, as in “not particularly busy.”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他并不怎么努力读书，但成绩却很好。" traditional="他並不怎麼努力讀書，但成績卻很好。" pinyin="tā bìng bùzěnme nǔlì dúshū， dàn chéngjì què hěn hǎo。" jyutping="taa1 bing3 bat1 zam2 mo1 nou5 lik6 duk6 syu1， daan6 sing4 zik1 koek3 han2 hou2。" english="He does not study very hard, but his grades are good." />
