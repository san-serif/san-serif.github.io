You know the meeting starts around three, but you haven’t checked the exact time. Chinese has several easy ways to give a rough estimate: put 左右 or 前后 after a number, use 大约/大概 before it, or say two neighboring numbers together.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们下午三点左右开会。" traditional="我們下午三點左右開會。" pinyin="wǒmen xiàwǔ sān diǎn zuǒyòu kāihuì." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 haa6 ng5 saam1 dim2 zo2 jau6 hoi1 wui6." english="We’ll have the meeting at around three in the afternoon." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="教室里大约有四十个学生。" traditional="教室裡大約有四十個學生。" pinyin="jiàoshì lǐ dàyuē yǒu sìshí ge xuésheng." jyutping="gaau3 sat1 lei5 daai6 joek3 jau5 sei3 sap6 go3 hok6 saang1." english="There are about forty students in the classroom." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="从这里到车站要二十分钟前后。" traditional="從這裡到車站要二十分鐘前後。" pinyin="cóng zhèlǐ dào chēzhàn yào èrshí fēnzhōng qiánhòu." jyutping="cung4 ze5 leoi5 dou3 ce1 zaam6 jiu3 ji6 sap6 fan1 zung1 cin4 hau6." english="It takes about twenty minutes to get from here to the station." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="新学校有两三百名学生。" traditional="新學校有兩三百名學生。" pinyin="xīn xuéxiào yǒu liǎng sān bǎi míng xuésheng." jyutping="san1 hok6 haau6 jau5 loeng5 saam1 baak3 ming4 hok6 saang1." english="The new school has two or three hundred students." />

<Hint type="hint">左右 usually follows the number: 三点左右. 大约 and 大概 come before it: 大约四十个. When you say neighboring numbers like 两三百, the estimate is a range rather than an exact total.</Hint>
