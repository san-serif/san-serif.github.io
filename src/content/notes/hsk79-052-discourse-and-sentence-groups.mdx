“It’s late, it’s raining, so I’ll stay home.” A linking word makes the relationship between thoughts easy to hear. When the story is already clear, Chinese can leave the link unstated. Either way, follow how each sentence adds to, follows from, or changes the one before it.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="天色已经晚了，而且外面还在下雨，所以我们决定留在家里。" traditional="天色已經晚了，而且外面還在下雨，所以我們決定留在家裡。" pinyin="tiānsè yǐjīng wǎn le, érqiě wàimiàn hái zài xiàyǔ, suǒyǐ wǒmen juédìng liú zài jiālǐ." jyutping="tin1 sik1 ji5 ging1 maan5 liu5, ji4 ce2 ngoi6 min6 waan4 zoi6 haa6 jyu5, so2 ji5 ngo5 mun4 kyut3 ding6 lau4 zoi6 gaa1 leoi5." english="It’s already late, and it’s still raining outside, so we’ve decided to stay home." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我先去超市买菜，回来以后再做饭。" traditional="我先去超市買菜，回來以後再做飯。" pinyin="wǒ xiān qù chāoshì mǎi cài, huílai yǐhòu zài zuò fàn." jyutping="ngo5 sin1 heoi3 ciu1 si5 maai5 coi3, wui4 loi4 ji5 hau6 zoi3 zou6 faan6." english="I’ll go to the supermarket to buy groceries first, then cook when I get back." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我把门打开了。外面一个人也没有。" traditional="我把門打開了。外面一個人也沒有。" pinyin="wǒ bǎ mén dǎkāi le. Wàimiàn yí ge rén yě méiyǒu." jyutping="ngo5 baa2 mun4 daa2 hoi1 liu5. ngoi6 min6 jat1 go3 jan4 jaa5 mut6 jau5." english="I opened the door. There wasn’t anyone outside." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他准备了很久，最后还是决定放弃。" traditional="他準備了很久，最後還是決定放棄。" pinyin="tā zhǔnbèi le hěn jiǔ, zuìhòu háishi juédìng fàngqì." jyutping="taa1 zeon2 bei6 liu5 han2 gau2, zeoi3 hau6 waan4 si6 kyut3 ding6 fong3 hei3." english="He prepared for a long time, but in the end decided to give up." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="去机场可以坐公交，也可以坐地铁；两种办法都很方便。" traditional="去機場可以坐公交，也可以坐地鐵；兩種辦法都很方便。" pinyin="qù jīchǎng kěyǐ zuò gōngjiāo, yě kěyǐ zuò dìtiě; liǎng zhǒng bànfǎ dōu hěn fāngbiàn." jyutping="heoi3 gei1 coeng4 ho2 ji5 zo6 gung1 gaau1, jaa5 ho2 ji5 zo6 dei6 tit3; loeng5 zung2 baan6 faat3 dou1 han2 fong1 bin6." english="You can take a bus or the subway to the airport; both options are convenient." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这次活动有演讲，也有游戏，最后还有抽奖；总的来说，大家都玩得很开心。" traditional="這次活動有演講，也有遊戲，最後還有抽獎；總的來說，大家都玩得很開心。" pinyin="zhè cì huódòng yǒu yǎnjiǎng, yě yǒu yóuxì, zuìhòu hái yǒu chōujiǎng; zǒng de lái shuō, dàjiā dōu wán de hěn kāixīn." jyutping="ze5 ci3 wu6 dung6 jau5 jin2 gong2, jaa5 jau5 jau4 hei3, zeoi3 hau6 waan4 jau5 cau1 zoeng2; zung2 dik1 loi4 syut3, daai6 gaa1 dou1 waan2 dak1 han2 hoi1 sam1." english="The event had talks and games, followed by a prize draw; overall, everyone had a great time." />

<Hint type="hint">Listen for the relationship across the whole group: 而且 adds a point, 所以 gives a result, 以后 orders events, 也可以 offers another option, and 总的来说 gathers earlier details into a summary. When a linker is missing, the listener works out the connection from context.</Hint>
