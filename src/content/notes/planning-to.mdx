-(으)려고 하다 states an intention: -려고 하다 after a vowel or ㄹ-ending stem, -으려고 하다 after a consonant-ending one. Notice the 하다 tacked onto the end, the same auxiliary-verb trick from I Want To's 고 싶어 하다. Korean builds a lot of grammar this way, bolting 하다 onto a fixed phrase instead of inventing a whole new ending.

<SentenceBlock formal="제주도에 가려고 합니다." formalRom="jejudoe garyeogo hamnida." polite="제주도에 가려고 해요." politeRom="jejudoe garyeogo haeyo." casual="제주도에 가려고 해." casualRom="jejudoe garyeogo hae." english="I plan to go to Jeju Island." />

<SentenceBlock formal="청바지를 입으려고 합니다." formalRom="cheongbajireul ibeuryeogo hamnida." polite="청바지를 입으려고 해요." politeRom="cheongbajireul ibeuryeogo haeyo." casual="청바지를 입으려고 해." casualRom="cheongbajireul ibeuryeogo hae." english="I intend to wear jeans." />

<SentenceBlock formal="책을 많이 읽으려고 합니다." formalRom="chaegeul mani ilgeuryeogo hamnida." polite="책을 많이 읽으려고 해요." politeRom="chaegeul mani ilgeuryeogo haeyo." casual="책을 많이 읽으려고 해." casualRom="chaegeul mani ilgeuryeogo hae." english="I plan to read many books." />

<Hint>-(으)려고 해요 and -(으)ㄹ 거예요 both point at the future, but -(으)려고 해요 leaves more room for change of mind, it's what you're currently planning, not a locked commitment. 여행을 가려고 해요 reads closer to "I'm thinking of traveling"; 여행을 갈 거예요 reads like the ticket's already booked.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="호수에서 배를 타려고 합니다." formalRom="hosueseo baereul taryeogo hamnida." polite="호수에서 배를 타려고 해요." politeRom="hosueseo baereul taryeogo haeyo." casual="호수에서 배를 타려고 해." casualRom="hosueseo baereul taryeogo hae." english="I plan to ride a boat on the lake." />

<SentenceBlock formal="이번 휴가에 여행을 가려고 합니다." formalRom="ibeon hyugae yeohaengeul garyeogo hamnida." polite="이번 휴가에 여행을 가려고 해요." politeRom="ibeon hyugae yeohaengeul garyeogo haeyo." casual="이번 휴가에 여행을 가려고 해." casualRom="ibeon hyugae yeohaengeul garyeogo hae." english="I plan to go on a trip this vacation." />

<Hint>여권, the first word in the vocabulary below, is a reminder that this whole grammar point shows up constantly in travel planning specifically. 짐을 싸려고 해요 (I'm planning to pack), 환전하려고 해요 (I'm planning to exchange money), the pattern is the default way Koreans talk through a trip before it's locked in.</Hint>
