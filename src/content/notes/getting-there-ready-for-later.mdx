Your application is nearly finished, and you have saved copies of the documents for later. -아/어 가다 follows progress toward a point ahead; -아/어 두다 keeps the result of an action available for when you need it.

### Progress Toward What Comes Next

Use the -아/어 form plus 가다 for a process developing forward: 끝나 가다, 알아 가다, 닮아 가다. It can describe approaching completion or an ongoing change. The ending on 가다 carries tense and speech level.

### Keep It Ready for Later

Add 두다 to the -아/어 form: 저장해 두다, 예약해 두다, 사 두다. It maintains the result, often in preparation for something later. 두어요 commonly contracts to 둬요, and 두었어요 to 뒀어요.

<SentenceBlock formal="서류를 미리 복사해 두었습니다." formalRom="seoryureul miri boksahae dueotseumnida." polite="서류를 미리 복사해 두었어요." politeRom="seoryureul miri boksahae dueosseoyo." casual="서류를 미리 복사해 두었어." casualRom="seoryureul miri boksahae dueosseo." english="I made copies of the documents in advance." />

<SentenceBlock formal="면접 장소를 지도에 저장해 두십시오." formalRom="myeonjeop jangsoreul jidoe jeojanghae dusipsio." polite="면접 장소를 지도에 저장해 두세요." politeRom="myeonjeop jangsoreul jidoe jeojanghae duseyo." casual="면접 장소를 지도에 저장해 둬." casualRom="myeonjeop jangsoreul jidoe jeojanghae dwo." english="Save the interview location on your map for later." />

<Hint type="hint">[-아/어 놓다](/notes/leave-it-ready-then) and -아/어 두다 overlap in leaving a result in place. 두다 often foregrounds keeping it for later use; this is a tendency rather than a strict boundary.</Hint>
