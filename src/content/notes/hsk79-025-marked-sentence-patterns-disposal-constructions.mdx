The baby is finally asleep when a loud bang comes from the kitchen. The noise makes the baby cry. With this causative 把 pattern, the cause comes first, 把 introduces the person or thing affected, and the verb describes the change it causes.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="厨房里的一声巨响把孩子吓哭了。" traditional="廚房裡的一聲巨響把孩子嚇哭了。" pinyin="chúfáng lǐ de yì shēng jùxiǎng bǎ háizi xià kū le." jyutping="cyu4 fong2 leoi5 dik1 jat1 sing1 geoi6 hoeng2 baa2 haai4 zi2 haak3 huk1 liu5." english="A loud bang from the kitchen scared the child into crying." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="一阵风把桌上的文件吹散了。" traditional="一陣風把桌上的文件吹散了。" pinyin="yí zhèn fēng bǎ zhuōshàng de wénjiàn chuī sàn le." jyutping="jat1 zan6 fung1 baa2 zoek3 soeng6 dik1 man4 gin6 ceoi1 saan3 liu5." english="A gust of wind scattered the papers on the table." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他讲了个笑话，把大家都逗笑了。" traditional="他講了個笑話，把大家都逗笑了。" pinyin="tā jiǎng le ge xiàohua, bǎ dàjiā dōu dòu xiào le." jyutping="taa1 gong2 liu5 go3 siu3 waa2, baa2 daai6 gaa1 dou1 dau6 siu3 liu5." english="He told a joke that made everyone laugh." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="突然响起的警报把整栋楼的人都叫醒了。" traditional="突然響起的警報把整棟樓的人都叫醒了。" pinyin="tūrán xiǎngqǐ de jǐngbào bǎ zhěng dòng lóu de rén dōu jiào xǐng le." jyutping="dat6 jin4 hoeng2 hei2 dik1 ging2 bou3 baa2 zing2 dung6 lau4 dik1 jan4 dou1 giu3 sing2 liu5." english="The sudden alarm woke everyone in the building." />

<Hint type="hint">This 把 sentence starts with the cause and names the person or thing that changes after 把. The verb phrase needs a clear outcome, such as 吓哭, 吹散, 逗笑, or 叫醒.</Hint>
