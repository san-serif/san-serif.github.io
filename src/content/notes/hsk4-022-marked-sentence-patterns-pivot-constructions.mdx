The class votes for a new chair, and the winner now has a job to do. In these pivot sentences, the person after the first verb becomes part of the next action: someone praises the student for winning, names a child, or elects a chair. The next verb phrase shows the role they step into.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="老师表扬他做得好。" traditional="老師表揚他做得好。" pinyin="lǎoshī biǎoyáng tā zuò de hǎo。" jyutping="lou5 si1 biu2 joeng4 taa1 zou6 dak1 hou2。" english="The teacher praised him for doing well." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="一些人批评了我们的决定。" traditional="一些人批評了我們的決定。" pinyin="yìxiē rén pīpíng le wǒmen de juédìng。" jyutping="jat1 se1 jan4 pai1 ping4 liu5 ngo5 mun4 dik1 kyut3 ding6。" english="Some people criticized our decision." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我叫他来的。" traditional="我叫他來的。" pinyin="wǒ jiào tā lái de。" jyutping="ngo5 giu3 taa1 loi4 dik1。" english="I told him to come." />
