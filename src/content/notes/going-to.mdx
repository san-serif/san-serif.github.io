-(으)ㄹ 거예요 states future intention: vowel-ending stems take -ㄹ 거예요, consonant-ending stems take -을 거예요. It's the concrete version of "will" or "going to," a decided plan rather than a hope.

<SentenceBlock formal="이번 주말에 뭐 할 겁니까?" formalRom="ibeon jumare mwo hal geomnikka?" polite="이번 주말에 뭐 할 거예요?" politeRom="ibeon jumare mwo hal geoyeyo?" casual="이번 주말에 뭐 할 거야?" casualRom="ibeon jumare mwo hal geoya?" english="What are you going to do this weekend?" />

<SentenceBlock formal="백화점에서 쇼핑할 겁니다." formalRom="baekhwajeomeseo syopinghal geomnida." polite="백화점에서 쇼핑할 거예요." politeRom="baekhwajeomeseo syopinghal geoyeyo." casual="백화점에서 쇼핑할 거야." casualRom="baekhwajeomeseo syopinghal geoya." english="I'm going to shop at the department store." />

<SentenceBlock formal="저녁에 책을 읽을 겁니다." formalRom="jeonyeoge chaegeul ilgeul geomnida." polite="저녁에 책을 읽을 거예요." politeRom="jeonyeoge chaegeul ilgeul geoyeyo." casual="저녁에 책을 읽을 거야." casualRom="jeonyeoge chaegeul ilgeul geoya." english="I will read a book in the evening." />

<Hint>[고 싶어요](/notes/i-want-to) states a wish, no commitment attached. -(으)ㄹ 거예요 states a plan. 여행을 가고 싶어요 is "I want to travel," someday, maybe. 여행을 갈 거예요 is "I'm going to travel," it's decided.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="공원에서 자전거를 탈 겁니다." formalRom="gongwoneseo jajeongeoreul tal geomnida." polite="공원에서 자전거를 탈 거예요." politeRom="gongwoneseo jajeongeoreul tal geoyeyo." casual="공원에서 자전거를 탈 거야." casualRom="gongwoneseo jajeongeoreul tal geoya." english="I'm going to ride a bike in the park." />

<SentenceBlock formal="내일 신발을 살 겁니다." formalRom="naeil sinbareul sal geomnida." polite="내일 신발을 살 거예요." politeRom="naeil sinbareul sal geoyeyo." casual="내일 신발을 살 거야." casualRom="naeil sinbareul sal geoya." english="I will buy shoes tomorrow." />

<SentenceBlock formal="바다에서 수영할 겁니다." formalRom="badaeseo suyeonghal geomnida." polite="바다에서 수영할 거예요." politeRom="badaeseo suyeonghal geoyeyo." casual="바다에서 수영할 거야." casualRom="badaeseo suyeonghal geoya." english="I'm going to swim in the ocean." />

<Hint>The same ending covers predictions too, not just personal plans. 내일 비가 올 거예요, "it will probably rain tomorrow," uses the identical grammar even though weather doesn't have intentions. Anything you're stating with confidence about the future, plan or forecast, reaches for -(으)ㄹ 거예요.</Hint>
