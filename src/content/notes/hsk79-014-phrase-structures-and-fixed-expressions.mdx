The meeting room is dim, a teammate is fed up with being asked to help, and there’s little time left to finish the plan. These compact phrases help you describe mixed qualities, wave off an unwanted choice, and make a small amount of time count. The middle expression can sound sharp, so pay attention to the relationship between the speakers.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家咖啡馆的灯光半明半暗，坐久了也不觉得刺眼。" traditional="這家咖啡館的燈光半明半暗，坐久了也不覺得刺眼。" pinyin="zhè jiā kāfēiguǎn de dēngguāng bàn míng bàn àn, zuò jiǔ le yě bù juéde cìyǎn." jyutping="ze5 gaa1 gaa3 fei1 gun2 dik1 dang1 gwong1 bun3 ming4 bun3 am3, co5 gau2 liu5 jaa5 bat1 gok3 dak1 ci3 ngaan5." english="The café lighting is half bright and half dim; even after sitting awhile, it doesn’t hurt your eyes." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这事你爱管不管，我已经把情况告诉你了。" traditional="這事你愛管不管，我已經把情況告訴你了。" pinyin="zhè shì nǐ ài guǎn bù guǎn, wǒ yǐjīng bǎ qíngkuàng gàosu nǐ le." jyutping="ze5 si6 nei5 oi3 gun2 bat1 gun2, ngo5 ji5 ging1 baa2 cing4 fong3 gou3 sou3 nei5 liu5." english="You can get involved or not as you like; I’ve already told you what’s going on." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="会议只剩一点儿时间，咱们先把最重要的问题谈完。" traditional="會議只剩一點兒時間，咱們先把最重要的問題談完。" pinyin="huìyì zhǐ shèng yìdiǎnr shíjiān, zánmen xiān bǎ zuì zhòngyào de wèntí tán wán." jyutping="wui6 ji5 zi2 sing6 jat1 dim2 ji4 si4 gaan3, zaam1 mun4 sin1 baa2 zeoi3 zung6 jiu3 dik1 man6 tai4 taam4 jyun4." english="There’s only a little time left in the meeting, so let’s finish discussing the most important issue first." />

<Hint type="hint">半明半暗 pairs opposite qualities; 爱管不管 repeats the same verb with 爱…不… to signal indifference; 一点儿时间 uses a number and measure word with an abstract noun. 爱A不A can sound dismissive, so avoid it when you want to sound polite.</Hint>
