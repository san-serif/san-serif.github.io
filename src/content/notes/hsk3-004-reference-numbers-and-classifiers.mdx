You’ve saved seats for your friends, but they can arrive whenever they like. A question word such as 谁 or 什么 can pair with 都 to mean “whoever” or “whatever.” Use 各, 每, and 任何 to talk about each member, every kind, or any person.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="谁先到，谁就先进去；我们给每个人都留了座位。" traditional="誰先到，誰就先進去；我們給每個人都留了座位。" pinyin="shéi xiān dào, shéi jiù xiān jìnqù; wǒmen gěi měi ge rén dōu liú le zuòwèi." jyutping="seoi4 sin1 dou3, seoi4 zau6 sin1 zeon3 heoi3; ngo5 mun4 kap1 mui5 go3 jan4 dou1 lau4 liu5 zo6 wai2." english="Whoever arrives first can go in first; we saved a seat for everyone." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你想吃什么，我们都可以做。" traditional="你想吃什麼，我們都可以做。" pinyin="nǐ xiǎng chī shénme, wǒmen dōu kěyǐ zuò." jyutping="nei5 soeng2 hek3 sam6 mo1, ngo5 mun4 dou1 ho2 ji5 zou6." english="Whatever you want to eat, we can make it." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你什么时候来都行，每个人的名字都在名单上。" traditional="你什麼時候來都行，每個人的名字都在名單上。" pinyin="nǐ shénme shíhou lái dōu xíng, měi ge rén de míngzi dōu zài míngdān shàng." jyutping="nei5 sam6 mo1 si4 hau6 loi4 dou1 hang4, mui5 go3 jan4 dik1 meng4 zi6 dou1 zoi6 ming4 daan1 soeng6." english="You can come whenever you like; everyone’s name is on the list." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="任何人都能报名，各种水平的学生都欢迎。" traditional="任何人都能報名，各種水平的學生都歡迎。" pinyin="rènhé rén dōu néng bàomíng, gèzhǒng shuǐpíng de xuésheng dōu huānyíng." jyutping="jam6 ho4 jan4 dou1 nang4 bou3 meng2, gok3 zung2 seoi2 ping4 dik1 hok6 saang1 dou1 fun1 jing4." english="Anyone can sign up; students of every level are welcome." />

<Hint type="hint">The first 谁/什么/什么时候 is the open choice; 都 makes the statement cover every possible answer. In the last example, 任何 already means “any,” so 都 adds an all-inclusive emphasis.</Hint>
