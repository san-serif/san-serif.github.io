として attaches after a noun and names the role, capacity, or category something is acting in, "as X," "in the capacity of X."

<JSentenceBlock furigana="沙耶[さや]さんは、アナウンサーとして働[はたら]いています。" romaji="Saya-san wa, anaunsaa toshite hataraite imasu." english="Saya works as a news announcer." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="直樹[なおき]さんは、国際[こくさい]弁護士[べんごし]として働[はたら]いています。" romaji="Naoki-san wa, kokusai bengoshi toshite hataraite imasu." english="Naoki works as an international lawyer." />

<Hint>として specifically names the role someone is filling, distinct from just describing what they are. "Working として an announcer" frames it as a professional capacity, the position they occupy, rather than simply stating a fact about their identity.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="このナイフはフォークとしても使[つか]えるんです。" romaji="Kono naifu wa fooku toshite mo tsukaeru n desu." english="This knife can also be used as a fork." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="この箱[はこ]はベッドとして使[つか]えるんです。" romaji="Kono hako wa beddo toshite tsukaeru n desu." english="This box can be used as a bed." />

<Hint>として works just as naturally with objects as with people, describing a secondary or alternate function something can serve, "usable as a fork," beyond its original intended purpose.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="栃木県[とちぎけん]は「いちごをたくさん作[つく]っている県[けん]」として知[し]られているそうです。" romaji="Tochigi-ken wa 'ichigo o takusan tsukutte iru ken' toshite shirarete iru sou desu." english="Apparently Tochigi Prefecture is known as 'the prefecture that grows a lot of strawberries.'" />

<Hint>としてよく知られている, "well known as," is a fixed pattern for describing reputation, pairing として with the passive 知られる to state what something is famous for being.</Hint>
