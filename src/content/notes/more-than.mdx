보다 attaches to whatever you're comparing against, "than [noun]." 더 (more) is optional, tucked in right before the adjective for emphasis, but the comparison already works without it.

<SentenceBlock formal="수박이 딸기보다 비쌉니다." formalRom="subagi ttalgiboda bissamnida." polite="수박이 딸기보다 비싸요." politeRom="subagi ttalgiboda bissayo." casual="수박이 딸기보다 비싸." casualRom="subagi ttalgiboda bissa." english="Watermelon is more expensive than strawberries." />

<SentenceBlock formal="호주가 한국보다 더 큽니다." formalRom="hojuga hangukboda deo keumnida." polite="호주가 한국보다 더 커요." politeRom="hojuga hangukboda deo keoyo." casual="호주가 한국보다 더 커." casualRom="hojuga hangukboda deo keo." english="Australia is bigger than Korea." />

<Hint>It's easy to flip which noun takes 보다. The thing serving as the baseline gets 보다; the thing that wins the comparison gets 이/가. 호주가 한국보다 커요, Australia (이/가) is bigger than Korea (보다). Swap them and you've reversed the meaning entirely.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="여름이 겨울보다 좋습니다." formalRom="yeoreumi gyeoulboda joseumnida." polite="여름이 겨울보다 좋아요." politeRom="yeoreumi gyeoulboda joayo." casual="여름이 겨울보다 좋아." casualRom="yeoreumi gyeoulboda joa." english="I like summer more than winter." />

<SentenceBlock formal="지하철이 버스보다 더 편합니다." formalRom="jihacheori beoseuboda deo pyeonhamnida." polite="지하철이 버스보다 더 편해요." politeRom="jihacheori beoseuboda deo pyeonhaeyo." casual="지하철이 버스보다 더 편해." casualRom="jihacheori beoseuboda deo pyeonhae." english="The subway is more comfortable than the bus." />

<SentenceBlock formal="삼계탕이 불고기보다 더 맛있습니다." formalRom="samgyetangi bulgogiboda deo masisseumnida." polite="삼계탕이 불고기보다 더 맛있어요." politeRom="samgyetangi bulgogiboda deo masisseoyo." casual="삼계탕이 불고기보다 더 맛있어." casualRom="samgyetangi bulgogiboda deo masisseo." english="Samgyetang is more delicious than bulgogi." />

<SentenceBlock formal="저는 축구를 수영보다 더 좋아합니다." formalRom="jeoneun chukgureul suyeongboda deo joahamnida." polite="저는 축구를 수영보다 더 좋아해요." politeRom="jeoneun chukgureul suyeongboda deo joahaeyo." casual="나는 축구를 수영보다 더 좋아해." casualRom="naneun chukgureul suyeongboda deo joahae." english="I like soccer more than swimming." />

<SentenceBlock formal="숙제가 생각보다 어렵습니다." formalRom="sukjega saenggakboda eoryeopseumnida." polite="숙제가 생각보다 어려워요." politeRom="sukjega saenggakboda eoryeowoyo." casual="숙제가 생각보다 어려워." casualRom="sukjega saenggakboda eoryeowo." english="The homework is harder than I thought." />

<Hint>생각보다 isn't comparing against another noun at all, 생각 just means "what I expected." It's one of the most common uses of 보다 in casual speech: less about ranking two things and more about a reality check against your own assumption.</Hint>
