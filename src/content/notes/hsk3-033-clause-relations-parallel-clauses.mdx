Your friend asks about a little café you found. You liked that it was quiet and cozy, so you can give both reasons the same weight with 又……又……. It also works when one person does two things at once.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家咖啡馆又安静又舒服。" traditional="這家咖啡館又安靜又舒服。" pinyin="zhè jiā kāfēiguǎn yòu ānjìng yòu shūfu." jyutping="ze5 gaa1 gaa3 fe1 gun2 jau6 on1 zing6 jau6 syu1 fu." english="This café is both quiet and comfortable." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她又会做饭，又会开车。" traditional="她又會做飯，又會開車。" pinyin="tā yòu huì zuò fàn, yòu huì kāi chē." jyutping="taa1 jau6 wui5 zou6 faan6, jau6 wui5 hoi1 ce1." english="She can both cook and drive." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="周末我们又看电影又吃火锅。" traditional="週末我們又看電影又吃火鍋。" pinyin="zhōumò wǒmen yòu kàn diànyǐng yòu chī huǒguō." jyutping="zau1 mut6 ngo5 mun4 jau6 hon3 din6 jing2 jau6 hek3 fo2 wo1." english="At the weekend, we watched a movie and ate hotpot." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="孩子又哭又笑，大家都不知道怎么办。" traditional="孩子又哭又笑，大家都不知道怎麼辦。" pinyin="háizi yòu kū yòu xiào, dàjiā dōu bù zhīdào zěnme bàn." jyutping="haai4 zi2 jau6 huk1 jau6 siu3, daai6 gaa1 dou1 m4 zi1 dou3 zam2 mo1 baan6." english="The child was laughing and crying, and no one knew what to do." />

<Hint type="hint">Repeat 又 before each of the two qualities or actions. The pattern presents them as a pair; it does not mean that the second one happened later.</Hint>
