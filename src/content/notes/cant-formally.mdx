-지 않다 and -지 못하다 are the long-form versions of 안 and 못. They attach directly to the stem the same way -지만 does, no vowel harmony to check. 안 가요 and 가지 않아요 mean exactly the same thing; the long form just reads more formal, closer to written Korean than spoken.

<SentenceBlock formal="비행기표가 너무 비싸서 올해 한국에 가지 못합니다." formalRom="bihaenggipyoga neomu bissaseo olhae hanguge gaji mothamnida." polite="비행기표가 너무 비싸서 올해 한국에 가지 못해요." politeRom="bihaenggipyoga neomu bissaseo olhae hanguge gaji mothaeyo." casual="비행기표가 너무 비싸서 올해 한국에 가지 못해." casualRom="bihaenggipyoga neomu bissaseo olhae hanguge gaji mothae." english="Tickets are too expensive, so I can't go to Korea this year." />

<SentenceBlock formal="제 방은 큰 창문이 있어서 어둡지 않습니다." formalRom="je bangeun keun changmuni isseoseo eodupji anseumnida." polite="제 방은 큰 창문이 있어서 어둡지 않아요." politeRom="je bangeun keun changmuni isseoseo eodupji anayo." casual="내 방은 큰 창문이 있어서 어둡지 않아." casualRom="nae bangeun keun changmuni isseoseo eodupji ana." english="My room has a big window, so it's not dark." />

<SentenceBlock formal="매일 청소해서 지저분하지 않습니다." formalRom="maeil cheongsohaeseo jijeobunhaji anseumnida." polite="매일 청소해서 지저분하지 않아요." politeRom="maeil cheongsohaeseo jijeobunhaji anayo." casual="매일 청소해서 지저분하지 않아." casualRom="maeil cheongsohaeseo jijeobunhaji ana." english="I clean daily, so it's not messy." />

<SentenceBlock formal="너무 무거워서 혼자 버리지 못합니다." formalRom="neomu mugeowoseo honja beoriji mothamnida." polite="너무 무거워서 혼자 버리지 못해요." politeRom="neomu mugeowoseo honja beoriji mothaeyo." casual="너무 무거워서 혼자 버리지 못해." casualRom="neomu mugeowoseo honja beoriji mothae." english="It's too heavy, so I can't throw it away alone." />

<Hint>This long form shows up constantly in exactly the settings [-습니다](/notes/when-it-counts) gets used for: news reports, official notices, formal writing. The short forms 안/못 and the long forms -지 않다/-지 못하다 aren't tied to a specific register by grammar rule, but in practice they cluster with the same formal-versus-casual split.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="완두콩을 전혀 먹지 못합니다." formalRom="wandukongeul jeonhyeo meokji mothamnida." polite="완두콩을 전혀 먹지 못해요." politeRom="wandukongeul jeonhyeo meokji mothaeyo." casual="완두콩을 전혀 먹지 못해." casualRom="wandukongeul jeonhyeo meokji mothae." english="I can't eat peas at all." />

<Hint>전혀 ("not at all") almost always demands a negative verb right after it. You'll essentially never see 전혀 in a positive sentence; it exists specifically to intensify a denial, the Korean equivalent of "not even a little."</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="냄새가 강해서 양고기를 먹지 않습니다." formalRom="naemsaega ganghaeseo yanggogireul meokji anseumnida." polite="냄새가 강해서 양고기를 먹지 않아요." politeRom="naemsaega ganghaeseo yanggogireul meokji anayo." casual="냄새가 강해서 양고기를 먹지 않아." casualRom="naemsaega ganghaeseo yanggogireul meokji ana." english="I don't eat lamb because the smell is strong." />
