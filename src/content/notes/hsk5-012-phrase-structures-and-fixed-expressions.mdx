“If you say you’re leaving, just go.” 说走就走 makes the action feel immediate. 有A有B pairs two things someone has or offers, while 不得了 turns the dial up on an intense or extraordinary description.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我把他叫做「矮子」，他就气得不得了。" traditional="我把他叫做「矮子」，他就氣得不得了。" pinyin="wǒ bǎ tā jiàozuò 「 ǎizi 」， tā jiù qì de bùdéliǎo。" jyutping="ngo5 baa2 taa1 giu3 zou6 「 ai2 zi2 」， taa1 zau6 hei3 dak1 bat1 dak1 liu5。" english="I called him “Shorty,” and he got extremely angry." />
