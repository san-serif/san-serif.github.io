You text a friend: “I’ll call you as soon as I get home.” Put 一 before the first action and 就 before what happens right after it. The two actions can have the same subject or different ones.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我一到家就给你打电话。" traditional="我一到家就給你打電話。" pinyin="wǒ yí dào jiā jiù gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà." jyutping="ngo5 jat1 dou3 gaa1 zau6 kap1 nei5 daa2 din6 waa2." english="I’ll call you as soon as I get home." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他一听到这个消息就笑了。" traditional="他一聽到這個消息就笑了。" pinyin="tā yì tīngdào zhège xiāoxi jiù xiào le." jyutping="taa1 jat1 teng1 dou3 ze5 go3 siu1 sik1 zau6 siu3 liu5." english="As soon as he heard the news, he laughed." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="孩子们一看见老师，就跑过去了。" traditional="孩子們一看見老師，就跑過去了。" pinyin="háizimen yí kànjiàn lǎoshī, jiù pǎo guòqù le." jyutping="haai4 zi2 mun4 jat1 hon3 gin3 lou5 si1, zau6 paau2 gwo3 heoi3 liu5." english="As soon as the children saw the teacher, they ran over." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你一按这个按钮，灯就亮了。" traditional="你一按這個按鈕，燈就亮了。" pinyin="nǐ yí àn zhège ànniǔ, dēng jiù liàng le." jyutping="nei5 jat1 on3 ze5 go3 on3 nau2, dang1 zau6 loeng6 liu5." english="As soon as you press this button, the light comes on." />

<Hint type="hint">一 and 就 frame the two events in order: the first happens, then the second follows at once. The subject may change after 就, as it does in the example about the children and their teacher.</Hint>
