You watched a character hide a letter, and later everything in the story changed. -더니 connects a remembered observation with what followed. -는 것이다 then gives the explanation that makes the plot fit together.

### An Observation and What Followed

Stem + -더니 recalls a situation you observed, then adds a change, contrast, or result: 울더니 웃어요, 열심히 연습하더니…. In the basic observed-action pattern, both clauses concern the same subject. For your own deliberate action and its result, [-았/었더니](/notes/i-noticed-when-i-did) is usually the relevant form.

### Bring a Story Moment Into Focus

-는 것이다 can draw attention to a fact or moment as you tell a story: 지갑이 없는 거예요. Use -는 for present actions and 있다/없다, -(으)ㄴ for a completed action or present adjective, and -인 for a noun. 거예요 contracts 것이에요. It can also explain or interpret a situation, but in a story it helps the listener picture what you discovered.

<SentenceBlock formal="택시에서 내리려고 하는데 지갑이 없는 것입니다." formalRom="taeksieseo naeriryeogo haneunde jigabi eomneun geosimnida." polite="택시에서 내리려고 하는데 지갑이 없는 거예요." politeRom="taeksieseo naeriryeogo haneunde jigabi eomneun geoyeyo." casual="택시에서 내리려고 하는데 지갑이 없는 거야." casualRom="taeksieseo naeriryeogo haneunde jigabi eomneun geoya." english="I was about to get out of the taxi, and there I was without my wallet." />

<SentenceBlock formal="편지를 열었는데 안에 사진이 들어 있는 것입니다." formalRom="pyeonjireul yeoreonneunde ane sajini deureo inneun geosimnida." polite="편지를 열었는데 안에 사진이 들어 있는 거예요." politeRom="pyeonjireul yeoreonneunde ane sajini deureo inneun geoyeyo." casual="편지를 열었는데 안에 사진이 들어 있는 거야." casualRom="pyeonjireul yeoreonneunde ane sajini deureo inneun geoya." english="I opened the letter, and there was a photo inside." />

<Hint type="hint">The ㄹ in 갈 거예요 is the future modifier. The 는 in 가는 거예요 can frame a present action as an explanation. Keep the modifier’s meaning separate from the fact that both expressions contain 거예요.</Hint>
