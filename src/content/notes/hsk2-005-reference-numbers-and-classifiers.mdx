Your friend asks how long you’ve been waiting, then how long you’ve lived in your apartment. Put a number before a duration word such as 分钟, 天, 周, or 年. These answer “how long?”; compare [time and frequency](/notes/hsk1-time-and-frequency) for words like “first,” “again,” and “right now.”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我想我们还是再多等三十分钟吧。" traditional="我想我們還是再多等三十分鐘吧。" pinyin="wǒ xiǎng wǒmen háishi zài duō děng sānshí fēnzhōng ba。" jyutping="ngo5 soeng2 ngo5 mun4 waan4 si6 zoi3 do1 dang2 saam1 sap6 fan1 zung1 baa6。" english="I think we had better wait another thirty minutes." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我在这里住了三十年了。" traditional="我在這裡住了三十年了。" pinyin="wǒ zài zhèlǐ zhù le sānshí nián le。" jyutping="ngo5 zoi6 ze5 leoi5 zyu6 liu5 saam1 sap6 nin4 liu5。" english="I have lived here for thirty years." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我等了你两个小时，差点儿以为你不来了。" traditional="我等了你兩個小時，差點兒以為你不來了。" pinyin="wǒ děng le nǐ liǎng ge xiǎoshí, chàdiǎnr yǐwéi nǐ bù lái le." jyutping="ngo5 dang2 liu5 nei5 loeng5 go3 siu2 si4, caa1 dim2 ji4 ji5 wai4 nei5 bat1 loi4 liu5." english="I waited two hours for you and almost thought you weren’t coming." />
