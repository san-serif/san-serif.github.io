Your friend says they’re going to train for the race, then quits after ten minutes. You encourage them: “If you’re going to train, do it properly.” 要……就…… compresses the condition and response into one brisk phrase. A related frame gives direct advice: do the action, and don’t hold back.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="要学就学个痛快，别半途而废。" traditional="要學就學個痛快，別半途而廢。" pinyin="yào xué jiù xué ge tòngkuai, bié bàntú'érfèi." jyutping="jiu3 hok6 zau6 hok6 go3 tung3 faai3, bit6 bun3 tou4 ji4 fai3." english="If you’re going to study, really get into it—don’t give up halfway." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="要做就做个彻底，别留下问题。" traditional="要做就做個徹底，別留下問題。" pinyin="yào zuò jiù zuò ge chèdǐ, bié liúxià wèntí." jyutping="jiu3 zou6 zau6 zou6 go3 cit3 dai2, bit6 lau4 haa6 man6 tai4." english="If you’re going to do it, do it thoroughly and don’t leave problems behind." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="想休息就休息一会儿，别硬撑。" traditional="想休息就休息一會兒，別硬撐。" pinyin="xiǎng xiūxi jiù xiūxi yíhuìr, bié yìngchēng." jyutping="soeng2 jau1 sik1 zau6 jau1 sik1 jat1 wui5 ji4, bit6 ngaang6 caang1." english="If you need a rest, take one; don’t push yourself." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你要买就买个够，今天正好打折。" traditional="你要買就買個夠，今天正好打折。" pinyin="nǐ yào mǎi jiù mǎi ge gòu, jīntiān zhènghǎo dǎzhé." jyutping="nei5 jiu3 maai5 zau6 maai5 go3 gau3, gam1 tin1 zing3 hou2 daa2 zit3." english="If you want to shop, go ahead and buy enough; everything’s on sale today." />

<Hint type="hint">In 要A就A个……, the second verb is followed by 个 and a complement to intensify or complete the action. In A就A，别……, the repeated verb sounds like direct advice: do it, and don’t hesitate or hold back.</Hint>
