Sometimes a reaction arrives before you have time to choose it: a song brings back a memory, a child needs help, or something makes you laugh. Put 不由得 before the action or feeling that happens almost automatically. It means “couldn’t help…” or “found oneself…”

### When a Memory Comes Back

<CSentenceBlock simplified="每次听到这首歌，我不由得想起小时候的暑假。" traditional="每次聽到這首歌，我不由得想起小時候的暑假。" pinyin="Měi cì tīngdào zhè shǒu gē, wǒ bùyóude xiǎngqǐ xiǎoshíhou de shǔjià." jyutping="mui5 ci3 teng1 dou2 ze5 sau2 go1, ngo5 bat1 jau4 dak1 soeng2 hei2 siu2 si4 hau6 dik1 syu2 gaa3." english="Whenever I hear this song, I can’t help remembering summer holidays when I was a child." />

The speaker is not deliberately trying to remember. Hearing the song triggers the memory, and 不由得 makes that involuntary reaction clear.

### When You React Without Thinking

<CSentenceBlock simplified="看到老人一个人搬行李，她不由得上前帮忙。" traditional="看到老人一個人搬行李，她不由得上前幫忙。" pinyin="Kàndào lǎorén yí ge rén bān xíngli, tā bùyóude shàngqián bāngmáng." jyutping="hon3 dou3 lou5 jan4 jat1 go3 jan4 bun1 hang4 lei5, taa1 bat1 jau4 dak1 soeng6 cin4 bong1 mong4." english="Seeing an older person carrying luggage alone, she couldn’t help going over to help." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="大家听完他的故事，不由得安静下来。" traditional="大家聽完他的故事，不由得安靜下來。" pinyin="Dàjiā tīngwán tā de gùshi, bùyóude ānjìng xiàlái." jyutping="daai6 gaa1 teng1 jyun4 taa1 dik1 gu3 si6, bat1 jau4 dak1 on1 zing6 haa6 loi4." english="After hearing his story, everyone fell quiet without meaning to." />

不由得 usually introduces a response rather than a planned decision. For a conscious choice, state the decision directly: 我决定帮忙 (“I decided to help”).

<Hint type="hint">不由得 and 忍不住 are close, but their focus differs: 不由得 presents a reaction as beyond one’s control; 忍不住 often suggests trying, and failing, to hold an action back.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">In conversation, people also say 我忍不住笑了 (“I couldn’t stop myself laughing”). 不由得 appears comfortably in both speech and writing, especially with thoughts, feelings, and spontaneous responses.</Hint>
