Someone asks how tall your cousin is, and you answer with a number. Chinese can use 有 before the amount to show that something reaches a particular size or value. In comparisons, 有…那么… means “as … as that.”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我弟弟有一米八高。" traditional="我弟弟有一米八高。" pinyin="wǒ dìdi yǒu yì mǐ bā gāo." jyutping="ngo5 dai6 dai2 jau5 jat1 mai5 baat3 gou1." english="My younger brother is 1.8 metres tall." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这本词典有一千多页。" traditional="這本詞典有一千多頁。" pinyin="zhè běn cídiǎn yǒu yì qiān duō yè." jyutping="ze5 bun2 ci4 din2 jau5 jat1 cin1 do1 jip6." english="This dictionary has more than a thousand pages." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家店有那家店那么贵吗？" traditional="這家店有那家店那麼貴嗎？" pinyin="zhè jiā diàn yǒu nà jiā diàn nàme guì ma?" jyutping="ze5 gaa1 dim3 jau5 naa5 gaa1 dim3 naa5 mo1 gwai3 maa3?" english="Is this shop as expensive as that one?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他的中文没有你那么好。" traditional="他的中文沒有你那麼好。" pinyin="tā de Zhōngwén méiyǒu nǐ nàme hǎo." jyutping="taa1 dik1 zung1 man4 mut6 jau5 nei5 naa5 mo1 hou2." english="His Chinese isn’t as good as yours." />

<Hint type="hint">The comparison 有…那么… sets a standard (“as tall as him”). Negative 没有…那么… means “not as…as.” For a taller/shorter comparison, 比 works differently: 他比我高 (“He’s taller than me”).</Hint>
