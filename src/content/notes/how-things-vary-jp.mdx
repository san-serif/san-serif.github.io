Use によって違[ちが]う to say that something varies depending on the person, place, or other factor. と同[おな]じくらい compares the degree of an action or quality: "about as much as" or "to the same extent as."

<JSentenceBlock furigana="必要[ひつよう]な時間[じかん]は人[ひと]によって違[ちが]います。" romaji="Hitsuyou na jikan wa hito ni yotte chigaimasu." english="The time needed varies from person to person." />

<Hint type="hint">Here によって names the factor that changes the result. It often appears in practical explanations: 会社によって違う, "it differs by company"; 季節によって変わる, "it changes with the season." によって also marks means or an agent in other sentences, so use context to tell the jobs apart.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="私[わたし]も姉[あね]と同[おな]じくらい食[た]べます。" romaji="Watashi mo ane to onaji kurai tabemasu." english="I eat about as much as my older sister." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="この町[まち]は東京[とうきょう]と同[おな]じくらい便利[べんり]です。" romaji="Kono machi wa Toukyou to onaji kurai benri desu." english="This town is about as convenient as Tokyo." />

<Hint type="hint">と同じくらい works with verbs and adjectives. For a broad estimate, くらい leaves room for a small difference; it doesn't claim the two amounts are mathematically identical.</Hint>
