You are describing a reliable friend: thoughtful, and helpful too. Then you give them a choice of where to meet. -데다가 adds another point in the same direction; -든지 makes clear that either option is acceptable.

### Add Another Supporting Point

Use -는 데다가 after present action verbs, -(으)ㄴ 데다가 after adjectives or completed actions, and -인 데다가 after nouns. The second clause adds another quality or circumstance, often reinforcing the first. Leave a space before 데다가.

<SentenceBlock formal="제 친구는 친절한 데다가 약속도 잘 지킵니다." formalRom="je chinguneun chinjeolhan dedaga yaksokdo jal jikimnida." polite="제 친구는 친절한 데다가 약속도 잘 지켜요." politeRom="je chinguneun chinjeolhan dedaga yaksokdo jal jikyeoyo." casual="내 친구는 친절한 데다가 약속도 잘 지켜." casualRom="nae chinguneun chinjeolhan dedaga yaksokdo jal jikyeo." english="My friend is kind and also keeps promises well." />

<SentenceBlock formal="비가 오는 데다가 바람도 많이 붑니다." formalRom="biga oneun dedaga baramdo mani bumnida." polite="비가 오는 데다가 바람도 많이 불어요." politeRom="biga oneun dedaga baramdo mani bureoyo." casual="비가 오는 데다가 바람도 많이 불어." casualRom="biga oneun dedaga baramdo mani bureo." english="It’s raining, and on top of that it’s very windy." />

### Any of These Options Will Do

Attach -든지 to verbs or adjectives, and -(이)든지 to nouns: 가든지, 먹든지, 커피든지 차든지. With a question word, it makes the choice unrestricted: 언제든지, 어디든지, 누구든지.

<SentenceBlock formal="커피든지 차든지 다 괜찮습니다." formalRom="keopideunji chadeunji da gwaenchanseumnida." polite="커피든지 차든지 다 괜찮아요." politeRom="keopideunji chadeunji da gwaenchanayo." casual="커피든지 차든지 다 괜찮아." casualRom="keopideunji chadeunji da gwaenchana." english="Coffee or tea is fine." />

<SentenceBlock formal="시간이 있으면 언제든지 연락하십시오." formalRom="sigani isseumyeon eonjedeunji yeollakhasipsio." polite="시간이 있으면 언제든지 연락하세요." politeRom="sigani isseumyeon eonjedeunji yeollakhaseyo." casual="시간이 있으면 언제든지 연락해." casualRom="sigani isseumyeon eonjedeunji yeollakhae." english="Contact me whenever you have time." />

<Hint type="hint">Use 든지 for alternatives or an unrestricted choice. 던 in [자주 가던 곳](/notes/thought-it-was-remembered) looks back at experience. The vowels distinguish different grammar.</Hint>
