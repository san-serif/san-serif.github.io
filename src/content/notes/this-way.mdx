로 (으로 after a consonant) attaches to a mode of transport to mean "by": 택시로, "by taxi." You can also build the same idea out of 타다 (to ride) plus 가다: 택시를 타고 가요, "I ride a taxi and go." Both are correct; the second is just more vivid about the act of getting on board.

### By Bus, By Subway, By Plane

<SentenceBlock formal="버스를 타고 갑니다." formalRom="beoseureul tago gamnida." polite="버스를 타고 가요." politeRom="beoseureul tago gayo." casual="버스를 타고 가." casualRom="beoseureul tago ga." english="I go by bus." />

<SentenceBlock formal="은행까지 지하철로 갑니다." formalRom="eunhaengkkaji jihacheollo gamnida." polite="은행까지 지하철로 가요." politeRom="eunhaengkkaji jihacheollo gayo." casual="은행까지 지하철로 가." casualRom="eunhaengkkaji jihacheollo ga." english="I go to the bank by subway." />

<SentenceBlock formal="백화점까지 택시로 갑니다." formalRom="baekhwajeomkkaji taeksiro gamnida." polite="백화점까지 택시로 가요." politeRom="baekhwajeomkkaji taeksiro gayo." casual="백화점까지 택시로 가." casualRom="baekhwajeomkkaji taeksiro ga." english="I go to the department store by taxi." />

<SentenceBlock formal="서울에서 제주도까지 비행기를 타고 갑니다." formalRom="seoureseo jejudokkaji bihaenggireul tago gamnida." polite="서울에서 제주도까지 비행기를 타고 가요." politeRom="seoureseo jejudokkaji bihaenggireul tago gayo." casual="서울에서 제주도까지 비행기를 타고 가." casualRom="seoureseo jejudokkaji bihaenggireul tago ga." english="I take a plane from Seoul to Jeju." />

<Hint>[몇 시예요?](/notes/what-time-is-it) taught 부터~까지 for a time range. Places use a different pair: 에서~까지. 서울에서 제주도까지 ("from Seoul to Jeju") never takes 부터, even though English "from" covers both jobs. Swapping them is a very common beginner slip.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="부산에서 경주까지 얼마나 걸립니까?" formalRom="busaneseo gyeongjukkaji eolmana geollimnikka?" polite="부산에서 경주까지 얼마나 걸려요?" politeRom="busaneseo gyeongjukkaji eolmana geollyeoyo?" casual="부산에서 경주까지 얼마나 걸려?" casualRom="busaneseo gyeongjukkaji eolmana geollyeo?" english="How long does it take from Busan to Gyeongju?" />

<Hint>걸리다 (to take time) is the verb every travel-time question runs on. It never takes 을/를: 시간이 얼마나 걸려요? not 시간을 얼마나 걸려요?. Think of it as the time itself being the one doing the "taking," not something you're acting on.</Hint>
