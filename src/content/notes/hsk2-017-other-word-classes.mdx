When someone answers your call, start with 喂 (wèi), then greet them or say why you’re calling. It works like “hello?” on the phone. After the opening, use the same ordinary sentences you’d use face to face.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="喂，你好，请问你找谁？" traditional="喂，你好，請問你找誰？" pinyin="wèi, nǐ hǎo, qǐngwèn nǐ zhǎo shéi?" jyutping="wai3, nei5 hou2, cing2 man6 nei5 zaau2 seoi4?" english="Hello, who would you like to speak to?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="喂，李老师吗？我是王明。" traditional="喂，李老師嗎？我是王明。" pinyin="wèi, Lǐ lǎoshī ma? wǒ shì Wáng Míng." jyutping="wai3, lei5 lou5 si1 maa3? ngo5 si6 wong4 ming4." english="Hello, is that Teacher Li? This is Wang Ming." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="喂，妈妈，我已经到火车站了。" traditional="喂，媽媽，我已經到火車站了。" pinyin="wèi, māma, wǒ yǐjīng dào huǒchēzhàn le." jyutping="wai3, maa4 maa1, ngo5 ji5 ging1 dou3 fo2 ce1 zaam6 liu5." english="Hi, Mom. I’ve arrived at the train station." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="喂？你听得见我说话吗？" traditional="喂？你聽得見我說話嗎？" pinyin="wèi? nǐ tīng de jiàn wǒ shuōhuà ma?" jyutping="wai3? nei5 teng1 dak1 gin3 ngo5 syut3 waa6 maa3?" english="Hello? Can you hear me?" />

<Hint type="hint">喂 is mainly used when answering or starting a call. With a friend, it sounds casual; in a more formal call, follow it with 您好 or 请问.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">On a call with someone you don’t know well, 喂，您好？ sounds warmer than a bare 喂？ Once they answer, introduce yourself or say who you’re trying to reach.</Hint>
