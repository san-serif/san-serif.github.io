In a formal heading, 国家之未来 (“the country’s future”) may use 之 where everyday speech would use 的. A writer might also finish with 而已 or 矣 for a literary or set tone. These forms help you read formal prose; in casual conversation, 的 and ordinary sentence endings are the usual choices.
