“The report was written with Professor Lin; as for the budget, that’s a separate question.” 同 and 与 can join people or things, especially in formal writing. 至于 shifts the conversation to a new person or topic. Listen for whether the words are joining the sentence or turning it.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="眼下我不得不和朋友同住一个房间。" traditional="眼下我不得不和朋友同住一個房間。" pinyin="yǎnxià wǒ bùdébù hé péngyou tóngzhù yí ge fángjiān." jyutping="ngaan5 haa6 ngo5 bat1 dak1 bat1 wo4 pang4 jau5 tung4 zyu6 jat1 go3 fong4 gaan1" english="For now, I have to share a room with a friend." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="至于我，我比较喜欢待在家看小说。" traditional="至於我，我比較喜歡待在家看小說。" pinyin="zhìyú wǒ, wǒ bǐjiào xǐhuan dāi zài jiā kàn xiǎoshuō." jyutping="zi3 jyu1 ngo5, ngo5 bei2 gaau3 hei2 fun1 doi6 zoi6 gaa1 hon3 siu2 syut3" english="As for me, I prefer to stay home and read novels." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我永远不会忘记那天我第一次与他见面。" traditional="我永遠不會忘記那天我第一次與他見面。" pinyin="wǒ yǒngyuǎn bú huì wàngjì nà tiān wǒ dì yī cì yǔ tā jiànmiàn." jyutping="ngo5 wing5 jyun5 bat1 wui5 mong4 gei3 naa5 tin1 ngo5 dai6 jat1 ci3 jyu5 taa1 gin3 min6" english="I will never forget the day I first met him." />
