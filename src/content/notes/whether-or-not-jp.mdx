かどうか attaches to the plain form of a verb, adjective, or noun and embeds a yes/no question inside a larger sentence, usually followed by something like わかりません or 聞きました.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="この町[まち]は住[す]みやすいかどうかまだわかりません。" romaji="Kono machi wa sumiyasui ka dou ka mada wakarimasen." english="I still don't know whether this town is easy to live in or not." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="拓也[たくや]が来[く]るかどうか知[し]りません。" romaji="Takuya ga kuru ka dou ka shirimasen." english="I don't know whether Takuya is coming or not." />

<Hint>か alone, which you already met for embedding an open question like 何か食べたか, only works when there's a question word involved. かどうか is for closed yes/no questions instead, "is it or isn't it," "will they or won't they," with no question word at all.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="このレストランが安[やす]いかどうか聞[き]いてみます。" romaji="Kono resutoran ga yasui ka dou ka kiitemimasu." english="I'll ask whether this restaurant is cheap or not." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="由紀[ゆき]が元気[げんき]かどうか、心配[しんぱい]しています。" romaji="Yuki ga genki ka dou ka, shinpai shiteimasu." english="I'm worried about whether Yuki is doing well or not." />

<Hint>な-adjectives and nouns drop だ before かどうか, just as they do before plain-form か: 元気だ becomes 元気かどうか, not 元気だかどうか. い-adjectives and verbs attach directly with no change at all.</Hint>
