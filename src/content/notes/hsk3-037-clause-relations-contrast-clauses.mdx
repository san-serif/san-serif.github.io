“It is expensive, I’ll give you that—but the view is worth it.” X是X acknowledges the first point, then leaves room for a qualification or pushback. The second clause tells the listener how the speaker feels about that drawback.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="那就是我等的人。" traditional="那就是我等的人。" pinyin="nà jiù shì wǒ děng de rén。" jyutping="naa5 zau6 si6 ngo5 dang2 dik1 jan4。" english="That’s the person I’m waiting for." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我在电视上看到过他，不过没有看到过他本人。" traditional="我在電視上看到過他，不過沒有看到過他本人。" pinyin="wǒ zài diànshì shàng kàndào guò tā， bùguò méiyǒu kàndào guò tā běnrén。" jyutping="ngo5 zoi6 din6 si6 soeng5 hon3 dou3 gwo3 taa1， bat1 gwo3 mut6 jau5 hon3 dou3 gwo3 taa1 bun2 jan4。" english="I have seen him on TV but not in the flesh." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="是我的，不是他的。" traditional="是我的，不是他的。" pinyin="shì wǒ de， bùshì tā de。" jyutping="si6 ngo5 dik1， bat1 si6 taa1 dik1。" english="It's mine, not his." />
