Once the doors open, the first guests rush in. 一旦 sets up the event that triggers what follows, often with 就 before the result. 要是 opens a broader possibility; 要不然 warns what could happen if the plan isn’t followed.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="一旦养成坏习惯，就很难改掉。" traditional="一旦養成壞習慣，就很難改掉。" pinyin="yīdàn yǎngchéng huài xíguàn, jiù hěn nán gǎidiào." jyutping="jat1 daan3 joeng5 sing4 waai6 zaap6 gwaan3, zau6 han2 naan4 goi2 diu6." english="Once you develop a bad habit, it is hard to break." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="要是我很有钱的话，我就会到处去旅游。" traditional="要是我很有錢的話，我就會到處去旅遊。" pinyin="yàoshi wǒ hěn yǒuqián dehuà， wǒ jiù huì dàochù qù lǚyóu。" jyutping="jiu3 si6 ngo5 han2 jau5 cin2 dik1 waa6， ngo5 zau6 wui2 dou3 cyu3 heoi3 leoi5 jau4。" english="If I were rich, I would travel everywhere." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="立刻起床，否则你上学会迟到。" traditional="立刻起床，否則你上學會遲到。" pinyin="lìkè qǐchuáng， fǒuzé nǐ shàngxué huì chídào。" jyutping="laap6 hak1 hei2 cong4， fau2 zak1 nei5 soeng5 hok6 wui2 ci4 dou3。" english="Get up at once, or you’ll be late for school." />
