You’re giving a friend directions: start 从家, go 到学校, then wait 在门口. 从 marks where a route begins, 到 where it ends, and 在 gives the location of a person or action. Put the place phrase before the action it belongs to.

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我从家到学校。" traditional="我從家到學校。" pinyin="Wǒ cóng jiā dào xuéxiào." jyutping="ngo5 cung4 gaa1 dou3 hok6 haau6." english="I go from home to school." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他在学校学习。" traditional="他在學校學習。" pinyin="Tā zài xuéxiào xuéxí." jyutping="taa1 zoi6 hok6 haau6 hok6 zaap6." english="He studies at school." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们从这里走。" traditional="我們從這裡走。" pinyin="Wǒmen cóng zhèlǐ zǒu." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 cung4 ze5 leoi5 zau2." english="We’ll leave from here." />

<Hint type="hint">从 gives the starting point; it does not mean “at.” Use 在 for location: 我在家.</Hint>
