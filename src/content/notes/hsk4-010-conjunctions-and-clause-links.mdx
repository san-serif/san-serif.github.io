You’ve checked the forecast, so you change the plan; your friend adds one more reason; then you wrap up with a summary. 并、而、而是, and 以及 connect ideas in different ways, while 既然 starts from a known fact, 假如 opens a possibility, and 总之 signals that you’re tying things together.
