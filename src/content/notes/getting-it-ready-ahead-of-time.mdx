ておく attaches to a verb's て-form and describes doing something in advance, in preparation for later, "do it now so it's ready when needed."

<JSentenceBlock furigana="ホテルを予約[よやく]しておきます。" romaji="Hoteru o yoyaku shite okimasu." english="I'll go ahead and reserve a hotel." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="フランスの歴史[れきし]を勉強[べんきょう]しておきます。" romaji="Furansu no rekishi o benkyou shite okimasu." english="I'll study up on French history beforehand." />

<Hint>ておく always has an eye on the future, doing something now so a later situation goes smoothly. Compare this to てある, which describes a state already set up and left that way, ておく is the action of setting it up; てある describes the resulting state afterward.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="じゃあ、色々[いろいろ]な大学[だいがく]を調[しら]べておいた方[ほう]がいいですよ。" romaji="Jaa, iroiro na daigaku o shirabete oita hou ga ii desu yo." english="Well then, you'd better look into various universities ahead of time." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="じゃあ、今[いま]から準備[じゅんび]しておいた方[ほう]がいいですよ。" romaji="Jaa, ima kara junbi shite oita hou ga ii desu yo." english="Well then, you'd better start preparing from now." />

<Hint>ておいた方がいい, combining ておく with ほうがいい, is a very natural way to recommend advance preparation, "you'd be better off getting this ready now," rather than leaving it for later.</Hint>
