Your friend wants to borrow your bike. 会 can tell them you know how to ride; 可以 asks whether they have your permission; 可能 leaves the plan uncertain. Each modal comes before the main verb, and the choice tells your listener what kind of “can” you mean.

### Ability, Permission, and Possibility

会 can describe a learned ability or something expected to happen. 可以 often asks or gives permission. 可能 leaves the outcome uncertain.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他会用很多词语。" traditional="他會用很多詞語。" pinyin="Tā huì yòng hěn duō cíyǔ." jyutping="taa1 wui2 jung6 han2 do1 ci4 jyu5." english="He can use many words and expressions." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他会成为一个好队长。" traditional="他會成為一個好隊長。" pinyin="Tā huì chéngwéi yí ge hǎo duìzhǎng." jyutping="taa1 wui2 sing4 wai4 jat1 go3 hou2 deoi6 zoeng2." english="He’ll become a good team captain." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我可以去吗？" traditional="我可以去嗎？" pinyin="Wǒ kěyǐ qù ma?" jyutping="ngo5 ho2 ji5 heoi3 maa3?" english="May I go?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他可能来。" traditional="他可能來。" pinyin="Tā kěnéng lái." jyutping="taa1 ho2 nang4 loi4." english="He might come." />

### Advice and Willingness

该 and 应该 recommend an action; 愿意 says that the person is willing to do it. All three come before the main verb.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你看起来很累。你应该休息一两个小时。" traditional="你看起來很累。你應該休息一兩個小時。" pinyin="Nǐ kànqǐlai hěn lèi. Nǐ yīnggāi xiūxi yì liǎng ge xiǎoshí." jyutping="nei5 hon1 hei2 loi4 han2 leoi6. nei5 jing1 goi1 jau1 sik1 jat1 loeng5 go3 siu2 si4." english="You look tired. You ought to rest for an hour or two." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我愿意跟你一起去。" traditional="我願意跟你一起去。" pinyin="Wǒ yuànyì gēn nǐ yìqǐ qù." jyutping="ngo5 jyun6 ji3 gan1 nei5 jat1 hei2 heoi3." english="I’m willing to go with you." />

<Hint type="hint">会 and 能 can both translate as “can,” but they point to different things: 会 often means a learned skill or a likely future event, while 能 focuses on ability or whether circumstances allow it. 可以 commonly asks whether an action is permitted.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">A short question with 可以吗? is a natural way to ask permission. For example, 我可以坐这儿吗？ means “May I sit here?”</Hint>
