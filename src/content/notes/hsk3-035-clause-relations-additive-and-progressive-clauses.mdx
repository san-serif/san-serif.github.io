You’re telling a friend why you like your new neighborhood. First, it’s close to work; on top of that, there’s a great market nearby. 并且 adds another related point. 不仅…还/而且… makes the build-up more explicit: “not only…but also…”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我家离公司很近，并且附近有一个大市场。" traditional="我家離公司很近，並且附近有一個大市場。" pinyin="wǒ jiā lí gōngsī hěn jìn, bìngqiě fùjìn yǒu yí ge dà shìchǎng." jyutping="ngo5 gaa1 lei4 gung1 si1 han2 gan6, bing6 ce2 fu6 gan6 jau5 jat1 go3 daai6 si5 coeng4." english="My home is close to work, and there’s also a big market nearby." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她不仅会说中文，还会写汉字。" traditional="她不僅會說中文，還會寫漢字。" pinyin="tā bùjǐn huì shuō Zhōngwén, hái huì xiě Hànzì." jyutping="taa1 bat1 gan2 wui5 syut3 zung1 man4, waan4 wui5 se2 hon3 zi6." english="She can not only speak Chinese, but also write Chinese characters." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家小店不仅东西好吃，而且服务也很好。" traditional="這家小店不僅東西好吃，而且服務也很好。" pinyin="zhè jiā xiǎodiàn bùjǐn dōngxi hǎochī, érqiě fúwù yě hěn hǎo." jyutping="ze5 gaa1 siu2 dim3 bat1 gan2 dung1 sai1 hou2 hek3, ji4 ce2 fuk6 mou6 jaa5 han2 hou2." english="The food at this little shop is not only tasty; the service is great too." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们不光看了比赛，还跟运动员聊了天。" traditional="我們不光看了比賽，還跟運動員聊了天。" pinyin="wǒmen bùguāng kàn le bǐsài, hái gēn yùndòngyuán liáo le tiān." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 bat1 gwong1 hon3 liu5 bei2 coi3, waan4 gan1 wan6 dung6 jyun4 liu4 liu5 tin1." english="We not only watched the game, we also chatted with the athletes." />

<Hint type="hint">不仅 can introduce either a verb or a noun phrase. Pairing it with 还 or 而且 helps the listener hear that a second point is coming. 并且 is a more neutral way to add a related fact.</Hint>
