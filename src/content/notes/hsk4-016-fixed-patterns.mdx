“As for comfort, this chair wins. For price, the other one does.” 在……方面 sets the area you’re talking about; 在……上/下/中 places a quality or activity within a broader field. Name the frame first so the listener knows what your comment applies to.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你想找哪个方面的工作？" traditional="你想找哪個方面的工作？" pinyin="nǐ xiǎng zhǎo nǎge fāngmiàn de gōngzuò？" jyutping="nei5 soeng2 zaau2 naa5 go3 fong1 min6 dik1 gung1 zok3？" english="What kind of job are you looking for?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你在吗？" traditional="你在嗎？" pinyin="nǐ zài ma？" jyutping="nei5 zoi6 maa3？" english="Are you there?" />
