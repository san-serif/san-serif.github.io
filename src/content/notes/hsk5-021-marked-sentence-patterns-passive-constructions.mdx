The room is ready, the problem is solved, and the new novel has sold out. When the result matters more than who caused it, Chinese can put the affected thing first and leave the doer unsaid. Context fills in the rest.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这本小说卖得特别好，已经加印了两次。" traditional="這本小說賣得特別好，已經加印了兩次。" pinyin="zhè běn xiǎoshuō mài de tèbié hǎo, yǐjīng jiāyìn le liǎng cì." jyutping="ze5 bun2 siu2 syut3 maai6 dak1 dak6 bit6 hou2, ji5 ging1 gaa1 jan3 liu5 loeng5 ci3." english="This novel is selling very well; it has already been reprinted twice." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这个问题已经解决了，大家可以放心。" traditional="這個問題已經解決了，大家可以放心。" pinyin="zhège wèntí yǐjīng jiějué le, dàjiā kěyǐ fàngxīn." jyutping="ze5 go3 man6 tai4 ji5 ging1 gaai2 kyut3 liu5, daai6 gaa1 ho2 ji5 fong3 sam1." english="The problem has been solved; everyone can relax." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="房间收拾好了，新客人马上就能住进来。" traditional="房間收拾好了，新客人馬上就能住進來。" pinyin="fángjiān shōushi hǎo le, xīn kèrén mǎshàng jiù néng zhù jìnlai." jyutping="fong4 gaan1 sau1 sik1 hou2 liu5, san1 haak3 jan4 maa5 soeng6 zau6 nang4 zyu6 zeon3 loi4." english="The room is ready, so the next guest can move in right away." />

<Hint type="hint">Here the sentence subject is the thing affected: the novel is sold, the problem gets solved, and the room gets cleaned. With an implied passive, the context supplies the action’s doer, so no 被 phrase is needed.</Hint>
