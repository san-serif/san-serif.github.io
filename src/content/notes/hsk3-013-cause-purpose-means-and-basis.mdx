The train was delayed, so you took a taxi; you left early to catch the next one; apart from the fare, the ride was easy. 因为/由于 give a reason, 为了 names a goal, and 除了 changes meaning with what follows: it can leave something out or add it to the group. Ask what relationship the sentence needs.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="为了找个好位子，我很早就出门了。" traditional="為了找個好位子，我很早就出門了。" pinyin="wèile zhǎo yí ge hǎo wèizi， wǒ hěn zǎo jiù chūmén le。" jyutping="wai6 liu5 zaau2 jat1 go3 hou2 wai2 zi2， ngo5 han2 zou2 zau6 ceot1 mun4 liu5。" english="I left home early to get a good seat." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="由于缺水，花园里所有的花都死了。" traditional="由於缺水，花園裡所有的花都死了。" pinyin="yóuyú quēshuǐ， huāyuán lǐ suǒyǒu de huā dōu sǐ le。" jyutping="jau4 jyu1 kyut3 seoi2， faa1 jyun2 lei5 so2 jau5 dik1 faa1 dou1 sei2 liu5。" english="Because of the water shortage, all the flowers in the garden died." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我是因为太忙，所以才没有回信给你的。" traditional="我是因為太忙，所以才沒有回信給你的。" pinyin="wǒ shì yīnwèi tài máng， suǒyǐ cái méiyǒu huíxìn gěi nǐ de。" jyutping="ngo5 si6 jan1 wai6 taai3 mong4， so2 ji5 coi4 mut6 jau5 wui4 seon3 kap1 nei5 dik1。" english="I was too busy, so I didn’t write back to you." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="除了雨天，我都是骑车去上班的。" traditional="除了雨天，我都是騎車去上班的。" pinyin="chúle yǔtiān， wǒ dōu shì qíchē qù shàngbān de。" jyutping="ceoi4 liu5 jyu5 tin1， ngo5 dou1 si6 ke4 ce1 heoi3 soeng5 baan1 dik1。" english="I ride to work except on rainy days." />
