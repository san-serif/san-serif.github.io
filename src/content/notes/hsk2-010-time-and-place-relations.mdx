You get home and realize the lights are on: 当我到家的时候，灯已经开了. 当…的时候 sets the moment as a little stage for the main event. Add 就 when you want to show what happened as soon as that moment arrived.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他当老师。" traditional="他當老師。" pinyin="tā dāng lǎoshī." jyutping="taa1 dong1 lou5 si1." english="He works as a teacher." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="当时我很忙。" traditional="當時我很忙。" pinyin="dāngshí wǒ hěn máng." jyutping="dong1 si4 ngo5 han2 mong4." english="At that time, I was very busy." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你去的话，我也去。" traditional="你去的話，我也去。" pinyin="nǐ qù dehuà, wǒ yě qù." jyutping="nei5 heoi3 dik1 waa6, ngo5 jaa5 heoi3." english="If you go, I'll go too." />
