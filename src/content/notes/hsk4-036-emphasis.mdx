The whole group loved the trip, so you look around and ask, “Who wouldn’t want to go again?” A rhetorical question like this expects the listener to understand your point. Chinese also uses a double negative or 一……也/都……不…… to make an assertion emphatic.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这么好的机会，谁不想试一试呢？" traditional="這麼好的機會，誰不想試一試呢？" pinyin="zhème hǎo de jīhuì, shéi bù xiǎng shì yi shì ne?" jyutping="ze5 mo1 hou2 dik1 gei1 wui6, seoi4 bat1 soeng2 si3 jat1 si3 ne1?" english="Who wouldn’t want to try such a good opportunity?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这么重要的事，我怎么会忘呢？" traditional="這麼重要的事，我怎麼會忘呢？" pinyin="zhème zhòngyào de shì, wǒ zěnme huì wàng ne?" jyutping="ze5 mo1 zung6 jiu3 dik1 si6, ngo5 zam2 mo1 wui5 mong4 ne1?" english="How could I forget something so important?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="没有人比她更了解这座城市。" traditional="沒有人比她更了解這座城市。" pinyin="méiyǒu rén bǐ tā gèng liǎojiě zhè zuò chéngshì." jyutping="mut6 jau5 jan4 bei2 taa1 gang3 liu5 gaai2 ze5 zo6 sing4 si5." english="No one knows this city better than she does." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他连一口水也没喝，就继续开会了。" traditional="他連一口水也沒喝，就繼續開會了。" pinyin="tā lián yì kǒu shuǐ yě méi hē, jiù jìxù kāihuì le." jyutping="taa1 lin4 jat1 hau2 seoi2 jaa5 mut6 hot3, zau6 gai3 zuk6 hoi1 wui6 liu5." english="He didn’t even drink a sip of water before continuing the meeting." />

<Hint type="hint">A rhetorical 谁不……呢？ or 怎么会……呢？ usually expects agreement with the implied answer. 没有人……比……更…… and 连……也没…… make a strong negative claim directly.</Hint>
