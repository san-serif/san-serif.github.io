You’re not sure whether to apologize or explain what happened. “I don’t know what to say,” you tell your friend. 不知……好 captures that stuck feeling. In the same conversation, you can define a word with 所谓……就是…… or downplay something with 无非……而已.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我该先道歉还是解释？真不知说什么好。" traditional="我該先道歉還是解釋？真不知說什麼好。" pinyin="wǒ gāi xiān dàoqiàn háishi jiěshì? zhēn bùzhī shuō shénme hǎo." jyutping="ngo5 goi1 sin1 dou6 hip3 waan4 si6 gaai2 sik1? zan1 bat1 zi1 syut3 sam6 mo1 hou2." english="Should I apologize first or explain? I really don’t know what to say." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="所谓成熟，就是能对自己的选择负责。" traditional="所謂成熟，就是能對自己的選擇負責。" pinyin="suǒwèi chéngshú, jiù shì néng duì zìjǐ de xuǎnzé fùzé." jyutping="so2 wai6 sing4 suk6, zau6 si6 nang4 deoi3 zi6 gei2 dik1 syun2 zaak6 fu6 zaak3." english="Being mature means taking responsibility for your own choices." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这次聚会无非是想让大家见个面而已。" traditional="這次聚會無非是想讓大家見個面而已。" pinyin="zhè cì jùhuì wúfēi shì xiǎng ràng dàjiā jiàn ge miàn éryǐ." jyutping="ze5 ci3 zeoi6 wui6 mou4 fei1 si6 soeng2 joeng6 daai6 gaa1 gin3 go3 min6 ji4 ji5." english="This get-together is simply a chance for everyone to see one another, nothing more." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他只不过晚了十分钟，没必要这么生气。" traditional="他只不過晚了十分鐘，沒必要這麼生氣。" pinyin="tā zhǐbúguò wǎn le shí fēnzhōng, méi bìyào zhème shēngqì." jyutping="taa1 zi2 bat1 gwo3 maan5 liu5 sap6 fan1 zung1, mut6 bit1 jiu3 ze5 mo1 saang1 hei3." english="He’s only ten minutes late; there’s no need to get so angry." />

<Hint type="hint">不知……好 presents a choice or uncertainty the speaker can’t resolve. 所谓……就是…… gives a plain definition. 无非……而已 and 只不过…… downplay something as no more than what follows.</Hint>
