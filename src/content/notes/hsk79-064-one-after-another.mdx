If something happens once, then again, then again without a break, 一连 lets you package that run into one phrase. Put it before the number and time or event being counted: 一连三天 (“three days in a row”), 一连开了四个会 (“held four meetings back to back”).

### A Run of Consecutive Days

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家店一连三天没开门，大家都以为它关了。" traditional="這家店一連三天沒開門，大家都以為它關了。" pinyin="Zhè jiā diàn yìlián sān tiān méi kāimén, dàjiā dōu yǐwéi tā guān le." jyutping="ze5 gaa1 dim3 jat1 lin4 saam1 tin1 mut6 hoi1 mun4, daai6 gaa1 dou1 ji5 wai4 taa1 gwaan1 zo2." english="This shop stayed closed for three days in a row, so everyone thought it had shut down." />

一连 often appears with a duration or count. It emphasizes an uninterrupted stretch, not simply that the events happened several times.

### A Run of Events

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她一连开了四个会，连午饭都没时间吃。" traditional="她一連開了四個會，連午飯都沒時間吃。" pinyin="Tā yìlián kāi le sì ge huì, lián wǔfàn dōu méi shíjiān chī." jyutping="taa1 jat1 lin4 hoi1 zo2 sei3 go3 wui6, lin4 ng5 faan6 dou1 mut6 si4 gaan3 sik6." english="She had four meetings back to back and didn’t even have time for lunch." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="球队一连赢了五场比赛，终于拿到冠军。" traditional="球隊一連贏了五場比賽，終於拿到冠軍。" pinyin="Qiúduì yìlián yíng le wǔ chǎng bǐsài, zhōngyú ná dào guànjūn." jyutping="kau4 deoi6 jat1 lin4 jing4 zo2 ng5 coeng4 bei2 coi3, zung1 jyu1 naa4 dou3 gun3 gwan1." english="The team won five matches in a row and finally took the championship." />

<Hint type="hint">一连 makes the consecutive run prominent. 连续 is broader and can describe an uninterrupted process or period; 一连 is especially natural with counts such as 一连三天、一连问了五个问题.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">You’ll often see 一连 with a number in news reports: 一连几天, 一连多次. The number helps the reader picture just how long or how often the streak lasted.</Hint>
