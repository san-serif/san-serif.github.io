代わりに attaches after a noun with の, or after a verb in its plain form, and means "instead of," naming what got swapped out for something else.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="今日[きょう]は、ご飯[はん]の代[か]わりにパンを食[た]べました。" romaji="Kyou wa, gohan no kawari ni pan o tabemashita." english="Today I ate bread instead of rice." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="今日[きょう]は、課長[かちょう]の代[か]わりに私[わたし]が会議[かいぎ]に出[で]ました。" romaji="Kyou wa, kachou no kawari ni watashi ga kaigi ni demashita." english="Today I attended the meeting in place of the manager." />

<Hint>ご飯の代わりに, 課長の代わりに, both attach directly to a noun with の, naming exactly what's being substituted, whether it's a food swap or standing in for a person.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="今日[きょう]は、バスに乗[の]る代[か]わりに自転車[じてんしゃ]で会社[かいしゃ]に行[い]きました。" romaji="Kyou wa, basu ni noru kawari ni jitensha de kaisha ni ikimashita." english="Today I biked to the office instead of taking the bus." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="今日[きょう]は、家[いえ]でご飯[はん]を作[つく]る代[か]わりに店[みせ]でうどんを食[た]べました。" romaji="Kyou wa, ie de gohan o tsukuru kawari ni mise de udon o tabemashita." english="Today, instead of cooking at home, I ate udon at a restaurant." />

<Hint>Attached to a verb's plain form instead of a noun, 代わりに still works the same way, swapping one action for another, バスに乗る代わりに自転車で, "instead of the action of riding the bus, by bicycle."</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="昨日[きのう]あまり寝[ね]られなかった代[か]わりに今日[きょう]はたくさん寝[ね]ようと思[おも]っているんです。" romaji="Kinou amari nerarenakatta kawari ni kyou wa takusan neyou to omotte iru n desu." english="Since I couldn't sleep much yesterday, I'm planning to sleep a lot today to make up for it." />

<Hint>Used with two contrasting facts like this, 代わりに shifts toward "to make up for," a trade-off across time rather than a simple substitution, one shortfall balanced out by a later compensation.</Hint>
