The parcel is missing, but no one knows who moved it. Start with the thing affected, then use 被 to show what happened; name the doer after 被 only if that detail matters. This lets the listener follow the result first.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我的自行车被人偷走了，只好坐公交上班。" traditional="我的自行車被人偷走了，只好坐公交上班。" pinyin="wǒ de zìxíngchē bèi rén tōu zǒu le, zhǐhǎo zuò gōngjiāo shàngbān." jyutping="ngo5 dik1 zi6 hang4 ce1 bei6 jan4 tau1 zau2 liu5, zi2 hou2 zo6 gung1 gaau1 soeng5 baan1." english="My bike was stolen, so I had to take the bus to work." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="那封信被邮局退回来了，地址好像写错了。" traditional="那封信被郵局退回來了，地址好像寫錯了。" pinyin="nà fēng xìn bèi yóujú tuì huílái le, dìzhǐ hǎoxiàng xiě cuò le." jyutping="naa5 fung1 seon3 bei6 jau4 guk6 teoi3 wui4 loi4 liu5, dei6 zi2 hou2 zoeng6 se2 co3 liu5." english="The letter was returned by the post office; it looks like the address was written incorrectly." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我的名字被老师念错了，大家都笑了。" traditional="我的名字被老師唸錯了，大家都笑了。" pinyin="wǒ de míngzi bèi lǎoshī niàn cuò le, dàjiā dōu xiào le." jyutping="ngo5 dik1 ming4 zi6 bei6 lou5 si1 nim6 co3 liu5, daai6 gaa1 dou1 siu3 liu5." english="My name was read incorrectly by the teacher, and everyone laughed." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="门让风吹开了，屋里的纸也飞得到处都是。" traditional="門讓風吹開了，屋裡的紙也飛得到處都是。" pinyin="mén ràng fēng chuī kāi le, wūli de zhǐ yě fēi de dàochù dōu shì." jyutping="mun4 joeng6 fung1 ceoi1 hoi1 liu5, uk1 leoi5 dik1 zi2 jaa5 fei1 dak1 dou3 cyu3 dou1 si6." english="The door was blown open by the wind, and the papers inside flew everywhere." />

<Hint type="hint">被 and 叫/让 all introduce the doer in this pattern. 被 is the most neutral choice in writing; 叫 and 让 are common in speech. The affected thing comes first in every example.</Hint>
