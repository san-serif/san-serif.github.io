“Because the train was delayed and the rain started, we took a taxi.” A longer sentence can carry several links: reason, added detail, result. Follow each connection in turn, then listen for the main point the whole sentence is building toward.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="因为路上堵车，所以我迟到了，老师已经开始上课了。" traditional="因為路上堵車，所以我遲到了，老師已經開始上課了。" pinyin="yīnwèi lùshang dǔchē, suǒyǐ wǒ chídào le, lǎoshī yǐjīng kāishǐ shàngkè le." jyutping="jan1 wai6 lou6 soeng6 dou2 ce1, so2 ji5 ngo5 ci4 dou3 liu5, lou5 si1 ji5 ging1 hoi1 ci2 soeng5 fo3 liu5." english="Because there was traffic, I arrived late, and the teacher had already started class." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我早就到了，但是朋友还在路上，我们只好先找个地方坐下。" traditional="我早就到了，但是朋友還在路上，我們只好先找個地方坐下。" pinyin="wǒ zǎo jiù dào le, dànshì péngyou hái zài lùshang, wǒmen zhǐhǎo xiān zhǎo ge dìfang zuòxia." jyutping="ngo5 zou2 zau6 dou3 liu5, daan6 si6 pang4 jau5 waan4 zoi6 lou6 soeng6, ngo5 mun4 zi2 hou2 sin1 zaau2 go3 dei6 fong1 co5 haa5." english="I got here ages ago, but my friend is still on the way, so we’ll find somewhere to sit first." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="车票已经买好了，酒店也订好了，所以我们明天可以安心出发。" traditional="車票已經買好了，酒店也訂好了，所以我們明天可以安心出發。" pinyin="chēpiào yǐjīng mǎi hǎo le, jiǔdiàn yě dìng hǎo le, suǒyǐ wǒmen míngtiān kěyǐ ānxīn chūfā." jyutping="ce1 piu3 ji5 ging1 maai5 hou2 liu5, zau2 dim3 jaa5 ding6 hou2 liu5, so2 ji5 ngo5 mun4 ming4 tin1 ho2 ji5 on1 sam1 ceot1 faat3." english="The tickets are bought and the hotel is booked, so we can set off without worrying tomorrow." />

<Hint type="hint">The first example nests a reason-and-result pair before adding what happened next. In the second, a contrast leads into the decision that follows. Group the ideas by their relationship as you read; a long sentence is easier to follow when you track each link.</Hint>
