He runs around the garden 像个小孩儿似的, “like a little kid.” 似的 completes a comparison and leaves the listener with a quick picture. Put it after the image you’re comparing someone or something to.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="弟弟高兴得像中了大奖似的，跑进屋里告诉我们。" traditional="弟弟高興得像中大獎似的，跑進屋裡告訴我們。" pinyin="dìdi gāoxìng de xiàng zhòng le dàjiǎng shìde, pǎo jìn wūli gàosu wǒmen." jyutping="dai6 dai2 gou1 hing3 dak1 zoeng6 zung3 daai6 zoeng2 ci5 dik1, paau2 zeon3 uk1 leoi5 gou3 sou3 ngo5 mun4." english="My younger brother was so happy he looked like he’d won the grand prize; he ran inside to tell us." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="外面冷得像冰似的，大家都不想出门。" traditional="外面冷得像冰似的，大家都不想出門。" pinyin="wàimiàn lěng de xiàng bīng shìde, dàjiā dōu bù xiǎng chūmén." jyutping="ngoi6 min6 laang5 dak1 zoeng6 bing1 ci5 dik1, daai6 gaa1 dou1 bat1 soeng2 ceot1 mun4." english="It’s freezing outside; everyone looks as if they’d rather stay in." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她穿得像要去参加晚会似的，原来今天是她的生日。" traditional="她穿得像要去參加晚會似的，原來今天是她的生日。" pinyin="tā chuān de xiàng yào qù cānjiā wǎnhuì shìde, yuánlái jīntiān shì tā de shēngrì." jyutping="taa1 cyun1 dak1 zoeng6 jiu3 heoi3 caam1 gaa1 maan5 wui6 ci5 dik1, jyun4 loi4 gam1 tin1 si6 taa1 dik1 saang1 jat6." english="She’s dressed as if she’s going to a party; it turns out today is her birthday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这条路安静得像没人住似的，晚上却很热闹。" traditional="這條路安靜得像沒人住似的，晚上卻很熱鬧。" pinyin="zhè tiáo lù ānjìng de xiàng méi rén zhù shìde, wǎnshang què hěn rènao." jyutping="ze5 tiu4 lou6 on1 zing6 dak1 zoeng6 mui5 jan4 zyu6 ci5 dik1, maan5 soeng6 koek3 han2 jit6 naau6." english="This street is so quiet it seems no one lives here, but it gets lively at night." />

<Hint type="hint">似的 usually follows the image you are comparing something to: 像个孩子似的, “like a child”; 冷得像冰似的, “so cold it’s like ice.” The comparison can describe someone’s appearance, behavior, or a strong impression.</Hint>
