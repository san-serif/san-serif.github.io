You introduce someone at school: 她是我的老师. Here 是 connects a person to a role. It also labels or identifies a thing—这是茶, 前面是学校. When the next word is an adjective, though, Chinese usually lets the adjective describe the subject directly without 是.

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她是我的老师。" traditional="她是我的老師。" pinyin="Tā shì wǒ de lǎoshī." jyutping="taa1 si6 ngo5 dik1 lou5 si1." english="She is my teacher." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这是茶。" traditional="這是茶。" pinyin="Zhè shì chá." jyutping="ze5 si6 caa4." english="This is tea." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="前面是学校。" traditional="前面是學校。" pinyin="Qiánmiàn shì xuéxiào." jyutping="cin4 min6 si6 hok6 haau6." english="The school is up ahead." />

<Hint type="hint">Use 是 with nouns and noun phrases. With a simple adjective, say 天气很好, not 天气是很好.</Hint>
