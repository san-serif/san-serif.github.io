These expressions set one topic or condition aside to make a different point. はともかく and は別として bracket off a topic; ならともかく says “if it were X, that would be one thing,” implying the actual case is different.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="細[こま]かい点[てん]はともかく、全体[ぜんたい]の計画[けいかく]はよくできています。" romaji="Komakai ten wa tomokaku, zentai no keikaku wa yoku dekite imasu." english="Putting the details aside, the overall plan is well made." />

はともかく means “putting X aside for now.” It often shifts attention away from a secondary issue and toward the main evaluation.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="費用[ひよう]は別[べつ]として、準備[じゅんび]には時間[じかん]がかかります。" romaji="Hiyou wa betsu to shite, junbi ni wa jikan ga kakarimasu." english="Setting cost aside, preparation takes time." />

は別として also separates a point from the discussion. It sounds matter-of-fact and works well in careful speech or writing; it doesn't necessarily imply that the point is unimportant.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="子供[こども]ならともかく、大人[おとな]が約束[やくそく]を忘[わす]れるのは困[こま]ります。" romaji="Kodomo nara tomokaku, otona ga yakusoku o wasureru no wa komarimasu." english="If it were a child, that would be one thing, but it's a problem when an adult forgets a promise." />

ならともかく presents a hypothetical exception, then contrasts it with the real case. The speaker suggests that the first situation might be understandable, but the second is not.

<Hint type="insight">In a review, 細かい点はともかく can keep the conversation focused on the overall result. In a policy discussion, は別として cleanly separates a side issue. Use ならともかく when you want to say one case might be acceptable but another isn't.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">はともかく = set aside for now; は別として = consider separately; ならともかく = if it were X, that would be one thing. The last one sets up a clearer contrast with the actual situation.</Hint>
