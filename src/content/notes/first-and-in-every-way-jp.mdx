第一に is a formal way to say “first of all” when laying out reasons or priorities. 姿 refers to a visible form, appearance, or state, often in expressions such as 本来の姿, “the original form.” The related N2 expression 何から何まで means “everything, from beginning to end”; it is included here as a forward link for learners ready to extend the same scope vocabulary.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="第一[だいいち]に安全[あんぜん]を、第二[だいに]に使[つか]いやすさを考[かんが]えています。" romaji="Daiichi ni anzen o, daini ni tsukaiyasusa o kangaete imasu." english="First, we are considering safety, and second, ease of use." />

第一に and 第二に organize points in a presentation, report, or explanation. They are more structured than simply saying まず and 次に.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="修理[しゅうり]を終[お]えて、機械[きかい]は本来[ほんらい]の姿[すがた]に戻[もど]りました。" romaji="Shuuri o oete, kikai wa honrai no sugata ni modorimashita." english="After repairs, the machine returned to its original form." />

姿 can mean a person's appearance, a thing's visible shape, or the state in which something presents itself. It is used with context to clarify which kind of “form” is intended.

<Hint type="insight">In everyday descriptions, 姿 often appears with 見せる or 現す: 新しい姿を見せた, “showed a new face/form.” In a report, numbered points such as 第一に and 第二に make the order of an argument easy to follow.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Use 第一に to mark a first point, 姿 for an observable form or state, and 何から何まで for the whole range of details. They overlap in broad English translations about “everything,” but their grammar and role differ.</Hint>
