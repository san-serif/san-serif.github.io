Three huge volumes, either one route or another, and no electricity or Wi-Fi: these compact frames create different shapes. A number plus an adjective and measure word makes an amount stand out; 或A或B leaves alternatives open; 无A无B pairs two absences. Read the whole phrase to hear the relationship.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她整理出三大箱旧书，准备周末捐给社区图书馆。" traditional="她整理出三大箱舊書，準備週末捐給社區圖書館。" pinyin="tā zhěnglǐ chū sān dà xiāng jiùshū, zhǔnbèi zhōumò juān gěi shèqū túshūguǎn." jyutping="taa1 zing2 lei5 ceot1 saam1 daai6 soeng1 gau6 syu1, zeon2 bei6 zau1 mut6 gyun1 kap1 se5 keoi1 tou4 syu1 gun2." english="She sorted out three big boxes of old books to donate to the community library this weekend." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="周末你或坐火车或开车去都可以，我都能配合。" traditional="週末你或坐火車或開車去都可以，我都能配合。" pinyin="zhōumò nǐ huò zuò huǒchē huò kāichē qù dōu kěyǐ, wǒ dōu néng pèihé." jyutping="zau1 mut6 nei5 waak6 zo6 fo2 ce1 waak6 hoi1 ce1 heoi3 dou1 ho2 ji5, ngo5 dou1 nang4 pui3 hap6." english="This weekend you can go either by train or by car; I can work with either plan." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家小店无电无网，老板说大家正好可以放下手机聊天。" traditional="這家小店無電無網，老闆說大家正好可以放下手機聊天。" pinyin="zhè jiā xiǎodiàn wú diàn wú wǎng, lǎobǎn shuō dàjiā zhènghǎo kěyǐ fàngxià shǒujī liáotiān." jyutping="ze5 gaa1 siu2 dim3 mou4 din6 mou4 mong5, lou5 baan2 syut3 daai6 gaa1 zing3 hou2 ho2 ji5 fong3 haa6 sau2 gei1 liu4 tin1." english="This little café has no electricity or Wi-Fi; the owner says it’s a good chance for everyone to put their phones away and chat." />

<Hint type="hint">三大箱 counts three boxes and describes them as large. 或A或B offers alternatives, often in a set phrase such as 或坐火车或开车. 无A无B is emphatic and literary in feel; use it when both absences belong together, not as a routine replacement for 没有.</Hint>
