A guest asks how far the café is from the station. You need a starting point and a way to identify the place you will meet. 에서부터 marks where a route begins; -(으)ㄹ can put a future action before a noun.

### Make the Starting Point Clear

Place + 에서부터 means starting from that place. Pair it with 까지 for the destination. Plain 에서 often works too; 부터 makes the starting boundary explicit.

<SentenceBlock formal="역에서부터 카페까지 걸어서 십 분 걸립니다." formalRom="yeogeseobuteo kapekkaji georeoseo sip bun geollimnida." polite="역에서부터 카페까지 걸어서 십 분 걸려요." politeRom="yeogeseobuteo kapekkaji georeoseo sip bun geollyeoyo." casual="역에서부터 카페까지 걸어서 십 분 걸려." casualRom="yeogeseobuteo kapekkaji georeoseo sip bun geollyeo." english="It takes ten minutes to walk from the station to the café." />

<SentenceBlock formal="여기에서부터 같이 걷습니다." formalRom="yeogieseobuteo gachi geotseumnida." polite="여기에서부터 같이 걸어요." politeRom="yeogieseobuteo gachi georeoyo." casual="여기에서부터 같이 걸어." casualRom="yeogieseobuteo gachi georeo." english="We walk together from here." />

### Describe What Will Happen There

Attach -ㄹ after a vowel or ㄹ, -을 after another consonant, before a noun: 만날 장소, 먹을 음식, 만들 선물. It describes a future or intended action. You have already met this ending inside [갈 거예요](/notes/going-to); now the noun can be 장소, 음식, or anything the action describes.

<SentenceBlock formal="우리가 만날 카페는 역 앞에 있습니다." formalRom="uriga mannal kapeneun yeok ape itseumnida." polite="우리가 만날 카페는 역 앞에 있어요." politeRom="uriga mannal kapeneun yeok ape isseoyo." casual="우리가 만날 카페는 역 앞에 있어." casualRom="uriga mannal kapeneun yeok ape isseo." english="The café where we will meet is in front of the station." />

<SentenceBlock formal="내일 먹을 음식을 샀습니다." formalRom="naeil meogeul eumsigeul satseumnida." polite="내일 먹을 음식을 샀어요." politeRom="naeil meogeul eumsigeul sasseoyo." casual="내일 먹을 음식을 샀어." casualRom="naeil meogeul eumsigeul sasseo." english="I bought food to eat tomorrow." />

<Hint type="hint">Keep the modifier and the main sentence’s tense separate. 먹을 음식을 샀어요 means the buying is finished but the eating is still to come.</Hint>
