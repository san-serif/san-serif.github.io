如果……就…… sets out a condition and its likely result. In conversation, putting ……的话 before 就 gives a more spoken “if that’s the case…” frame.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="如果他来找我们的话，我们就会帮忙。" traditional="如果他來找我們的話，我們就會幫忙。" pinyin="rúguǒ tā lái zhǎo wǒmen dehuà， wǒmen jiù huì bāngmáng。" jyutping="jyu4 gwo2 taa1 loi4 zaau2 ngo5 mun4 dik1 waa6， ngo5 mun4 zau6 wui2 bong1 mong4。" english="If he comes to us, we’ll help him." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我要是你的话，我就不去。" traditional="我要是你的話，我就不去。" pinyin="wǒ yàoshi nǐ dehuà， wǒ jiù bú qù。" jyutping="ngo5 jiu3 si6 nei5 dik1 waa6， ngo5 zau6 bat1 heoi3。" english="If I were you, I wouldn’t go." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="“我明天再做。” “这话你昨天就说过了！”" traditional="“我明天再做。” “這話你昨天就說過了！”" pinyin="“wǒ míngtiān zài zuò。” “zhè huà nǐ zuótiān jiù shuō guò le！”" jyutping="“ngo5 ming4 tin1 zoi3 zou6。” “ze5 waa6 nei5 zok6 tin1 zau6 seoi3 gwo3 liu5！”" english="“I’ll do it again tomorrow.” “You said that just yesterday!”" />
