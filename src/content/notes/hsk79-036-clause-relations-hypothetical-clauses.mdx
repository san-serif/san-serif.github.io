倘若天气转晴，我们就出发；假设航班取消，我们得改计划. These forms introduce a hypothetical situation, with 若 especially at home in formal writing. 幸亏……要不然…… looks back at a near miss: something went well, and a worse result was avoided.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天天气很冷，您多穿衣服吧，要不然会感冒的。" traditional="今天天氣很冷，您多穿衣服吧，要不然會感冒的。" pinyin="jīntiān tiānqì hěn lěng, nín duō chuān yīfu ba, yàobùrán huì gǎnmào de." jyutping="gam1 tin1 tin1 hei3 han2 laang5, nei5 do1 cyun1 ji1 fuk6 baa6, jiu3 bat1 jin4 wui5 gam2 mou6 dik1" english="It is very cold today. Dress warmly, or you might catch a cold." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="倘若下雨，活动就取消。" traditional="倘若下雨，活動就取消。" pinyin="tǎngruò xiàyǔ, huódòng jiù qǔxiāo." jyutping="tong2 joek6 haa6 jyu5, wut6 dung6 zau6 ceoi2 siu1." english="If it rains, the event will be cancelled." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们现在能肯定地说，这个假设是成立的。" traditional="我們現在能肯定地說，這個假設是成立的。" pinyin="wǒmen xiànzài néng kěndìng de shuō， zhège jiǎshè shì chénglì de。" jyutping="ngo5 mun4 jin6 zoi6 nang4 hang2 ding6 dei6 seoi3， ze2 go3 gaa2 cit3 si6 sing4 laap6 dik1。" english="We can now say definitely that this hypothesis holds water." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="假使明天下雨，比赛就取消。" traditional="假使明天下雨，比賽就取消。" pinyin="jiǎshǐ míngtiān xiàyǔ, bǐsài jiù qǔxiāo." jyutping="gaa2 si2 ming4 tin1 haa6 jyu5, bei2 coi3 zau6 ceoi2 siu1." english="In case it rains tomorrow, the match will be cancelled." />
