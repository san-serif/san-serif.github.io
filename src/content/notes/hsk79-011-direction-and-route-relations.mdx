The trail follows the river until it reaches the old bridge. 顺着 introduces the route or line you follow; 逆着 points the other way, against its direction. The same contrast works for a road, a current, or a line of reasoning.







<CSentenceBlock simplified="从车站出来以后，顺着这条路一直走，就能看见博物馆。" traditional="從車站出來以後，順著這條路一直走，就能看見博物館。" pinyin="cóng chēzhàn chūlái yǐhòu, shùnzhe zhè tiáo lù yìzhí zǒu, jiù néng kànjiàn bówùguǎn." jyutping="cung4 ce1 zaam6 ceot1 loi4 ji5 hau6, seon6 zoek6 ze5 tiu4 lou6 jat1 zik6 zau2, zau6 nang4 hon3 gin3 bok3 mat6 gun2." english="After leaving the station, follow this road straight and you’ll see the museum." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="沿着河边顺着人行道走，十分钟后就到公园入口。" traditional="沿著河邊順著人行道走，十分鐘後就到公園入口。" pinyin="yánzhe hébiān shùnzhe rénxíngdào zǒu, shí fēnzhōng hòu jiù dào gōngyuán rùkǒu." jyutping="jin4 zoek6 ho4 bin1 seon6 zoek6 jan4 hang4 dou6 zau2, sap6 fan1 zung1 hau6 zau6 dou3 gung1 jyun4 jap6 hau2." english="Walk along the riverside path, and you’ll reach the park entrance in ten minutes." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们顺着山路往上走，快到山顶时天就黑了。" traditional="我們順著山路往上走，快到山頂時天就黑了。" pinyin="wǒmen shùnzhe shānlù wǎng shàng zǒu, kuài dào shāndǐng shí tiān jiù hēi le." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 seon6 zoek6 saan1 lou6 wong5 soeng6 zau2, faai3 dou3 saan1 ding2 si4 tin1 zau6 hak1 liu5." english="We followed the mountain path uphill; by the time we were near the top, it was getting dark." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="顺着这条线往下找，应该很快就能找到接线口。" traditional="順著這條線往下找，應該很快就能找到接線口。" pinyin="shùnzhe zhè tiáo xiàn wǎng xià zhǎo, yīnggāi hěn kuài jiù néng zhǎodào jiēxiànkǒu." jyutping="seon6 zoek6 ze5 tiu4 sin3 wong5 haa6 zaau2, jing1 goi1 han2 faai3 zau6 nang4 zaau2 dou3 zip3 sin3 hau2." english="Follow this cable down; you should find the connection port quickly." />
