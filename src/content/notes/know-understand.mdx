分かる means to understand or comprehend something, a rule, a language, a feeling. 知っている means to know a specific fact, person, or place, information you already have stored, not something you're processing in the moment.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="田中[たなか]さんは英語[えいご]がわかります。" romaji="Tanaka-san wa eigo ga wakarimasu." english="Tanaka understands English." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="妹[いもうと]はサッカーのルールがわかりません。" romaji="Imouto wa sakkaa no ruuru ga wakarimasen." english="My younger sister doesn't understand the rules of soccer." />

<Hint>分かる always takes が for the thing being understood, and it describes comprehension happening, a rule making sense, a language becoming readable. It's not about having met or memorized something, it's about it clicking.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="リン[りん]さんは美味[おい]しいレストランを知[し]っています。" romaji="Rin-san wa oishii resutoran o shitteimasu." english="Lin knows a good restaurant." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="ケン[けん]さんは私[わたし]の弟[おとうと]を知[し]りません。" romaji="Ken-san wa watashi no otouto o shirimasen." english="Ken doesn't know my younger brother." />

<Hint>知っている takes を, and the negative is 知りません or 知らない, never 知っていません. This is one of the more common trip-ups: 知る describes gaining information as an action, and once you already know something the state is 知っている, but denying that state uses the plain verb's negative, not the ている form's negative.</Hint>
