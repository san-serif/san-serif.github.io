“Can you ask Mei to come in?” With 叫、派、请, or 让, the person named after the first verb carries out the next action: 请她进来. That person is the hinge between the two verbs, so there’s no need to say her name twice.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="让我来帮你吧。你的包看起来很重。" traditional="讓我來幫你吧。你的包看起來很重。" pinyin="ràng wǒ lái bāng nǐ ba。 nǐ de bāo kànqǐlai hěn chóng。" jyutping="joeng6 ngo5 loi4 bong1 nei5 baa6。 nei5 dik1 baau1 hon1 hei2 loi4 han2 cung5。" english="Let me help you. Your bag looks very heavy." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我叫你的名字的时候，请你把手举起来。" traditional="我叫你的名字的時候，請你把手舉起來。" pinyin="wǒ jiào nǐ de míngzi de shíhou， qǐng nǐ bǎ shǒu jǔ qǐlai。" jyutping="ngo5 giu3 nei5 dik1 ming4 zi6 dik1 si4 hau6， cing2 nei5 baa2 sau2 geoi2 hei2 loi4。" english="Please raise your hand when I call your name." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你认为我们应该派人去请医生来吗？" traditional="你認為我們應該派人去請醫生來嗎？" pinyin="nǐ rènwéi wǒmen yīnggāi pài rén qù qǐng yīshēng lái ma？" jyutping="nei5 jing6 wai4 ngo5 mun4 jing1 goi1 paai3 jan4 heoi3 cing2 ji1 sang1 loi4 maa3？" english="Do you think we should send someone to invite the doctor over?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我请他在这里等。" traditional="我請他在這裡等。" pinyin="wǒ qǐng tā zài zhèlǐ děng。" jyutping="ngo5 cing2 taa1 zoi6 ze5 leoi5 dang2。" english="I asked him to wait here." />
