为 can introduce the person who benefits from an action: 我为你留了座位, “I saved you a seat.” Put the beneficiary after 为 and before the action; compare 给, which more often marks a recipient or transfer.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她成为了医生。" traditional="她成為了醫生。" pinyin="tā chéngwéi le yīshēng." jyutping="taa1 sing4 wai4 liu5 ji1 sang1." english="She became a doctor." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我认为他是对的。" traditional="我認為他是對的。" pinyin="wǒ rènwéi tā shì duì de." jyutping="ngo5 jing6 wai4 taa1 si6 deoi3 dik1." english="I think he's right." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我为你高兴。" traditional="我為你高興。" pinyin="wǒ wèi nǐ gāoxìng." jyutping="ngo5 wai6 nei5 gou1 hing3." english="I'm happy for you." />
