The report is due tonight, and you have not finished it. 非……不可 makes the need sound firm: the action has to happen, with no real alternative. The pattern can emphasize the action itself or the person required for it.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="下班前，我非把这份报告写完不可。" traditional="下班前，我非把這份報告寫完不可。" pinyin="xiàbān qián, wǒ fēi bǎ zhè fèn bàogào xiě wán bùkě." jyutping="haa6 baan1 cin4, ngo5 fei1 baa2 ze5 fan6 bou3 gou3 se2 jyun4 bat1 ho2." english="I absolutely have to finish this report before work ends." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这件事非他不可。" traditional="這件事非他不可。" pinyin="zhè jiàn shì fēi tā bùkě." jyutping="ze5 gin6 si6 fei1 taa1 bat1 ho2." english="This matter absolutely requires him." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="去机场接客人，我们非现在出发不可。" traditional="去機場接客人，我們非現在出發不可。" pinyin="qù jīchǎng jiē kèrén, wǒmen fēi xiànzài chūfā bùkě." jyutping="heoi3 gei1 coeng4 zip3 haak3 jan4, ngo5 mun4 fei1 jin6 zoi6 ceot1 faat3 bat1 ho2." english="We have to leave now to meet the guest at the airport." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="有些话非当面说清楚不可，发消息不够。" traditional="有些話非當面說清楚不可，發消息不夠。" pinyin="yǒuxiē huà fēi dāngmiàn shuō qīngchu bùkě, fā xiāoxi búgòu." jyutping="jau5 se1 waa6 fei1 dong1 min6 syut3 cing1 co2 bat1 ho2, faat3 siu1 sik1 bat1 gau3." english="Some things have to be said face to face; a message isn’t enough." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="为了按时交稿，我们今晚非加班不可。" traditional="為了按時交稿，我們今晚非加班不可。" pinyin="wèile ànshí jiāogǎo, wǒmen jīnwǎn fēi jiābān bùkě." jyutping="wai6 liu5 on3 si4 gaau1 gou2, ngo5 mun4 gam1 maan5 fei1 gaa1 baan1 bat1 ho2." english="To meet the deadline, we absolutely have to work overtime tonight." />

<Hint type="hint">非 comes before the required action or person, and 不可 closes the frame. To make someone necessary, use 非 + person + 不可, as in 这事非他不行.</Hint>
