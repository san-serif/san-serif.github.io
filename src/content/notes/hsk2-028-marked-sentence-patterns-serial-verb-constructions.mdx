You finish class, go to the bank, then pick up groceries on the way home. Chinese can line up the actions in the order they happen: finish, go, pick up. The same subject often carries through the whole sequence.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="下课以后，我去银行取钱。" traditional="下課以後，我去銀行取錢。" pinyin="xiàkè yǐhòu, wǒ qù yínháng qǔ qián." jyutping="haa6 fo3 ji5 hau6, ngo5 heoi3 ngan4 hong4 ceoi2 cin2." english="After class, I’m going to the bank to withdraw money." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我去超市买菜，晚上在家做饭。" traditional="我去超市買菜，晚上在家做飯。" pinyin="wǒ qù chāoshì mǎi cài, wǎnshang zài jiā zuò fàn." jyutping="ngo5 heoi3 ciu1 si5 maai5 coi3, maan5 soeng6 zoi6 gaa1 zou6 faan6." english="I’m going to the supermarket to buy vegetables, then cook dinner at home." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他吃完早饭就开车去上班。" traditional="他吃完早飯就開車去上班。" pinyin="tā chī wán zǎofàn jiù kāichē qù shàngbān." jyutping="taa1 hek3 jyun4 zou2 faan6 zau6 hoi1 ce1 heoi3 soeng5 baan1." english="After breakfast, he drives to work." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们先去取票，再进电影院。" traditional="我們先去取票，再進電影院。" pinyin="wǒmen xiān qù qǔ piào, zài jìn diànyǐngyuàn." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 sin1 heoi3 ceoi2 piu3, zoi3 zeon3 din6 jing2 jyun2." english="We’ll pick up the tickets first, then go into the cinema." />

<Hint type="hint">These verbs share one subject and happen in sequence. If you want to make the order extra clear, add 先……再…… (“first…then…”).</Hint>
