You’re reading a class list and see 老王, 小李, 同学们, and 画家. These short pieces help you read the relationship between words: 老/小 often come before familiar names, 们 gathers people into a group, and 家 can turn a field of work into a person who does it.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="那位画家给同学们介绍了自己的作品。" traditional="那位畫家給同學們介紹了自己的作品。" pinyin="nà wèi huàjiā gěi tóngxuémen jièshào le zìjǐ de zuòpǐn." jyutping="naa5 wai2 waa6 gaa1 kap1 tung4 hok6 mun4 gaai3 siu6 liu5 zi6 gei2 dik1 zok3 ban2." english="That artist showed the students their work." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="小李和老张都在第三排。" traditional="小李和老張都在第三排。" pinyin="Xiǎo Lǐ hé Lǎo Zhāng dōu zài dì sān pái." jyutping="siu2 lei5 wo4 lou5 zoeng1 dou1 zoi6 dai6 saam1 paai4." english="Xiao Li and Lao Zhang are both in the third row." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="孩子们把石头放进了小盒子里。" traditional="孩子們把石頭放進了小盒子裡。" pinyin="háizimen bǎ shítou fàng jìn le xiǎo hézi lǐ." jyutping="haai4 zi2 mun4 baa2 sek6 tau4 fong3 zeon3 liu5 siu2 hap6 zi2 lei5." english="The children put the stones into the little box." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="小孩儿和大人们都在门口排队。" traditional="小孩兒和大人們都在門口排隊。" pinyin="xiǎoháir hé dàrénmen dōu zài ménkǒu páiduì." jyutping="siu2 haai4 ji4 wo4 daai6 jan4 mun4 dou1 zoi6 mun4 hau2 paai4 deoi6." english="The children and adults are all waiting in line at the entrance." />

<Hint type="hint">These pieces don’t work like English endings in every word. Learn them through familiar examples: 画家 is an artist, but 家 in 第三家小店 is a measure word for shops.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">儿 is often heard as a colloquial ending in northern speech, as in 小孩儿. It’s useful to recognize, but you don’t need to add it to every word.</Hint>
