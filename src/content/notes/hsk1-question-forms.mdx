You’re trying to find a classroom, so you ask where it is; then you check whether your friend wants tea or coffee. Chinese questions keep the missing information in its usual place: 哪儿 asks for the place, while 还是 offers a choice. Add 吗 when you simply want a yes or no.

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你是老师吗？" traditional="你是老師嗎？" pinyin="Nǐ shì lǎoshī ma?" jyutping="nei5 si6 lou5 si1 maa3?" english="Are you a teacher?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你要几杯茶？" traditional="你要幾杯茶？" pinyin="Nǐ yào jǐ bēi chá?" jyutping="nei5 jiu3 gei2 bui1 caa4?" english="How many cups of tea do you want?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你怎么去学校？" traditional="你怎麼去學校？" pinyin="Nǐ zěnme qù xuéxiào?" jyutping="nei5 zam2 mo1 heoi3 hok6 haau6?" english="How do you get to school?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你喝水还是茶？" traditional="你喝水還是茶？" pinyin="Nǐ hē shuǐ háishi chá?" jyutping="nei5 hot3 seoi2 waan4 si6 caa4?" english="Would you like water or tea?" />

<Hint type="hint">Do not combine 吗 with a question word or 还是 in the same basic question. The question word stays in the place of the answer.</Hint>
