The forecast has already confirmed the rain, so the picnic is moving indoors. 既然 treats that reason as settled and leads to a next step, often with 就. 可见 looks back at what you’ve observed and draws a conclusion: “as you can see…”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="既然雨停了，我们就回家吧。" traditional="既然雨停了，我們就回家吧。" pinyin="jìrán yǔ tíng le， wǒmen jiù huíjiā ba。" jyutping="gei3 jin4 jyu5 ting4 liu5， ngo5 mun4 zau6 wui4 gaa1 baa6。" english="Since the rain has stopped, let’s go home." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他没来，可见他不想参加。" traditional="他沒來，可見他不想參加。" pinyin="tā méi lái， kějiàn tā bùxiǎng cānjiā。" jyutping="taa1 mut6 loi4， ho2 gin3 taa1 bat1 soeng2 caam1 gaa1。" english="He did not come, which shows that he does not want to participate." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="“我明天再做。” “这话你昨天就说过了！”" traditional="“我明天再做。” “這話你昨天就說過了！”" pinyin="“wǒ míngtiān zài zuò。” “zhè huà nǐ zuótiān jiù shuō guò le！”" jyutping="“ngo5 ming4 tin1 zoi3 zou6。” “ze5 waa6 nei5 zok6 tin1 zau6 seoi3 gwo3 liu5！”" english="“I’ll do it again tomorrow.” “You said that just yesterday!”" />
