Without the team's practice, there would be no performance. 没有……就没有…… makes the result depend on a necessary condition. 再……也…… means “no matter how…,” often showing that even a great effort or an extreme case won’t change the outcome.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我没有读过他的小说，我哥哥也没有。" traditional="我沒有讀過他的小說，我哥哥也沒有。" pinyin="wǒ méiyǒu dú guò tā de xiǎoshuō， wǒ gēge yě méiyǒu。" jyutping="ngo5 mut6 jau5 duk6 gwo3 taa1 dik1 siu2 syut3， ngo5 go1 go1 jaa5 mut6 jau5。" english="I haven't read his novel, and my brother hasn't either." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我不知道他什么时候会再来的。" traditional="我不知道他什麼時候會再來的。" pinyin="wǒ bù zhīdào tā shénme shíhou huì zài lái de。" jyutping="ngo5 bat1 zi1 dou6 taa1 sam6 mo1 si4 hau6 wui2 zoi3 loi4 dik1。" english="I don't know when he'll come again." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我也不是。" traditional="我也不是。" pinyin="wǒ yě bùshì。" jyutping="ngo5 jaa5 bat1 si6。" english="Nor am I." />
