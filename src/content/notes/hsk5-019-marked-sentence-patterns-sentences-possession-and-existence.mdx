Look at the wall: the photos are still hanging there. 有着 can present a lasting feature or possession; after an action verb, 有 can show what remains in place, as in 墙上挂有照片. The word order tells whether you’re describing a feature or a result left behind.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你们有吗？" traditional="你們有嗎？" pinyin="nǐmen yǒu ma？" jyutping="nei5 mun4 jau5 maa3？" english="Do you have it?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他具有才能。" traditional="他具有才能。" pinyin="tā jùyǒu cáinéng。" jyutping="taa1 geoi6 jau5 coi4 nang4。" english="He has talent." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我会在这里等着他回来。" traditional="我會在這裡等著他回來。" pinyin="wǒ huì zài zhèlǐ děng zhe tā huílái。" jyutping="ngo5 wui2 zoi6 ze5 leoi5 dang2 zoek3 taa1 wui4 loi4。" english="I’ll wait here for him to come back." />
