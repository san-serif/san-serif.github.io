In English, negation is lazy. You slap "not" after "is" and move on. Korean doesn't let you get away with that. The opposite of 이다 (to be) isn't "안 이다." It's a completely different word, 아니다, and like everything else in this language, it reshapes itself depending on who you're talking to. This isn't one of the numbered grammar points in the class notes, but it comes up constantly once you start forming your own sentences, so it's worth its own lesson.

### 이다 → 아니다

Most Korean verbs go negative by parking 안 in front of them. The copula ignores that rule. 이다 and 아니다 are two unrelated words that happen to mean opposite things, and 아니다 insists on its own subject marker, 이/가, even in a sentence that would normally take 은/는.

<SentenceBlock formal="학생입니다." formalRom="haksaengimnida." polite="학생이에요." politeRom="haksaengieyo." casual="학생이야." casualRom="haksaengiya." english="I'm a student." />

<SentenceBlock formal="학생이 아닙니다." formalRom="haksaengi animnida." polite="학생이 아니에요." politeRom="haksaengi anieyo." casual="학생이 아니야." casualRom="haksaengi aniya." english="I'm not a student." />

<SentenceBlock formal="의사입니다." formalRom="uisaimnida." polite="의사예요." politeRom="uisayeyo." casual="의사야." casualRom="uisaya." english="I'm a doctor." />

<SentenceBlock formal="의사가 아닙니다." formalRom="uisaga animnida." polite="의사가 아니에요." politeRom="uisaga anieyo." casual="의사가 아니야." casualRom="uisaga aniya." english="I'm not a doctor." />

<Hint>아니요 (the answer word for "no") and 아니다/아니에요 (the verb for "is not") look related and share the same root idea, but they're not interchangeable. 아니요 answers a yes/no question on its own. 아니에요 has to attach to whatever's being denied. Mixing them up is one of the most common beginner slips.</Hint>

### Yes, But Actually No

This is the sentence shape Koreans reach for constantly: deny one thing, then immediately supply the real answer. **아니요, X 아니에요. Y예요.** "No, not X. Y." Learn this pattern once and half of small talk becomes filling in the blanks. The examples below start simple and add a piece of grammar each time.

<SentenceBlock formal="모자가 아닙니다. 티셔츠입니다." formalRom="mojaga animnida. tisyeocheuimnida." polite="모자가 아니에요. 티셔츠예요." politeRom="mojaga anieyo. tisyeocheuyeyo." casual="모자 아니야. 티셔츠야." casualRom="moja aniya. tisyeocheuya." english="It's not a hat. It's a T-shirt." />

<SentenceBlock formal="아니요, 물이 아닙니다. 주스입니다." formalRom="aniyo, muri animnida. juseuimnida." polite="아니요, 물이 아니에요. 주스예요." politeRom="aniyo, muri anieyo. juseuyeyo." casual="아니, 물 아니야. 주스야." casualRom="ani, mul aniya. juseuya." english="No, it's not water. It's juice." />

<SentenceBlock formal="아니요, 컴퓨터가 아닙니다. 텔레비전입니다." formalRom="aniyo, keompyuteoga animnida. tellebijeonimnida." polite="아니요, 컴퓨터가 아니에요. 텔레비전이에요." politeRom="aniyo, keompyuteoga anieyo. tellebijeonieyo." casual="아니, 컴퓨터 아니야. 텔레비전이야." casualRom="ani, keompyuteo aniya. tellebijeoniya." english="No, it's not a computer. It's a television." />

<SentenceBlock formal="아니요, 남자친구가 아닙니다. 동생입니다." formalRom="aniyo, namjachinguga animnida. dongsaengimnida." polite="아니요, 남자친구가 아니에요. 동생이에요." politeRom="aniyo, namjachinguga anieyo. dongsaengieyo." casual="아니, 남자친구 아니야. 동생이야." casualRom="ani, namjachingu aniya. dongsaengiya." english="No, he's not my boyfriend. He's my younger brother." />

<SentenceBlock formal="아니요, 선생님이 아닙니다. 학생입니다." formalRom="aniyo, seonsaengnimi animnida. haksaengimnida." polite="아니요, 선생님 아니에요. 학생이에요." politeRom="aniyo, seonsaengnim anieyo. haksaengieyo." casual="아니, 선생님 아니야. 학생이야." casualRom="ani, seonsaengnim aniya. haksaengiya." english="No, I'm not a teacher. I'm a student." />

<Hint>Look closely at the polite row above: 선생님 아니에요, not the grammatically fuller 선생님이 아니에요. In fast, natural speech the 이/가 marker gets dropped constantly, especially in short answers. You'll hear both. Textbooks teach the full form because it's always correct, but don't be thrown when a real conversation skips it.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="유진 씨는 한국 사람이 아닙니다. 미국 사람입니다." formalRom="yujin ssineun hanguk sarami animnida. miguk saramimnida." polite="유진 씨는 한국 사람이 아니에요. 미국 사람이에요." politeRom="yujin ssineun hanguk sarami anieyo. miguk saramieyo." casual="유진 씨 한국 사람 아니야. 미국 사람이야." casualRom="yujin ssi hanguk saram aniya. miguk saramiya." english="Yujin isn't Korean. She's American." />
