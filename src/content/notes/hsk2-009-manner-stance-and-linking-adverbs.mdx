The movie starts at seven, but your friend arrives at 6:59: 正好赶上. If they arrive at 7:20, 才 can underline how late it is; 就 often makes a result feel sooner or easier, while 都 can sweep in everyone or everything. These adverbs tell you how the timing feels, not just when it happened.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="现在正好三点。" traditional="現在正好三點。" pinyin="xiànzài zhènghǎo sān diǎn。" jyutping="jin6 zoi6 zing3 hou2 saam1 dim2。" english="It’s exactly three o’clock now." />
