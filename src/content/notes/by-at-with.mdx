で marks the means something happens by, the tool, the material, or the method. English splits this across "by," "with," and "in," Japanese just uses で for all of it.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="自転車[じてんしゃ]で家[いえ]に帰[かえ]ります。" romaji="Jitensha de ie ni kaerimasu." english="I go home by bicycle." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="ハサミで紙[かみ]を切[き]ります。" romaji="Hasami de kami o kirimasu." english="I cut paper with scissors." />

<Hint>で covers the physical tool doing the cutting (ハサミで) and the transport getting you home (自転車で) with the exact same particle. English needs "with" for one and "by" for the other; Japanese doesn't bother making the distinction.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="レポートを日本語[にほんご]で書[か]いてください。" romaji="Repooto o nihongo de kaite kudasai." english="Please write the report in Japanese." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="カードで払[はら]ってもいいですか。" romaji="Kaado de haratte mo ii desu ka." english="Is it okay to pay by card?" />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="実[じつ]は、ネットオークションで買[か]ったんですよ。" romaji="Jitsu wa, netto ookushon de katta n desu yo." english="Actually, I bought it at an online auction." />

<Hint>で also marks the language something is done in (日本語で書く) and the venue or platform something happens on (ネットオークションで買う). If you can rephrase the English as "by means of X," で is almost always the particle you want.</Hint>
