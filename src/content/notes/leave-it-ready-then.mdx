Friends are coming for dinner. You prepare the ingredients ahead of time, then wait until they arrive to cook. -아/어 놓다 keeps a result ready; -(으)ㄴ 다음에 puts the next action after a completed step.

### Leave the Result in Place

Add 놓다 to the -아/어 form: 썰어 놓다, 열어 놓다, 준비해 놓다. The action is completed and its result is maintained, often for later use. In conversation 놓아요 often contracts to 놔요, and 놓았어요 to 놨어요.

<SentenceBlock formal="채소를 미리 썰어 놓았습니다." formalRom="chaesoreul miri sseoreo noatseumnida." polite="채소를 미리 썰어 놓았어요." politeRom="chaesoreul miri sseoreo noasseoyo." casual="채소를 미리 썰어 놓았어." casualRom="chaesoreul miri sseoreo noasseo." english="I chopped the vegetables in advance and left them ready." />

<SentenceBlock formal="물을 냉장고에 넣어 놓았습니다." formalRom="mureul naengjanggoe neoeo noatseumnida." polite="물을 냉장고에 넣어 놓았어요." politeRom="mureul naengjanggoe neoeo noasseoyo." casual="물을 냉장고에 넣어 놓았어." casualRom="mureul naengjanggoe neoeo noasseo." english="I put the water in the fridge ready for later." />

### After the Next Step Is Complete

Use -ㄴ 다음에 after a vowel and -은 다음에 after most consonants. A ㄹ stem drops ㄹ: 산 다음에, 만든 다음에, 씻은 다음에. Like [-(으)ㄴ 후에](/notes/then-what) and [-고 나서](/notes/finish-first-ill-handle-it), it marks sequence after completion.

<SentenceBlock formal="친구들이 온 다음에 고기를 구울 것입니다." formalRom="chingudeuri on daeume gogireul guul geosimnida." polite="친구들이 온 다음에 고기를 구울 거예요." politeRom="chingudeuri on daeume gogireul guul geoyeyo." casual="친구들이 온 다음에 고기를 구울 거야." casualRom="chingudeuri on daeume gogireul guul geoya." english="I’ll grill the meat after my friends arrive." />

<SentenceBlock formal="밥을 먹은 다음에 같이 설거지합니다." formalRom="babeul meogeun daeume gachi seolgeojihamnida." polite="밥을 먹은 다음에 같이 설거지해요." politeRom="babeul meogeun daeume gachi seolgeojihaeyo." casual="밥을 먹은 다음에 같이 설거지해." casualRom="babeul meogeun daeume gachi seolgeojihae." english="We wash the dishes together after eating." />

<Hint type="hint">온 다음에 can refer to a future arrival. The completed modifier shows that the arrival must be complete before the cooking; the main verb places the whole plan in the future.</Hint>
