In a formal notice, you might explain that an event has been moved because a storm is forecast. These written expressions make the reason-result link explicit: 鉴于 presents the circumstances, 由于 introduces the cause, and 因……故 frames a concise formal explanation.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="鉴于天气原因，活动取消。" traditional="鑒於天氣原因，活動取消。" pinyin="jiànyú tiānqì yuányīn, huódòng qǔxiāo." jyutping="gaam3 jyu1 tin1 hei3 jyun4 jan1, wut6 dung6 ceoi2 siu1." english="In view of the weather, the event is cancelled." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="由于缺水，花园里所有的花都死了。" traditional="由於缺水，花園裡所有的花都死了。" pinyin="yóuyú quēshuǐ， huāyuán lǐ suǒyǒu de huā dōu sǐ le。" jyutping="jau4 jyu1 kyut3 seoi2， faa1 jyun2 lei5 so2 jau5 dik1 faa1 dou1 sei2 liu5。" english="Because of the water shortage, all the flowers in the garden died." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="由于长期缺乏沟通，双方误会加深，以致合作关系破裂。" traditional="由於長期缺乏溝通，雙方誤會加深，以致合作關係破裂。" pinyin="yóuyú chángqī quēfá gōutōng, shuāngfāng wùhuì jiāshēn, yǐzhì hézuò guānxi pòliè." jyutping="jau4 jyu1 coeng4 kei4 kyut3 fat6 kau1 tung1, soeng1 fong1 ng6 wui6 gaa1 sam1, ji5 zi3 hap6 zok3 gwaan1 hai6 po3 lit6." english="Because communication had been lacking for a long time, the misunderstanding deepened and the partnership eventually broke down." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="因天气恶劣，故活动取消。" traditional="因天氣惡劣，故活動取消。" pinyin="yīn tiānqì èliè, gù huódòng qǔxiāo." jyutping="jan1 tin1 hei3 ok3 lit6, gu3 wut6 dung6 ceoi2 siu1." english="Because of the severe weather, the event is canceled." />

<Hint type="hint">鉴于 often appears in notices or formal explanations. 因……故 is concise and written; everyday conversation more often uses 因为……所以…… or 由于…….</Hint>
