Notice whether the speaker is repeating an action, accepting that something cannot be changed, or rejecting both alternatives. Each frame signals a different response.

### Turning It Over in Your Mind

X来X去 describes repeating an action or thought from different angles before reaching a result.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我想来想去，还是决定去一趟。" traditional="我想來想去，還是決定去一趟。" pinyin="Wǒ xiǎng lái xiǎng qù, háishi juédìng qù yí tàng." jyutping="ngo5 soeng2 loi4 soeng2 heoi3, waan4 si6 kyut3 ding6 heoi3 jat1 tong3." english="After thinking it over, I decided to go after all." />


### Accepting What Has Happened

X了就X了 accepts a completed event instead of arguing about it. The next clause often says what to do now.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="错了就错了，以后改正就是了。" traditional="錯了就錯了，以後改正就是了。" pinyin="Cuò le jiù cuò le, yǐhòu gǎizhèng jiù shì le." jyutping="co3 liu5 zau6 co3 liu5, ji5 hau6 goi2 zing3 zau6 si6 liu5." english="If it was wrong, it was wrong; just correct it next time." />


### Rejecting Both Options

这也不X，那也不Y rejects both choices and often invites the other person to say what would work.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这也不行，那也不行，你到底想要什么？" traditional="這也不行，那也不行，你到底想要什麼？" pinyin="Zhè yě bù xíng, nà yě bù xíng, nǐ dàodǐ xiǎng yào shénme?" jyutping="ze2 jaa5 bat1 hang4, naa5 jaa5 bat1 hang4, nei5 dou3 dai2 soeng2 jiu3 sam6 mo1?" english="This won’t do, that won’t do either—what do you actually want?" />

<Hint type="hint">Read the frame as a whole before translating each word. The repeated words are part of the pattern, not accidental repetition.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">These forms are conversational: speakers use them to show annoyance, resignation, or emphasis. Tone of voice helps you tell playful teasing from real criticism.</Hint>
