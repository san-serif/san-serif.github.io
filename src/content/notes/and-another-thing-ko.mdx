You want to say two things about your plans: meet a friend and eat dinner. Or describe a café as small and quiet. -고 joins the clauses without requiring a reason, a contrast, or a strict sequence.

### List Two Actions

Attach -고 directly to the action verb stem: 만나고, 먹고, 읽고. The clauses may have the same subject or different subjects. The last verb usually gives the tense for a simple list of planned or completed actions.

<SentenceBlock formal="친구를 만나고 저녁을 먹을 것입니다." formalRom="chingureul mannago jeonyeogeul meogeul geosimnida." polite="친구를 만나고 저녁을 먹을 거예요." politeRom="chingureul mannago jeonyeogeul meogeul geoyeyo." casual="친구를 만나고 저녁을 먹을 거야." casualRom="chingureul mannago jeonyeogeul meogeul geoya." english="I’ll meet a friend and have dinner." />

<SentenceBlock formal="저는 책을 읽고 동생은 음악을 듣습니다." formalRom="jeoneun chaegeul ilgo dongsaengeun eumageul deutseumnida." polite="저는 책을 읽고 동생은 음악을 들어요." politeRom="jeoneun chaegeul ilgo dongsaengeun eumageul deureoyo." casual="나는 책을 읽고 동생은 음악을 들어." casualRom="naneun chaegeul ilgo dongsaengeun eumageul deureo." english="I read a book, and my younger sibling listens to music." />

### Add Another Description

Adjectives also take -고 directly: 작고, 조용하고, 맵고. Nouns take -(이)고: 학생이고, 의사이고. A list can describe several features of one person or thing. For an explicit contrast use [-지만](/notes/but).

<SentenceBlock formal="이 카페는 작고 조용합니다." formalRom="i kapeneun jakgo joyonghamnida." polite="이 카페는 작고 조용해요." politeRom="i kapeneun jakgo joyonghaeyo." casual="이 카페는 작고 조용해." casualRom="i kapeneun jakgo joyonghae." english="This café is small and quiet." />

<SentenceBlock formal="이 음식은 맵고 맛있습니다." formalRom="i eumsigeun maepgo masitseumnida." polite="이 음식은 맵고 맛있어요." politeRom="i eumsigeun maepgo masisseoyo." casual="이 음식은 맵고 맛있어." casualRom="i eumsigeun maepgo masisseo." english="This food is spicy and tasty." />

<Hint type="hint">-고 can describe actions in order, but does not by itself insist that the first is complete before the second starts. [-(으)ㄴ 후에](/notes/then-what) and [-고 나서](/notes/finish-first-ill-handle-it) make that completion explicit. For a linked action such as going somewhere and doing something there, compare [-아서/어서](/notes/and-then).</Hint>
