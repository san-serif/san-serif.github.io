<JapaneseLessonHead />

すぎる attaches to a verb's stem or an adjective's stem, and means "too much," pushing whatever it attaches to past a reasonable limit.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="沙耶[さや]さんは、お酒[さけ]を飲[の]みすぎます。" romaji="Saya-san wa, osake o nomisugimasu." english="Saya drinks too much." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="沙耶[さや]さんは、電話[でんわ]で話[はな]しすぎます。" romaji="Saya-san wa, denwa de hanashisugimasu." english="Saya talks too much on the phone." />

<Hint>飲む becomes 飲みすぎる, 話す becomes 話しすぎる, attaching to the same stem you already used for たい and はじめる. すぎる then conjugates like an ordinary る-verb from there, すぎます, すぎました, すぎない.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="そうですね、母[はは]にはちょっと派手[はで]すぎると思[おも]います。" romaji="Sou desu ne, haha ni wa chotto hadesugiru to omoimasu." english="Yeah, I think that's a bit too flashy for my mother." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="お金[かね]がかかるから、使[つか]いすぎない方[ほう]がいいですよ。" romaji="Okane ga kakaru kara, tsukaisuginai hou ga ii desu yo." english="It costs money, so you'd better not spend too much." />

<Hint>い-adjectives drop the final い before すぎる, 派手 (a な-adjective) attaches directly with no change at all, and combined with the negative ない-form, すぎない方がいい becomes a natural way to warn against overdoing something.</Hint>
