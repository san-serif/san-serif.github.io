“You were brilliant in that meeting.” “不敢当—there was a good team behind me.” This modest reply means “you’re too kind.” In other moments, 得了 can end a discussion with “that’s enough,” and 用不着 reassures someone that there’s no need to do something.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="谢谢，我不敢当。" traditional="謝謝，我不敢當。" pinyin="xièxie， wǒ bùgǎndāng。" jyutping="ze6 ze6， ngo5 bat1 gam2 dong1。" english="Thank you, but I don’t deserve the praise." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="对这种人，用不着客气。" traditional="對這種人，用不著客氣。" pinyin="duì zhèzhǒng rén， yòngbuzháo kèqi。" jyutping="deoi3 ze2 zung2 jan4， jung6 bat1 zoek6 haak3 hei3。" english="There's no need to be polite with this kind of person." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你也真敢说。你不过也才得了65分呀。" traditional="你也真敢說。你不過也才得了65分呀。" pinyin="nǐ yě zhēn gǎn shuō。 nǐ bùguò yě cái déle 65 fēn ya。" jyutping="nei5 jaa5 zan1 gam2 seoi3。 nei5 bat1 gwo3 jaa5 coi4 dak1 liu5 65 fan1 aa1。" english="You should talk. You only got a 65." />
