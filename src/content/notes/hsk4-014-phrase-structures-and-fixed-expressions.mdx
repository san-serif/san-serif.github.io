The train leaves in ten minutes. Can you still make it? 来得及 and 来不及 answer whether there’s enough time; 说不定 leaves the outcome open, and 一般来说 describes what’s usually true without promising that it always is.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="明天说不定会下雨。" traditional="明天說不定會下雨。" pinyin="míngtiān shuōbudìng huì xiàyǔ。" jyutping="ming4 tin1 syut3 bat1 ding6 wui5 haa6 jyu5。" english="It might rain tomorrow." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="一般来说，冬天比夏天冷。" traditional="一般來說，冬天比夏天冷。" pinyin="yìbān láishuō， dōngtiān bǐ xiàtiān lěng。" jyutping="jat1 bun1 loi4 syut3， dung1 tin1 bei2 haa6 tin1 laang5。" english="Generally speaking, winter is colder than summer." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我还来得及吗？" traditional="我還來得及嗎？" pinyin="wǒ hái lái de jí ma？" jyutping="ngo5 waan4 loi4 dak1 kap6 maa3？" english="Do I still have time?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="来不及了。" traditional="來不及了。" pinyin="lái bu jí le。" jyutping="loi4 bat1 kap6 liu5。" english="It’s too late." />
