You buy a new phone and drop it immediately. There is hardly a moment between the events, and the damage is already done. -자마자 closes the time gap; -아/어 버리다 highlights the completed result and your reaction to it.

### One Event Follows Immediately

Attach -자마자 directly to an action verb stem: 사자마자, 도착하자마자, 끝나자마자. Put past or future tense on the final verb. Unlike a general after-expression, it leaves no meaningful delay.

<SentenceBlock formal="휴대폰을 사자마자 떨어뜨렸습니다." formalRom="hyudaeponeul sajamaja tteoreotteuryeotseumnida." polite="휴대폰을 사자마자 떨어뜨렸어요." politeRom="hyudaeponeul sajamaja tteoreotteuryeosseoyo." casual="휴대폰을 사자마자 떨어뜨렸어." casualRom="hyudaeponeul sajamaja tteoreotteuryeosseo." english="I dropped the phone as soon as I bought it." />

<SentenceBlock formal="집에 도착하자마자 연락하겠습니다." formalRom="jibe dochakhajamaja yeollakhagetseumnida." polite="집에 도착하자마자 연락할게요." politeRom="jibe dochakhajamaja yeollakhalgeyo." casual="집에 도착하자마자 연락할게." casualRom="jibe dochakhajamaja yeollakhalge." english="I’ll contact you as soon as I get home." />

### Finished, With a Feeling About It

Use the -아/어 form plus 버리다: 먹어 버리다, 잊어버리다, 끝내 버리다. It emphasises completion, often with regret, frustration, or relief. The feeling comes from the situation; 버리다 does not always make the result bad.

<SentenceBlock formal="제가 빵을 다 먹어 버렸습니다." formalRom="jega ppangeul da meogeo beoryeotseumnida." polite="제가 빵을 다 먹어 버렸어요." politeRom="jega ppangeul da meogeo beoryeosseoyo." casual="내가 빵을 다 먹어 버렸어." casualRom="naega ppangeul da meogeo beoryeosseo." english="I ate all the bread up." />

<SentenceBlock formal="밀린 일을 다 끝내 버렸습니다." formalRom="millin ireul da kkeunnae beoryeotseumnida." polite="밀린 일을 다 끝내 버렸어요." politeRom="millin ireul da kkeunnae beoryeosseoyo." casual="밀린 일을 다 끝내 버렸어." casualRom="millin ireul da kkeunnae beoryeosseo." english="I finished all the work that had piled up." />

<Hint type="hint">버리다 alone means throw away, but 먹어 버리다 means finish eating, rather than throw the food out. The auxiliary adds completion and attitude to the action before it.</Hint>
