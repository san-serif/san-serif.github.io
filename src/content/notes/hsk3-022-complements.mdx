“Did you find the keys?” “Yes—on the way in.” A complement can tell you whether an action reached its goal, where something moved, or whether the result was possible. 到 marks a result, 进来 brings movement toward the speaker, and 得/不 before a complement gives the action a green light or blocks it.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我找了半天，终于在抽屉里找到护照了。" traditional="我找了半天，終於在抽屜裡找到護照了。" pinyin="wǒ zhǎo le bàntiān, zhōngyú zài chōuti lǐ zhǎodào hùzhào le." jyutping="ngo5 zaau2 liu5 bun3 tin1, zung1 jyu1 zoi6 cau1 tai3 leoi5 zaau2 dou3 wu6 ziu3 liu5." english="I searched for ages and finally found the passport in the drawer." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="外面很冷，快把门关上吧。" traditional="外面很冷，快把門關上吧。" pinyin="wàimiàn hěn lěng, kuài bǎ mén guān shàng ba." jyutping="ngoi6 min6 han2 laang5, faai3 baa2 mun4 gwaan1 soeng5 baa6." english="It’s cold outside, so please close the door." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="医生刚进来，请你先坐下等一会儿。" traditional="醫生剛進來，請你先坐下等一會兒。" pinyin="yīshēng gāng jìnlái, qǐng nǐ xiān zuò xià děng yíhuìr." jyutping="ji1 sang1 gong1 zeon3 loi4, cing2 nei5 sin1 co5 haa6 dang2 jat1 wui6 ji4." english="The doctor just came in; please sit down and wait a moment." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这里太吵，我听不清你说什么。" traditional="這裡太吵，我聽不清你說什麼。" pinyin="zhèlǐ tài chǎo, wǒ tīng bu qīng nǐ shuō shénme." jyutping="ze5 leoi5 taai3 caau2, ngo5 teng1 bat1 cing1 nei5 syut3 sam6 mo1." english="It’s too noisy here; I can’t hear what you’re saying clearly." />

<Hint type="hint">到 shows that the action reaches its result: 找到. A compound direction such as 进来 shows movement relative to the speaker. In 听不清, 不 says that the result 清楚 cannot be reached.</Hint>
