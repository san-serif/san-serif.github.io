間に marks a single action happening at some point inside a longer stretch of time. 間 alone, without に, marks an action stretching across that entire span. Both usually follow ている, describing an ongoing background activity.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="拓也[たくや]さんが朝[あさ]ご飯[はん]を食[た]べている間[あいだ]に、私[わたし]はコーヒーを入[い]れました。" romaji="Takuya-san ga asagohan o tabete iru aida ni, watashi wa koohii o iremashita." english="While Takuya was eating breakfast, I made coffee." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="拓也[たくや]さんが学校[がっこう]に行[い]っている間[あいだ]、私[わたし]はアルバイトをしていました。" romaji="Takuya-san ga gakkou ni itte iru aida, watashi wa arubaito o shite imashita." english="While Takuya was at school, I was working my part-time job." />

<Hint>間に marks a one-time event slipped into the gap, "I made coffee" happens once, at some point during breakfast. 間 without に marks an activity running the whole time alongside another one, "I was working" the entire time he was at school, not just a moment of it.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="赤[あか]ちゃんが寝[ね]ている間[あいだ]に、ご飯[はん]を作[つく]ってしまおうと思[おも]います。" romaji="Akachan ga nete iru aida ni, gohan o tsukutte shimaou to omoimasu." english="I'm thinking of getting dinner made while the baby's asleep." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="由紀[ゆき]さんが留守[るす]の間[あいだ]に、友達[ともだち]が来[き]てしまったので、会[あ]えませんでした。" romaji="Yuki-san ga rusu no aida ni, tomodachi ga kite shimatta node, aemasendeshita." english="A friend came by while Yuki was out, so they couldn't meet." />

<Hint>間 and 間に can also follow a plain noun directly, no verb needed, 留守の間に, "while out," 日本にいる間, "while in Japan," treating the whole stay as the span being described.</Hint>
