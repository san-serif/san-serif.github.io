にもとづいて means “based on,” and introduces the evidence, rules, or information that supports a decision. It is close to に基づいて and をもとに.

These formal expressions all connect an action to a basis, but describe different relationships. を踏まえて says a decision takes prior facts or experience into account; に照らして checks something against a standard; に則って says an action follows a rule or established principle. They are common in reports, policy explanations, and formal discussion.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="調査結果[ちょうさけっか]を踏[ふ]まえて、計画[けいかく]を見直[みなお]します。" romaji="Chousa kekka o fumaete, keikaku o minaoshimasu." english="Taking the survey results into account, we'll review the plan." />

を踏まえて presents earlier information or experience as the basis for the next decision or action. The noun before を is something the speaker has considered, not necessarily a rule that dictates what must happen.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="規則[きそく]に照[て]らして、申請[しんせい]の内容[ないよう]を審査[しんさ]します。" romaji="Kisoku ni terashite, shinsei no naiyou o shinsa shimasu." english="We'll review the application in light of the rules." />

に照らして means “in light of” or “against” a standard, rule, or evidence. It emphasizes evaluation: the case is checked by comparing it with the stated basis.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="法律[ほうりつ]に則[のっと]って、手続[てつづ]きを進[すす]めます。" romaji="Houritsu ni nottotte, tetsuzuki o susumemasu." english="We'll proceed with the process in accordance with the law." />

に則って says that an action follows a law, rule, policy, or established principle. It is formal and often appears in written or institutional language. The noun before に is the standard the action conforms to.

をもとに means “based on” or “using X as source material.” It often introduces data, interviews, or an original work used to create something: 調査結果をもとに報告書を作る, “prepare a report based on the survey results.” It focuses on source material, while を踏まえて stresses taking facts into consideration.

<Hint type="insight">In real life, these forms appear in public policy, workplace procedures, and formal reporting. A proposal may be made を踏まえて survey results, reviewed に照らして a rule, and implemented に則って the law. Choosing among them makes the reasoning behind a decision precise.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Ask what role the basis plays: information you take into account = を踏まえて; a yardstick for evaluation = に照らして; a rule you comply with = に則って. In casual conversation, simpler 〜を考えて or 〜に合わせて is usually more natural.</Hint>
