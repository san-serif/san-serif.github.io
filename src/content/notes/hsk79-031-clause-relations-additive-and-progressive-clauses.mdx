The storm closed the road, then the bridge, 乃至 the whole valley. 乃至 marks a progression to a further or more extreme point; 且 adds a related fact, while 甚至于 highlights a detail beyond what the listener might expect.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这对中国乃至世界都有影响。" traditional="這對中國乃至世界都有影響。" pinyin="zhè duì zhōngguó nǎizhì shìjiè dōu yǒu yǐngxiǎng." jyutping="ze2 deoi3 zung1 gwok3 naai5 zi3 sai3 gaai3 dou1 jau5 jing2 hoeng2." english="This has an impact on China and even the world." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他忙得甚至于忘了吃饭。" traditional="他忙得甚至於忘了吃飯。" pinyin="tā máng de shènzhìyú wàng le chīfàn." jyutping="taa1 mong4 dak1 sam6 zi3 jyu1 mong4 liu5 hek3 faan6." english="He was so busy that he even forgot to eat." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="且慢，我们还没决定。" traditional="且慢，我們還沒決定。" pinyin="qiěmàn， wǒmen hái méi juédìng。" jyutping="ce2 maan6， ngo5 mun4 waan4 mut6 kyut3 ding6。" english="Hold on; we have not decided yet." />
