Your friend asks how you manage to cook and keep up with your favorite show. You can explain that you do one while doing the other with 一面……一面……. The two actions overlap in time.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她一面做饭，一面听新闻。" traditional="她一面做飯，一面聽新聞。" pinyin="tā yímiàn zuò fàn, yímiàn tīng xīnwén." jyutping="taa1 jat1 min6 zou6 faan6, jat1 min6 teng1 san1 man4." english="She cooked while listening to the news." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他一面热情地招呼客人，一面指挥服务员上菜。" traditional="他一面熱情地招呼客人，一面指揮服務員上菜。" pinyin="tā yímiàn rèqíng de zhāohu kèrén, yímiàn zhǐhuī fúwùyuán shàngcài." jyutping="taa1 jat1 min6 jit6 cing4 dei6 ziu1 fu1 haak3 jan4, jat1 min6 zi2 fai1 fuk6 mou6 jyun4 soeng5 coi3." english="He warmly greeted the guests while simultaneously directing the waiters to serve the dishes." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="孩子一面画画，一面跟奶奶讲学校里的事。" traditional="孩子一面畫畫，一面跟奶奶講學校裡的事。" pinyin="háizi yímiàn huà huà, yímiàn gēn nǎinai jiǎng xuéxiào lǐ de shì." jyutping="haai4 zi2 jat1 min6 waak6 waa2, jat1 min6 gan1 naai4 naai4 gong2 hok6 haau6 leoi5 dik1 si6." english="The child drew while telling Grandma what happened at school." />

<Hint type="hint">一面……一面…… keeps two actions in view at once. It differs from 先……再……, which puts one action before the next.</Hint>
