The concert ends and your friend asks how it was. 太好了！ says “That was great!”; 真漂亮！ turns the spotlight on how beautiful something is. Chinese often builds an exclamation from an ordinary sentence, then lets words like 太 or 真 carry the extra feeling.

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="太好了！" traditional="太好了！" pinyin="Tài hǎo le!" jyutping="taai3 hou2 laa3!" english="That’s great!" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这里真漂亮！" traditional="這裡真漂亮！" pinyin="Zhèlǐ zhēn piàoliang!" jyutping="ze5 leoi5 zan1 piu3 loeng6!" english="It’s really beautiful here!" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天太冷了！" traditional="今天太冷了！" pinyin="Jīntiān tài lěng le!" jyutping="gam1 tin1 taai3 laang5 laa3!" english="It’s so cold today!" />

<Hint type="hint">太 can mean “too” or an emphatic “so,” depending on context. The sentence and situation make the intended meaning clear.</Hint>
