At the classroom door, you can say who you are, ask where to sit, and invite someone in. The sentence shape changes with your goal: 吗 asks for yes or no, question words stay where the answer belongs, and 请 makes a request polite. The examples take you through each move.

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你是学生吗？" traditional="你是學生嗎？" pinyin="Nǐ shì xuésheng ma?" jyutping="nei5 si6 hok6 saang1 maa3?" english="Are you a student?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你住在哪里？" traditional="你住在哪裡？" pinyin="Nǐ zhù zài nǎlǐ?" jyutping="nei5 zyu6 zoi6 naa5 leoi5?" english="Where do you live?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="请坐。" traditional="請坐。" pinyin="Qǐng zuò." jyutping="cing2 co5." english="Please sit down." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你喝茶还是水？" traditional="你喝茶還是水？" pinyin="Nǐ hē chá háishi shuǐ?" jyutping="nei5 hot3 caa4 waan4 si6 seoi2?" english="Would you like tea or water?" />

<Hint type="hint">Do not add 吗 to a question that already uses a question word: 你去哪儿？ is complete.</Hint>
