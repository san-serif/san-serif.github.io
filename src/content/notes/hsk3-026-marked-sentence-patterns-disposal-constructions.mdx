“The keys? I left them on the table.” 把 brings the object forward because what happens to it matters. Follow it with the action and its result or destination: put the keys away, hand the parcel to someone, or get the door shut.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我把钥匙放在门口的小盒子里了。" traditional="我把鑰匙放在門口的小盒子裡了。" pinyin="wǒ bǎ yàoshi fàng zài ménkǒu de xiǎo hézi lǐ le." jyutping="ngo5 baa2 joek6 si4 fong3 zoi6 mun4 hau2 dik1 siu2 hop6 zi2 leoi5 liu5." english="I put the keys in the little box by the door." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="请把这份文件交给王经理。" traditional="請把這份文件交給王經理。" pinyin="qǐng bǎ zhè fèn wénjiàn jiāo gěi Wáng jīnglǐ." jyutping="cing2 baa2 ze5 fan6 man4 gin6 gaau1 kap1 wong4 ging1 lei5." english="Please give this document to Manager Wang." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她把桌上的杯子洗干净了。" traditional="她把桌上的杯子洗乾淨了。" pinyin="tā bǎ zhuōshàng de bēizi xǐ gānjìng le." jyutping="taa1 baa2 zoek3 soeng6 dik1 bui1 zi2 sai2 gon1 zing6 liu5." english="She washed the cups on the table clean." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="天快下雨了，先把衣服收进来吧。" traditional="天快下雨了，先把衣服收進來吧。" pinyin="tiān kuài xiàyǔ le, xiān bǎ yīfu shōu jìnlái ba." jyutping="tin1 faai3 haa6 jyu5 liu5, sin1 baa2 ji1 fuk6 sau1 zeon3 loi4 baa6." english="It’s about to rain; bring the clothes inside first." />

<Hint type="hint">A 把 sentence needs a clear effect on the object. Add a place (放在盒子里), recipient (交给王经理), result (洗干净), or direction (收进来) after the verb.</Hint>
