There's no single verb for "wear" in Korean. What you're putting on decides the verb: 입다 for clothes, 신다 for shoes and socks, 쓰다 for anything on your head or face, 매다 for anything tied on like a tie, 차다 for watches and accessories, 들다 for bags you're carrying, 끼다 for rings and gloves.

<SentenceBlock formal="티셔츠를 입었습니다." formalRom="tisyeocheureul ibeotseumnida." polite="티셔츠를 입었어요." politeRom="tisyeocheureul ibeosseoyo." casual="티셔츠를 입었어." casualRom="tisyeocheureul ibeosseo." english="I wore a t-shirt." />

<SentenceBlock formal="청바지를 입습니다." formalRom="cheongbajireul ipseumnida." polite="청바지를 입어요." politeRom="cheongbajireul ibeoyo." casual="청바지를 입어." casualRom="cheongbajireul ibeo." english="I wear jeans." />

<SentenceBlock formal="운동화를 신습니다." formalRom="undonghwareul sinseumnida." polite="운동화를 신어요." politeRom="undonghwareul sineoyo." casual="운동화를 신어." casualRom="undonghwareul sineo." english="I wear sneakers." />

<SentenceBlock formal="모자를 씁니다." formalRom="mojareul sseumnida." polite="모자를 써요." politeRom="mojareul sseoyo." casual="모자를 써." casualRom="mojareul sseo." english="I wear a hat." />

<SentenceBlock formal="넥타이를 맵니다." formalRom="nektaireul maemnida." polite="넥타이를 매요." politeRom="nektaireul maeyo." casual="넥타이를 매." casualRom="nektaireul mae." english="I wear a tie." />

<Hint>Mixing these up is one of the more noticeable beginner slips, because unlike a lot of grammar mistakes, this one draws a mental picture. Say 모자를 신어요 and you've technically just claimed to wear a hat on your feet.</Hint>

### Shopping For It

<SentenceBlock formal="운동화를 사고 싶습니다." formalRom="undonghwareul sago sipseumnida." polite="운동화를 사고 싶어요." politeRom="undonghwareul sago sipeoyo." casual="운동화를 사고 싶어." casualRom="undonghwareul sago sipeo." english="I want to buy sneakers." />

<SentenceBlock formal="구두가 조금 불편합니다." formalRom="guduga jogeum bulpyeonhamnida." polite="구두가 조금 불편해요." politeRom="guduga jogeum bulpyeonhaeyo." casual="구두가 조금 불편해." casualRom="guduga jogeum bulpyeonhae." english="The shoes are a bit uncomfortable." />

<SentenceBlock formal="이 바지가 큽니다." formalRom="i bajiga keumnida." polite="이 바지가 커요." politeRom="i bajiga keoyo." casual="이 바지가 커." casualRom="i bajiga keo." english="These pants are big." />

<Hint>Every 색 word names a hue: 빨간색 is literally "red hue." Pair one with a clothing noun and you've described an outfit, 빨간 티셔츠, "red t-shirt," no separate adjective grammar required yet, that gets its own lesson soon.</Hint>
