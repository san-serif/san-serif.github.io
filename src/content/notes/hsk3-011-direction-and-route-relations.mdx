The guide points toward the door: 朝门口走. 朝 sets the direction first, then the verb tells what moves or happens that way. In simple direction phrases, 向 can often point to the same destination.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他朝我看了一眼，就继续往前走了。" traditional="他朝我看了一眼，就繼續往前走了。" pinyin="tā cháo wǒ kàn le yì yǎn, jiù jìxù wǎng qián zǒu le." jyutping="taa1 ciu4 ngo5 hon3 liu5 jat1 ngaan5, zau6 gai3 zuk6 wong5 cin4 zau2 liu5." english="He glanced at me, then kept walking." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="请朝右边看，入口就在红色招牌旁边。" traditional="請朝右邊看，入口就在紅色招牌旁邊。" pinyin="qǐng cháo yòubiān kàn, rùkǒu jiù zài hóngsè zhāopái pángbiān." jyutping="cing2 ciu4 jau6 bin1 hon3, jap6 hau2 zau6 zoi6 hung4 sik1 ziu1 paai4 pong4 bin1." english="Please look to the right; the entrance is beside the red sign." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="小狗一听见主人叫它，就朝门口跑过去。" traditional="小狗一聽見主人叫牠，就朝門口跑過去。" pinyin="xiǎogǒu yì tīngjiàn zhǔrén jiào tā, jiù cháo ménkǒu pǎo guòqu." jyutping="siu2 gau2 jat1 teng1 gin3 zyu2 jan4 giu3 taa1, zau6 ciu4 mun4 hau2 paau2 gwo3 heoi3." english="As soon as the dog heard its owner call, it ran toward the door." />

<Hint type="hint">朝 focuses on the direction of the action. For a route someone follows along, use 沿着; for the direction they face or move toward, 朝 is a natural choice.</Hint>
