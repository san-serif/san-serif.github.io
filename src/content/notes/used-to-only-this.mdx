An old photo shows long hair; today you keep it short. -는데 can set the earlier picture beside the present one. 밖에 narrows a statement to the one thing or amount left.

### Put Two Pictures Side by Side

You have used [-는데 for background](/notes/setting-the-scene). It also supports a contrast: 예전에는 길었는데 지금은 짧아요. Use -는데 for present action verbs, -(으)ㄴ데 for adjectives, and -았/었는데 for the past. Nouns take -인데.

<SentenceBlock formal="동생은 조용한데 저는 말이 많습니다." formalRom="dongsaengeun joyonghande jeoneun mari manseumnida." polite="동생은 조용한데 저는 말이 많아요." politeRom="dongsaengeun joyonghande jeoneun mari manayo." casual="동생은 조용한데 나는 말이 많아." casualRom="dongsaengeun joyonghande naneun mari mana." english="My younger sibling is quiet, but I talk a lot." />

### Only This Much Remains

Put 밖에 after a noun or quantity and follow it with a negative expression: 하나밖에 없어요, 한국어밖에 못 해요. The whole sentence means only that much is available or possible. 만 can appear with an affirmative verb; 밖에 needs the negative partner.

<SentenceBlock formal="시간이 십 분밖에 없습니다." formalRom="sigani sip bunbakke eopseumnida." polite="시간이 십 분밖에 없어요." politeRom="sigani sip bunbakke eopseoyo." casual="시간이 십 분밖에 없어." casualRom="sigani sip bunbakke eopseo." english="I only have ten minutes." />

<SentenceBlock formal="저는 한국어밖에 못 합니다." formalRom="jeoneun hangugeobakke mot hamnida." polite="저는 한국어밖에 못 해요." politeRom="jeoneun hangugeobakke mot haeyo." casual="나는 한국어밖에 못 해." casualRom="naneun hangugeobakke mot hae." english="I can only speak Korean." />

<Hint type="hint">Inside a room and outside a room use location nouns: 방 안에, 방 밖에. The limiting particle in 십 분밖에 없어요 attaches directly to the quantity and belongs with a negative.</Hint>
