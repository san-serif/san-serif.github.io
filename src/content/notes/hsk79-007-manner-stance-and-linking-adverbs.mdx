The lights came back on just as the guests arrived. “怪不得，屋里这么亮”—no wonder the room is so bright. 怪不得 links a clue to its explanation; 好在 points out what saved a situation; 乃 can make a formal identification in written Chinese.

### Connect a Clue to Its Result

怪不得 means “no wonder.” Put the result first, then use it to show that the reason you have just learned explains what happened.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家商店的员工态度很好，怪不得顾客这么多。" traditional="這家商店的員工態度很好，怪不得顧客這麼多。" pinyin="zhè jiā shāngdiàn de yuángōng tàidu hěn hǎo, guàibude gùkè zhème duō." jyutping="ze2 gaa1 soeng1 dim3 dik1 jyun4 gung1 dik1 taai3 dou6 han2 hou2, gwaai3 bat1 dak1 gu3 haak3 ze2 mo1 do1." english="The staff at this shop are very friendly; no wonder it has so many customers." />

<Hint type="hint">怪不得 often appears with 原来 when the speaker has just discovered the explanation.</Hint>

### Point Out What Went Right

好在 means “fortunately” or “at least.” It introduces the detail that kept a situation from becoming worse.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="好在我带了伞，没被雨淋湿。" traditional="好在我帶了傘，沒被雨淋濕。" pinyin="hǎozài wǒ dài le sǎn, méi bèi yǔ lín shī." jyutping="hou2 zoi6 ngo5 daai3 liu5 saan3, mut6 bei6 jyu5 lam4 sap1." english="Luckily, I brought an umbrella and did not get wet." />

<Hint type="hint">好在 usually precedes the fortunate fact, not the result that was avoided.</Hint>

### Make a Formal Identification

乃 is a formal written equivalent of 是 in statements such as “failure is the mother of success.” It links two noun phrases and sounds literary.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="失败乃成功之母。" traditional="失敗乃成功之母。" pinyin="shībài nǎi chénggōng zhī mǔ." jyutping="sat1 baai6 naai5 sing4 gung1 zi1 mou5." english="Failure is the mother of success." />

<Hint type="hint">In everyday conversation, use 是. Reserve 乃 for formal writing, quotations, or an elevated style.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">怪不得 looks back from a result to its newly understood reason. 好在 marks a detail that helped, while 乃 belongs to formal statements and quotations.</Hint>
