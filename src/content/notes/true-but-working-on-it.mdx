Your study-abroad plans are moving, but the paperwork is taking time. You can acknowledge the part that is true, then explain the limit. -기는 하다 makes that concession; -는 중이다 tells us what is in progress.

### Agree Up to a Point

Attach -기는 하다 to a verb or adjective stem: 가기는 해요, 좋기는 해요. It often leads into -지만 or -는데: 좋기는 하지만…. For a completed action use 신청하기는 했어요. The acknowledgement is real even when a reservation follows.

### In the Middle of a Task

Verb stem + -는 중이다 marks an action or process in progress: 준비하는 중이에요, 고치는 중이에요. Action nouns can take 중이다 directly: 준비 중이에요, 회의 중이에요. Leave a space before 중.

<SentenceBlock formal="지금 서류를 준비하는 중입니다." formalRom="jigeum seoryureul junbihaneun jungimnida." polite="지금 서류를 준비하는 중이에요." politeRom="jigeum seoryureul junbihaneun jungieyo." casual="지금 서류를 준비하는 중이야." casualRom="jigeum seoryureul junbihaneun jungiya." english="I’m preparing the documents now." />

<SentenceBlock formal="어느 학교에 갈지 고민하는 중입니다." formalRom="eoneu hakgyoe galji gominhaneun jungimnida." polite="어느 학교에 갈지 고민하는 중이에요." politeRom="eoneu hakgyoe galji gominhaneun jungieyo." casual="어느 학교에 갈지 고민하는 중이야." casualRom="eoneu hakgyoe galji gominhaneun jungiya." english="I’m thinking over which school to go to." />

<Hint type="hint">[-고 있어요](/notes/right-now) covers many ongoing actions. -는 중이에요 highlights being in the middle of a task or process, which makes it useful when explaining why you are occupied.</Hint>
