“The guests arrived.” There’s no one acting on the guests; they are the ones who arrived. In Chinese, an object can name the doer or the way an action happens, as well as the thing acted on. With several details in one sentence, time usually comes before place and manner.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="门口来了一位客人，大家快去看看。" traditional="門口來了一位客人，大家快去看看。" pinyin="ménkǒu lái le yí wèi kèrén, dàjiā kuài qù kànkan." jyutping="mun4 hau2 loi4 liu5 jat1 wai2 haak3 jan4, daai6 gaa1 faai3 heoi3 hon3 hon3." english="A guest has arrived at the door; everyone, go take a look." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我昨天在书店买了一本关于旅行的书。" traditional="我昨天在書店買了一本關於旅行的書。" pinyin="wǒ zuótiān zài shūdiàn mǎi le yì běn guānyú lǚxíng de shū." jyutping="ngo5 zok6 tin1 zoi6 syu1 dim3 maai5 liu5 jat1 bun2 gwaan1 jyu1 leoi5 hang4 dik1 syu1." english="I bought a book about travel at the bookstore yesterday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="妹妹今天在图书馆认真地准备考试。" traditional="妹妹今天在圖書館認真地準備考試。" pinyin="mèimei jīntiān zài túshūguǎn rènzhēn de zhǔnbèi kǎoshì." jyutping="mui6 mui6 gam1 tin1 zoi6 tou4 syu1 gun2 jing6 zan1 dei6 zeon2 bei6 haau2 si3." english="My younger sister is studying carefully at the library today." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们上周特意去市场买了新鲜的鱼。" traditional="我們上週特意去市場買了新鮮的魚。" pinyin="wǒmen shàng zhōu tèyì qù shìchǎng mǎi le xīnxiān de yú." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 soeng5 zau1 dak6 ji3 heoi3 si5 coeng4 maai5 liu5 san1 sin1 dik1 jyu4." english="We made a special trip to the market last week to buy fresh fish." />

<Hint type="hint">In 来了一位客人, 客人 is the object in the sentence but also the person who arrives. In 买了一本书, the book is the thing affected by the action. In the last examples, time, place, and manner details build up before the main action.</Hint>
