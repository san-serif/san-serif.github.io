Someone asks which app you use and why. You briefly repeat the question to frame your answer, then explain what the app makes easy. -냐면 opens the answer; -기가 쉽다 evaluates doing the task.

### Pick Up the Question

-냐면 shortens a question plus -고 하면. It can echo an actual question or introduce one you anticipate: 왜 쓰냐면…, 어디냐면…. Keep the question word that gives the answer its focus. Nouns use -(이)냐면 and past questions use -았/었냐면.

<SentenceBlock polite="이 앱을 왜 쓰냐면 길을 쉽게 찾을 수 있어서예요." politeRom="i aebeul wae sseunyamyeon gireul swipge chajeul su isseoseoyeyo." casual="이 앱을 왜 쓰냐면 길을 쉽게 찾을 수 있어서야." casualRom="i aebeul wae sseunyamyeon gireul swipge chajeul su isseoseoya." english="If you’re asking why I use this app, it’s because I can find my way easily." />

<SentenceBlock polite="자주 가는 카페가 어디냐면 역 바로 앞이에요." politeRom="jaju ganeun kapega eodinyamyeon yeok baro apieyo." casual="자주 가는 카페가 어디냐면 역 바로 앞이야." casualRom="jaju ganeun kapega eodinyamyeon yeok baro apiya." english="As for where the café I often visit is, it’s right in front of the station." />

### Evaluate Doing the Task

Attach -기가 to an action verb, then 쉽다, 어렵다, 힘들다, or 편하다: 사용하기가 쉽다, 혼자 하기가 힘들다. 가 can be omitted in many ordinary sentences. The adjective evaluates the action as a whole.

<SentenceBlock formal="이 지도는 사용하기가 쉽습니다." formalRom="i jidoneun sayonghagiga swipseumnida." polite="이 지도는 사용하기가 쉬워요." politeRom="i jidoneun sayonghagiga swiwoyo." casual="이 지도는 사용하기가 쉬워." casualRom="i jidoneun sayonghagiga swiwo." english="This map is easy to use." />

<SentenceBlock formal="작은 화면에서는 글을 읽기가 불편합니다." formalRom="jageun hwamyeoneseoneun geureul ilgiga bulpyeonhamnida." polite="작은 화면에서는 글을 읽기가 불편해요." politeRom="jageun hwamyeoneseoneun geureul ilgiga bulpyeonhaeyo." casual="작은 화면에서는 글을 읽기가 불편해." casualRom="jageun hwamyeoneseoneun geureul ilgiga bulpyeonhae." english="Reading text on a small screen is inconvenient." />

<Hint type="hint">Distinguish answer-framing -냐면 from reported-question -냬요: 어디냐면… introduces where it is; 어디냬요 passes on someone’s question about where it is.</Hint>
