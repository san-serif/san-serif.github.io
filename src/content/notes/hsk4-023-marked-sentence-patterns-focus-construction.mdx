Your team is debating whether to cancel an outdoor event because of the storm. You agree with the decision and want to make that judgment clear: 取消活动是对的. Putting 是……的 around the evaluation gives it a settled, emphatic feel.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="取消活动是对的。" traditional="取消活動是對的。" pinyin="qǔxiāo huódòng shì duì de." jyutping="ceoi2 siu1 wut6 dung6 si6 deoi3 dik1." english="Canceling the event was the right thing to do." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你先问清楚是有必要的。" traditional="你先問清楚是有必要的。" pinyin="nǐ xiān wèn qīngchu shì yǒu bìyào de." jyutping="nei5 sin1 man6 cing1 co2 si6 jau5 bit1 jiu3 dik1." english="It was necessary for you to ask and make sure first." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他一个人完成这么多工作是不容易的。" traditional="他一個人完成這麼多工作是不容易的。" pinyin="tā yí ge rén wánchéng zhème duō gōngzuò shì bù róngyì de." jyutping="taa1 jat1 go3 jan4 jyun4 sing4 gam3 mo1 do1 gung1 zok3 si6 bat1 jung4 ji6 dik1." english="It isn’t easy for him to finish so much work alone." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这次失败是可以理解的，大家别怪她。" traditional="這次失敗是可以理解的，大家別怪她。" pinyin="zhè cì shībài shì kěyǐ lǐjiě de, dàjiā bié guài tā." jyutping="ze5 ci3 sat1 baai6 si6 ho2 ji5 lei5 gaai2 dik1, daai6 gaa1 bit6 gwaai3 taa1." english="This failure is understandable; don’t blame her." />

<Hint type="hint">Here the words inside 是……的 express a judgment: 对, 有必要, 不容易, 可以理解. This use is different from highlighting when or how a completed event took place.</Hint>
