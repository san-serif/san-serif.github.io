Two jackets, one mirror: this one feels warmer than yesterday’s, but the other costs more. Chinese puts the thing you’re describing first, then 比 and the point of comparison: A 比 B + adjective. To say A falls short of B on that quality, use 没有 instead: A 没有 B + adjective.

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天比昨天热。" traditional="今天比昨天熱。" pinyin="Jīntiān bǐ zuótiān rè." jyutping="gam1 tin1 bei2 zok3 tin1 jit6." english="Today is hotter than yesterday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这本书比那本书贵。" traditional="這本書比那本書貴。" pinyin="Zhè běn shū bǐ nà běn shū guì." jyutping="ze5 bun2 syu1 bei2 naa5 bun2 syu1 gwai3." english="This book is more expensive than that one." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我没有姐姐高。" traditional="我沒有姐姐高。" pinyin="Wǒ méiyǒu jiějie gāo." jyutping="ngo5 mut6 jau5 ze2 ze2 gou1." english="I’m not as tall as my older sister." />

<Hint type="hint">Do not add 很 before the adjective in a basic 比 sentence. 没有 in a comparison means “not as…as,” not simply “do not have.”</Hint>
