せっかく draws attention to the effort it took to get something, or to a valuable chance that has come up. It often leads to a suggestion to make use of that effort or opportunity, or expresses regret when it is wasted.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="せっかく京都[きょうと]まで来[き]たので、お寺[てら]を見[み]ていきましょう。" romaji="Sekkaku Kyouto made kita node, otera o mite ikimashou." english="Since we came all the way to Kyoto, let's go see a temple." />

せっかく can describe a special chance or the trouble already taken. せっかくなら often means “since we're doing this / if we have the chance,” and introduces a suggestion to make the most of it.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="せっかく準備[じゅんび]したのに、雨[あめ]で試合[しあい]が中止[ちゅうし]になりました。" romaji="Sekkaku junbi shita noni, ame de shiai ga chuushi ni narimashita." english="After all that preparation, the game was canceled because of rain." />

With のに, せっかく points to effort or a good opportunity that did not lead to the hoped-for result. The speaker often feels disappointed that it went to waste.

<Hint type="insight">Use せっかくなら when making a practical suggestion during a trip or outing: せっかくなら地元の料理を食べよう, “Since we're here, let's try the local food.” Use せっかく〜のに to show sympathy when someone's effort was wasted.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Think “this took effort” or “this chance is special.” The next clause usually recommends using the opportunity or laments that it wasn't used.</Hint>
