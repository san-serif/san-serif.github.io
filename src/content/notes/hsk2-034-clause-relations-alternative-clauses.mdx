You’re making plans with a friend and need them to choose. Put 还是 between the options in a question. It can link two things (“tea or coffee?”) or two actions (“stay or go?”).

<CSentenceBlock simplified="晚饭我们在家吃还是出去吃？" traditional="晚飯我們在家吃還是出去吃？" pinyin="wǎnfàn wǒmen zài jiā chī háishi chūqù chī?" jyutping="maan5 faan6 ngo5 mun4 zoi6 gaa1 hek3 waan4 si6 ceot1 heoi3 hek3?" english="For dinner, shall we eat at home or go out?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你想喝茶还是咖啡？" traditional="你想喝茶還是咖啡？" pinyin="nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi kāfēi?" jyutping="nei5 soeng2 hot3 caa4 waan4 si6 gaa3 fe1?" english="Would you like tea or coffee?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="周末你想看电影还是在家休息？" traditional="週末你想看電影還是在家休息？" pinyin="zhōumò nǐ xiǎng kàn diànyǐng háishi zài jiā xiūxi?" jyutping="zau1 mut6 nei5 soeng2 hon3 din6 jing2 waan4 si6 zoi6 gaa1 jau1 sik1?" english="Would you rather see a film or relax at home this weekend?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们坐地铁还是打车？" traditional="我們坐地鐵還是搭車？" pinyin="wǒmen zuò dìtiě háishi dǎchē?" jyutping="ngo5 mun4 co5 dei6 tit3 waan4 si6 daap3 ce1?" english="Shall we take the subway or a taxi?" />

<Hint type="hint">还是 offers alternatives in a question. Use 或者 when you’re listing possibilities in a statement: 我们可以坐地铁或者打车 (“We can take the subway or a taxi”).</Hint>
