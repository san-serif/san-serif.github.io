X是X states that something is true, then often adds a qualification. The repeated word acknowledges the fact before the second clause gives the speaker’s fuller view.

### Hear the Pattern in Context

<CSentenceBlock simplified="累是累，不过做完了很有成就感。" traditional="累是累，不過做完了很有成就感。" pinyin="Lèi shì lèi, búguò zuòwán le hěn yǒu chéngjiùgǎn." jyutping="leoi6 si6 leoi6, bat1 gwo3 zou6 jyun4 liu5 han2 jau5 sing4 zau6 gam2." english="I am tired, sure, but finishing it feels rewarding." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="远是远，不过风景很好。" traditional="遠是遠，不過風景很好。" pinyin="Yuǎn shì yuǎn, búguò fēngjǐng hěn hǎo." jyutping="jyun5 si6 jyun5, bat1 gwo3 fung1 ging2 han2 hou2." english="It is far, sure, but the scenery is lovely." />

<Hint type="hint">The repeated adjective acknowledges a real drawback; the next clause often adds a contrast or qualification. Keep the same word on both sides of 是.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">This pattern lets you agree with part of what someone said before adding your own point: 累是累，可是值得 (It is tiring, but it is worth it).</Hint>
