Your friends keep checking the weather before deciding whether to go camping. You tell them the plan won’t change: “Whatever the weather, we’re going.” 别管……都…… and 任……也…… introduce a condition that does not affect the result.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="别管天气怎么样，我们都按计划出发。" traditional="別管天氣怎麼樣，我們都按計劃出發。" pinyin="biéguǎn tiānqì zěnmeyàng, wǒmen dōu àn jìhuà chūfā." jyutping="bit6 gun2 tin1 hei3 zam2 mo1 joeng6, ngo5 mun4 dou1 on3 gai3 waak6 ceot1 faat3." english="Whatever the weather is like, we’ll set off as planned." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="别管他同不同意，我都要把事实告诉他。" traditional="別管他同不同意，我都要把事實告訴他。" pinyin="biéguǎn tā tóng bù tóngyì, wǒ dōu yào bǎ shìshí gàosu tā." jyutping="bit6 gun2 taa1 tung4 bat1 tung4 ji3, ngo5 dou1 jiu3 baa2 si6 sat6 gou3 sou3 taa1." english="Whether he agrees or not, I’m going to tell him the facts." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="任你怎么解释，我也不会改变主意。" traditional="任你怎麼解釋，我也不會改變主意。" pinyin="rèn nǐ zěnme jiěshì, wǒ yě bú huì gǎibiàn zhǔyi." jyutping="jam6 nei5 zam2 mo1 gaai2 sik1, ngo5 jaa5 bat1 wui5 goi2 bin3 zyu2 ji3." english="No matter how you explain it, I won’t change my mind." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="任谁来劝，他也不肯道歉。" traditional="任誰來勸，他也不肯道歉。" pinyin="rèn shéi lái quàn, tā yě bù kěn dàoqiàn." jyutping="jam6 seoi4 loi4 hyun3, taa1 jaa5 bat1 hang2 dou6 hip3." english="No matter who tries to persuade him, he won’t apologize." />

<Hint type="hint">别管 is followed by the condition or question that does not matter; 都 introduces the unchanged result. 任 can introduce a person or an open question such as 怎么, followed by 也 in the main clause.</Hint>
