走得越多，我对这座城市就越发熟悉了 (“the more I walk, the more familiar the city feels”). 越发 shows a quality or feeling growing over time. 凡 introduces a general set, and 皆 gathers every member of that set into the statement.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="随着年龄的增长，他越发怀念故乡了。" traditional="隨著年齡的增長，他越發懷念故鄉了。" pinyin="suízhe niánlíng de zēngzhǎng, tā yuèfā huáiniàn gùxiāng le." jyutping="ceoi4 zoek6 nin4 ling4 dik1 zang1 zoeng2, taa1 jyut6 faat3 waai4 nim6 gu3 hoeng1 liu5." english="As he grew older, he missed his hometown more and more." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="凡是参加的人都能领一份礼物。" traditional="凡是參加的人都能領一份禮物。" pinyin="fánshì cānjiā de rén dōu néng lǐng yí fèn lǐwù." jyutping="faan4 si6 caam1 gaa1 dik1 jan4 dou1 nang4 ling5 jat1 fan6 lai5 mat6." english="Everyone who takes part can receive a gift." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们知道人皆难免一死。" traditional="我們知道人皆難免一死。" pinyin="wǒmen zhīdào rén jiē nánmiǎn yī sǐ。" jyutping="ngo5 mun4 zi1 dou3 jan4 gaai1 naan4 min5 jat1 sei2。" english="We know that all men are mortal." />
