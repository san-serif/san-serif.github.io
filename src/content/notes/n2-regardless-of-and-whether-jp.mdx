The event is open to every age group, and it will run rain or shine. These expressions keep the main statement true across a condition, category, or either of two alternatives.

These forms say that a condition or category does not change the main statement. にかかわらず means “regardless of,” often with a noun or paired alternatives. を問わず means “irrespective of” and is common in formal notices listing categories. にせよ / にしろ and としても mean “even if” or “even assuming,” often followed by the same conclusion. しろ～しろ and にしろ～にしろ pair alternatives explicitly. に限らず means “not limited to,” extending a statement beyond the named example.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="経験[けいけん]の有無[うむ]を問[と]わず、誰[だれ]でも参加[さんか]できます。" romaji="Keiken no umu o towazu, dare demo sanka dekimasu." english="Anyone can participate, regardless of experience." />

を問わず often follows paired categories or nouns such as 年齢, 経験, or 国籍. It has a formal, concise tone and is common in recruitment notices and public information.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="天候[てんこう]にかかわらず、試合[しあい]は予定[よてい]どおり行[おこな]われます。" romaji="Tenkou ni kakawarazu, shiai wa yotei doori okonawaremasu." english="Regardless of the weather, the game will go ahead as scheduled." />

にかかわらず can follow a noun or a question phrase such as するかどうか. にせよ / にしろ treats alternatives alike: 行くにせよ行かないにせよ, “whether you go or not.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="この制度[せいど]は大企業[だいきぎょう]に限[かぎ]らず、小[ちい]さな会社[かいしゃ]にも広[ひろ]がっています。" romaji="Kono seido wa daikigyou ni kagirazu, chiisana kaisha ni mo hirogatte imasu." english="This system is spreading to small companies too, not just large ones." />

に限らず explicitly broadens the scope beyond the first example. It is related to “not only,” but its focus is that the range is not restricted to the named category.

<Hint type="insight">A job posting may say 年齢を問わず応募できます, “applications are welcome regardless of age.” にかかわらず is a flexible statement about a condition, while に限らず expands a category to include more cases.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Category-independent in a formal notice: を問わず. Condition does not affect outcome: にかかわらず. Either alternative: にせよ / にしろ. Broaden beyond one category: に限らず.</Hint>
