There are 十来个人 waiting outside—roughly a dozen, give or take. 来 after a number makes an estimate. Decimals, fractions, percentages, and multiples each have a spoken order of their own, so say the whole quantity as a phrase instead of reading symbols one at a time.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他得到了比我们还高的分数。" traditional="他得到了比我們還高的分數。" pinyin="tā dédào le bǐ wǒmen hái gāo de fēnshù。" jyutping="taa1 dak1 dou3 zo2 bei2 ngo5 mun4 waan4 gou1 dik1 fan1 sou3。" english="He got a higher score than we did." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他们没来这里，是吗？" traditional="他們沒來這裡，是嗎？" pinyin="tāmen méi lái zhèlǐ， shì ma？" jyutping="taa1 mun4 mut6 loi4 ze5 leoi5， si6 maa3？" english="They didn't come here, did they?" />
