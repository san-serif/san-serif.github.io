You are browsing flats without committing to a move yet. “I’m thinking of moving” keeps the plan open. “As long as it isn’t too far away” names the one condition you want to avoid.

### A Plan You Are Considering

Use -ㄹ까 하다 after a vowel or ㄹ, -을까 하다 after another consonant: 이사할까 해요, 먹을까 해요. It expresses a tentative intention. Compare [-(으)려고 해요](/notes/planning-to), which presents a more formed intention; context still matters more than a fixed scale of certainty.

<SentenceBlock formal="다음 달에 이사할까 합니다." formalRom="daeum dare isahalkka hamnida." polite="다음 달에 이사할까 해요." politeRom="daeum dare isahalkka haeyo." casual="다음 달에 이사할까 해." casualRom="daeum dare isahalkka hae." english="I’m thinking of moving next month." />

<SentenceBlock formal="주말에는 집에서 쉴까 합니다." formalRom="jumareneun jibeseo swilkka hamnida." polite="주말에는 집에서 쉴까 해요." politeRom="jumareneun jibeseo swilkka haeyo." casual="주말에는 집에서 쉴까 해." casualRom="jumareneun jibeseo swilkka hae." english="I’m thinking of resting at home this weekend." />

### Exclude One Unwanted Condition

Stem + -지만 않으면 means as long as it does not happen or is not that way: 멀지만 않으면, 비가 오지만 않으면. It combines -지 않다 with 만 and -(으)면. For nouns use noun + 만 아니면.

<SentenceBlock formal="학교에서 너무 멀지만 않으면 괜찮습니다." formalRom="hakgyoeseo neomu meoljiman aneumyeon gwaenchanseumnida." polite="학교에서 너무 멀지만 않으면 괜찮아요." politeRom="hakgyoeseo neomu meoljiman aneumyeon gwaenchanayo." casual="학교에서 너무 멀지만 않으면 괜찮아." casualRom="hakgyoeseo neomu meoljiman aneumyeon gwaenchana." english="It’s fine as long as it isn’t too far from school." />

<SentenceBlock formal="지하만 아니면 됩니다." formalRom="jihaman animyeon doemnida." polite="지하만 아니면 돼요." politeRom="jihaman animyeon dwaeyo." casual="지하만 아니면 돼." casualRom="jihaman animyeon dwae." english="It’s fine as long as it isn’t a basement." />

<Hint type="hint">The negative is essential. 멀지만 않으면 sets aside the unwanted condition “far away.” It does not mean “only if it is far away.”</Hint>
