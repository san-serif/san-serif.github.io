The train arrives on time, the sun is about to set, and the sky slowly turns pink. 按时 checks an event against its expected time; 即将 looks just ahead; 渐渐 lets a change unfold. 一再 and 再三 look back at something that has happened repeatedly.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="不管你选哪条路，你都能按时到那里。" traditional="不管你選哪條路，你都能按時到那裡。" pinyin="bùguǎn nǐ xuǎn nǎ tiáo lù， nǐ dōu néng ànshí dào nàli。" jyutping="bat1 gun2 nei5 syun2 naa5 tiu4 lou6， nei5 dou1 nang4 on3 si4 dou3 naa5 leoi5。" english="Whichever route you take, you will get there in time." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="音乐会即将开始。" traditional="音樂會即將開始。" pinyin="yīnyuèhuì jíjiāng kāishǐ。" jyutping="jam1 ngok6 wui2 zik1 zoeng1 hoi1 ci2。" english="The concert is about to start." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她急忙带着好消息跑回家。" traditional="她急忙帶著好消息跑回家。" pinyin="tā jímáng dài zhe hǎo xiāoxi pǎo huíjiā。" jyutping="taa1 gap1 mong4 daai3 zoek3 hou2 siu1 sik1 paau2 wui4 gaa1。" english="She rushed home with the good news." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="天渐渐黑了。我们回家吧。" traditional="天漸漸黑了。我們回家吧。" pinyin="tiān jiànjiàn hēi le。 wǒmen huíjiā ba。" jyutping="tin1 zim6 zim6 hak1 liu5。 ngo5 mun4 wui4 gaa1 baa6。" english="It’s gradually getting dark. Let’s go home." />
