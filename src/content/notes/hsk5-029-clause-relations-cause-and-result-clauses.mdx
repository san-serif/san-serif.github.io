The road was blocked; 因而 the delivery arrived late. This linker brings in a consequence and sounds at home in formal explanations or writing. In everyday conversation, 所以 is usually the more natural choice.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他很忙，因而没来。" traditional="他很忙，因而沒來。" pinyin="tā hěn máng, yīn'ér méi lái." jyutping="taa1 han2 mong4, jan1 ji4 mut6 loi4." english="He was busy, therefore didn't come." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这是他的记忆。" traditional="這是他的記憶。" pinyin="zhè shì tā de jìyì." jyutping="ze2 si6 taa1 dik1 gei3 jik1." english="This is his memory." />
