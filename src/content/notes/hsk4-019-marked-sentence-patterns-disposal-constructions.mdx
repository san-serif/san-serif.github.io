Your colleague hands you a long report to review. You read it through, tidy your notes, and pass the finished copy to the manager. 把 puts the object before the verb so the sentence can focus on what happened to it, how far the action went, or who received it.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我把那封信读了一遍，才看懂意思。" traditional="我把那封信讀了一遍，才看懂意思。" pinyin="wǒ bǎ nà fēng xìn dú le yí biàn, cái kàndǒng yìsi." jyutping="ngo5 baa2 naa5 fung1 seon3 duk6 liu5 jat1 pin3, coi4 hon3 dung2 ji3 si1." english="I read the letter through once before I understood it." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她把房间收拾干净了，准备请朋友来吃饭。" traditional="她把房間收拾乾淨了，準備請朋友來吃飯。" pinyin="tā bǎ fángjiān shōushi gānjìng le, zhǔnbèi qǐng péngyou lái chīfàn." jyutping="taa1 baa2 fong4 gaan1 sau1 sik1 gon1 zing6 liu5, zeon2 bei6 cing2 pang4 jau5 loi4 hek3 faan6." english="She cleaned up the room and got ready to invite friends over for a meal." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="请把这份文件交给经理，别放在桌上。" traditional="請把這份文件交給經理，別放在桌上。" pinyin="qǐng bǎ zhè fèn wénjiàn jiāo gěi jīnglǐ, bié fàng zài zhuōshàng." jyutping="cing2 baa2 ze5 fan6 man4 gin6 gaau1 kap1 ging1 lei5, bit6 fong3 zoi6 zoek3 soeng6." english="Please hand this document to the manager; don’t leave it on the table." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他把门关着，外面的声音小多了。" traditional="他把門關著，外面的聲音小多了。" pinyin="tā bǎ mén guānzhe, wàimiàn de shēngyīn xiǎo duō le." jyutping="taa1 baa2 mun4 gwaan1 zoek6, ngoi6 min6 dik1 sing1 jam1 siu2 do1 liu5." english="He kept the door closed, and the noise from outside got much quieter." />

<Hint type="hint">After 把, the verb phrase needs to show what happened to the object. 一遍 marks how far the reading went, 干净 gives the result, 交给 names the recipient, and 关着 describes the resulting state.</Hint>
