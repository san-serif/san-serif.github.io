ような気がする softens an impression: “I have a feeling that...” まるで～ようだ makes a vivid comparison, “just as if...” そうもない says something seems unlikely to happen. にしては judges something against an expectation (“considering it is X…”). もしかしたら introduces a possibility with uncertainty. These expressions let the speaker show how confident or tentative the judgment is.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="この道[みち]を行[い]けば、間[ま]に合[あ]いそうな気[き]がします。" romaji="Kono michi o ikeba, maniaisou na ki ga shimasu." english="I have a feeling we'll make it if we take this road." />

ような気がする presents a subjective impression rather than a firm conclusion. It often follows a plain clause and helps soften predictions or opinions.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="彼[かれ]はまるで何[なに]も聞[き]かなかったように、話[はな]し続[つづ]けました。" romaji="Kare wa marude nani mo kikanakatta you ni, hanashi tsuzukemashita." english="He kept talking as if he hadn't heard anything." />

まるで～ようだ draws a strong comparison, often one that is not literally true. そうもない and そうにない attach to a verb stem and express a negative prediction: 間に合いそうもない, “it doesn't look like we'll make it.” そうもない is more emphatic, often suggesting little chance; そうにない is a more straightforward prediction.

ふうに / というふうに mean “in such a way” or “like this,” and describe a manner or example: 先生が説明したというふうに, “in the way the teacher explained.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="この料理[りょうり]は初[はじ]めて作[つく]ったにしては、上手[じょうず]です。" romaji="Kono ryouri wa hajimete tsukutta ni shite wa, jouzu desu." english="Considering this is the first time you made it, it's good." />

にしては notes a surprising difference from what the stated category or situation would lead one to expect. もしかしたら marks a possibility the speaker is not sure about.

<Hint type="insight">ような気がする is a gentle way to share a hunch. にしては can be praise, criticism, or neutral surprise depending on the evaluation that follows. そうもない is a fairly pessimistic assessment, stronger than simply saying “maybe not.”</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Subjective hunch: ような気がする. As if: まるで～ようだ. Unlikely: そうもない. Surprising for its category: にしては. Perhaps: もしかしたら.</Hint>
