Your friend is trying to finish a report while you keep asking questions. They tell you to carry on with your own work. These conversational frames can also challenge an exaggerated claim or remind someone, with some frustration, that they were told what to do.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你忙你的吧，我自己能把桌子搬过去。" traditional="你忙你的吧，我自己能把桌子搬過去。" pinyin="nǐ máng nǐ de ba, wǒ zìjǐ néng bǎ zhuōzi bān guòqù." jyutping="nei5 mong4 nei5 dik1 baa6, ngo5 zi6 gei2 nang4 baa2 zoek3 zi2 baan1 gwo3 heoi3." english="You carry on with your work; I can move the table myself." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="还专家呢，这么简单的问题都答不上来。" traditional="還專家呢，這麼簡單的問題都答不上來。" pinyin="hái zhuānjiā ne, zhème jiǎndān de wèntí dōu dá bu shànglái." jyutping="waan4 zyun1 gaa1 ne1, ze5 mo1 gaan2 daan1 dik1 man6 tai4 dou1 daap3 bat1 soeng5 loi4." english="Some expert he is—he can’t even answer such a simple question." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="叫你早点儿出门你就不听，现在赶不上车了吧？" traditional="叫你早點兒出門你就不聽，現在趕不上車了吧？" pinyin="jiào nǐ zǎodiǎnr chūmén nǐ jiù bù tīng, xiànzài gǎn bu shàng chē le ba?" jyutping="giu3 nei5 zou2 dim2 ji4 ceot1 mun4 nei5 zau6 bat1 teng1, jin6 zoi6 gon2 bat1 soeng5 ce1 liu5 baa6?" english="I told you to leave early, but you wouldn’t listen. Now you’re going to miss the bus, aren’t you?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="让你带伞你就不带，淋湿了吧？" traditional="讓你帶傘你就不帶，淋濕了吧？" pinyin="ràng nǐ dài sǎn nǐ jiù bú dài, línshī le ba?" jyutping="joeng6 nei5 daai3 saan3 nei5 zau6 bat1 daai3, lam4 sap1 liu5 baa6?" english="I told you to bring an umbrella, but you didn’t. You got soaked, didn’t you?" />

<Hint type="hint">你做你的吧 tells someone to continue with their own task. 还X呢 often sounds sarcastic when the speaker doubts the claim; 叫/让你X你就…… recalls advice or an instruction the listener ignored.</Hint>
