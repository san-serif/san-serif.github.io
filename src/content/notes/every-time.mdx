마다 attaches to a time word to mean "every": 아침마다, "every morning." No conjugation rule to check, it attaches to the noun exactly as written.

<SentenceBlock formal="아침마다 주스를 마십니다." formalRom="achimmada juseureul masimnida." polite="아침마다 주스를 마셔요." politeRom="achimmada juseureul masyeoyo." casual="아침마다 주스를 마셔." casualRom="achimmada juseureul masyeo." english="I drink juice every morning." />

<SentenceBlock formal="목요일마다 운동을 합니다." formalRom="mogyoilmada undongeul hamnida." polite="목요일마다 운동을 해요." politeRom="mogyoilmada undongeul haeyo." casual="목요일마다 운동을 해." casualRom="mogyoilmada undongeul hae." english="I exercise every Thursday." />

<Hint>[에 한 번](/notes/once-a-week) counts occurrences: 일주일에 두 번 ("twice a week") could land on any two days. 마다 means every single instance, no exceptions. 목요일마다 means every Thursday without fail, not an average of roughly once a week.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="토요일마다 등산을 합니다." formalRom="toyoilmada deungsaneul hamnida." polite="토요일마다 등산을 해요." politeRom="toyoilmada deungsaneul haeyo." casual="토요일마다 등산을 해." casualRom="toyoilmada deungsaneul hae." english="I go hiking every Saturday." />

<SentenceBlock formal="월요일하고 수요일마다 수업을 듣습니다." formalRom="woryoilhago suyoilmada sueobeul deutseumnida." polite="월요일하고 수요일마다 수업을 들어요." politeRom="woryoilhago suyoilmada sueobeul deureoyo." casual="월요일하고 수요일마다 수업을 들어." casualRom="woryoilhago suyoilmada sueobeul deureo." english="I attend classes every Monday and Wednesday." />

<SentenceBlock formal="주말마다 고향에 갑니다." formalRom="jumalmada gohyange gamnida." polite="주말마다 고향에 가요." politeRom="jumalmada gohyange gayo." casual="주말마다 고향에 가." casualRom="jumalmada gohyange ga." english="I go to my hometown every weekend." />

<SentenceBlock formal="방학마다 여행을 갑니다." formalRom="banghakmada yeohaengeul gamnida." polite="방학마다 여행을 가요." politeRom="banghakmada yeohaengeul gayo." casual="방학마다 여행을 가." casualRom="banghakmada yeohaengeul ga." english="I travel every school vacation." />

<Hint>방학 specifically means a school or university break, a student's vacation. 휴가 (from [-려고 해요](/notes/planning-to)) is vacation from work. Mixing them up marks you as talking about the wrong kind of time off entirely.</Hint>
