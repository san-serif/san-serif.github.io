You missed the bus, but 幸亏 a friend had room in the car. 幸好 and 幸亏 point to the circumstance that saved the day; 终究 steps back and says what proved true after everything else played out.

### Say What Saved the Situation

幸好 means “luckily.” It introduces a helpful event or circumstance that prevented a problem or made things work out.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="幸好大家都赶上了最后一班车。" traditional="幸好大家都趕上了最後一班車。" pinyin="xìnghǎo dàjiā dōu gǎnshàng le zuìhòu yī bānchē。" jyutping="hang6 hou2 daai6 gaa1 dou1 gon2 soeng5 liu5 zeoi3 hau6 jat1 baan1 ce1。" english="Luckily, everyone caught the last bus." />

<Hint type="hint">幸好 can introduce a fortunate event or condition broadly.</Hint>

### Credit a Helpful Person or Condition

幸亏 also means “luckily,” and often points to someone’s help or a specific condition that mattered.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="幸亏有你帮忙。" traditional="幸虧有你幫忙。" pinyin="xìngkuī yǒu nǐ bāngmáng." jyutping="hang6 kwai1 jau5 nei5 bong1 mong4." english="Luckily I had your help." />

<Hint type="hint">幸亏 commonly appears before 有…帮忙 or another concrete reason for relief.</Hint>

### Look at What Holds in the End

终究 means “after all” or “in the end.” It signals a conclusion that remains true despite earlier hopes, doubts, or changes.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这终究不是一个很好的主意。" traditional="這終究不是一個很好的主意。" pinyin="zhè zhōngjiū bùshì yīge hěn hǎo de zhǔyi。" jyutping="ze2 zung1 gau3 bat1 si6 jat1 go3 han2 hou2 dik1 zyu2 ji3。" english="After all, this isn't a very good idea." />

<Hint type="hint">终究 does not simply mean “finally” in a sequence. It emphasizes the truth or outcome that ultimately stands.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">幸好 and 幸亏 spotlight the circumstance that brought relief. 终究 steps back from the immediate event to state what remains true in the end.</Hint>
