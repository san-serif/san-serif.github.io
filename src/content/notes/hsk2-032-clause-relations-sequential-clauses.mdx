先……再…… lays out steps in the order you should do them. If the order is already clear from context, Chinese can simply place one action before the next without either linker.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我先学习两个多小时，然后再去睡觉。" traditional="我先學習兩個多小時，然後再去睡覺。" pinyin="wǒ xiān xuéxí liǎng gè duō xiǎoshí， ránhòu zài qù shuìjiào。" jyutping="ngo5 sin1 hok6 zaap6 loeng5 go3 do1 siu2 si4， jin4 hau6 zoi3 heoi3 seoi6 gaau3。" english="I’ll study for a little over two hours, then go to bed." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他们不知道先做什么。" traditional="他們不知道先做什麼。" pinyin="tāmen bù zhīdào xiān zuò shénme。" jyutping="taa1 mun4 bat1 zi1 dou6 sin1 zou6 sam6 mo1。" english="They didn't know what to do first." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我不知道他什么时候会再来的。" traditional="我不知道他什麼時候會再來的。" pinyin="wǒ bù zhīdào tā shénme shíhou huì zài lái de。" jyutping="ngo5 bat1 zi1 dou6 taa1 sam6 mo1 si4 hau6 wui2 zoi3 loi4 dik1。" english="I don't know when he'll come again." />
