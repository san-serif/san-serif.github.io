It’s late, the train is about to leave, and you need to head home. 该……了 says the time has come to do something; 快要/就要……了 says an event is expected very soon. The situation tells you whether it’s a reminder or a prediction.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我想快要下雨了。" traditional="我想快要下雨了。" pinyin="wǒ xiǎng kuài yào xià yǔ le." jyutping="ngo5 soeng2 faai3 jiu3 haa6 jyu5 liu5." english="I think it’s about to rain." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们就要吃饭了。" traditional="我們就要吃飯了。" pinyin="wǒmen jiùyào chīfàn le。" jyutping="ngo5 mun4 zau6 jiu3 hek3 faan6 liu5。" english="We’re about to eat." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我该回家了。" traditional="我該回家了。" pinyin="wǒ gāi huíjiā le。" jyutping="ngo5 goi1 wui4 gaa1 zo2。" english="I should go home now." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="火车快要开了，我们得赶紧上车。" traditional="火車快要開了，我們得趕緊上車。" pinyin="huǒchē kuàiyào kāi le, wǒmen děi gǎnjǐn shàngchē." jyutping="fo2 ce1 faai3 jiu3 hoi1 liu5, ngo5 mun4 dak1 gon2 gan2 soeng5 ce1." english="The train is about to leave; we need to get on quickly." />
