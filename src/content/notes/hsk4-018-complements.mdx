You study hard and 考上 the program; later, you 看出 a familiar face in the crowd. Some directional-looking complements describe a result rather than movement through space. The action reaches somewhere useful: passing, noticing, remembering, or getting something down in writing.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她准备了半年，终于考上了理想的大学。" traditional="她準備了半年，終於考上了理想的大學。" pinyin="tā zhǔnbèi le bàn nián, zhōngyú kǎoshàng le lǐxiǎng de dàxué." jyutping="taa1 zeon2 bei6 liu5 bun3 nin4, zung1 jyu1 haau2 soeng5 liu5 lei5 soeng2 dik1 daai6 hok6." english="She prepared for half a year and finally got into her ideal university." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="从他的表情，我看出他有点儿紧张。" traditional="從他的表情，我看出他有點兒緊張。" pinyin="cóng tā de biǎoqíng, wǒ kànchū tā yǒudiǎnr jǐnzhāng." jyutping="cung4 taa1 dik1 biu2 cing4, ngo5 hon3 ceot1 taa1 jau5 dim2 ji4 gan2 zoeng1." english="From his expression, I could tell he was a little nervous." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我突然想起钥匙还放在办公室里。" traditional="我突然想起鑰匙還放在辦公室裡。" pinyin="wǒ tūrán xiǎngqǐ yàoshi hái fàng zài bàngōngshì lǐ." jyutping="ngo5 dat6 jin4 soeng2 hei2 joek6 si4 waan4 fong3 zoi6 baan6 gung1 sat1 leoi5." english="I suddenly remembered that I’d left the keys in the office." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="请把你的电话号码写下来，方便我们联系。" traditional="請把你的電話號碼寫下來，方便我們聯繫。" pinyin="qǐng bǎ nǐ de diànhuà hàomǎ xiě xiàlai, fāngbiàn wǒmen liánxì." jyutping="cing2 baa2 nei5 dik1 din6 waa2 hou6 maa5 se2 haa6 loi4, fong1 bin6 ngo5 mun4 lin4 hai6." english="Please write down your phone number so we can contact you." />

<Hint type="hint">These complements describe a result, not just a direction: 考上 is a successful outcome, 看出 is a conclusion you reach, 想起 is a memory returning, and 写下 is information recorded.</Hint>
