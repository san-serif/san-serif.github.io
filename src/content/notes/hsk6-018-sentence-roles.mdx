Some verbs take a place directly after them: you can arrive at a city, climb a mountain, or enter a room without adding 在. Other verbs take the thing their action creates, such as a letter you write or a design you produce. In both cases, the noun after the verb is the object, but its relationship to the action differs.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="火车下午到上海，我们在车站见。" traditional="火車下午到上海，我們在車站見。" pinyin="huǒchē xiàwǔ dào Shànghǎi, wǒmen zài chēzhàn jiàn." jyutping="fo2 ce1 haa6 ng5 dou3 Soeng6 hoi2, ngo5 mun4 zoi6 ce1 zaam6 gin3." english="The train arrives in Shanghai this afternoon; let’s meet at the station." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他们打算明天爬黄山，晚上住在山脚下。" traditional="他們打算明天爬黃山，晚上住在山腳下。" pinyin="tāmen dǎsuàn míngtiān pá Huángshān, wǎnshang zhù zài shānjiǎo xià." jyutping="taa1 mun4 daa2 syun3 ming4 tin1 paa4 Wong4 saan1, maan5 soeng6 zyu6 zoi6 saan1 goek3 haa6." english="They plan to climb Mount Huang tomorrow and stay at its foot that night." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="姐姐亲手做了一张木桌，准备送给奶奶。" traditional="姐姐親手做了一張木桌，準備送給奶奶。" pinyin="jiějie qīnshǒu zuò le yì zhāng mùzhuō, zhǔnbèi sòng gěi nǎinai." jyutping="ze2 ze2 can1 sau2 zou6 liu5 jat1 zoeng1 muk6 zoek3, zeon2 bei6 sung3 kap1 naai4 naai4." english="My older sister made a wooden table herself and plans to give it to Grandma." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="建筑师为学校设计了一座新图书馆。" traditional="建築師為學校設計了一座新圖書館。" pinyin="jiànzhùshī wèi xuéxiào shèjì le yí zuò xīn túshūguǎn." jyutping="gin3 zuk1 si1 wai6 hok6 haau6 cit3 gai3 liu5 jat1 zo6 san1 tou4 syu1 gun2." english="The architect designed a new library for the school." />

<Hint type="hint">A place object answers “where to?” or “where through?” after a motion verb: 到上海, 爬黄山. A result object names what the action brings into existence: 做了一张木桌, 设计了一座图书馆.</Hint>
