The forecast may change, but 无论如何，活动照常举行 (“in any case, the event goes ahead”). 由此可见 draws a conclusion from the evidence you just heard; 与此同时 keeps another development happening at the same time in view.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="无论如何，我一定要在明天前完成这项工作。" traditional="無論如何，我一定要在明天前完成這項工作。" pinyin="wúlùnrúhé， wǒ yīdìng yào zài míngtiān qián wánchéng zhè xiàng gōngzuò。" jyutping="mou4 leon6 jyu4 ho4， ngo5 jat1 ding6 jiu3 zoi6 ming4 tin1 cin4 jyun4 sing4 ze2 hong6 gung1 zok3。" english="In any case, I must finish this work by tomorrow." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这件事情处理得如此圆满，由此可见他的办事能力非常强。" traditional="這件事情處理得如此圓滿，由此可見他的辦事能力非常強。" pinyin="zhè jiàn shìqing chǔlǐ de rúcǐ yuánmǎn, yóucǐkějiàn tā de bànshì nénglì fēicháng qiáng." jyutping="ze5 gin6 si6 cing4 cyu2 lei5 dak1 jyu4 ci2 jyun4 mun5, jau4 ci2 ho2 gin3 taa1 dik1 baan6 si6 nang4 lik6 fei1 soeng4 koeng4。" english="This matter was handled so perfectly; it shows that his ability to handle affairs is very strong." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他在努力工作赚钱，与此同时，也没有忘记照顾年迈的父母。" traditional="他在努力工作賺錢，與此同時，也沒有忘記照顧年邁的父母。" pinyin="tā zài nǔlì gōngzuò zhuànqián, yǔcǐtóngshí, yě méiyǒu wàngjì zhàogù niánmài de fùmǔ." jyutping="taa1 zoi6 nou5 lik6 gung1 zok3 zaan6 cin2, jyu5 ci2 tung4 si4, jaa5 mut6 jau5 mong4 gei3 ziu3 gu3 nin4 maai6 dik1 fu6 mou6." english="He is working hard to make money, and at the same time, he has not forgotten to take care of his elderly parents." />
