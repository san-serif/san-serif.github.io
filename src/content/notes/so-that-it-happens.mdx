<JapaneseLessonHead />

ように attaches to a verb's plain form (often the potential form) and means "so that," setting up a goal for the action that follows. Combined with なる, する, or 言う, it branches into three related patterns: a change that happened, an effort you're committing to, or an instruction given to someone else.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="町[まち]で漢字[かんじ]が読[よ]めるように、電子辞書[でんしじしょ]を買[か]いました。" romaji="Machi de kanji ga yomeru you ni, denshi jisho o kaimashita." english="I bought an electronic dictionary so that I could read kanji around town." />

<Hint>ように on its own sets up a purpose, similar to ために, but ために needs a verb someone actively controls, while ように is required whenever the verb is a state, a potential form, or something outside direct control, 読めるように, "so that it becomes possible to read," not a deliberate action itself.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="社会人[しゃかいじん]になってから、朝[あさ]ご飯[はん]を毎日[まいにち]食[た]べるようになりました。" romaji="Shakaijin ni natte kara, asagohan o mainichi taberu you ni narimashita." english="Ever since becoming a working adult, I've come to eat breakfast every day." />

<Hint>ようになる marks a change that happened over time, from not doing something to doing it, or from being unable to do something to being able, 話せるようになる, "came to be able to speak," a natural pairing with なる, which you already know describes change.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="明日[あした]から野菜[やさい]をたくさん食[た]べるようにします。" romaji="Ashita kara yasai o takusan taberu you ni shimasu." english="Starting tomorrow, I'll make an effort to eat plenty of vegetables." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="できるだけタバコを吸[す]わないようにしています。" romaji="Dekiru dake tabako o suwanai you ni shite imasu." english="I try not to smoke as much as possible." />

<Hint>ようにする marks a deliberate, ongoing effort toward a goal, distinct from ようになる, which just reports that a change already occurred naturally. ようにしています, with ている, describes a standing habit you actively maintain, "I make a point of."</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="課長[かちょう]はスミスさんに電話[でんわ]をするように言[い]いました。" romaji="Kachou wa Sumisu-san ni denwa o suru you ni iimashita." english="The manager told Smith to make a phone call." />

<Hint>ように言う reports an instruction given to someone else, "told them to," softer and less direct than the causative させる, useful for relaying a request or order secondhand rather than commanding someone outright.</Hint>
