These formal patterns set a viewpoint or boundary. にあって means “in the circumstances of” and frames a situation. に至るまで extends a range all the way to a far endpoint, while に至っては singles out a case for special comment. すら adds an emphatic “even.” にして can mark a point or stage reached, and にしてみれば means “from that person's perspective.” からする gives an approximate price or quantity, while からある emphasizes a large minimum amount.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="困難[こんなん]な状況[じょうきょう]にあっても、研究[けんきゅう]を続[つづ]けました。" romaji="Konnan na joukyou ni atte mo, kenkyuu o tsuzukemashita." english="Even in difficult circumstances, they continued their research." />

にあって frames a particular place, period, or situation, often in formal writing. It can be followed by も to say that something remains true even under those conditions.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="この博物館[はくぶつかん]には、古代[こだい]の道具[どうぐ]から現代[げんだい]の機械[きかい]に至[いた]るまで展示[てんじ]されています。" romaji="Kono hakubutsukan ni wa, kodai no dougu kara gendai no kikai ni itaru made tenji sarete imasu." english="This museum displays everything from ancient tools through modern machines." />

に至るまで marks the full extent of a range. に至っては takes a topic and then comments on it, often to highlight an extreme or surprising case: 兄に至っては, “as for my older brother...” すら is an emphatic “even,” often marking something unexpected.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="彼[かれ]にしてみれば、当然[とうぜん]の判断[はんだん]だったのでしょう。" romaji="Kare ni shite mireba, touzen no handan datta no deshou." english="From his perspective, it was probably a natural decision." />

にしてみれば explicitly adopts someone's point of view. からする often gives a starting price or quantity, as in 一万円からする品, “an item costing ten thousand yen or more.” からある also emphasizes a substantial minimum, especially for weight, distance, or attendance.

<Hint type="insight">In formal reporting, にあって describes the setting for a decision, while に至っては zooms in on a striking example. に至るまで instead stretches a list from one endpoint all the way to another.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Frame a situation: にあって. Extend a range: に至るまで. Spotlight a case: に至っては. Emphatic inclusion: すら. Adopt a person's perspective: にしてみれば. Approximate lower bounds: からする / からある.</Hint>
