You were heading to class when you ran into a friend. Later, that friend helped you with a bag. -다가 interrupts the first activity; -아/어 주다 tells us someone did an action for somebody else.

### An Action Gets Interrupted

Attach -다가 to the verb stem: 가다가, 먹다가, 공부하다가. The first activity is under way when another event happens or you switch to something else. In the basic pattern, keep the actor the same across both clauses.

<SentenceBlock formal="학교에 가다가 친구를 만났습니다." formalRom="hakgyoe gadaga chingureul mannatseumnida." polite="학교에 가다가 친구를 만났어요." politeRom="hakgyoe gadaga chingureul mannasseoyo." casual="학교에 가다가 친구를 만났어." casualRom="hakgyoe gadaga chingureul mannasseo." english="I met a friend on my way to school." />

<SentenceBlock formal="공부하다가 잠깐 쉬었습니다." formalRom="gongbuhadaga jamkkan swieotseumnida." polite="공부하다가 잠깐 쉬었어요." politeRom="gongbuhadaga jamkkan swieosseoyo." casual="공부하다가 잠깐 쉬었어." casualRom="gongbuhadaga jamkkan swieosseo." english="I took a short break while studying." />

### Do Something for Someone

Use the -아/어 form plus 주다: 도와주다, 읽어 주다, 열어 주다. A request becomes -아/어 주세요; a completed favour becomes -아/어 줬어요. The helper is the subject, and the person benefiting can be named with 에게 or 한테.

<SentenceBlock formal="친구가 제 가방을 들어 줬습니다." formalRom="chinguga je gabangeul deureo jwotseumnida." polite="친구가 제 가방을 들어 줬어요." politeRom="chinguga je gabangeul deureo jwosseoyo." casual="친구가 내 가방을 들어 줬어." casualRom="chinguga nae gabangeul deureo jwosseo." english="My friend carried my bag for me." />

<SentenceBlock formal="이 문장을 읽어 주십시오." formalRom="i munjangeul ilgeo jusipsio." polite="이 문장을 읽어 주세요." politeRom="i munjangeul ilgeo juseyo." casual="이 문장을 읽어 줘." casualRom="i munjangeul ilgeo jwo." english="Please read this sentence for me." />

<Hint type="hint">가다가 stops the journey’s flow to tell you what happened along the way. [가서](/notes/and-then) normally moves the story on after reaching the destination.</Hint>
