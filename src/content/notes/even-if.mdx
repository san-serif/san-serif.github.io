Even if it rains, the festival goes on: 雨が降っても祭りはあります. ても sets up a condition, then says the result still holds. The ending follows the て-form or the matching で-form.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="拓也[たくや]さんは、仕事[しごと]が大変[たいへん]でも頑張[がんば]ります。" romaji="Takuya-san wa, shigoto ga taihen demo ganbarimasu." english="Takuya does his best even when the work is tough." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="風邪[かぜ]を引[ひ]いても会社[かいしゃ]に来[き]ます。" romaji="Kaze o hiitemo kaisha ni kimasu." english="He comes to the office even if he catches a cold." />

<Hint type="hint">ても builds directly on て, which you already know, plus も, "even." な-adjectives and nouns use でも instead of だ, 大変でも, い-adjectives drop い for くても, 忙しくても, following the same pattern as すぎる and ほうがいい.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="朝[あさ]になっても、起[お]きられません。" romaji="Asa ni nattemo, okiraremasen." english="Even once morning comes, I can't get up." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="難[むずか]しい仕事[しごと]でもチャレンジします。" romaji="Muzukashii shigoto demo charenji shimasu." english="I'll take on the challenge even if it's difficult work." />

<Hint type="hint">ても pairs naturally with a negative result, "even if X, still can't Y," making it useful for describing something stubbornly unaffected by a condition that should normally change it, an alarm that keeps ringing, a habit that won't budge.</Hint>

In casual speech, たって can replace ても after a past-form verb or adjective: 雨が降ったって行く, “I'll go even if it rains.” It sounds more conversational and emphatic than the standard form.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="少[すこ]し無理[むり]をしてでも、今日中[きょうじゅう]に終[お]わらせます。" romaji="Sukoshi muri o shite demo, kyoujuu ni owarasemasu." english="Even if I have to push myself a little, I'll finish it today." />

てでも means “even if I have to do X to make it happen.” It presents the action as a sacrifice or inconvenient step the speaker is still willing to take. Unlike ordinary ても, it highlights the determination to reach the result.

<Hint type="insight">Among friends, たって gives a firm “even if…” stance. Use てでも when explaining how far you are willing to go: 何時間かかってでも探す, “I'll find it even if it takes hours.” In careful writing, prefer ても for the plain concession.</Hint>
