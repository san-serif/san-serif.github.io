A classmate tells you they have been accepted overseas. You react to the news, then offer a wish for what comes next. -다니 highlights the surprising fact; -기를 바라다 says what you hope will happen.

### React to the Fact Itself

Present action verbs take -ㄴ다니 after a vowel and -는다니 after a consonant. Adjectives take -다니 and nouns -(이)라니. Past facts use -았/었다니. It can express surprise, admiration, dismay, or disbelief; the feeling depends on what follows.

<SentenceBlock polite="벌써 졸업한다니 시간이 참 빠르네요." politeRom="beolsseo joreophandani sigani cham ppareuneyo." casual="벌써 졸업한다니 시간이 참 빠르네." casualRom="beolsseo joreophandani sigani cham ppareune." english="To think we’re graduating already—time passes so quickly." />

<SentenceBlock polite="원하던 학교에 합격했다니 정말 기뻐요." politeRom="wonhadeon hakgyoe hapgyeokhaetdani jeongmal gippeoyo." casual="원하던 학교에 합격했다니 정말 기뻐." casualRom="wonhadeon hakgyoe hapgyeokhaetdani jeongmal gippeo." english="I’m so happy to hear you got into the school you wanted." />

### Offer a Wish for What Comes Next

Attach -기를 바라다 to an action verb or adjective stem: 합격하기를 바라다, 행복하기를 바라다. The wish often concerns another person’s future and is common in messages, greetings, and formal speech. 를 can be omitted: 잘 지내기 바라요.

<SentenceBlock formal="새 학교에서 좋은 친구들을 만나기를 바랍니다." formalRom="sae hakgyoeseo joeun chingudeureul mannagireul baramnida." polite="새 학교에서 좋은 친구들을 만나기를 바라요." politeRom="sae hakgyoeseo joeun chingudeureul mannagireul barayo." casual="새 학교에서 좋은 친구들을 만나기를 바라." casualRom="sae hakgyoeseo joeun chingudeureul mannagireul bara." english="I hope you meet good friends at your new school." />

<SentenceBlock formal="앞으로도 건강하고 행복하기를 바랍니다." formalRom="apeurodo geonganghago haengbokhagireul baramnida." polite="앞으로도 건강하고 행복하기를 바라요." politeRom="apeurodo geonganghago haengbokhagireul barayo." casual="앞으로도 건강하고 행복하기를 바라." casualRom="apeurodo geonganghago haengbokhagireul bara." english="I hope you stay healthy and happy in the years ahead." />

<Hint type="hint">The standard spelling is 바라요, from 바라다, even though 바래요 is often heard. For a wish about your own intended action, [-고 싶어요](/notes/i-want-to) or [-았으면 해요](/notes/if-it-were-possible) often fits more naturally.</Hint>
