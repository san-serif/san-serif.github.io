When you tell a story, you can build a phrase around a thing, an action, or a description: 一个安全的地方 (“a safe place”), 去买咖啡 (“go buy coffee”), 非常安全 (“very safe”). And if you want to move the story along, 不一会儿 means “a little while later.”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们去买咖啡，不一会儿就回来了。" traditional="我們去買咖啡，不一會兒就回來了。" pinyin="wǒmen qù mǎi kāfēi, bù yíhuìr jiù huílai le." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 heoi3 maai5 gaa3 fe1, bat1 jat1 wui6 ji4 zau6 wui4 loi4 liu5." english="We went to get coffee and came back a little while later." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我想找一个安静的地方看书。" traditional="我想找一個安靜的地方看書。" pinyin="wǒ xiǎng zhǎo yí ge ānjìng de dìfang kàn shū." jyutping="ngo5 soeng2 zaau2 jat1 go3 on1 zing6 dik1 dei6 fong1 hon3 syu1." english="I want to find a quiet place to read." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家咖啡店非常安静。" traditional="這家咖啡店非常安靜。" pinyin="zhè jiā kāfēidiàn fēicháng ānjìng." jyutping="ze5 gaa1 gaa3 fe1 dim3 fei1 soeng4 on1 zing6." english="This café is very quiet." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他到家的时候，朋友已经等了一会儿。" traditional="他到家的時候，朋友已經等了一會兒。" pinyin="tā dào jiā de shíhou, péngyou yǐjīng děng le yíhuìr." jyutping="taa1 dou3 gaa1 dik1 si4 hau6, pang4 jau5 ji5 ging1 dang2 liu5 jat1 wui6 ji4." english="By the time he got home, his friend had already been waiting for a while." />

<Hint type="hint">A phrase can do the job of one part of speech: 安静的地方 names a thing, 去买咖啡 describes an action, and 非常安静 describes a quality. 不一会儿 is a time phrase that moves the story forward.</Hint>
