When a problem, duty, or unwanted situation suddenly lands on someone, use 到……头上来了. The person affected comes before 头上; the thing that lands there comes first.

### Hear the Pattern in Context

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这件麻烦事怎么落到我头上来了？" traditional="這件麻煩事怎麼落到我頭上來了？" pinyin="Zhè jiàn máfan shì zěnme luò dào wǒ tóu shàng lái le?" jyutping="ze3 gin6 maa4 faan4 si6 zam2 mo1 lok6 dou3 ngo5 tau4 soeng6 loi4 liu5?" english="How did this troublesome matter land on me?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="责任怎么又落到我头上来了？" traditional="責任怎麼又落到我頭上來了？" pinyin="Zérèn zěnme yòu luò dào wǒ tóu shàng lái le?" jyutping="zaak3 jam6 zam2 mo1 jau6 lok6 dou3 ngo5 tau4 soeng6 loi4 liu5?" english="How did the responsibility land on me again?" />

<Hint type="hint">头上 is literal in other contexts, but in this expression it means the person has been made responsible for something. The tone often carries surprise or annoyance.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">You can use the pattern for an unexpected task, blame, or expense: the situation arrives “on someone’s head,” whether they asked for it or not.</Hint>
