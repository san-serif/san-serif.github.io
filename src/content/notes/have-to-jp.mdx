なければならない attaches to a verb or adjective's negative stem and expresses obligation, literally "if not, it won't do." It's built from two pieces you already have, ば (if) and ならない (won't become okay), stacked together into a single fixed phrase.

ないことには～ない presents an action as a necessary condition: 説明を聞かないことには、判断できません, “unless we hear the explanation, we can't decide.” Unlike a direct obligation, it focuses on what must happen before a result is possible.

The written form ねばならない means the same thing and sounds more formal or old-fashioned. It is formed from the verb's negative stem plus ねば: 行かねばならない, “must go.” In everyday speech, なければならない is much more common.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="このクラスでは5時[ごじ]までに宿題[しゅくだい]を出[だ]さなければなりません。" romaji="Kono kurasu de wa goji made ni shukudai o dasanakereba narimasen." english="In this class, homework has to be turned in by 5:00." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="漢字[かんじ]を300覚[おぼ]えなければなりません。" romaji="Kanji o sanbyaku oboenakereba narimasen." english="We have to memorize 300 kanji." />

<Hint type="hint">The literal shape is "verb-ば-negative + ならない," if you don't do it, it doesn't work out. In practice it just reads as a plain obligation, "have to," with none of that literal if-then logic surfacing in translation.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="これが売[う]れるためには、環境[かんきょう]に優[やさ]しくなければなりません。" romaji="Kore ga ureru tame ni wa, kankyou ni yasashiku nakereba narimasen." english="For this to sell, it has to be environmentally friendly." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="このレポートは日本語[にほんご]で書[か]かなければなりませんか。" romaji="Kono repooto wa nihongo de kakanakereba narimasen ka." english="Does this report have to be written in Japanese?" />

<Hint type="hint">い-adjectives swap い for くなければならない (優しくなければならない), and な-adjectives and nouns add でなければならない (便利でなければならない), following the same negative-stem pattern that なくて also builds on.</Hint>

In everyday speech, the longer obligation forms often shorten. なければならない can become なければいけない, なくてはいけない, or, very casually, なきゃいけない / なきゃ. ないと is another common spoken shortcut, often left unfinished because the listener can infer “I have to.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="もう帰[かえ]らないと。" romaji="Mou kaeranai to." english="I have to get going now." />

<Hint type="insight">In real life, もう帰らないと and 宿題をしなきゃ are common casual reminders to yourself or close friends. The full なければなりません sounds much more deliberate and is a safe choice in formal situations, but it can feel stiff among friends.</Hint>

The opposite idea, “don't have to,” is なくてもいい: the action is optional, not forbidden.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="明日[あした]は早[はや]く来[こ]なくてもいいです。" romaji="Ashita wa hayaku konakute mo ii desu." english="You don't have to come early tomorrow." />

<Hint type="hint">Don't confuse “don't have to” with “must not.” なくてもいい allows the action but says it is unnecessary; てはいけない prohibits it. The も in なくてもいい gives the sense “even if you don't do it, that's okay.”</Hint>

When there is no alternative, verb dictionary form + より仕方[しかた]がない means “there is no choice but to…” It expresses resignation rather than a rule or personal obligation.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="電車[でんしゃ]が止[と]まったので、歩[ある]いて帰[かえ]るより仕方[しかた]がありません。" romaji="Densha ga tomatta node, aruite kaeru yori shikata ga arimasen." english="Since the train stopped, we have no choice but to walk home." />

<Hint type="insight">Use なければならない for a requirement such as a deadline or rule. Use より仕方がない when circumstances leave no workable alternative: there may be a preferred option, but it isn't available.</Hint>

For a formal, reluctant “have no choice but to,” use ざるを得ない: 規則を変更せざるを得ません, “we have no choice but to change the rule.” ないわけにはいかない stresses that an action cannot be left undone because of responsibility or expectation. Both are stronger than a plain requirement, but ざるを得ない foregrounds the lack of alternatives.

よりほかない is another written “there is no choice but to,” close to より仕方がない. It presents the remaining action as the only available path: 結果を待つよりほかありません, “there is nothing to do but wait for the result.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="全員[ぜんいん]に説明[せつめい]しないわけにはいきません。" romaji="Zen'in ni setsumei shinai wake ni wa ikimasen." english="We can't leave everyone without an explanation." />
