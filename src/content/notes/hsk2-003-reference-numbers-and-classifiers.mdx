Your friend asks how long the film is, what the new café is like, and who should order for the group. Words like 多久 and 怎么样 go right where the answer belongs. Once the people are already clear, 大家 or 自己 can gather them together without repeating every name.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="走到火车站要多久？" traditional="走到火車站要多久？" pinyin="zǒu dào huǒchēzhàn yào duōjiǔ？" jyutping="zau2 dou3 fo2 ce1 zaam6 jiu3 do1 gau2？" english="How long does it take to walk to the train station?" />
