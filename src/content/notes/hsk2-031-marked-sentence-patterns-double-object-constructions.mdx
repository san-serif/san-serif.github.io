Your friend asks for a menu, and you hand it to her. With verbs like 给 and 送, Chinese can name both the recipient and the thing being passed along: “give her the menu.” The usual order is verb + recipient + thing; 给 can also introduce the recipient later in the sentence.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="请交给他。" traditional="請交給他。" pinyin="qǐng jiāogěi tā." jyutping="cing2 gaau1 kap1 taa1." english="Please hand it to him." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="请给我筷子。" traditional="請給我筷子。" pinyin="qǐng gěi wǒ kuàizi." jyutping="cing2 kap1 ngo5 faai3 zi2." english="Please give me chopsticks." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这是给你的礼物。" traditional="這是給你的禮物。" pinyin="zhè shì gěi nǐ de lǐwù." jyutping="ze2 si6 kap1 nei5 dik1 lai5 mat6." english="This is a gift for you." />
