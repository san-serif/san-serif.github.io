The rain starts, then keeps going through the night. 起来 can mark a beginning; 下去 can carry an action on. Add 得 or 不 before a result complement to say whether you can reach it: 看得懂 means you can understand, while 看不出来 says you can’t make it out.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="听见熟悉的歌声，孩子们一下子都笑了起来。" traditional="聽見熟悉的歌聲，孩子們一下子都笑了起來。" pinyin="tīngjiàn shúxī de gēshēng, háizimen yíxiàzi dōu xiào le qǐlai." jyutping="teng1 gin3 suk6 sik1 dik1 go1 seng1, haai4 zi2 mun4 jat1 haa5 zi2 dou1 siu3 liu5 hei2 loi4." english="When they heard the familiar song, the children all started laughing." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="演出结束后，观众还想听下去，可是场地方已经要关门了。" traditional="演出結束後，觀眾還想聽下去，可是場地方已經要關門了。" pinyin="yǎnchū jiéshù hòu, guānzhòng hái xiǎng tīng xiàqù, kěshì chǎngdìfāng yǐjīng yào guānmén le." jyutping="jin2 ceot1 zit3 cuk1 hau6, gun1 zung3 waan4 soeng2 teng1 haa6 heoi3, ho2 si6 coeng4 dei6 fong1 ji5 ging1 jiu3 gwaan1 mun4 liu5." english="After the show ended, the audience still wanted to listen, but the venue had to close." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这张地图太旧了，我看不出来我们现在在哪儿。" traditional="這張地圖太舊了，我看不出來我們現在在哪兒。" pinyin="zhè zhāng dìtú tài jiù le, wǒ kàn bu chūlái wǒmen xiànzài zài nǎr." jyutping="ze5 zoeng1 dei6 tou4 taai3 gau6 liu5, ngo5 hon3 bat1 ceot1 loi4 ngo5 mun4 jin6 zoi6 zoi6 naa5 ji4." english="This map is too old; I can’t make out where we are now." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我看得懂这份说明，你不用再解释一遍。" traditional="我看得懂這份說明，你不用再解釋一遍。" pinyin="wǒ kàn de dǒng zhè fèn shuōmíng, nǐ búyòng zài jiěshì yí biàn." jyutping="ngo5 hon3 dak1 dung2 ze5 fan6 syut3 ming4, nei5 bat1 jung6 zoi3 gaai2 sik1 jat1 bin3." english="I can understand these instructions; you don’t need to explain them again." />

<Hint type="hint">起来 can mark an action beginning, while 下去 can show it continuing. In 看不出来, the negative potential form says the result cannot be reached. Compare 看得懂 for an action that is possible.</Hint>
