You’re planning a picnic and checking the forecast: 내일 날씨가 좋으면 좋겠어요 (“I hope the weather is nice tomorrow”). Use -(으)면 좋겠어요 to say you hope or wish something were the case. The stem ending determines -면 or -으면.

<SentenceBlock formal="가격이 좀 더 싸면 좋겠습니다." formalRom="gagyeogi jom deo ssamyeon joketseumnida." polite="가격이 좀 더 싸면 좋겠어요." politeRom="gagyeogi jom deo ssamyeon jokesseoyo." casual="가격이 좀 더 싸면 좋겠어." casualRom="gagyeogi jom deo ssamyeon jokesseo." english="I hope the price is a little cheaper." />

<SentenceBlock formal="빨리 점심을 먹으면 좋겠습니다." formalRom="ppalli jeomsimeul meogeumyeon joketseumnida." polite="빨리 점심을 먹으면 좋겠어요." politeRom="ppalli jeomsimeul meogeumyeon jokesseoyo." casual="빨리 점심을 먹으면 좋겠어." casualRom="ppalli jeomsimeul meogeumyeon jokesseo." english="I hope we can have lunch soon." />

<Hint type="hint">-(으)면 좋겠다 can express a straightforward hope without implying that the situation is impossible. For example, 빨리 끝나면 좋겠어요 means “I hope it finishes soon.”</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="이 방이 조금 밝았으면 좋겠습니다." formalRom="i bangi jogeum balgatseumyeon joketseumnida." polite="이 방이 조금 밝았으면 좋겠어요." politeRom="i bangi jogeum balgatseumyeon jokesseoyo." casual="이 방이 조금 밝았으면 좋겠어." casualRom="i bangi jogeum balgatseumyeon jokesseo." english="I wish this room were a little brighter." />

<Hint type="insight">In real life, -았/었으면 좋겠어요 often sounds like a stronger or more wistful wish about a situation you would like to be different: 조금 더 가까웠으면 좋겠어요, “I wish it were a little closer.” The past-looking form does not mean the wish is about the past.</Hint>
