At dinner, your friend takes one bite at a time while the rest of the group is already halfway through. Repeat a measure word to show that something happens to each item separately. Without 一, a repeated measure word can also mean “every single one.”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他一口一口地喝完了这碗汤。" traditional="他一口一口地喝完了這碗湯。" pinyin="tā yì kǒu yì kǒu de hē wán le zhè wǎn tāng." jyutping="taa1 jat1 hau2 jat1 hau2 dei6 hot3 jyun4 liu5 ze5 wun2 tong1." english="He drank this bowl of soup one sip at a time." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="客人一位一位地进来，店员忙着安排座位。" traditional="客人一位一位地進來，店員忙著安排座位。" pinyin="kèrén yí wèi yí wèi de jìnlai, diànyuán mángzhe ānpái zuòwèi." jyutping="haak3 jan4 jat1 wai2 jat1 wai2 dei6 zeon3 loi4, dim3 jyun4 mong4 zoek6 on1 paai4 zo6 wai2." english="The guests came in one by one while the staff arranged their seats." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这几本书本本都很有意思，你可以慢慢挑。" traditional="這幾本書本本都很有意思，你可以慢慢挑。" pinyin="zhè jǐ běn shū běn běn dōu hěn yǒuyìsi, nǐ kěyǐ mànmàn tiāo." jyutping="ze5 gei2 bun2 syu1 bun2 bun2 dou1 han2 jau5 ji3 si1, nei5 ho2 ji5 maan6 maan6 tiu1." english="Every one of these books is interesting; take your time choosing." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我看了菜单一眼，就知道要点什么了。" traditional="我看了菜單一眼，就知道要點什麼了。" pinyin="wǒ kàn le càidān yì yǎn, jiù zhīdào yào diǎn shénme le." jyutping="ngo5 hon3 liu5 coi3 daan1 jat1 ngaan5, zau6 zi1 dou3 jiu3 dim2 sam6 mo1 liu5." english="I glanced at the menu and immediately knew what to order." />

<Hint type="hint">一口一口地 and 一位一位地 describe an action done one at a time; the repeated word is followed by 地. In 本本都, the classifier stands on its own and means “every single book.”</Hint>
