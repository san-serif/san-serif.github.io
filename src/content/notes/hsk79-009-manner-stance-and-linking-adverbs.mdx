“万万不能把密码告诉别人”—under no circumstances should you share the password. 未免 can judge something as a bit much; 无非 reduces it to “nothing more than.” These words narrow or evaluate the claim, each with a different force.

### Make a Strong Warning

万万 is most often used with a negative such as 不 or 没想到. It strengthens a warning or emphasizes how unexpected something was.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我万万没想到我会得第一名。" traditional="我萬萬沒想到我會得第一名。" pinyin="wǒ wànwàn méi xiǎngdào wǒ huì dé dì yī míng。" jyutping="ngo5 maan6 maan6 mut6 soeng2 dou3 ngo5 wui2 dak1 dai6 jat1 ming4。" english="Never did I dream of winning first prize." />

<Hint type="hint">万万不可 is a firm “absolutely not.” In ordinary positive statements, 万万 is uncommon.</Hint>

### Say That Something Goes Too Far

未免 often appears before 太 plus an adjective. The speaker suggests that a quality or action is excessive, often with mild criticism.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你也未免太小气了。" traditional="你也未免太小氣了。" pinyin="nǐ yě wèimiǎn tài xiǎoqì le." jyutping="nei5 jaa5 mei6 min5 taai3 siu2 hei3 liu5." english="You are really a bit too stingy." />

<Hint type="hint">未免 is not simply “a little”; it signals that the degree is more than appropriate.</Hint>

### Reduce Something to Its Main Explanation

无非 means “nothing more than” or “simply.” It narrows an explanation to a small set of motives or facts.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他这么做无非是为了钱。" traditional="他這麼做無非是為了錢。" pinyin="tā zhème zuò wúfēi shì wèile qián." jyutping="taa1 ze2 mo1 zou6 mou4 fei1 si6 wai6 liu5 cin4." english="He did this simply for money." />

<Hint type="hint">无非 often appears before 是 or 为了 to introduce the speaker’s simplified account.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">Look at what comes next: 万万 strengthens a prohibition or surprise, 未免 often leads into 太 plus an adjective, and 无非 narrows an explanation.</Hint>
