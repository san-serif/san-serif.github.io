The notice says 尚未开放: “not open yet.” 未 is a formal way to say something has not happened by now; 勿 appears in written prohibitions and instructions, such as 请勿入内 (“please do not enter”).

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这个问题仍然未解决。" traditional="這個問題仍然未解決。" pinyin="zhège wèntí réngrán wèi jiějué。" jyutping="ze2 go3 man6 tai4 jing4 jin4 mei6 gaai2 kyut3。" english="The problem remains unsolved." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="事故发生时，敬勿使用电梯，请使用楼梯。" traditional="事故發生時，敬勿使用電梯，請使用樓梯。" pinyin="shìgù fāshēng shí， jìng wù shǐyòng diàntī， qǐng shǐyòng lóutī。" jyutping="si6 gu3 faat3 sang1 si4， ging3 mat6 si2 jung6 din6 tai1， cing2 si2 jung6 lau4 tai1。" english="In an emergency, use the stairway, not the elevator." />
