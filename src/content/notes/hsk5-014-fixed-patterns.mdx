From the turnout, the event looks like a success. 从……来看 names the evidence or viewpoint behind a judgement; 到……为止 gives a clear endpoint; 够……的 comments that something is quite a lot, perhaps more than expected.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我从她还是个小女孩儿的时候就认识她。" traditional="我從她還是個小女孩兒的時候就認識她。" pinyin="wǒ cóng tā háishi gè xiǎo nǚháir de shíhou jiù rènshi tā。" jyutping="ngo5 cung4 taa1 waan4 si6 go3 siu2 neoi5 haai4 ji4 dik1 si4 hau6 zau6 jing6 sik1 taa1。" english="I have known her since she was a little girl." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你怎么到那边的？" traditional="你怎麼到那邊的？" pinyin="nǐ zěnme dào nàbiān de？" jyutping="nei5 zam2 mo1 dou3 naa5 bin1 dik1？" english="How did you get over there?" />
