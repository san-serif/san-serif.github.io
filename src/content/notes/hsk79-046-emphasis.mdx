Your friend wants to drive across town for one missing ingredient. 何必跑那么远呢？ (“Why go all that way?”) is not really asking for a reason; it suggests the effort isn’t worth it. The question form makes that advice more pointed.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="既然都知道了，何必再问？" traditional="既然都知道了，何必再問？" pinyin="jìrán dōu zhīdào le， hébì zài wèn？" jyutping="gei3 jin4 dou1 zi1 dou6 liu5, ho4 bit1 zoi3 man6?" english="Since you already know, why bother asking again?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你何苦为了这点小事生气？" traditional="你何苦為了這點小事生氣？" pinyin="nǐ hékǔ wèile zhèdiǎn xiǎoshì shēngqì?" jyutping="nei5 ho4 fu2 wai6 liu5 ze2 dim2 siu2 si6 sang1 hei3?" english="Why bother getting angry over such a small thing?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我很好，你呢？" traditional="我很好，你呢？" pinyin="wǒ hěn hǎo， nǐ ne？" jyutping="ngo5 han2 hou2， nei5 ne1？" english="I'm fine, and you?" />
