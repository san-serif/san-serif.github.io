“If the weather clears, we’ll take the ferry; otherwise, we’ll drive.” The opening clause sets the scene; the next gives the decision. A condition, reason, purpose, or concession can all shape what follows, so listen for the relationship between the two thoughts.

<Hint type="hint">The opening sentence frames the main point. 要是 sets a condition, 由于 gives a reason, 为了 gives a goal, and 虽然 sets up an expectation that the next clause goes against.</Hint>
