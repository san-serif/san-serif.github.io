These patterns arrange events across time. ぶりに says an event happens again after a long interval. 再び means “again / once more.” を通じて / を通して can mean “through” a route or medium, or “throughout” a period. てからというもの describes a continuing change since a turning point. を皮切りに presents a first event that opens a series; に先駆けて says something happened ahead of another event; を機に marks an occasion that prompts a change; を境に divides a before and after; and を経て describes reaching a point by passing through stages or experience.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="引[ひ]っ越[こ]してからというもの、毎朝[まいあさ]歩[ある]くようになりました。" romaji="Hikkoshite kara to iu mono, maiasa aruku you ni narimashita." english="Ever since moving, I've started walking every morning." />

ぶりに follows a time period to mark the first time an event has happened in that long: 三年ぶりに会った, “met for the first time in three years.” 以来 is another formal “since,” usually marking the point from which a state has continued: 卒業以来, “since graduation.” てからというもの highlights a change that began after an event and has continued. It often introduces a noticeable new habit, condition, or relationship.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="新店舗[しんてんぽ]の開店[かいてん]を皮切[かわき]りに、各地[かくち]で催[もよお]しが始[はじ]まりました。" romaji="Shintenpo no kaiten o kawakiri ni, kakuchi de moyooshi ga hajimarimashita." english="Starting with the new store's opening, events began in locations across the region." />

を皮切りに frames the first of a sequence, usually with more events of the same kind following. を機に focuses on the occasion as an opportunity or trigger for change. に先駆けて means “ahead of” a scheduled event, while を境に marks a dividing point between two states.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="研修[けんしゅう]を経[へ]て、正式[せいしき]な担当者[たんとうしゃ]に任命[にんめい]されました。" romaji="Kenshuu o hete, seishiki na tantousha ni ninmei saremashita." english="After going through training, I was appointed as the official person in charge." />

を経て describes a route, process, or experience passed through before reaching a later stage. It is common in formal biographies, reports, and descriptions of organizational change.

<Hint type="insight">A new policy can be launched に先駆けて a public briefing, then the launch itself may become an occasion を機に for changing procedures. を皮切りに is useful when a series begins with one named event; を経て emphasizes the steps taken along the way.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Continuing change since an event: てからというもの. First in a series: を皮切りに. Before a scheduled milestone: に先駆けて. An occasion prompting change: を機に. A boundary between eras: を境に. A process passed through: を経て.</Hint>
