“Mei called the restaurant. Then booked a table for six.” English needs a subject in the second sentence; Chinese can leave it out once the conversation makes it clear. Bring the person back into focus when the topic changes; otherwise, let the thread carry on.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这便于使用。" traditional="這便於使用。" pinyin="zhè biànyú shǐyòng." jyutping="ze2 bin6 jyu1 si2 jung6." english="This is easy to use." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这是指示。" traditional="這是指示。" pinyin="zhè shì zhǐshì." jyutping="ze2 si6 zi2 si6." english="This is an instruction." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这很漫长。" traditional="這很漫長。" pinyin="zhè hěn màncháng." jyutping="ze2 han2 maan6 coeng4." english="This is very long." />
