“You can borrow the car only if you bring it back by six.” 只有……才…… sets a condition the result depends on. The 才 in the result clause makes that requirement clear: without the condition, there’s no result.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="只有努力学习，才能通过考试。" traditional="只有努力學習，才能通過考試。" pinyin="zhǐyǒu nǔlì xuéxí， cái néng tōngguò kǎoshì。" jyutping="zi2 jau5 nou5 lik6 hok6 zaap6， coi4 nang4 tung1 gwo3 haau2 si5。" english="Only by studying hard can you pass the exam." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我才是那个想和你在一起的人！" traditional="我才是那個想和你在一起的人！" pinyin="wǒ cái shì nà ge xiǎng hé nǐ zài yìqǐ de rén！" jyutping="ngo5 coi4 si6 naa5 go3 soeng2 wo4 nei5 zoi6 jat1 hei2 dik1 jan4！" english="I'm the one who wants to be with you." />
