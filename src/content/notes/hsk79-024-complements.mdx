The train is so crowded that nobody can find a seat. Add 得要命 after an adjective or verb to say it’s extreme. A separate everyday frame, verb + 个 + phrase, can show an action carrying on without a break.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="火车挤得要命，我们都找不到位子坐。" traditional="火車擠得要命，我們都找不到位子坐。" pinyin="huǒchē jǐ de yàomìng, wǒmen dōu zhǎo bu dào wèizi zuò." jyutping="fo2 ce1 zai1 dak1 jiu3 meng6, ngo5 mun4 dou1 zaau2 bat1 dou3 wai2 zi2 co5." english="The train was so crowded that none of us could find a seat." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天热得不行，大家都想找个有空调的地方。" traditional="今天熱得不行，大家都想找個有空調的地方。" pinyin="jīntiān rè de bùxíng, dàjiā dōu xiǎng zhǎo ge yǒu kōngtiáo de dìfang." jyutping="gam1 tin1 jit6 dak1 bat1 hang4, daai6 gaa1 dou1 soeng2 zaau2 go3 jau5 hung1 tiu4 dik1 dei6 fong1." english="It’s unbearably hot today; everyone wants to find somewhere with air-conditioning." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他忙个不停，连午饭都没顾上吃。" traditional="他忙個不停，連午飯都沒顧上吃。" pinyin="tā máng ge bù tíng, lián wǔfàn dōu méi gùshàng chī." jyutping="taa1 mong4 go3 bat1 ting4, lin4 ng5 faan6 dou1 mut6 gu3 soeng5 hek3." english="He kept busy without stopping and didn’t even get a chance to eat lunch." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="几个孩子笑个不停，把老师也逗笑了。" traditional="幾個孩子笑個不停，把老師也逗笑了。" pinyin="jǐ ge háizi xiào ge bù tíng, bǎ lǎoshī yě dòuxiào le." jyutping="gei2 go3 haai4 zi2 siu3 go3 bat1 ting4, baa2 lou5 si1 jaa5 dau6 siu3 liu5." english="The children couldn’t stop laughing, and they made the teacher laugh too." />

<Hint type="hint">得要命 and 得不行 follow the word they intensify: 挤得要命, 热得不行. In 忙个不停 and 笑个不停, 个 introduces a phrase meaning “without stopping.”</Hint>
