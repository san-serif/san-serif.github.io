-(으)니까 attaches to a reason the same way -아서/어서 does, vowel or ㄹ-ending stems take -니까, consonant-ending stems take -으니까, but it can do something -아서/어서 can't: lead directly into a command or a suggestion.

<SentenceBlock formal="오늘은 바쁘니까 내일 만납니다." formalRom="oneureun bappeunikka naeil mannamnida." polite="오늘은 바쁘니까 내일 만나요." politeRom="oneureun bappeunikka naeil mannayo." casual="오늘은 바쁘니까 내일 만나." casualRom="oneureun bappeunikka naeil manna." english="Since I am busy today, let's meet tomorrow." />

<Hint>This is the reason-connector [-아서/어서](/notes/because) promised you. -아서/어서 can't sit in front of a command like -세요 or a suggestion; -(으)니까 is built for exactly that job. 추우니까 창문을 닫아 주세요 wouldn't work with -아서/어서 dropped into the same slot.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="추우니까 창문을 닫아 주십시오." formalRom="chuunikka changmuneul dada jusipsio." polite="추우니까 창문을 닫아 주세요." politeRom="chuunikka changmuneul dada juseyo." casual="추우니까 창문을 닫아 줘." casualRom="chuunikka changmuneul dada jwo." english="It's cold, so please close the window." />

<SentenceBlock formal="날씨가 좋으니까 산책합니다." formalRom="nalssiga joeunikka sanchaekhamnida." polite="날씨가 좋으니까 산책해요." politeRom="nalssiga joeunikka sanchaekhaeyo." casual="날씨가 좋으니까 산책해." casualRom="nalssiga joeunikka sanchaekhae." english="The weather is nice, so let's take a walk." />

<SentenceBlock formal="이 식당이 맛있으니까 여기로 갑니다." formalRom="i sikdangi masisseunikka yeogiro gamnida." polite="이 식당이 맛있으니까 여기로 가요." politeRom="i sikdangi masisseunikka yeogiro gayo." casual="이 식당이 맛있으니까 여기로 가." casualRom="i sikdangi masisseunikka yeogiro ga." english="Since this restaurant is delicious, let's go here." />

<SentenceBlock formal="시간이 없으니까 택시를 탑니다." formalRom="sigani eopseunikka taeksireul tamnida." polite="시간이 없으니까 택시를 타요." politeRom="sigani eopseunikka taeksireul tayo." casual="시간이 없으니까 택시를 타." casualRom="sigani eopseunikka taeksireul ta." english="We don't have time, so let's take a taxi." />

<SentenceBlock formal="피곤하니까 푹 쉬십시오." formalRom="pigonhanikka puk swisipsio." polite="피곤하니까 푹 쉬세요." politeRom="pigonhanikka puk swiseyo." casual="피곤하니까 푹 쉬어." casualRom="pigonhanikka puk swieo." english="You're tired, so rest well." />

<Hint>-아서/어서 tends to state a plain, fact-based cause. -(으)니까 carries a more subjective, persuasive edge, closer to "given that..." than a flat "because." It's the reason you offer right before asking for something or proposing a plan, which is exactly why it pairs so naturally with commands and suggestions.</Hint>
