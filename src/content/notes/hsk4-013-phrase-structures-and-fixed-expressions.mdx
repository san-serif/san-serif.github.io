他们花钱大手大脚，看来该做预算了. 大手大脚 describes careless spending, while 一心一意 means giving one thing your full attention. 看来 introduces the conclusion you draw from the clues.

<Hint type="hint">A大A大 and 一A一B are fixed combinations, so learn them as complete expressions. 看来 is not part of those patterns: it signals your inference, often based on what you can see or hear.</Hint>
