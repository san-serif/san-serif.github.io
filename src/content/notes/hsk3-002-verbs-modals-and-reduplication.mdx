One person finally dares to ask a question; another needs a hand with a suitcase. 敢 and 需要 lead into the action. Other common verbs in this lesson split around an object: 帮了一个忙 and 洗了个澡 each tuck a little phrase inside the verb.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我会在一个月后回来和你们见面。" traditional="我會在一個月後回來和你們見面。" pinyin="wǒ huì zài yí ge yuè hòu huílái hé nǐmen jiànmiàn。" jyutping="ngo5 wui2 zoi6 jat1 go3 jyut6 hau6 wui4 loi4 wo4 nei5 mun4 gin3 min6。" english="I’ll come back to meet you in a month." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="如果你需要我的建议，我很愿意告诉你。" traditional="如果你需要我的建議，我很願意告訴你。" pinyin="rúguǒ nǐ xūyào wǒ de jiànyì， wǒ hěn yuànyì gàosu nǐ。" jyutping="jyu4 gwo2 nei5 seoi1 jiu3 ngo5 dik1 gin3 ji5， ngo5 han2 jyun6 ji3 hou2 ji3 tung3 nei5。" english="If you need my advice, I’d be glad to share it with you." />
