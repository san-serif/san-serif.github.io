Your friend says they’ll finish the report tomorrow. You remind them that they promised the same thing yesterday. Put 就 before the time or detail you want to stress: it can mean “as soon as,” “already by,” or “just/exactly.”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你昨天就说过这句话了！" traditional="你昨天就說過這句話了！" pinyin="nǐ zuótiān jiù shuō guo zhè jù huà le!" jyutping="nei5 zok3 tin1 zau6 syut3 gwo3 ze5 geoi3 waa6 liu5!" english="You said that just yesterday!" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我一到家就给你打电话。" traditional="我一到家就給你打電話。" pinyin="wǒ yí dào jiā jiù gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà." jyutping="ngo5 jat1 dou3 gaa1 zau6 kap1 nei5 daa2 din6 waa6." english="I’ll call you as soon as I get home." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这就是我昨天买的那本书。" traditional="這就是我昨天買的那本書。" pinyin="zhè jiù shì wǒ zuótiān mǎi de nà běn shū." jyutping="ze5 zau6 si6 ngo5 zok3 tin1 maai5 dik1 naa5 bun2 syu1." english="This is exactly the book I bought yesterday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他五岁就会游泳了。" traditional="他五歲就會游泳了。" pinyin="tā wǔ suì jiù huì yóuyǒng le." jyutping="taa1 ng5 seoi3 zau6 wui5 jau4 wing2 liu5." english="He could already swim at five." />

<Hint type="hint">就 points to something earlier, sooner, or more exact than expected. The sentence pattern 一……就…… means “as soon as…”. 是……的 can also put focus on a specific detail, such as the book you bought yesterday.</Hint>
