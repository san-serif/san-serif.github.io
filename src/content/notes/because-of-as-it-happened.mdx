A road closure causes delays. A new bridge then changes how people travel. Formal reports can name the cause with (으)로 인해서; -(으)면서 can show a change that accompanies or brings another change.

### A Cause in a Report

Attach 로 인해서 after a vowel or ㄹ, 으로 인해서 after another consonant: 사고로 인해서, 공사로 인해서. It is common in reports and formal explanations. (으)로 인해 is a shorter version; (으)로 인한 modifies a noun.

<SentenceBlock formal="도로 공사로 인해서 버스가 늦었습니다." formalRom="doro gongsaro inhaeseo beoseuga neujeotseumnida." polite="도로 공사로 인해서 버스가 늦었어요." politeRom="doro gongsaro inhaeseo beoseuga neujeosseoyo." casual="도로 공사로 인해서 버스가 늦었어." casualRom="doro gongsaro inhaeseo beoseuga neujeosseo." english="The bus was late because of roadworks." />

<SentenceBlock formal="폭우로 인한 피해가 컸습니다." formalRom="poguro inhan pihaega keotseumnida." polite="폭우로 인한 피해가 컸어요." politeRom="poguro inhan pihaega keosseoyo." casual="폭우로 인한 피해가 컸어." casualRom="poguro inhan pihaega keosseo." english="The damage caused by the heavy rain was extensive." />

### One Development Changes the Situation

Beyond [two simultaneous actions](/notes/while), -(으)면서 can introduce an event or change that leads into a new situation: 다리가 생기면서…. Use -면서 after a vowel or ㄹ and -으면서 after another consonant. The clauses can have different subjects in this use.

<SentenceBlock formal="새 다리가 생기면서 교통이 편리해졌습니다." formalRom="sae dariga saenggimyeonseo gyotongi pyeollihaejyeotseumnida." polite="새 다리가 생기면서 교통이 편리해졌어요." politeRom="sae dariga saenggimyeonseo gyotongi pyeollihaejyeosseoyo." casual="새 다리가 생기면서 교통이 편리해졌어." casualRom="sae dariga saenggimyeonseo gyotongi pyeollihaejyeosseo." english="Transport became more convenient when the new bridge was built." />

<SentenceBlock formal="날씨가 추워지면서 감기 환자가 늘었습니다." formalRom="nalssiga chuwojimyeonseo gamgi hwanjaga neureotseumnida." polite="날씨가 추워지면서 감기 환자가 늘었어요." politeRom="nalssiga chuwojimyeonseo gamgi hwanjaga neureosseoyo." casual="날씨가 추워지면서 감기 환자가 늘었어." casualRom="nalssiga chuwojimyeonseo gamgi hwanjaga neureosseo." english="As the weather grew colder, the number of cold patients increased." />

<Hint type="hint">In 교통이 편리해지면서 방문객이 늘었어요, the transport change sets up the increase in visitors. This does not require one person to be doing both things at the same moment.</Hint>
