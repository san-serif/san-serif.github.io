I Like It, I Don't only covered 안, the negative for personal choice. Korean has a second one, 못, for things you're physically or circumstantially unable to do. Same position in the sentence, right before the verb, completely different meaning behind it.

<SentenceBlock formal="저는 우유를 안 마십니다." formalRom="jeoneun uyureul an masimnida." polite="저는 우유를 안 마셔요." politeRom="jeoneun uyureul an masyeoyo." casual="나는 우유를 안 마셔." casualRom="naneun uyureul an masyeo." english="I don't drink milk." />

<SentenceBlock formal="저는 우유를 못 마십니다." formalRom="jeoneun uyureul mot masimnida." polite="저는 우유를 못 마셔요." politeRom="jeoneun uyureul mot masyeoyo." casual="나는 우유를 못 마셔." casualRom="naneun uyureul mot masyeo." english="I can't drink milk." />

<Hint>안 마셔요 sounds like preference, you just don't drink milk. 못 마셔요 signals an obstacle: you're lactose intolerant, or it makes you sick. Say 못 먹어요 about a food you simply dislike and it can land strangely, like you're claiming a physical inability you don't actually have.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="저는 수영을 못 합니다." formalRom="jeoneun suyeongeul mot hamnida." polite="저는 수영을 못 해요." politeRom="jeoneun suyeongeul mot haeyo." casual="나는 수영을 못 해." casualRom="naneun suyeongeul mot hae." english="I can't swim." />

<SentenceBlock formal="떡볶이를 못 먹습니다." formalRom="tteokbokkireul mot meokseumnida." polite="떡볶이를 못 먹어요." politeRom="tteokbokkireul mot meogeoyo." casual="떡볶이를 못 먹어." casualRom="tteokbokkireul mot meogeo." english="I can't eat tteokbokki (too spicy)." />

<SentenceBlock formal="표가 없어서 콘서트에 못 갑니다." formalRom="pyoga eopseoseo konseoteue mot gamnida." polite="표가 없어서 콘서트에 못 가요." politeRom="pyoga eopseoseo konseoteue mot gayo." casual="표가 없어서 콘서트에 못 가." casualRom="pyoga eopseoseo konseoteue mot ga." english="I can't go to the concert, there are no tickets." />

<Hint>없어서 in that last sentence means "because there are none," built on 없다 plus a reason ending, -아서/-어서. It's not this lesson's grammar point, that gets its own lesson later, but it's common enough that it's worth recognizing on sight before then.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="오늘 친구를 안 만납니다." formalRom="oneul chingureul an mannamnida." polite="오늘 친구를 안 만나요." politeRom="oneul chingureul an mannayo." casual="오늘 친구를 안 만나." casualRom="oneul chingureul an manna." english="I'm not meeting a friend today." />

<SentenceBlock formal="지금 전화를 안 합니다." formalRom="jigeum jeonhwareul an hamnida." polite="지금 전화를 안 해요." politeRom="jigeum jeonhwareul an haeyo." casual="지금 전화를 안 해." casualRom="jigeum jeonhwareul an hae." english="I'm not calling right now." />
