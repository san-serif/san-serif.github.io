The notice says the results will be announced Friday; a review cites the evidence behind its recommendation. 将 is a formal written way to put an object before its verb; 凭 and 依据 introduce the basis someone relies on. Their neighbors tell you whether the phrase is being acted on or justifying an action.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="三月十五日将是我上学的最后一天。" traditional="三月十五日將是我上學的最後一天。" pinyin="sān yuè shíwǔ rì jiāng shì wǒ shàngxué de zuìhòu yī tiān。" jyutping="saam1 jyut6 sap6 ng5 jat6 zoeng1 si6 ngo5 soeng5 hok6 dik1 zeoi3 hau6 jat1 tin1。" english="March 15 will be my last day at school." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这个结论有依据。" traditional="這個結論有依據。" pinyin="zhège jiélùn yǒu yījù." jyutping="ze2 go3 git3 leon6 jau5 ji1 geoi3." english="This conclusion is supported by evidence." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="单凭力量不能保证取胜。" traditional="單憑力量不能保證取勝。" pinyin="dān píng lìliang bùnéng bǎozhèng qǔshèng。" jyutping="daan1 pang4 lik6 loeng6 bat1 nang4 bou2 zing3 ceoi2 sing3。" english="Strength alone cannot guarantee victory." />
