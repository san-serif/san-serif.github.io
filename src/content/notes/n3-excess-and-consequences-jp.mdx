あまり and あまりに mark an excessive degree that can lead to a result. ばかりだ can describe a one-way trend that keeps progressing, or in another context an action that has just happened. ところが introduces an outcome that goes against expectation. ことはない says there is no need to do something. Watch the surrounding grammar: these forms share broad translations but serve different roles.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="急[いそ]いだあまり、財布[さいふ]を忘[わす]れてしまいました。" romaji="Isoida amari, saifu o wasurete shimaimashita." english="I was in such a hurry that I forgot my wallet." />

Verb past form + あまり, or noun + のあまり, presents an extreme state as the cause of a result. あまりに + adjective or verb similarly means “so ... that,” emphasizing the degree.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="毎日[まいにち]練習[れんしゅう]した。ところが、本番[ほんばん]では緊張[きんちょう]してしまった。" romaji="Mainichi renshuu shita. Tokoroga, honban de wa kinchou shite shimatta." english="I practiced every day. But on the day, I got nervous." />

ところが signals a surprising or contrary result. It often begins a new sentence and carries a clearer reversal than a neutral でも.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="物価[ぶっか]は上[あ]がるばかりです。" romaji="Bukka wa agaru bakari desu." english="Prices keep rising." />

Verb dictionary form + ばかりだ describes a continuing one-direction trend. This is distinct from Vたばかり, which means an action happened recently.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="一人[ひとり]で悩[なや]むことはありません。相談[そうだん]してください。" romaji="Hitori de nayamu koto wa arimasen. Soudan shite kudasai." english="You don't need to worry about it alone. Please talk to someone." />

Verb dictionary form + ことはない reassures someone that an action is unnecessary. It is different from ことがない, which describes something that does not happen or has never happened.

<Hint type="insight">A speaker may use ことはない to reduce pressure: 急ぐことはないよ, “there's no need to rush.” ところが is useful in stories when the next event overturns what the listener expects.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Excessive cause: あまり / あまりに. Unexpected reversal: ところが. No need to do: Vることはない. Ongoing one-way trend: Vてばかりだ / ばかりだ, depending on the construction.</Hint>
