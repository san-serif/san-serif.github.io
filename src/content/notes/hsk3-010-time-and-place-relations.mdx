“I haven’t seen you since you moved.” 自从搬家以后 gives the move as the starting point, then the rest of the sentence follows the change from there. Use 自从 when the past event still matters to the situation now.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="自从我开始学汉语以来已经有两年了。" traditional="自從我開始學漢語以來已經有兩年了。" pinyin="zìcóng wǒ kāishǐ xué Hànyǔ yǐlái yǐjīng yǒu liǎng nián le。" jyutping="zi6 cung4 ngo5 hoi1 ci2 hok6 hon3 jyu5 ji5 loi4 ji5 ging1 jau5 loeng5 nin4 liu5。" english="It's been two years since I started learning Chinese." />
