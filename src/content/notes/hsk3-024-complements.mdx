Your friend asks when you graduated. You can answer with the year, or tell them how long ago it was: “I graduated three years ago.” In Chinese, put the time interval after the event, then add 了 to show that this much time has now passed.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我毕业三年了。" traditional="我畢業三年了。" pinyin="wǒ bìyè sān nián le." jyutping="ngo5 bat1 jip6 saam1 nin4 liu5." english="I graduated three years ago." />

The same pattern works with other changes or events. The event itself comes first; the interval tells us how long it has been since then.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他搬来这里两个月了。" traditional="他搬來這裡兩個月了。" pinyin="tā bān lái zhèlǐ liǎng ge yuè le." jyutping="taa1 bun1 loi4 ze5 leoi5 loeng5 go3 jyut6 liu5." english="He moved here two months ago." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们认识十年了。" traditional="我們認識十年了。" pinyin="wǒmen rènshi shí nián le." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 jing6 sik1 sap6 nin4 liu5." english="We’ve known each other for ten years." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家店开了半年了。" traditional="這家店開了半年了。" pinyin="zhè jiā diàn kāi le bàn nián le." jyutping="ze5 gaa1 dim3 hoi1 liu5 bun3 nin4 liu5." english="This shop has been open for half a year." />

<Hint type="hint">This pattern looks back from now to an earlier event. For an action that has continued for a length of time, put the duration after the verb instead: 我在这里住了三年 (“I lived here for three years”).</Hint>
