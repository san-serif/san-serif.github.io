You tell a friend to meet around eight and say the walk takes about twenty minutes. ごろ softens a point on the clock; くらい softens a duration or amount. Both mean “about,” but they attach to different kinds of measure.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="毎日[まいにち]8時[はちじ]ごろ会社[かいしゃ]に着[つ]きます。" romaji="Mainichi hachiji goro kaisha ni tsukimasu." english="I get to the office around 8 every day." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="毎日[まいにち]7時半[しちじはん]ごろ家[いえ]に帰[かえ]ります。" romaji="Mainichi shichiji han goro ie ni kaerimasu." english="I get home around 7:30 every day." />

<Hint type="hint">ごろ only pairs with a point on the clock or calendar, a specific moment you're rounding off. 8時ごろ means "around 8 o'clock," never "about 8 [of something]," that job belongs entirely to くらい.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="毎日[まいにち]2時間[にじかん]くらい宿題[しゅくだい]をしました。" romaji="Mainichi nijikan kurai shukudai o shimashita." english="I did about two hours of homework every day." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="週末[しゅうまつ]5時間[ごじかん]くらいアルバイトをしました。" romaji="Shuumatsu gojikan kurai arubaito o shimashita." english="I worked about five hours of part-time work on the weekend." />

<Hint type="hint">くらい measures a span or a count: how long something lasted, how much of something there is. 2時間くらい is "about two hours," a stretch of time, not a point on the clock, which is exactly why it can't swap with ごろ even though both translate as "about" in English.</Hint>

For broader estimates, おおよそ means “approximately.” After a count, 弱[じゃく] means slightly under that amount, while 強[きょう] means slightly over. 僅[わず]かに describes a small change or difference.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="駅[えき]まで10分[じゅっぷん]弱[じゃく]かかります。" romaji="Eki made juppun-jaku kakarimasu." english="It takes just under ten minutes to the station." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="人口[じんこう]はおおよそ50万人[ごじゅうまんにん]です。" romaji="Jinkou wa ooyoso gojuuman-nin desu." english="The population is approximately 500,000." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="売上[うりあげ]は前年[ぜんねん]より僅[わず]かに増[ふ]えました。" romaji="Uriage wa zennen yori wazuka ni fuemashita." english="Sales increased slightly from the previous year." />

<Hint type="insight">Schedules often use 10分弱 and 1時間強 for quick estimates. These are compact approximations, not exact measurements; use 約 or おおよそ when you want to give a broad estimate instead.</Hint>
