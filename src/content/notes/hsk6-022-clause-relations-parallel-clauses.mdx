The lights flicker from bright to dim; one moment you’re calm, the next you’re worried. 时而……时而…… describes alternation; 一时……一时…… can show changing states or reactions. The repeated frame makes the shift easy to hear.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他时而唱歌，时而跳舞。" traditional="他時而唱歌，時而跳舞。" pinyin="tā shí’ér chànggē, shí’ér tiàowǔ." jyutping="taa1 si4 ji4 coeng3 go1, si4 ji4 tiu3 mou5" english="He sometimes sings and sometimes dances." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我一时想不起他的名字。" traditional="我一時想不起他的名字。" pinyin="wǒ yìshí xiǎng bù qǐ tā de míngzi." jyutping="ngo5 jat1 si4 soeng2 bat1 hei2 taa1 dik1 ming4 zi6" english="For a moment, I could not remember his name." />
