You walk into the classroom and notice three new students. To introduce what has appeared, begin with the place, then give the arrival or appearance, and finally say who or what appeared. The same order works for something that has disappeared.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="教室里走进来三个新同学。" traditional="教室裡走進來三個新同學。" pinyin="jiàoshì lǐ zǒu jìnlai sān ge xīn tóngxué." jyutping="gaau3 sat1 leoi5 zau2 zeon3 loi4 saam1 go3 san1 tung4 hok6." english="Three new classmates walked into the classroom." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="门口开来了一辆出租车。" traditional="門口開來了一輛出租車。" pinyin="ménkǒu kāi lái le yí liàng chūzūchē." jyutping="mun4 hau2 hoi1 loi4 liu5 jat1 loeng6 ceot1 zou1 ce1." english="A taxi pulled up at the entrance." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="桌子上放着两杯茶，旁边还摆着一盘水果。" traditional="桌子上放著兩杯茶，旁邊還擺著一盤水果。" pinyin="zhuōzi shàng fàngzhe liǎng bēi chá, pángbiān hái bǎizhe yì pán shuǐguǒ." jyutping="zoek3 zi2 soeng6 fong3 zoek6 loeng5 bui1 caa4, pong4 bin1 waan4 baai2 zoek6 jat1 pun4 seoi2 gwo2." english="There are two cups of tea on the table and a plate of fruit beside them." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="院子里跑出去一只小狗，草地上还掉了一只鞋。" traditional="院子裡跑出去一隻小狗，草地上還掉了一隻鞋。" pinyin="yuànzi lǐ pǎo chūqù yì zhī xiǎogǒu, cǎodì shàng hái diào le yì zhī xié." jyutping="jyun6 zi2 leoi5 paau2 ceot1 heoi3 jat1 zek3 siu2 gau2, cou2 dei6 soeng6 waan4 diu6 liu5 jat1 zek3 haai4." english="A little dog ran out of the yard, and a shoe was left on the grass." />

<Hint type="hint">The place comes first because you are setting the scene. The new or missing person or thing usually comes at the end, after the verb and its result or direction.</Hint>
