You move the meeting online so everyone can join from home. Put the action first, then add 为的是 or 以便 to explain its purpose. 为的是 names the goal directly; 以便 points to the practical result it makes possible.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我把材料提前整理好，为的是让大家开会时能直接讨论。" traditional="我把材料提前整理好，為的是讓大家開會時能直接討論。" pinyin="wǒ bǎ cáiliào tíqián zhěnglǐ hǎo, wèi de shì ràng dàjiā kāihuì shí néng zhíjiē tǎolùn." jyutping="ngo5 baa2 coi4 liu6 tai4 cin4 zing2 lei5 hou2, wai6 dik1 si6 joeng6 daai6 gaa1 hoi1 wui6 si4 nang4 zik6 zip3 tou2 leon6" english="I organize the materials in advance so everyone can discuss them directly at the meeting." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她每天练习口语，以便跟客户沟通得更顺畅。" traditional="她每天練習口語，以便跟客戶溝通得更順暢。" pinyin="tā měitiān liànxí kǒuyǔ, yǐbiàn gēn kèhù gōutōng de gèng shùnchàng." jyutping="taa1 mui5 tin1 lin6 zaap6 hau2 jyu5, ji5 bin6 gan1 haak3 wu6 kau1 tung1 dak1 gang3 seon6 coeng3" english="She practices speaking every day so she can communicate more smoothly with clients." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="请把门关上，以便里面的人安静地工作。" traditional="請把門關上，以便裡面的人安靜地工作。" pinyin="qǐng bǎ mén guānshàng, yǐbiàn lǐmiàn de rén ānjìng de gōngzuò." jyutping="cing2 baa2 mun4 gwaan1 soeng5, ji5 bin6 leoi5 min6 dik1 jan4 on1 zing6 dei6 gung1 zok3" english="Please close the door so the people inside can work quietly." />

<Hint type="hint">The purpose clause must describe the goal of the first action. If it states a result that happened by chance, use a result connector instead.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">以便 often appears in plans, instructions, and work messages because it links an action to a practical next step.</Hint>
