On a formal notice, you might read 自去年起, “starting last year.” 自 sets a point of departure in time or place, and the phrase after it tells the reader where the statement begins. It’s more at home in writing than in casual conversation.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他独自来了。" traditional="他獨自來了。" pinyin="tā dúzì lái le." jyutping="taa1 duk6 zi6 loi4 liu5." english="He came alone." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="自那时以来。" traditional="自那時以來。" pinyin="zì nàshí yǐlái." jyutping="zi6 naa5 si4 ji5 loi4." english="Since that time." />
