“Regarding the budget, we need one more estimate.” 对于 frames what you’re evaluating; 关于 introduces the subject of a discussion or text; 替 puts you in someone else’s place as you do them a favor. The small word tells the listener how the next idea is connected.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="对于自己的工资，我感到挺满足。" traditional="對於自己的工資，我感到挺滿足。" pinyin="duìyú zìjǐ de gōngzī， wǒ gǎndào tǐng mǎnzú。" jyutping="deoi3 jyu1 zi6 gei2 dik1 gung1 zi1， ngo5 gam2 dou3 ting5 mun5 zuk1。" english="I'm content with my salary." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="关于这件事，我真的不知道。" traditional="關於這件事，我真的不知道。" pinyin="guānyú zhè jiàn shì， wǒ zhēn de bù zhīdào。" jyutping="gwaan1 jyu1 ze2 gin6 si6， ngo5 zan1 dik1 bat1 zi1 dou6。" english="As for this matter, I really don’t know." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我没有办法替你做这个决定。" traditional="我沒有辦法替你做這個決定。" pinyin="wǒ méiyǒu bànfǎ tì nǐ zuò zhège juédìng。" jyutping="ngo5 mut6 jau5 baan6 faat3 tai3 nei5 zou6 ze2 go3 kyut3 ding6。" english="I can't make that decision for you." />
