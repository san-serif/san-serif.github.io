“Bring a jacket, or you’ll freeze on the walk home.” 要不然 and 不然 introduce the consequence that may follow if the listener ignores the advice. The warning usually feels like a real possibility, not a far-off hypothetical.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你最好现在出发，要不然就赶不上最后一班车了。" traditional="你最好現在出發，要不然就趕不上最後一班車了。" pinyin="nǐ zuìhǎo xiànzài chūfā, yàobùrán jiù gǎn bu shàng zuìhòu yì bān chē le." jyutping="nei5 zeoi3 hou2 jin6 zoi6 ceot1 faat3, jiu3 bat1 jin4 zau6 gon2 bat1 soeng5 zeoi3 hau6 jat1 baan1 ce1 liu5" english="You’d better leave now, or you’ll miss the last bus." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="记得带上雨伞，不然淋湿了会很麻烦。" traditional="記得帶上雨傘，不然淋濕了會很麻煩。" pinyin="jìde dài shàng yǔsǎn, bùrán línshī le huì hěn máfan." jyutping="gei3 dak1 daai3 soeng5 jyu5 saan3, bat1 jin4 lam4 sap1 liu5 wui5 han2 maa4 faan4" english="Remember to bring an umbrella, or getting soaked will be a real hassle." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们得早点儿走，要不然会迟到。" traditional="我們得早點兒走，要不然會遲到。" pinyin="wǒmen děi zǎodiǎnr zǒu, yàobùrán huì chídào." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 dak1 zou2 dim2 ji4 zau2, jiu3 bat1 jin4 wui5 ci4 dou3" english="We need to leave early, or we’ll be late." />

<Hint type="hint">The second clause gives the likely consequence of ignoring the advice. Don’t use 不然 when you only want to introduce a neutral alternative.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">A quick reminder followed by 要不然 or 不然 is common in everyday warnings: leave now, bring something, or do it before a deadline.</Hint>
