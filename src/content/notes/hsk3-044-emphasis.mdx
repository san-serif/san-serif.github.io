“I’m not tired at all.” “Didn’t we agree to meet at seven?” “It was Mei who called.” Chinese can make a negative absolute, use a rhetorical question to nudge the listener, or focus one detail with 是…的. Each pattern gives emphasis a different target.

### Make a Negative Absolute

一点儿也不 means “not at all.” Put it before the quality or action being denied.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这道题一点儿也不难。" traditional="這道題一點兒也不難。" pinyin="zhè dào tí yìdiǎnr yě bù nán." jyutping="ze2 dou6 tai4 jat1 dim2 ji4 jaa5 bat1 naan4" english="This question isn’t difficult at all." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我一点儿也不喜欢喝咖啡。" traditional="我一點兒也不喜歡喝咖啡。" pinyin="wǒ yìdiǎnr yě bù xǐhuan hē kāfēi." jyutping="ngo5 jat1 dim2 ji4 jaa5 bat1 hei2 fun1 hot3 gaa3 fe1" english="I don’t like coffee at all." />

<Hint type="hint">Put 不 immediately before the word being denied: 一点儿也不难, 一点儿也不喜欢.</Hint>

### Turn a Reminder Into a Question

不是…吗 and 难道…吗 are questions in form, but the speaker usually expects agreement rather than new information.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你不是已经看过这部电影了吗？" traditional="你不是已經看過這部電影了嗎？" pinyin="nǐ bú shì yǐjīng kànguò zhè bù diànyǐng le ma?" jyutping="nei5 bat1 si6 ji5 ging1 hon3 gwo3 ze2 bou6 din6 jing2 liu5 maa3" english="Haven’t you already seen this movie?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="难道他不知道这件事吗？" traditional="難道他不知道這件事嗎？" pinyin="nándào tā bù zhīdào zhè jiàn shì ma?" jyutping="naan4 dou6 taa1 bat1 zi1 dou6 ze2 gin6 si6 maa3" english="Doesn’t he know about this? Surely he does." />

<Hint type="hint">A rhetorical question can sound reproachful. Use it when the listener is expected to know or agree with the point.</Hint>

### Focus a Detail

Use 是…的 to stress a detail such as who, when, or how an event happened.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这本书是我昨天买的。" traditional="這本書是我昨天買的。" pinyin="zhè běn shū shì wǒ zuótiān mǎi de." jyutping="ze2 bun2 syu1 si6 ngo5 zok3 tin1 maai5 dik1" english="I bought this book yesterday (that is the detail being emphasized)." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我是坐火车来的。" traditional="我是坐火車來的。" pinyin="wǒ shì zuò huǒchē lái de." jyutping="ngo5 si6 co5 fo2 ce1 loi4 dik1" english="I came by train (that is the detail being emphasized)." />

<Hint type="hint">In 是…的 sentences, the event is usually already known; 是…的 points to the detail that answers the current question.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">Use these forms to correct, reassure, or highlight a detail. The context and intonation tell the listener whether the emphasis sounds firm, surprised, or corrective.</Hint>
