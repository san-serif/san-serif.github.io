Your friend hears some surprising news, searches for an address, and then asks you for help. Chinese can place two verb phrases together to show a cause and its result, a contrast, or a condition. Let the relationship between the actions guide the listener.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="听了这个消息，他高兴得跳了起来。" traditional="聽了這個消息，他高興得跳了起來。" pinyin="tīng le zhège xiāoxi, tā gāoxìng de tiào le qǐlai." jyutping="teng1 liu5 ze5 go3 siu1 sik1, taa1 gou1 hing3 dak1 tiu3 liu5 hei2 loi4." english="Hearing the news, he was so happy that he jumped up." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我找了半天，还是没找到那家店。" traditional="我找了半天，還是沒找到那家店。" pinyin="wǒ zhǎo le bàntiān, háishi méi zhǎodào nà jiā diàn." jyutping="ngo5 zaau2 liu5 bun3 tin1, waan4 si6 mut6 zaau2 dou3 naa5 gaa1 dim3." english="I searched for ages, but still couldn’t find that shop." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你有时间就来我家坐坐，我们一起吃饭。" traditional="你有時間就來我家坐坐，我們一起吃飯。" pinyin="nǐ yǒu shíjiān jiù lái wǒ jiā zuòzuo, wǒmen yìqǐ chīfàn." jyutping="nei5 jau5 si4 gaan3 zau6 loi4 ngo5 gaa1 co5 co5, ngo5 mun4 jat1 hei2 hek3 faan6." english="If you have time, come by my place and have a meal with us." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她站起来走到窗边，打开窗户透透气。" traditional="她站起來走到窗邊，打開窗戶透透氣。" pinyin="tā zhàn qǐlai zǒu dào chuāngbiān, dǎkāi chuānghu tou tou qì." jyutping="taa1 zaam6 hei2 loi4 zau2 dou3 coeng1 bin1, daa2 hoi1 coeng1 wu6 tau3 tau3 hei3." english="She stood up, walked to the window, and opened it for some fresh air." />

<Hint type="hint">The first verb phrase sets up the next one: hearing brings a reaction, searching fails to find the place, and having time makes the visit possible. Keep the same subject through a serial verb sequence unless the sentence gives a clear reason to change it.</Hint>
