You finally get a day off, and 偏偏下起雨来了 (“it just had to rain”). 难怪 links a result to its explanation; 索性 shows the decision you make next—stay home and enjoy the day anyway. One explains, one marks the unlucky turn, and one moves the story along.

### Understand Why It Happened

难怪 means “no wonder.” Use it when a new detail explains a result that seemed puzzling a moment ago.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="难怪他这么累。" traditional="難怪他這麼累。" pinyin="Nánguài tā zhème lèi." jyutping="naan4 gwaai3 taa1 ze2 mo1 leoi6." english="No wonder he is so tired." />

<Hint type="hint">难怪 and 怪不得 both introduce an explanation. This lesson uses 难怪; see [No Wonder](/notes/hsk79-007-manner-stance-and-linking-adverbs) for 怪不得.</Hint>

### Mark the Unwelcome Coincidence

偏偏 highlights that something happened at exactly the wrong time or against someone’s hopes. It often appears before the event that causes the frustration.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我想去的时候，偏偏下雨了。" traditional="我想去的時候，偏偏下雨了。" pinyin="wǒ xiǎng qù de shíhou, piānpiān xiàyǔ le." jyutping="ngo5 soeng2 heoi3 dik1 si4 hau6, pin1 pin1 haa6 jyu5 liu5." english="Just when I wanted to go, it rained (contrary to my wish)." />

<Hint type="hint">偏偏 reflects the speaker’s perspective: the event clashes with what they wanted or expected.</Hint>

### Make a Decisive Choice

索性 introduces a clear decision after considering the situation: “might as well” or “just go ahead and…”.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="雨下得太大，索性不去了。" traditional="雨下得太大，索性不去了。" pinyin="yǔ xià de tài dà, suǒxìng bú qù le." jyutping="jyu5 haa6 dak1 taai3 daai6, sok3 sing3 bat1 heoi3 liu5." english="The rain is too heavy, might as well not go." />

<Hint type="hint">索性 usually comes before the chosen action. It suggests settling on that option instead of hesitating.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">A useful sequence is: explain the situation with 难怪, name the frustrating coincidence with 偏偏, then use 索性 for the decision that follows.</Hint>
