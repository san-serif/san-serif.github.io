“他不是不愿意帮忙。也就是说，他只是现在抽不开身。” 也就是说 restates an idea in clearer or more specific terms. Use it to unpack a point, rather than to add a new reason.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他没来，也就是说他放弃了这次机会。" traditional="他沒來，也就是說他放棄了這次機會。" pinyin="tā méi lái, yějiùshìshuō tā fàngqì le zhè cì jīhuì." jyutping="taa1 mut6 loi4, jaa5 zau6 si6 syut3 taa1 fong3 hei3 liu5 ze2 ci3 gei1 wui6." english="He didn't come, which means he gave up this opportunity." />
