[에게](/notes/just-so-you-know) (and its conversational twin 한테) marks who's receiving an action, "to [someone]." Talking about an elder or someone senior swaps it for 께, and the verb itself often changes too: 주다 (to give) becomes 드리다.

<SentenceBlock formal="친구에게 선물을 줍니다." formalRom="chinguege seonmureul jumnida." polite="친구에게 선물을 줘요." politeRom="chinguege seonmureul jwoyo." casual="친구에게 선물을 줘." casualRom="chinguege seonmureul jwo." english="I give a gift to a friend." />

<Hint>에게 and 한테 mean exactly the same thing. 한테 is what you'll actually hear in conversation; 에게 shows up more in writing. It's the same written-versus-spoken split as [하고](/notes/with-a-friend) versus 와/과.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="동생한테 전화합니다." formalRom="dongsaenghante jeonhwahamnida." polite="동생한테 전화해요." politeRom="dongsaenghante jeonhwahaeyo." casual="동생한테 전화해." casualRom="dongsaenghante jeonhwahae." english="I call my younger sibling." />

<SentenceBlock formal="부모님께 선물을 드렸습니다." formalRom="bumonimkke seonmureul deuryeotseumnida." polite="부모님께 선물을 드렸어요." politeRom="bumonimkke seonmureul deuryeosseoyo." casual="부모님께 선물을 드렸어." casualRom="bumonimkke seonmureul deuryeosseo." english="I gave a gift to my parents." />

<SentenceBlock formal="선생님께 질문을 합니다." formalRom="seonsaengnimkke jilmuneul hamnida." polite="선생님께 질문을 해요." politeRom="seonsaengnimkke jilmuneul haeyo." casual="선생님께 질문을 해." casualRom="seonsaengnimkke jilmuneul hae." english="I ask the teacher a question." />

<Hint>께 and 드리다 travel together. Pairing 께 with plain 주다 instead of 드리다, or the reverse, sounds mismatched, like starting a sentence formally and finishing it casually. Elevate the whole verb phrase, not just the particle.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="안나 씨가 재민 씨에게 편지를 썼습니다." formalRom="anna ssiga jaemin ssiege pyeonjireul sseotseumnida." polite="안나 씨가 재민 씨에게 편지를 썼어요." politeRom="anna ssiga jaemin ssiege pyeonjireul sseosseoyo." casual="안나 씨가 재민 씨에게 편지를 썼어." casualRom="anna ssiga jaemin ssiege pyeonjireul sseosseo." english="Anna wrote a letter to Jaemin." />

<SentenceBlock formal="할머니께 전화드립니다." formalRom="halmeonikke jeonhwadeurimnida." polite="할머니께 전화드려요." politeRom="halmeonikke jeonhwadeuryeoyo." casual="할머니께 전화드려." casualRom="halmeonikke jeonhwadeuryeo." english="I call my grandmother." />
