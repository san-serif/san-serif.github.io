The report lists staff, equipment, and supplies; 及 joins a formal written list. 继而 moves the account to the next step. 要不是及时处理，损失会更大 (“if it hadn’t been handled in time, the damage would have been worse”). These linkers connect a list, a sequence, and a counterfactual explanation in different registers.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这个问题涉及你、我及他。" traditional="這個問題涉及你、我及他。" pinyin="zhège wèntí shèjí nǐ, wǒ jí tā." jyutping="ze2 go3 man6 tai4 sip3 kap6 nei5, ngo5 kap6 taa1." english="This issue involves you, me, and him." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="要不是你帮忙，我肯定完成不了这个任务。" traditional="要不是你幫忙，我肯定完成不了這個任務。" pinyin="yàobúshì nǐ bāngmáng， wǒ kěndìng wán bùchéng zhège rènwu。" jyutping="jiu3 bat1 si6 nei5 bong1 mong4， ngo5 hang2 ding6 jyun4 bat1 sing4 ze2 go3 jam6 mou6。" english="If it weren't for your help, I definitely couldn't have finished this task." />
