A snowy town looks as though it belongs in a painting. The streets are covered, and voices can barely be heard. One pattern gives the comparison; passive verbs shift attention to what is seen, heard, or affected.

### As Though Something Were True

Use -는 듯이 for an ongoing action, -(으)ㄴ 듯이 for a completed action or present adjective, and -(으)ㄹ 듯이 for an anticipated event: 아는 듯이, 놀란 듯이, 울 듯이. Nouns take -인 듯이. The comparison need not be literally true.

<SentenceBlock formal="아이는 다 아는 듯이 고개를 끄덕였습니다." formalRom="aineun da aneun deusi gogaereul kkeudeogyeotseumnida." polite="아이는 다 아는 듯이 고개를 끄덕였어요." politeRom="aineun da aneun deusi gogaereul kkeudeogyeosseoyo." casual="아이는 다 아는 듯이 고개를 끄덕였어." casualRom="aineun da aneun deusi gogaereul kkeudeogyeosseo." english="The child nodded as though they knew everything." />

<SentenceBlock formal="곧 비가 올 듯이 하늘이 어두워졌습니다." formalRom="got biga ol deusi haneuri eoduwojyeotseumnida." polite="곧 비가 올 듯이 하늘이 어두워졌어요." politeRom="got biga ol deusi haneuri eoduwojyeosseoyo." casual="곧 비가 올 듯이 하늘이 어두워졌어." casualRom="got biga ol deusi haneuri eoduwojyeosseo." english="The sky grew dark as though it would rain soon." />

### What Is Seen, Heard, or Affected

Some verbs form a passive with -이-, -히-, -리-, or -기-: 보다 → 보이다, 닫다 → 닫히다, 듣다 → 들리다, 안다 → 안기다. Learn the passive with its base verb; you cannot reliably choose the suffix from the final consonant alone. The affected thing becomes the subject.

<SentenceBlock formal="창문이 닫혀 있어서 밖의 소리가 잘 안 들립니다." formalRom="changmuni dachyeo isseoseo bakkui soriga jal an deullimnida." polite="창문이 닫혀 있어서 밖의 소리가 잘 안 들려요." politeRom="changmuni dachyeo isseoseo bakkui soriga jal an deullyeoyo." casual="창문이 닫혀 있어서 밖의 소리가 잘 안 들려." casualRom="changmuni dachyeo isseoseo bakkui soriga jal an deullyeo." english="The window is closed, so I can’t hear outside sounds well." />

<SentenceBlock formal="멀리 눈 덮인 산이 보입니다." formalRom="meolli nun deopin sani boimnida." polite="멀리 눈 덮인 산이 보여요." politeRom="meolli nun deopin sani boyeoyo." casual="멀리 눈 덮인 산이 보여." casualRom="meolli nun deopin sani boyeo." english="A snow-covered mountain is visible in the distance." />

<Hint type="hint">[-아/어 보이다](/notes/you-look-well) in 피곤해 보여요 means “look tired.” The passive verb 보이다 in 산이 보여요 means the mountain is visible. The same written verb participates in different constructions.</Hint>
