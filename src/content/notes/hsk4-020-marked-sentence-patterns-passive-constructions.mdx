The lights were left on, the bill was paid, the tickets were sold. When the result matters more than the person who caused it, start with the affected thing and use 被. Name the doer after 被 only when that detail matters. 被迫 is a fixed word meaning “be forced to.”







<CSentenceBlock simplified="她被迫和他结婚了。" traditional="她被迫和他結婚了。" pinyin="tā bèipò hé tā jiéhūn le。" jyutping="taa1 bei6 bik1 wo4 taa1 git3 fan1 liu5。" english="She was forced to marry him." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她告诉我们道路被封闭了。" traditional="她告訴我們道路被封閉了。" pinyin="tā gàosu wǒmen dàolù bèi fēngbì le。" jyutping="taa1 gou3 sou3 ngo5 mun4 dou6 lou6 bei6 fung1 bai3 liu5。" english="She told us the road had been closed." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她忘记关灯，被妈妈批评了。" traditional="她忘記關燈，被媽媽批評了。" pinyin="tā wàngjì guān dēng， bèi māma pīpíng le。" jyutping="taa1 mong4 gei3 gwaan1 dang1， bei6 maa1 maa1 pai1 ping4 liu5。" english="Her mother scolded her for forgetting to turn off the light." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这种错误很容易被人忽视。" traditional="這種錯誤很容易被人忽視。" pinyin="zhèzhǒng cuòwù hěn róngyì bèi rén hūshì。" jyutping="ze2 zung2 co3 ng6 han2 jung4 ji6 bei6 jan4 fat1 si6。" english="Mistakes like these are easily overlooked." />
