You are busy, but you still want to help your teacher carry some books. -아/어도 says the result holds despite a condition. -아/어 드리다 describes doing the favour for someone you honour.

### Despite That Condition

Add 도 to the -아/어 form: 바빠도, 먹어도, 해도. It can mean even if for a hypothetical condition or even though for a known fact. 아무리 strengthens the idea: 아무리 바빠도, “however busy I am.” Nouns take 이어도/여도.

<SentenceBlock formal="아무리 바빠도 아침은 먹습니다." formalRom="amuri bappado achimeun meokseumnida." polite="아무리 바빠도 아침은 먹어요." politeRom="amuri bappado achimeun meogeoyo." casual="아무리 바빠도 아침은 먹어." casualRom="amuri bappado achimeun meogeo." english="However busy I am, I eat breakfast." />

<SentenceBlock formal="비가 와도 행사에 갈 것입니다." formalRom="biga wado haengsae gal geosimnida." polite="비가 와도 행사에 갈 거예요." politeRom="biga wado haengsae gal geoyeyo." casual="비가 와도 행사에 갈 거야." casualRom="biga wado haengsae gal geoya." english="Even if it rains, I’ll go to the event." />

### A Favour for Someone You Honour

Use the -아/어 form plus 드리다 instead of [-아/어 주다](/notes/on-the-way-helping-out) when the beneficiary is someone you honour: 읽어 드리다, 보내 드리다. 께 can mark that person. The subject is the helper, so you do not add -(으)시- simply to honour yourself.

<SentenceBlock formal="선생님께 책을 가져다 드렸습니다." formalRom="seonsaengnimkke chaegeul gajyeoda deuryeotseumnida." polite="선생님께 책을 가져다 드렸어요." politeRom="seonsaengnimkke chaegeul gajyeoda deuryeosseoyo." casual="선생님께 책을 가져다 드렸어." casualRom="seonsaengnimkke chaegeul gajyeoda deuryeosseo." english="I took the books to my teacher." />

<SentenceBlock formal="제가 사용 방법을 설명해 드리겠습니다." formalRom="jega sayong bangbeobeul seolmyeonghae deurigetseumnida." polite="제가 사용 방법을 설명해 드릴게요." politeRom="jega sayong bangbeobeul seolmyeonghae deurilgeyo." casual="내가 사용 방법을 설명해 드릴게." casualRom="naega sayong bangbeobeul seolmyeonghae deurilge." english="I’ll explain how to use it for you." />

<Hint type="hint">드리다 honours the recipient of the favour. -(으)시- honours the subject doing an action. If your grandmother makes something for you, 할머니가 만들어 주셨어요 honours her without treating you as the honoured beneficiary.</Hint>
