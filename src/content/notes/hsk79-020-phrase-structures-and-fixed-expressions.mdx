火车晚点了，这样一来，我们赶不上下一班车了 (“the train is late, so we’ll miss the connection”). When you’ve finished explaining, 综上所述 or 总的来说 can gather the points into a summary; 综上所述 sounds more formal on the page.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="要是大家都同意这个方案，这样一来，问题就容易解决了。" traditional="要是大家都同意這個方案，這樣一來，問題就容易解決了。" pinyin="yàoshi dàjiā dōu tóngyì zhège fāng'àn, zhèyàng yīlái, wèntí jiù róngyì jiějué le。" jyutping="jiu3 si6 daai6 gaa1 dou1 tung4 ji3 ze5 go3 fong1 on3, ze5 joeng6 jat1 loi4, man6 tai4 zau6 jung4 ji6 gaai2 kyut3 liu5。" english="If everyone agrees to this plan, then the problem will be easily solved." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="综上所述，这个项目具有很高的可行性，建议公司立即立项开发。" traditional="綜上所述，這個項目具有很高的可行性，建議公司立即立項開發。" pinyin="zōngshàngsuǒshù， zhège xiàngmù jùyǒu hěn gāo de kěxíngxìng， jiànyì gōngsī lìjí lìxiàng kāifā。" jyutping="zung1 soeng6 so2 seot6, ze5 go3 hong6 muk6 geoi6 jau5 han2 gou1 dik1 ho2 hang4 sing3, gin3 ji5 gung1 si1 lap6 zik6 lap6 hong6 hoi1 faat3。" english="In summary, this project has high feasibility, and it is recommended that the company immediately initiate development." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="总的来说，我对这个结果很满意。" traditional="總的來說，我對這個結果很滿意。" pinyin="zǒngdeláishuō， wǒ duì zhège jiēguǒ hěn mǎnyì。" jyutping="zung2 dik1 loi4 syut3， ngo5 deoi3 ze2 go3 git3 gwo2 han2 mun5 ji3。" english="On the whole I am satisfied with the result." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="总而言之，生命是很短的。" traditional="總而言之，生命是很短的。" pinyin="zǒngéryánzhī， shēngmìng shì hěn duǎn de。" jyutping="zung2 ji4 jin4 zi1， sang1 ming6 si6 han2 dyun2 dik1。" english="In a word, life is short." />
