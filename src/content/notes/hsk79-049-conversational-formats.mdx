话是这么说，不过我们还是得想个办法 (“That’s true, but we still need a plan”). The phrase grants the other person’s point before adding a qualification. 看/瞧你那样 turns attention to someone’s appearance or behaviour; depending on the relationship and voice, it can sound affectionate, teasing, or disapproving.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你怎么什么话也没说？" traditional="你怎麼什麼話也沒說？" pinyin="nǐ zěnme shénme huà yě méi shuō？" jyutping="nei5 zam2 mo1 sam6 mo1 waa6 jaa5 mut6 seoi3？" english="How come you didn't say anything?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他不知该不该进去，在门前走来走去。" traditional="他不知該不該進去，在門前走來走去。" pinyin="tā bùzhī gāi bù gāi jìnqù, zài ménqián zǒu lái zǒu qù." jyutping="taa1 bat1 zi1 goi1 bat1 goi1 zeon3 heoi3, zoi6 mun4 cin4 zau2 loi4 zau2 heoi3" english="He did not know whether to go in and paced back and forth in front of the door." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我在电视上看到过他，不过没有看到过他本人。" traditional="我在電視上看到過他，不過沒有看到過他本人。" pinyin="wǒ zài diànshì shàng kàndào guò tā， bùguò méiyǒu kàndào guò tā běnrén。" jyutping="ngo5 zoi6 din6 si6 soeng5 hon3 dou3 gwo3 taa1， bat1 gwo3 mut6 jau5 hon3 dou3 gwo3 taa1 bun2 jan4。" english="I have seen him on TV but not in the flesh." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你们的计划很好，可是我的更好。" traditional="你們的計劃很好，可是我的更好。" pinyin="nǐmen de jìhuà hěn hǎo， kěshì wǒ de gèng hǎo。" jyutping="nei5 mun4 dik1 gai3 waak6 han2 hou2， ho2 si6 ngo5 dik1 gang3 hou2。" english="Your plan is a good one, but mine is a better one." />
