When you’re choosing between two workable plans, put 也好 after each option: 坐地铁也好，打车也好，都能赶上. It means either option is fine. With a single option, 也好 can soften a suggestion: 先休息一下也好, “we may as well rest for a bit.”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="坐地铁也好，打车也好，我们都能准时到机场。" traditional="坐地鐵也好，打車也好，我們都能準時到機場。" pinyin="zuò dìtiě yěhǎo, dǎchē yěhǎo, wǒmen dōu néng zhǔnshí dào jīchǎng." jyutping="co5 dei6 tit3 jaa5 hou2, daa2 ce1 jaa5 hou2, ngo5 mun4 dou1 nang4 zeon2 si4 dou3 gei1 coeng4." english="Whether we take the subway or a taxi, we can still get to the airport on time." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天出发也好，明天出发也好，你先看一下天气预报。" traditional="今天出發也好，明天出發也好，你先看一下天氣預報。" pinyin="jīntiān chūfā yěhǎo, míngtiān chūfā yěhǎo, nǐ xiān kàn yíxià tiānqì yùbào." jyutping="gam1 tin1 ceot1 faat3 jaa5 hou2, ming4 tin1 ceot1 faat3 jaa5 hou2, nei5 sin1 hon3 jat1 haa5 tin1 hei3 jyu6 bou3." english="Whether you leave today or tomorrow, check the weather forecast first." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你想先吃饭也好，先休息也好，我都可以等你。" traditional="你想先吃飯也好，先休息也好，我都可以等你。" pinyin="nǐ xiǎng xiān chīfàn yěhǎo, xiān xiūxi yěhǎo, wǒ dōu kěyǐ děng nǐ." jyutping="nei5 soeng2 sin1 hek3 faan6 jaa5 hou2, sin1 jau1 sik1 jaa5 hou2, ngo5 dou1 ho2 ji5 dang2 nei5." english="Whether you want to eat first or rest first, I can wait for you." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天太晚了，先住一晚也好，明早再继续赶路。" traditional="今天太晚了，先住一晚也好，明早再繼續趕路。" pinyin="jīntiān tài wǎn le, xiān zhù yì wǎn yěhǎo, míngzǎo zài jìxù gǎnlù." jyutping="gam1 tin1 taai3 maan5 liu5, sin1 zyu6 jat1 maan5 jaa5 hou2, ming4 zou2 zoi3 gai3 zuk6 gon2 lou6." english="It’s too late today; we may as well stay one night and continue the trip tomorrow morning." />

<Hint type="hint">In the paired pattern X也好，Y也好, both choices are acceptable; 都 often introduces the shared result. In a sentence like 先休息一下也好, 也好 makes one suggestion sound like a reasonable option.</Hint>
