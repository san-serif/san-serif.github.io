The neighborhood is quiet, though a train rolls past from time to time. The shop is still open, as it has been for years, and a new one is about to open next door. 不时/偶尔 mark occasional events; 将/将要 look ahead; 仍旧、依旧, and 一向 describe what continues.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我不时地觉得难过。" traditional="我不時地覺得難過。" pinyin="wǒ bùshí de juéde nánguò。" jyutping="ngo5 bat1 si4 dei6 gok3 dak1 naan4 gwo3。" english="I feel sad every now and then." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他将要负责一个关系到公司前途的项目。" traditional="他將要負責一個關係到公司前途的項目。" pinyin="tā jiāngyào fùzé yī ge guānxì dào gōngsī qiántú de xiàngmù。" jyutping="taa1 zoeng1 jiu3 fu6 zaak3 jat1 go3 gwaan1 hai6 dou3 gung1 si1 cin4 tou4 dik1 hong6 muk6。" english="He will take charge of a project that will affect the company’s future." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我仍旧无法相信我居然做了这种事。" traditional="我仍舊無法相信我居然做了這種事。" pinyin="wǒ réngjiù wúfǎ xiāngxìn wǒ jūrán zuò le zhè zhǒng shì。" jyutping="ngo5 jing4 gau6 mou4 faat3 soeng1 seon3 ngo5 geoi1 jin4 zou6 liu5 ze5 zung2 si6。" english="I still can’t believe I did such a thing." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他时常来看我。" traditional="他時常來看我。" pinyin="tā shícháng lái kàn wǒ。" jyutping="taa1 si4 soeng4 loi4 hon3 ngo5。" english="He often comes to see me." />
