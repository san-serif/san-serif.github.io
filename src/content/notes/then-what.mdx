후 means "after," as a standalone noun. Attach -(으)ㄴ to a verb stem in front of it, vowel-ending stems take -ㄴ 후에, consonant-ending stems take -은 후에, and you've built "after doing [verb]."

<SentenceBlock formal="밥을 먹은 후에 약을 먹습니다." formalRom="babeul meogeun hue yageul meokseumnida." polite="밥을 먹은 후에 약을 먹어요." politeRom="babeul meogeun hue yageul meogeoyo." casual="밥을 먹은 후에 약을 먹어." casualRom="babeul meogeun hue yageul meogeo." english="After eating, I take medicine." />

<SentenceBlock formal="운동을 한 후에 샤워를 합니다." formalRom="undongeul han hue syaworeul hamnida." polite="운동을 한 후에 샤워를 해요." politeRom="undongeul han hue syaworeul haeyo." casual="운동을 한 후에 샤워를 해." casualRom="undongeul han hue syaworeul hae." english="After exercising, I shower." />

<Hint>This is the same -ㄴ/은 ending from [큰 가방](/notes/a-big-bag), doing a different job. There, it described a fixed trait: 큰 가방, "a big bag," permanently big. Here, attached to a verb, it marks something already completed: 먹은 (having eaten), 만난 (having met). Same shape, but on a verb it signals past, not a permanent quality.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="책을 읽은 후에 음악을 듣습니다." formalRom="chaegeul ilgeun hue eumageul deutseumnida." polite="책을 읽은 후에 음악을 들어요." politeRom="chaegeul ilgeun hue eumageul deureoyo." casual="책을 읽은 후에 음악을 들어." casualRom="chaegeul ilgeun hue eumageul deureo." english="After reading, I listen to music." />

<SentenceBlock formal="공연을 본 후에 사진을 찍습니다." formalRom="gongyeoneul bon hue sajineul jjikseumnida." polite="공연을 본 후에 사진을 찍어요." politeRom="gongyeoneul bon hue sajineul jjigeoyo." casual="공연을 본 후에 사진을 찍어." casualRom="gongyeoneul bon hue sajineul jjigeo." english="After watching the performance, I take photos." />

<SentenceBlock formal="손을 씻은 후에 밥을 먹습니다." formalRom="soneul ssiseun hue babeul meokseumnida." polite="손을 씻은 후에 밥을 먹어요." politeRom="soneul ssiseun hue babeul meogeoyo." casual="손을 씻은 후에 밥을 먹어." casualRom="soneul ssiseun hue babeul meogeo." english="After washing hands, I eat." />

<SentenceBlock formal="친구를 만난 후에 집에 갑니다." formalRom="chingureul mannan hue jibe gamnida." polite="친구를 만난 후에 집에 가요." politeRom="chingureul mannan hue jibe gayo." casual="친구를 만난 후에 집에 가." casualRom="chingureul mannan hue jibe ga." english="After meeting a friend, I go home." />

<Hint>후 works alone too, not just glued to a verb. 이 후에 ("after this") and 삼 년 후에 ("three years later") both use the same word on its own, attached straight to a noun instead of a verb clause.</Hint>
