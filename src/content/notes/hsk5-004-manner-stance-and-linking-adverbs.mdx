Someone sneaks a cookie, even though it’s obvious the plate is empty. 偷偷 describes the hidden action; 明明 points to the plain fact; 居然 marks the surprise; 毕竟 reminds the listener of what matters. These adverbs put the speaker’s angle on the story.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她偷偷把礼物放在这里。" traditional="她偷偷把禮物放在這裡。" pinyin="tā tōutōu bǎ lǐwù fàng zài zhèlǐ。" jyutping="taa1 tau1 tau1 baa2 lai5 mat6 fong3 zoi6 ze5 leoi5。" english="She secretly put the present here." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他毕竟是我的朋友。" traditional="他畢竟是我的朋友。" pinyin="tā bìjìng shì wǒ de péngyou。" jyutping="taa1 bat1 ging2 si6 ngo5 dik1 pang4 jau5。" english="After all, he’s my friend." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他不免担心。" traditional="他不免擔心。" pinyin="tā bùmiǎn dānxīn." jyutping="taa1 bat1 min5 daam1 sam1." english="He can't help but worry." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我倒是很想去看这场比赛，可是不知道有没有时间。" traditional="我倒是很想去看這場比賽，可是不知道有沒有時間。" pinyin="wǒ dàoshi hěn xiǎng qù kàn zhè chǎng bǐsài， kěshì bùzhī dào yǒuméiyǒu shíjiān。" jyutping="ngo5 dou3 si6 han2 soeng2 heoi3 hon3 ze2 coeng4 bei2 coi3， ho2 si6 bat1 zi1 dou6 jau5 mut6 jau5 si4 gaan3。" english="I do really want to watch this match, but I'm not sure if I have the time." />
