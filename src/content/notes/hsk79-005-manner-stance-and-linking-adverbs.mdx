“Why buy another umbrella?” 何必再买一把伞？ If the lights are on but no one answers, 莫非他们出门了？ 按说 gives the usual expectation—“normally, this should work.” These words let you question, guess, or measure what happened against what you expected.

### Ask Whether It Is Worth Doing

何必 often appears in a rhetorical question. The speaker already thinks the action is unnecessary, so the question challenges the reason for doing it.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="既然都知道了，何必再问？" traditional="既然都知道了，何必再問？" pinyin="jìrán dōu zhīdào le， hébì zài wèn？" jyutping="gei3 jin4 dou1 zi1 dou6 liu5, ho4 bit1 zoi3 man6?" english="Since you already know, why bother asking again?" />

<Hint type="hint">何必 is not a neutral request for information. It usually carries the view that there is no need.</Hint>

### Float a Possible Explanation

莫非 introduces a guess, often in a question: “could it be that…?” The speaker has noticed a clue but is not certain.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="莫非他忘了？" traditional="莫非他忘了？" pinyin="mòfēi tā wàng le?" jyutping="mok6 fei1 taa1 mong4 liu5?" english="Could it be that he forgot?" />

<Hint type="hint">莫非 presents a possibility, not a fact.</Hint>

### State What Should Normally Be True

按说 means “normally” or “by rights.” It sets up an expectation, which the next clause may confirm or show to be wrong.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="按说这件事不该发生。" traditional="按說這件事不該發生。" pinyin="ànshuō zhè jiàn shì bù gāi fāshēng." jyutping="on3 syut3 ze3 gin6 si6 bat1 goi1 faat3 sang1" english="This should not have happened, under normal circumstances." />

<Hint type="hint">按说 often signals a contrast between the expected situation and what actually happened.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">按说 gives the expected baseline, 莫非 turns a clue into a tentative guess, and 何必 questions whether an action is needed at all.</Hint>
