“They checked in repeatedly, but for a moment no one knew what to say.” 时时 looks at a recurring pattern; 一时 can hold a short, temporary moment. And 早晚 often means “sooner or later,” so the surrounding sentence matters more than the characters’ separate meanings.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他时时想起你。" traditional="他時時想起你。" pinyin="tā shíshí xiǎngqǐ nǐ." jyutping="taa1 si4 si4 soeng2 hei2 nei5" english="He often thinks of you." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我一时想不起他的名字。" traditional="我一時想不起他的名字。" pinyin="wǒ yìshí xiǎng bù qǐ tā de míngzi." jyutping="ngo5 jat1 si4 soeng2 bat1 hei2 taa1 dik1 ming4 zi6" english="For a moment, I could not remember his name." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他早晚会把一切都告诉我的。" traditional="他早晚會把一切都告訴我的。" pinyin="tā zǎowǎn huì bǎ yíqiè dōu gàosu wǒ de." jyutping="taa1 zou2 maan5 wui5 baa2 jat1 cai3 dou1 gou3 sou3 ngo5 dik1" english="Sooner or later, he’ll tell me everything." />
