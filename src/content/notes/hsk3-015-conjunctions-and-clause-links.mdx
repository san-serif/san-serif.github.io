Use 不但/不仅 at the start of the first point, then add 而且 or 还 to introduce the second. The second point often adds information that is just as important, or even more surprising.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她不但会说中文，而且说得很流利。" traditional="她不但會說中文，而且說得很流利。" pinyin="Tā búdàn huì shuō Zhōngwén, érqiě shuō de hěn liúlì." jyutping="taa1 bat1 daan6 wui5 syut3 zung1 man4, ji4 ce2 syut3 dak1 han2 lau4 lei6." english="She not only speaks Chinese, but speaks it fluently." />

不但 introduces the first fact; 而且 adds the second. The same verb, 说, appears again so the contrast is easy to hear.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家饭店不光菜好吃，而且服务也很好。" traditional="這家飯店不光菜好吃，而且服務也很好。" pinyin="Zhè jiā fàndiàn bùguāng cài hǎochī, érqiě fúwù yě hěn hǎo." jyutping="ze5 gaa1 faan6 dim3 bat1 gwong1 coi3 hou2 hek3, ji4 ce2 fuk6 mou6 jaa5 han2 hou2." english="The food at this restaurant is good, and the service is great too." />

不光…而且… works the same way. Adding 也 before 很好 makes the second positive point explicit.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家餐厅不仅提供早餐，还免费送咖啡。" traditional="這家餐廳不僅提供早餐，還免費送咖啡。" pinyin="Zhè jiā cāntīng bùjǐn tígōng zǎocān, hái miǎnfèi sòng kāfēi." jyutping="ze5 gaa1 caan1 teng1 bat1 gan2 tai4 gung1 zou2 caan1, waan4 min5 fai3 sung3 gaa3 fe1." english="This café serves breakfast and gives away coffee for free as well." />

不仅 can pair with 还 instead of 而且. The pattern is useful when you are adding a second service, feature, or reason.
