“Call me when you arrive”: 도착할 때 전화해요. Attach -(으)ㄹ 때 to a verb or adjective to name the time something happens. The stem ending determines whether -ㄹ or -을 comes before 때.

<SentenceBlock formal="저는 보통 출퇴근할 때 음악을 듣습니다." formalRom="jeoneun botong chultoegeunhal ttae eumageul deutseumnida." polite="저는 보통 출퇴근할 때 음악을 들어요." politeRom="jeoneun botong chultoegeunhal ttae eumageul deureoyo." casual="나는 보통 출퇴근할 때 음악을 들어." casualRom="naneun botong chultoegeunhal ttae eumageul deureo." english="I usually listen to music when commuting." />

<SentenceBlock formal="저녁을 먹을 때 한국 드라마를 봅니다." formalRom="jeonyeogeul meogeul ttae hanguk deuramareul bomnida." polite="저녁을 먹을 때 한국 드라마를 봐요." politeRom="jeonyeogeul meogeul ttae hanguk deuramareul bwayo." casual="저녁을 먹을 때 한국 드라마를 봐." casualRom="jeonyeogeul meogeul ttae hanguk deuramareul bwa." english="I watch Korean dramas while eating dinner." />

<SentenceBlock formal="한국에서 살았을 때 한국어를 많이 배웠습니다." formalRom="hangugeseo sarasseul ttae hangugeoreul mani baewotseumnida." polite="한국에서 살았을 때 한국어를 많이 배웠어요." politeRom="hangugeseo sarasseul ttae hangugeoreul mani baewosseoyo." casual="한국에서 살았을 때 한국어를 많이 배웠어." casualRom="hangugeseo sarasseul ttae hangugeoreul mani baewosseo." english="I learned a lot of Korean when I lived in Korea." />

<SentenceBlock formal="어렸을 때는 부모님과 많이 놀았습니다." formalRom="eoryeosseul ttaeneun eommoemnimgwa mani noratseumnida." polite="어렸을 때는 부모님과 많이 놀았어요." politeRom="eoryeosseul ttaeneun eommoemnimgwa mani norasseoyo." casual="어렸을 때는 부모님과 많이 놀았어." casualRom="eoryeosseul ttaeneun eommoemnimgwa mani norasseo." english="I played a lot with my parents when I was young." />

<Hint type="hint">Attach 을 때 to a past-tense stem instead of the plain one and the meaning shifts from "whenever this happens" to "back when this was the case." 한국에서 살 때 describes a recurring or hypothetical scenario; 한국에서 살았을 때 points at a finished period, the actual stretch of time you lived there.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="퇴근할 때 전화 주십시오." formalRom="toegeunhal ttae jeonhwa jusipsio." polite="퇴근할 때 전화 주세요." politeRom="toegeunhal ttae jeonhwa juseyo." casual="퇴근할 때 전화 줘." casualRom="toegeunhal ttae jeonhwa jwo." english="Please call me when you finish work." />

<SentenceBlock formal="조카를 만날 때 제일 행복합니다." formalRom="jokareul mannal ttae jeil haengbokhamnida." polite="조카를 만날 때 제일 행복해요." politeRom="jokareul mannal ttae jeil haengbokhaeyo." casual="조카를 만날 때 제일 행복해." casualRom="jokareul mannal ttae jeil haengbokhae." english="I'm happiest when I meet my niece or nephew." />

<SentenceBlock formal="저는 보통 회사에 갈 때 정장을 입습니다." formalRom="jeoneun botong hoesae gal ttae jeongjangeul ipseumnida." polite="저는 보통 회사에 갈 때 정장을 입어요." politeRom="jeoneun botong hoesae gal ttae jeongjangeul ibeoyo." casual="나는 보통 회사에 갈 때 정장을 입어." casualRom="naneun botong hoesae gal ttae jeongjangeul ibeo." english="I usually wear a suit when going to the office." />

<Hint type="insight">제일 and 가장 (from [큰 가방](/notes/a-big-bag)) both mean "the most," near-synonyms with a slight register difference: 제일 leans conversational, 가장 leans a touch more formal or written. Both are correct in casual speech; you'll hear 제일 more often between friends.</Hint>
