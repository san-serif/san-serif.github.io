놓다 (to place) and 걸다 (to hang) are active verbs, things you do to an object. Add 여/려 있다 and they describe the resulting state instead: 놓여 있다, "is placed," 걸려 있다, "is hanging."

<SentenceBlock formal="꽃병을 테이블 위에 놓습니다." formalRom="kkotbyeongeul teibeul wie noseumnida." polite="꽃병을 테이블 위에 놓아요." politeRom="kkotbyeongeul teibeul wie noayo." casual="꽃병을 테이블 위에 놓아." casualRom="kkotbyeongeul teibeul wie noa." english="I place the vase on the table." />

<SentenceBlock formal="꽃병이 테이블 위에 놓여 있습니다." formalRom="kkotbyeongi teibeul wie noyeo isseumnida." polite="꽃병이 테이블 위에 놓여 있어요." politeRom="kkotbyeongi teibeul wie noyeo isseoyo." casual="꽃병이 테이블 위에 놓여 있어." casualRom="kkotbyeongi teibeul wie noyeo isseo." english="The vase is placed on the table." />

<Hint>놓여 있다 and 걸려 있다 are more specific versions of Where Is It?'s 있어요. 꽃병이 테이블 위에 있어요 is also technically true, but naming how something sits there, laid flat versus hanging, sounds more natural and detailed, the kind of thing a native speaker adds without even thinking about it.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="옷이 옷걸이에 걸려 있습니다." formalRom="osi otgeorie geollyeo isseumnida." polite="옷이 옷걸이에 걸려 있어요." politeRom="osi otgeorie geollyeo isseoyo." casual="옷이 옷걸이에 걸려 있어." casualRom="osi otgeorie geollyeo isseo." english="Clothes are hanging on a hanger." />

<SentenceBlock formal="등이 천장에 걸려 있습니다." formalRom="deungi cheonjange geollyeo isseumnida." polite="등이 천장에 걸려 있어요." politeRom="deungi cheonjange geollyeo isseoyo." casual="등이 천장에 걸려 있어." casualRom="deungi cheonjange geollyeo isseo." english="The light is hanging on the ceiling." />

<SentenceBlock formal="메뉴가 벽에 걸려 있습니다." formalRom="menyuga byeoge geollyeo isseumnida." polite="메뉴가 벽에 걸려 있어요." politeRom="menyuga byeoge geollyeo isseoyo." casual="메뉴가 벽에 걸려 있어." casualRom="menyuga byeoge geollyeo isseo." english="The menu is hanging on the wall." />

<SentenceBlock formal="쿠션이 소파 위에 놓여 있습니다." formalRom="kusyeoni sopa wie noyeo isseumnida." polite="쿠션이 소파 위에 놓여 있어요." politeRom="kusyeoni sopa wie noyeo isseoyo." casual="쿠션이 소파 위에 놓여 있어." casualRom="kusyeoni sopa wie noyeo isseo." english="The cushion is placed on the sofa." />

<Hint>The split isn't about size or weight, it's about orientation. Anything resting flat on a surface is 놓여 있다; anything suspended from something is 걸려 있다. A clock lying on a table is 놓여 있어요; the same clock mounted on the wall is 걸려 있어요.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="벽에 시계하고 그림이 걸려 있습니다." formalRom="byeoge sigyehago geurimi geollyeo isseumnida." polite="벽에 시계하고 그림이 걸려 있어요." politeRom="byeoge sigyehago geurimi geollyeo isseoyo." casual="벽에 시계하고 그림이 걸려 있어." casualRom="byeoge sigyehago geurimi geollyeo isseo." english="A clock and a picture are hanging on the wall." />
