趁天气好去爬山 (“make the most of the good weather and go hiking”). 趁 introduces an opportunity before it passes; 基于 names the basis for a decision, and 依 introduces a source or standard someone follows.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他们趁炸弹还没爆炸，就把它拆除了。" traditional="他們趁炸彈還沒爆炸，就把它拆除了。" pinyin="tāmen chèn zhàdàn hái méi bàozhà, jiù bǎ tā chāichú le." jyutping="taa1 mun4 can3 zaa3 daan2 waan4 mut6 baau3 zaa3, zau6 baa2 taa1 caak3 cyu4 liu5" english="They removed the bomb before it exploded." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们的结论基于之前对这个问题的研究。" traditional="我們的結論基於之前對這個問題的研究。" pinyin="wǒmen de jiélùn jīyú zhīqián duì zhège wèntí de yánjiū。" jyutping="ngo5 mun4 dik1 git3 leon6 gei1 jyu1 zi1 cin4 deoi3 ze2 go3 man6 tai4 dik1 jin4 gau3。" english="Our conclusion is based on previous studies of the problem." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="依我看来，你错了。" traditional="依我看來，你錯了。" pinyin="yī wǒ kànlai， nǐ cuò le。" jyutping="ji1 ngo5 hon3 loi4， nei5 co3 liu5。" english="In my opinion, you are wrong." />
