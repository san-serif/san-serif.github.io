You’ve been waiting for a friend and want to say exactly how long. When the verb already has an object, repeat the verb after that object to add a duration or description: 等你等了一个小时 (“waited for you for an hour”).

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我等你等了一个小时。" traditional="我等你等了一個小時。" pinyin="wǒ děng nǐ děng le yí ge xiǎoshí." jyutping="ngo5 dang2 nei5 dang2 liu5 jat1 go3 siu2 si4." english="I waited for you for an hour." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他看书看得很认真。" traditional="他看書看得很認真。" pinyin="tā kàn shū kàn de hěn rènzhēn." jyutping="taa1 hon3 syu1 hon3 dak1 han2 jing6 zan1." english="He reads very carefully." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="姐姐游泳游得特别快。" traditional="姐姐游泳游得特別快。" pinyin="jiějie yóuyǒng yóu de tèbié kuài." jyutping="ze2 ze2 jau4 wing2 jau4 dak1 dak6 bit6 faai3." english="My older sister swims especially fast." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她找工作找了三个月。" traditional="她找工作找了三個月。" pinyin="tā zhǎo gōngzuò zhǎo le sān ge yuè." jyutping="taa1 zaau2 gung1 zok3 zaau2 liu5 saam1 go3 jyut6." english="She looked for a job for three months." />

<Hint type="hint">The repeated verb carries the extra information. Put 得 before a description (看得认真) and 了 before a completed duration (等了一个小时).</Hint>
