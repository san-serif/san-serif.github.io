The library has one rule for everyone: 凡是借书的人都要出示证件. 凡是 introduces the group or situation; 都 gathers every member of it into the same rule.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="凡是报名的人都可以参加。" traditional="凡是報名的人都可以參加。" pinyin="fánshì bàomíng de rén dōu kěyǐ cānjiā." jyutping="faan4 si6 bou3 ming4 dik1 jan4 dou1 ho2 ji5 caam1 gaa1" english="Everyone who registers can take part." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们都很好。" traditional="我們都很好。" pinyin="wǒmen dōu hěn hǎo." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 dou1 han2 hou2." english="We're all very well." />
