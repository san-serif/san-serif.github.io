The notice cites a report, explains the delay, and names one exception. 因 introduces a cause in a compact formal phrase; 据 points to a source; 除 marks something removed or excepted. The noun phrase after each word tells you which job it is doing.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="运动会因下雨取消了。" traditional="運動會因下雨取消了。" pinyin="yùndònghuì yīn xiàyǔ qǔxiāo le." jyutping="wan6 dung6 wui2 jan1 haa6 jyu5 ceoi2 siu1 liu5" english="The sports meet was canceled because of the rain." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="除我以外，大家都在唱歌。" traditional="除我以外，大家都在唱歌。" pinyin="chú wǒ yǐwài, dàjiā dōu zài chànggē." jyutping="ceoi4 ngo5 ji5 ngoi6, daai6 gaa1 dou1 zoi6 coeng3 go1" english="Apart from me, everyone was singing." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="据报道，明天会下雪。" traditional="據報道，明天會下雪。" pinyin="jù bàodào, míngtiān huì xiàxuě." jyutping="geoi3 bou3 dou6, ming4 tin1 wui5 haa6 syut3" english="According to reports, it will snow tomorrow." />
