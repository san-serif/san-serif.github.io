“If you don’t ask, you won’t know.” 不……不…… can compress two negatives into a condition or a paired description. Read the two parts together: one may depend on the other, or both may describe the same situation.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这不便。" traditional="這不便。" pinyin="zhè bùbiàn." jyutping="ze2 bat1 bin6." english="This is inconvenient." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="东西不见了。" traditional="東西不見了。" pinyin="dōngxi bùjiàn le." jyutping="dung1 sai1 bat1 gin3 liu5." english="The thing has disappeared." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="不料他来了。" traditional="不料他來了。" pinyin="bùliào tā lái le." jyutping="bat1 liu6 taa1 loi4 liu5." english="Unexpectedly, he came." />
