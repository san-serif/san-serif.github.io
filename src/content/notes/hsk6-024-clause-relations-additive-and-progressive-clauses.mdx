He didn’t thank her for the help; he blamed her instead. 不但不/没有……反而…… sets up an expectation, then turns it around. 不是……还…… adds an unexpected fact, and 连……都/也 marks an extreme case that strengthens the point.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她还是个孩子的时候，唱歌很好听。" traditional="她還是個孩子的時候，唱歌很好聽。" pinyin="tā háishi ge háizi de shíhou， chànggē hěn hǎotīng。" jyutping="taa1 waan4 si6 go3 haai4 zi2 dik1 si4 hau6， coeng3 go1 han2 hou2 teng1。" english="She sang beautifully when she was a child." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你还记得我的生日，那真是太好了。" traditional="你還記得我的生日，那真是太好了。" pinyin="nǐ hái jìde wǒ de shēngrì， nà zhēn shì tài hǎo le。" jyutping="nei5 waan4 gei3 dak1 ngo5 dik1 saang1 jat6， naa5 zan1 si6 taai3 hou2 liu5。" english="It’s really great that you still remember my birthday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="那是一个许多人连听也没有听过的小国。" traditional="那是一個許多人連聽也沒有聽過的小國。" pinyin="nà shì yí ge xǔduō rén lián tīng yě méiyǒu tīng guo de xiǎo guó。" jyutping="naa5 si6 jat1 go3 heoi2 do1 jan4 lin4 teng1 jaa5 mut6 jau5 teng1 gwo3 dik1 siu2 gwok3。" english="That’s a small country many people have never even heard of." />
