거나 connects two verbs, offering a choice of action. (이)나 connects two nouns, consonant-ending nouns take 이나, vowel-ending nouns take 나, offering a choice of thing.

<SentenceBlock formal="등산을 하거나 운동 모임에 갑니다." formalRom="deungsaneul hageona undong moime gamnida." polite="등산을 하거나 운동 모임에 가요." politeRom="deungsaneul hageona undong moime gayo." casual="등산을 하거나 운동 모임에 가." casualRom="deungsaneul hageona undong moime ga." english="I go hiking or go to an exercise group." />

<SentenceBlock formal="텔레비전을 보거나 소설을 읽습니다." formalRom="tellebijeoneul bogeona soseoreul ikseumnida." polite="텔레비전을 보거나 소설을 읽어요." politeRom="tellebijeoneul bogeona soseoreul ilgeoyo." casual="텔레비전을 보거나 소설을 읽어." casualRom="tellebijeoneul bogeona soseoreul ilgeo." english="I watch TV or read a novel." />

<Hint>거나 connects actions; (이)나 connects things. 텔레비전을 보거나 소설을 읽어요 offers a choice of what to do; 우유나 물을 마셔요, below, offers a choice of what to drink. Mixing up which ending attaches to which word type is the easiest slip here.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="산책하거나 조깅을 합니다." formalRom="sanchaekhageona joginggeul hamnida." polite="산책하거나 조깅을 해요." politeRom="sanchaekhageona joginggeul haeyo." casual="산책하거나 조깅을 해." casualRom="sanchaekhageona joginggeul hae." english="I take a walk or jog." />

<SentenceBlock formal="시리얼이나 토스트를 먹습니다." formalRom="sirieorina toseuteureul meokseumnida." polite="시리얼이나 토스트를 먹어요." politeRom="sirieorina toseuteureul meogeoyo." casual="시리얼이나 토스트를 먹어." casualRom="sirieorina toseuteureul meogeo." english="I eat cereal or toast." />

<SentenceBlock formal="우유나 물을 마십니다." formalRom="uyuna mureul masimnida." polite="우유나 물을 마셔요." politeRom="uyuna mureul masyeoyo." casual="우유나 물을 마셔." casualRom="uyuna mureul masyeo." english="I drink milk or water." />

<SentenceBlock formal="게임이나 운동을 합니다." formalRom="geimina undongeul hamnida." polite="게임이나 운동을 해요." politeRom="geimina undongeul haeyo." casual="게임이나 운동을 해." casualRom="geimina undongeul hae." english="I play games or exercise." />

<SentenceBlock formal="카페나 편의점에서 커피를 삽니다." formalRom="kapena pyeonuijeomeseo keopireul samnida." polite="카페나 편의점에서 커피를 사요." politeRom="kapena pyeonuijeomeseo keopireul sayo." casual="카페나 편의점에서 커피를 사." casualRom="kapena pyeonuijeomeseo keopireul sa." english="I buy coffee at a cafe or convenience store." />

<Hint>That last sentence reflects how central 편의점 (convenience stores) are to daily life in Korea. They're everywhere, open 24 hours, and treated as a completely normal place to grab a coffee, not just snacks and essentials.</Hint>
