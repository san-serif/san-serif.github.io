You’ve watched two friends race and want to compare their results. When you’re comparing how someone does an action, put 得 after the verb, then add the comparison: 跑得比我快 (“runs faster than me”). You can also use 不比 to say one thing isn’t more than another.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="小王跑得比我快。" traditional="小王跑得比我快。" pinyin="Xiǎo Wáng pǎo de bǐ wǒ kuài." jyutping="siu2 wong4 paau2 dak1 bei2 ngo5 faai3." english="Xiao Wang runs faster than I do." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我今天起得比昨天早半个小时。" traditional="我今天起得比昨天早半個小時。" pinyin="wǒ jīntiān qǐ de bǐ zuótiān zǎo bàn ge xiǎoshí." jyutping="ngo5 gam1 tin1 hei2 dak1 bei2 zok3 tin1 zou2 bun3 go3 siu2 si4." english="I got up half an hour earlier today than yesterday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家饭馆不比那家便宜。" traditional="這家飯館不比那家便宜。" pinyin="zhè jiā fànguǎn bù bǐ nà jiā piányi." jyutping="ze5 gaa1 faan6 gun2 bat1 bei2 naa5 gaa1 pin4 ji4." english="This restaurant isn’t cheaper than that one." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="姐姐比我多看了两本书。" traditional="姐姐比我多看了兩本書。" pinyin="jiějie bǐ wǒ duō kàn le liǎng běn shū." jyutping="ze2 ze2 bei2 ngo5 do1 hon3 liu5 loeng5 bun2 syu1." english="My older sister read two more books than I did." />

<Hint type="hint">For an action comparison, the verb comes before 得: 跑得比我快. For a difference in amount, put 多/少/早/晚 before the verb, then give the number: 比我多看了两本.</Hint>
