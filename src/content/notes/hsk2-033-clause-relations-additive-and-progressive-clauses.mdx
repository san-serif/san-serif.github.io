You’re describing a colleague who’s both quick and careful. Link the first quality with 不但 and complete the thought with 而且. The second part adds to the first, so the listener hears a small build-up rather than a contrast.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他不但高，也很快。" traditional="他不但高，也很快。" pinyin="tā bùdàn gāo， yě hěn kuài。" jyutping="taa1 bat1 daan6 gou1, jaa5 han2 faai3." english="He is not only tall but also fast." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他很高，而且很快。" traditional="他很高，而且很快。" pinyin="tā hěn gāo， érqiě hěn kuài。" jyutping="taa1 han2 gou1， ji4 ce2 han2 faai3。" english="He's tall, and moreover fast." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他买不起汽车，更不要说买房子了。" traditional="他買不起汽車，更不要說買房子了。" pinyin="tā mǎi bu qǐ qìchē， gèng búyào shuō mǎi fángzi le。" jyutping="taa1 maai5 bat1 hei2 hei3 ce1， gang3 bat1 jiu3 seoi3 maai5 fong4 zi2 liu5。" english="He cannot buy a car, still less a house." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你还记得我的生日，那真是太好了。" traditional="你還記得我的生日，那真是太好了。" pinyin="nǐ hái jìde wǒ de shēngrì， nà zhēn shì tài hǎo le。" jyutping="nei5 waan4 gei3 dak1 ngo5 dik1 saang1 jat6， naa5 zan1 si6 taai3 hou2 liu5。" english="It’s really great that you still remember my birthday." />
