“Apart from Monday, I’m free any day.” “Besides coffee, we also have tea.”  Besides-style phrases can include or exclude the named item; the next words, like 还 or 都, tell you which reading is intended. 从……起 marks when something begins, while 对……来说 frames a view from one person’s perspective.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="除了雨天，我都是骑车去上班的。" traditional="除了雨天，我都是騎車去上班的。" pinyin="chúle yǔtiān， wǒ dōu shì qíchē qù shàngbān de。" jyutping="ceoi4 liu5 jyu5 tin1， ngo5 dou1 si6 ke4 ce1 heoi3 soeng5 baan1 dik1。" english="I ride to work except on rainy days." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他以外的人都很忙。" traditional="他以外的人都很忙。" pinyin="tā yǐwài de rén dōu hěn máng。" jyutping="taa1 ji5 ngoi6 dik1 jan4 dou1 han2 mong4。" english="Everyone else is busy except him." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你还记得我的生日，那真是太好了。" traditional="你還記得我的生日，那真是太好了。" pinyin="nǐ hái jìde wǒ de shēngrì， nà zhēn shì tài hǎo le。" jyutping="nei5 waan4 gei3 dak1 ngo5 dik1 saang1 jat6， naa5 zan1 si6 taai3 hou2 liu5。" english="It’s really great that you still remember my birthday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我也不是。" traditional="我也不是。" pinyin="wǒ yě bùshì。" jyutping="ngo5 jaa5 bat1 si6。" english="Nor am I." />
