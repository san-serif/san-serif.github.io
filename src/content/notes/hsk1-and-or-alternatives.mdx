At lunch, someone asks what you want: 你喝茶还是咖啡？ Here 还是 puts two choices on the table. 和 does a different job: it joins things you want together, as in 茶和咖啡. Hear the question mark and the meaning becomes easy to spot.

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你喝茶还是咖啡？" traditional="你喝茶還是咖啡？" pinyin="Nǐ hē chá háishi kāfēi?" jyutping="nei5 hot3 caa4 waan4 si6 gaa3 fe1?" english="Would you like tea or coffee?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我和姐姐在家。" traditional="我和姐姐在家。" pinyin="Wǒ hé jiějie zài jiā." jyutping="ngo5 wo4 ze2 ze2 zoi6 gaa1." english="My older sister and I are at home." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你坐车还是走路？" traditional="你坐車還是走路？" pinyin="Nǐ zuò chē háishi zǒulù?" jyutping="nei5 co5 ce1 waan4 si6 zau2 lou6?" english="Will you go by car or walk?" />

<Hint type="hint">还是 is the usual choice in a question asking someone to select. It is different from 和, which joins items rather than offering a choice.</Hint>
