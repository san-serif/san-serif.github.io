Your friend is at the door, keys in hand: 你去不去？ A positive-negative question offers both possibilities in the question itself. Repeat the verb with 不 between its forms—去不去, 是不是—and answer with the same verb or its negative form.

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你去不去？" traditional="你去不去？" pinyin="Nǐ qù bu qù?" jyutping="nei5 heoi3 bat1 heoi3?" english="Are you going or not?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你是不是学生？" traditional="你是不是學生？" pinyin="Nǐ shì bu shì xuésheng?" jyutping="nei5 si6 bat1 si6 hok6 saang1?" english="Are you a student?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他来不来？" traditional="他來不來？" pinyin="Tā lái bu lái?" jyutping="taa1 loi4 bat1 loi4?" english="Is he coming or not?" />

<Hint type="hint">The repeated word is the same verb or adjective: 喜不喜欢, 好不好. Do not add 吗 after this question pattern.</Hint>
