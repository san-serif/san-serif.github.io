Your friend asks why you’re leaving early: 明日早いから. から gives a direct reason for what you’re saying or doing. It’s a clear, everyday way to explain yourself.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="難[むずか]しいから、毎日[まいにち]日本語[にほんご]を勉強[べんきょう]しています。" romaji="Muzukashii kara, mainichi nihongo o benkyou shite imasu." english="Because it's difficult, I study Japanese every day." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="日本語[にほんご]が好[す]きだから、毎日[まいにち]日本語[にほんご]を勉強[べんきょう]しています。" romaji="Nihongo ga suki da kara, mainichi nihongo o benkyou shite imasu." english="Because I like Japanese, I study it every day." />

<Hint type="insight">から states a reason as a plain fact, more direct than ので, which softens the same idea into something more like a shared, polite explanation. から works fine in casual speech and in giving a blunt, matter-of-fact reason.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="いいえ、食[た]べません。好[す]きじゃないですから。" romaji="Iie, tabemasen. Suki janai desu kara." english="No, I won't eat it. Because I don't like it." />

<Hint type="hint">から can also stand alone at the very end of a sentence, added on after the fact as an afterthought explanation, "because," almost like English tacking "because I said so" onto the end of a sentence.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="日本[にほん]の車[くるま]が好[す]きだからです。" romaji="Nihon no kuruma ga suki da kara desu." english="It's because I like Japanese cars." />

<Hint type="hint">からです directly answers a どうして (why) question, naming the reason as the entire predicate of the sentence, a clean, complete way to answer "why" on its own.</Hint>

In casual speech, sentence-final もの or もん can give a reason with a personal or explanatory tone: 今日は行けないもん, “I can't go today, you see.” In a more careful explanation or apology, plain-form + ものですから or もので softens the reason: 道が混んでいたものですから, “because the roads were busy.” These forms can sound like an excuse, so use them when the context supports that tone.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="確認[かくにん]が遅[おく]れたものですから、返事[へんじ]が遅[おそ]くなりました。" romaji="Kakunin ga okureta mono desu kara, henji ga osoku narimashita." english="Because the check took longer, my reply was delayed." />
