You find a spare seat and an empty socket. Before unpacking your laptop, ask what is allowed. -아/어도 돼요 grants permission; -(으)면 안 돼요 states a rule against an action.

### Ask or Give Permission

Take the -아/어 form and add 도 되다: 앉아도 되다, 먹어도 되다, 사용해도 되다. Rising intonation makes it a permission question. 네, 돼요 is a short affirmative answer.

<SentenceBlock formal="여기에 앉아도 됩니까?" formalRom="yeogie anjado doemnikka?" polite="여기에 앉아도 돼요?" politeRom="yeogie anjado dwaeyo?" casual="여기에 앉아도 돼?" casualRom="yeogie anjado dwae?" english="May I sit here?" />

<SentenceBlock formal="이 컴퓨터를 사용해도 됩니다." formalRom="i keompyuteoreul sayonghaedo doemnida." polite="이 컴퓨터를 사용해도 돼요." politeRom="i keompyuteoreul sayonghaedo dwaeyo." casual="이 컴퓨터를 사용해도 돼." casualRom="i keompyuteoreul sayonghaedo dwae." english="You may use this computer." />

### Say What Is Against the Rules

Use -(으)면 안 되다: 가면 안 돼요, 먹으면 안 돼요. It means doing the action is not acceptable. In a question, 안 돼요? can also seek an exception: “Is it not allowed?”

<SentenceBlock formal="여기에서 음식을 먹으면 안 됩니다." formalRom="yeogieseo eumsigeul meogeumyeon an doemnida." polite="여기에서 음식을 먹으면 안 돼요." politeRom="yeogieseo eumsigeul meogeumyeon an dwaeyo." casual="여기에서 음식을 먹으면 안 돼." casualRom="yeogieseo eumsigeul meogeumyeon an dwae." english="You must not eat here." />

<SentenceBlock formal="도서관에서 전화하면 안 됩니다." formalRom="doseogwaneseo jeonhwahamyeon an doemnida." polite="도서관에서 전화하면 안 돼요." politeRom="doseogwaneseo jeonhwahamyeon an dwaeyo." casual="도서관에서 전화하면 안 돼." casualRom="doseogwaneseo jeonhwahamyeon an dwae." english="You must not make phone calls in the library." />

<Hint type="hint">“You don’t have to” needs -지 않아도 돼요: 오지 않아도 돼요, “You don’t have to come.” 오면 안 돼요 says the opposite: “You must not come.” Write 돼요, the contraction of 되어요.</Hint>
