They searched the room 来来回回, and halfway through, the lights went out. A来A去 gives a back-and-forth action; A着A着 can show a change during an ongoing one. 没A没B pairs two absent things, with the surrounding sentence setting the tone.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他们没来这里，是吗？" traditional="他們沒來這裡，是嗎？" pinyin="tāmen méi lái zhèlǐ， shì ma？" jyutping="taa1 mun4 mut6 loi4 ze5 leoi5， si6 maa3？" english="They didn't come here, did they?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他没有去，我也没去。" traditional="他沒有去，我也沒去。" pinyin="tā méiyǒu qù， wǒ yě méi qù。" jyutping="taa1 mut6 jau5 heoi3， ngo5 jaa5 mut6 heoi3。" english="He didn’t go, and neither did I." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我会在这里等着他回来。" traditional="我會在這裡等著他回來。" pinyin="wǒ huì zài zhèlǐ děng zhe tā huílái。" jyutping="ngo5 wui2 zoi6 ze5 leoi5 dang2 zoek3 taa1 wui4 loi4。" english="I’ll wait here for him to come back." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我没跟别人说过我什么时候会到。" traditional="我沒跟別人說過我什麼時候會到。" pinyin="wǒ méi gēn biéren shuō guò wǒ shénme shíhou huì dào。" jyutping="ngo5 mut6 gan1 bit6 jan4 seoi3 gwo3 ngo5 sam6 mo1 si4 hau6 wui2 dou3。" english="I didn’t tell anyone what time I’d arrive." />
