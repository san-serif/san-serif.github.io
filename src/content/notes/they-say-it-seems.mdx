Your friend is getting married, and another classmate has stopped coming to class. One piece of news came from somebody’s words; the other is your inference. Korean gives each a different frame.

### Pass On a Statement

Present action verbs take -ㄴ다고 after a vowel and -는다고 after a consonant: 간다고, 먹는다고. ㄹ drops before ㄴ: 살다 → 산다고. Adjectives take -다고: 바쁘다고. Nouns take -(이)라고: 학생이라고. Past statements use -았/었다고 and future plans can use -(으)ㄹ 거라고.

<SentenceBlock formal="민수가 다음 달에 결혼한다고 합니다." formalRom="minsuga daeum dare gyeolhonhandago hamnida." polite="민수가 다음 달에 결혼한다고 해요." politeRom="minsuga daeum dare gyeolhonhandago haeyo." casual="민수가 다음 달에 결혼한다고 해." casualRom="minsuga daeum dare gyeolhonhandago hae." english="Minsu says he is getting married next month." />

<SentenceBlock formal="지수는 오늘 바쁘다고 했습니다." formalRom="jisuneun oneul bappeudago haetseumnida." polite="지수는 오늘 바쁘다고 했어요." politeRom="jisuneun oneul bappeudago haesseoyo." casual="지수는 오늘 바쁘다고 했어." casualRom="jisuneun oneul bappeudago haesseo." english="Jisu said she was busy today." />

### Infer From What You See or Hear

Use -나 보다 with action verbs and 있다/없다, -(으)ㄴ가 보다 with adjectives, and -인가 보다 with nouns. Past situations use -았/었나 보다. The observation gives you a reason to infer something, without making it certain.

<SentenceBlock polite="불이 꺼져 있어요. 모두 퇴근했나 봐요." politeRom="buri kkeojyeo isseoyo. modu toegeunhaenna bwayo." casual="불이 꺼져 있어. 모두 퇴근했나 봐." casualRom="buri kkeojyeo isseo. modu toegeunhaenna bwa." english="The lights are off. It looks like everyone has gone home." />

<SentenceBlock polite="요즘 일이 많은가 봐요." politeRom="yojeum iri maneunga bwayo." casual="요즘 일이 많은가 봐." casualRom="yojeum iri maneunga bwa." english="It seems they have a lot of work these days." />

<Hint type="hint">Keep the source clear. 결혼한다고 해요 reports a statement; 결혼하나 봐요 offers an inference. Do not use -나 봐요 as the ordinary way to announce your own deliberate plan.</Hint>
