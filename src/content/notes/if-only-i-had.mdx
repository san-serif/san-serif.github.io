A trip went badly because you left too little time. You can say what you should have done, then imagine the better result that would probably have followed. Both expressions look back at a missed possibility.

### A Requirement You Failed to Meet

Use -았/었어야 했는데 to reflect on something that should have been done: 확인했어야 했는데, 일찍 왔어야 했는데. The unfinished -는데 often leaves the unfortunate consequence understood, though it can also be stated.

<SentenceBlock polite="기차 시간을 미리 확인했어야 했는데 놓쳤어요." politeRom="gicha siganeul miri hwaginhaesseoya haenneunde nochyeosseoyo." casual="기차 시간을 미리 확인했어야 했는데 놓쳤어." casualRom="gicha siganeul miri hwaginhaesseoya haenneunde nochyeosseo." english="I should have checked the train time beforehand, but I missed it." />

<SentenceBlock polite="친구 말을 더 잘 들었어야 했는데요." politeRom="chingu mareul deo jal deureosseoya haenneundeyo." casual="친구 말을 더 잘 들었어야 했는데." casualRom="chingu mareul deo jal deureosseoya haenneunde." english="I should have listened more carefully to my friend." />

### Imagine the Result That Did Not Happen

A counterfactual condition can lead into -았/었을 텐데: 일찍 나왔으면 도착했을 텐데. It presents a likely or expected past result under that different condition. The same ending can also express an assumption about a real past situation, so the condition and context matter.

<SentenceBlock polite="조금만 일찍 나왔으면 기차를 탔을 텐데요." politeRom="jogeumman iljjik nawasseumyeon gichareul tasseul tendeyo." casual="조금만 일찍 나왔으면 기차를 탔을 텐데." casualRom="jogeumman iljjik nawasseumyeon gichareul tasseul tende." english="If I’d left just a little earlier, I would probably have caught the train." />

<SentenceBlock polite="지도를 봤으면 길을 찾았을 텐데요." politeRom="jidoreul bwasseumyeon gireul chajasseul tendeyo." casual="지도를 봤으면 길을 찾았을 텐데." casualRom="jidoreul bwasseumyeon gireul chajasseul tende." english="If I’d looked at the map, I would probably have found the way." />

<Hint type="hint">[-(으)ㄹ걸 그랬어요](/notes/back-then-should-have) also expresses regret. -았/었어야 했는데 puts more emphasis on what was needed; -았/었을 텐데 imagines or assumes the result.</Hint>
