You are discussing whether public transport should be cheaper. One expression states your judgement clearly; another presents it as a possibility for discussion. The difference lies in how firmly you put your view forward.

### State Your Judgement

Use the same statement shapes as [reported speech](/notes/they-say-it-seems), followed by 생각하다: 간다고 생각해요, 좋다고 생각해요, 학생이라고 생각해요. Past and future claims can use -았/었다고 and -(으)ㄹ 거라고.

<SentenceBlock formal="대중교통을 늘려야 한다고 생각합니다." formalRom="daejunggyotongeul neullyeoya handago saenggakhamnida." polite="대중교통을 늘려야 한다고 생각해요." politeRom="daejunggyotongeul neullyeoya handago saenggakhaeyo." casual="대중교통을 늘려야 한다고 생각해." casualRom="daejunggyotongeul neullyeoya handago saenggakhae." english="I think we should expand public transport." />

<SentenceBlock formal="이 제도는 도움이 된다고 생각합니다." formalRom="i jedoneun doumi doendago saenggakhamnida." polite="이 제도는 도움이 된다고 생각해요." politeRom="i jedoneun doumi doendago saenggakhaeyo." casual="이 제도는 도움이 된다고 생각해." casualRom="i jedoneun doumi doendago saenggakhae." english="I think this system helps." />

### Offer a Possibility for Consideration

Use -는 거 아닐까 하다 with present actions, -(으)ㄴ 거 아닐까 하다 with present adjectives or completed actions, and -인 거 아닐까 하다 with nouns. 거 is a spoken form of 것. You can include the subject particle as 게 (= 것이), as in 좋은 게 아닐까 해요. The expression softens a positive possibility rather than simply denying it.

<SentenceBlock formal="요금을 낮추는 게 더 좋은 게 아닐까 합니다." formalRom="yogeumeul natchuneun ge deo joeun ge anilkka hamnida." polite="요금을 낮추는 게 더 좋은 게 아닐까 해요." politeRom="yogeumeul natchuneun ge deo joeun ge anilkka haeyo." casual="요금을 낮추는 게 더 좋은 게 아닐까 해." casualRom="yogeumeul natchuneun ge deo joeun ge anilkka hae." english="I wonder if lowering the fare might be better." />

<SentenceBlock formal="지금은 다른 방법이 필요한 게 아닐까 합니다." formalRom="jigeumeun dareun bangbeobi piryohan ge anilkka hamnida." polite="지금은 다른 방법이 필요한 게 아닐까 해요." politeRom="jigeumeun dareun bangbeobi piryohan ge anilkka haeyo." casual="지금은 다른 방법이 필요한 게 아닐까 해." casualRom="jigeumeun dareun bangbeobi piryohan ge anilkka hae." english="I wonder if a different approach might be needed now." />

<Hint type="hint">좋은 게 아닐까 해요 is a tentative positive opinion: “might it be good?” It is not the same as 좋지 않다고 생각해요, which directly says you think it is not good.</Hint>
