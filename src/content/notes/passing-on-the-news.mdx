A classmate messages you with a change of plans. Short quotation endings let you pass it on without repeating 해요 each time. The ending still tells the listener whether the original message was a statement, question, instruction, or suggestion.

### Shorten a Reported Statement

[-다고 해요](/notes/they-say-it-seems) contracts to -대요: 간다고 해요 → 간대요, 먹는다고 해요 → 먹는대요, 좋다고 해요 → 좋대요. Noun statements use -(이)래요: 학생이래요. Past statements use -았/었대요.

<SentenceBlock polite="민수가 내일 한국에 온대요." politeRom="minsuga naeil hanguge ondaeyo." casual="민수가 내일 한국에 온대." casualRom="minsuga naeil hanguge ondae." english="Minsu says he is coming to Korea tomorrow." />

<SentenceBlock polite="새 식당 음식이 맛있대요." politeRom="sae sikdang eumsigi masitdaeyo." casual="새 식당 음식이 맛있대." casualRom="sae sikdang eumsigi masitdae." english="They say the food at the new restaurant is good." />

### Keep the Original Message Type

A reported question can contract to -냬요: 언제 오냬요. An instruction uses -(으)래요: 기다리래요, 읽으래요. A suggestion uses -재요: 만나재요. These are reports of what someone said, rather than direct questions or instructions to your listener.

<SentenceBlock polite="지수가 몇 시에 만나냬요." politeRom="jisuga myeot sie mannanyaeyo." casual="지수가 몇 시에 만나냬." casualRom="jisuga myeot sie mannanyae." english="Jisu asks what time we are meeting." />

<SentenceBlock polite="선생님이 여기서 기다리래요. 친구는 밖에서 만나재요." politeRom="seonsaengnimi yeogiseo gidariraeyo. chinguneun bakkeseo mannajaeyo." casual="선생님이 여기서 기다리래. 친구는 밖에서 만나재." casualRom="seonsaengnimi yeogiseo gidarirae. chinguneun bakkeseo mannajae." english="The teacher says to wait here. My friend suggests meeting outside." />

<Hint type="hint">Distinguish reported -대요 from remembered-observation -데요. 맛있대요 passes on somebody’s claim. 맛있던데요 recalls your own experience. In 학생이래요, 래요 comes from a noun statement; in 기다리래요 it comes from an instruction.</Hint>
