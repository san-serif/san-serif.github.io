The venue is 极为安静—extremely quiet. 极为 raises the degree before a quality or state. 尽 works on extent, while 蛮 is a casual “quite”; they don’t all turn the same dial.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="所有成员都极为重视她的意见。" traditional="所有成員都極為重視她的意見。" pinyin="suǒyǒu chéngyuán dōu jíwéi zhòngshì tā de yìjiàn。" jyutping="so2 jau5 sing4 jyun4 dou1 gik6 wai4 zung6 si6 taa1 dik1 ji3 gin3。" english="All the members value her opinion highly." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他用尽了所有办法，还是没找到答案。" traditional="他用盡了所有辦法，還是沒找到答案。" pinyin="tā yòngjìn le suǒyǒu bànfǎ, háishi méi zhǎodào dá’àn." jyutping="taa1 jung6 zeon6 liu5 so2 jau5 baan6 faat3, waan4 si6 mut6 zaau2 dou3 daap3 on3." english="He tried every possible way but still could not find an answer." />
