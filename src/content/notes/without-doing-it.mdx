ないで attaches to a verb's negative stem and connects two clauses, marking the first action as one that didn't happen while the second one did. It's built from ない, which you already know, plus で instead of a full negative ending.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="朝[あさ]、コーヒーを飲[の]まないで家[いえ]を出[で]ました。" romaji="Asa, koohii o nomanaide ie o demashita." english="I left the house this morning without drinking coffee." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="お風呂[ふろ]に入[はい]らないで寝[ね]ました。" romaji="Ofuro ni hairanaide nemashita." english="I went to bed without taking a bath." />

<Hint>ないで always describes one event happening in place of, or instead of, another, "did B without doing A." It isn't a general-purpose negative connector, it specifically frames the missing action as notable, something you'd normally expect to happen first.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="コーラを飲[の]まないで水[みず]を飲[の]んでいます。" romaji="Koora o nomanaide mizu o nonde imasu." english="I drink water instead of cola." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="レストランで食[た]べないで自分[じぶん]で料理[りょうり]しています。" romaji="Resutoran de tabenaide jibun de ryouri shite imasu." english="Instead of eating out, I cook for myself." />

<Hint>Used with an ongoing habit like this, ないで shifts toward "instead of," swapping one habitual choice for another rather than describing a single missed event.</Hint>
