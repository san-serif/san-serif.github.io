You thought a classmate was quiet, until you worked together and discovered how funny she is. Tell us who gave you information, then use -(으)니까 to show what you found when you did something.

### Where the Information Came From

에게서 and the conversational 한테서 mark a person as the source of something received or heard. With someone you honour, 께 can mark the source too. For a place or organisation use 에서, rather than 에게서.

<SentenceBlock formal="마리한테서 그 이야기를 들었습니다." formalRom="marihanteseo geu iyagireul deureotseumnida." polite="마리한테서 그 이야기를 들었어요." politeRom="marihanteseo geu iyagireul deureosseoyo." casual="마리한테서 그 이야기를 들었어." casualRom="marihanteseo geu iyagireul deureosseo." english="I heard that story from Mari." />

<SentenceBlock formal="선생님께 한국어를 배웠습니다." formalRom="seonsaengnimkke hangugeoreul baewotseumnida." polite="선생님께 한국어를 배웠어요." politeRom="seonsaengnimkke hangugeoreul baewosseoyo." casual="선생님께 한국어를 배웠어." casualRom="seonsaengnimkke hangugeoreul baewosseo." english="I learned Korean from my teacher." />

### An Action Leads to a Discovery

The same -(으)니까 shape used for [a reason](/notes/since) can introduce a discovery: 만나 보니까 재미있었어요. In this use, an action in the first clause creates the opportunity to observe the second. The first action is commonly left without -았/었-; the main clause can place the discovery in the past.

<SentenceBlock formal="같이 일해 보니까 정말 친절했습니다." formalRom="gachi ilhae bonikka jeongmal chinjeolhaetseumnida." polite="같이 일해 보니까 정말 친절했어요." politeRom="gachi ilhae bonikka jeongmal chinjeolhaesseoyo." casual="같이 일해 보니까 정말 친절했어." casualRom="gachi ilhae bonikka jeongmal chinjeolhaesseo." english="When I tried working with them, I found they were really kind." />

<SentenceBlock formal="창문을 여니까 시원한 바람이 들어왔습니다." formalRom="changmuneul yeonikka siwonhan barami deureowatseumnida." polite="창문을 여니까 시원한 바람이 들어왔어요." politeRom="changmuneul yeonikka siwonhan barami deureowasseoyo." casual="창문을 여니까 시원한 바람이 들어왔어." casualRom="changmuneul yeonikka siwonhan barami deureowasseo." english="When I opened the window, a cool breeze came in." />

<Hint type="hint">창문을 여니까 shows 열다 losing ㄹ before ㄴ. The discovery reading comes from the sequence of experience and observation; -(으)니까 has not stopped being a reason connector in other contexts.</Hint>
