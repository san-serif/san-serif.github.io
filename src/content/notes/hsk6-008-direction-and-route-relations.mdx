The path follows the river until the old bridge comes into view. 沿（着） names the route or line something follows. Put the road, river, or other path after 沿, then say what moves or happens along it.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="沿着这条河往北走，前面就是老城区。" traditional="沿著這條河往北走，前面就是老城區。" pinyin="yánzhe zhè tiáo hé wǎng běi zǒu, qiánmiàn jiù shì lǎochéngqū." jyutping="jin4 zoek6 ze5 tiu4 ho4 wong5 bak1 zau2, cin4 min6 zau6 si6 lou5 sing4 keoi1." english="Follow this river north, and you’ll reach the old town." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="大家沿海岸线走了一个多小时，终于看到了灯塔。" traditional="大家沿海岸線走了一個多小時，終於看到了燈塔。" pinyin="dàjiā yán hǎiànxiàn zǒu le yí ge duō xiǎoshí, zhōngyú kàn dào le dēngtǎ." jyutping="daai6 gaa1 jin4 hoi2 ngon6 sin3 zau2 liu5 jat1 go3 do1 siu2 si4, zung1 jyu1 hon3 dou3 liu5 dang1 taap3." english="Everyone walked along the coastline for over an hour and finally saw the lighthouse." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="孩子们沿着墙边排好队，等老师发午餐。" traditional="孩子們沿著牆邊排好隊，等老師發午餐。" pinyin="háizimen yánzhe qiángbiān pái hǎo duì, děng lǎoshī fā wǔcān." jyutping="haai4 zi2 mun4 jin4 zoek6 coeng4 bin1 paai4 hou2 deoi6, dang2 lou5 si1 faat3 ng5 caan1." english="The children lined up along the wall and waited for the teacher to hand out lunch." />

<Hint type="hint">沿着 names the path you follow. A location phrase such as 在这里 tells where you are, but does not express a route.</Hint>
