において marks a formal location, field, or situation (“in/at”). と共に means “together with” or “as something changes.” 一方で can contrast two sides or describe two developments in parallel. をはじめ introduces a representative first example, followed by others. ことから presents a reason or clue from which a conclusion follows. によって can indicate a method, agent, cause, or difference, so the noun relationship determines its reading.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="この制度[せいど]は都市部[としぶ]において導入[どうにゅう]されました。" romaji="Kono seido wa toshibu ni oite dounyuu saremashita." english="This system was introduced in urban areas." />

において is a formal counterpart of で for places, fields, or situations. Its modifying form is における + noun: 国際社会における課題, “issues in the international community.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="利用者[りようしゃ]の増加[ぞうか]と共[とも]に、地方[ちほう]にも広[ひろ]まりました。" romaji="Riyousha no zouka to tomo ni, chihou ni mo hiromarimashita." english="As the number of users grew, it spread to rural areas too." />

と共に can mean people doing something together, or two changes progressing in parallel. 一方で then sets up another side: 都市では便利になった一方で、地方では課題が残る, “while it became more convenient in cities, issues remain in rural areas.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="首都[しゅと]をはじめ、多[おお]くの地域[ちいき]で同[おな]じ制度[せいど]が使[つか]われています。" romaji="Shuto o hajime, ooku no chiiki de onaji seido ga tsukawarete imasu." english="The same system is used in many regions, beginning with the capital." />

をはじめ names a prominent example before expanding to a larger group. ことから explains a conclusion based on an observed fact: 利用者が増えたことから, “because users increased.” によって can mean “by,” “due to,” or “depending on,” so identify whether the noun is an agent, cause, method, or variable.

<Hint type="insight">において is common in formal reports and presentations. と共に works for both joint action and parallel change; the subject and verb tell which. 一方で often helps a writer present benefits and remaining issues in a balanced way.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Formal setting: において. Parallel change or doing together: と共に. Contrast or two sides: 一方で. First example in a broader set: をはじめ. Reason inferred from a fact: ことから.</Hint>
