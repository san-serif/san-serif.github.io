At first the new job felt overwhelming. 起初 sets the listener at the beginning; 后来才 can show what became clear only after things changed. 此后 looks back to a point already mentioned and carries the story forward from there.

### What Changed Later

<CSentenceBlock simplified="起初，我以为这份工作很简单，后来才发现每天都要加班。" traditional="起初，我以為這份工作很簡單，後來才發現每天都要加班。" pinyin="Qǐchū, wǒ yǐwéi zhè fèn gōngzuò hěn jiǎndān, hòulái cái fāxiàn měitiān dōu yào jiābān." jyutping="hei2 co1, ngo5 ji5 wai4 ze5 fan6 gung1 zok3 han2 gaan2 daan1, hau6 loi4 coi4 faat3 jin6 mui5 tin1 dou1 jiu3 gaa1 baan1." english="At first, I thought this job would be easy; only later did I discover that we have to work overtime every day." />

才 comes before the later result. It marks the point when the result finally happened or became clear, rather than simply saying that it happened later.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="起初大家不太熟悉，合作了几次以后才慢慢了解彼此。" traditional="起初大家不太熟悉，合作了幾次以後才慢慢了解彼此。" pinyin="Qǐchū dàjiā bú tài shúxī, hézuò le jǐ cì yǐhòu cái mànmàn liǎojiě bǐcǐ." jyutping="hei2 co1 daai6 gaa1 bat1 taai3 suk6 sik1, hap6 zok3 zo2 gei2 ci3 ji5 hau6 coi4 maan6 maan6 liu5 gaai2 bei2 ci2." english="At first, everyone was not very familiar with one another; only after working together a few times did they gradually get to know each other." />

<Hint type="hint">起初 sets up an early stage; it does not mean “once” by itself. The change comes from the rest of the sentence: 起初…后来… (“at first…later…”) or 起初…后来才… (“at first…only later…”).</Hint>

### What Continued Afterward

<CSentenceBlock simplified="毕业后，她先在广州工作。此后，她每年都会回家乡过年。" traditional="畢業後，她先在廣州工作。此後，她每年都會回家鄉過年。" pinyin="Bìyè hòu, tā xiān zài Guǎngzhōu gōngzuò. Cǐhòu, tā měi nián dōu huì huí jiāxiāng guònián." jyutping="bit6 jip6 hau6, taa1 sin1 zoi6 gwong2 zau1 gung1 zok3. ci2 hau6, taa1 mui5 nin4 dou1 wui5 wui4 gaa1 hoeng1 gwo3 nin4." english="After graduating, she first worked in Guangzhou. From then on, she returned to her hometown for Lunar New Year every year." />

此后 points back to a time just given. It is common in writing and formal narration; everyday conversation often uses 那以后 (“after that”) instead.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="那次谈话以后，他开始认真学中文。此后，他每天都练习一个小时。" traditional="那次談話以後，他開始認真學中文。此後，他每天都練習一個小時。" pinyin="Nà cì tánhuà yǐhòu, tā kāishǐ rènzhēn xué Zhōngwén. Cǐhòu, tā měitiān dōu liànxí yí ge xiǎoshí." jyutping="naa5 ci3 taam4 waa6 ji5 hau6, taa1 hoi1 ci2 jing6 zan1 hok6 zung1 man4. ci2 hau6, taa1 mui5 tin1 dou1 lin6 zaap6 jat1 go3 siu2 si4." english="After that conversation, he began studying Chinese seriously. From then on, he practised for an hour every day." />

<Hint type="insight">后来 tells the reader what happened at a later point. 此后 shifts the timeline forward from a named event and often describes an ongoing consequence. That is why 此后 works naturally at the start of a new sentence.</Hint>
