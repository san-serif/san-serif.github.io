It’s getting late and the last bus is coming: 我得回家了. Here 得 (děi) means “have to” and comes before the action. The same character has another job after a verb—跑得快—so its position is the first clue to which 得 you’re reading.







<CSentenceBlock simplified="我得回家了。" traditional="我得回家了。" pinyin="wǒ děi huí jiā le。" jyutping="ngo5 dei2 wui4 gaa1 liu5。" english="I have to head home now." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="为了家人，我得努力奋斗。" traditional="為了家人，我得努力奮鬥。" pinyin="wèile jiārén， wǒ děi nǔlì fèndòu。" jyutping="wai6 liu5 gaa1 jan4， ngo5 dei2 nou5 lik6 fan5 dau3。" english="I have to work hard for my family." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她激动得说不出话来。" traditional="她激動得說不出話來。" pinyin="tā jīdòng de shuō bù chū huà lái。" jyutping="taa1 gik1 dung6 dak1 syut3 bat1 ceot1 waa6 loi4。" english="She was so excited she couldn’t speak." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="那个教授讲得太快了，一个人也听不懂。" traditional="那個教授講得太快了，一個人也聽不懂。" pinyin="nà ge jiàoshòu jiǎng de tài kuài le， yí ge rén yě tīng bù dǒng。" jyutping="naa5 go3 gaau3 sau6 gong2 dak1 taai3 faai3 liu5， jat1 go3 jan4 jaa5 ting1 bat1 dung2。" english="The professor spoke so fast that no one could understand." />
