時 (とき), the plain word for "time," attaches after a verb, adjective, or noun in its plain form and means "when." The tense of the clause before 時 doesn't have to match the main clause, each one is set independently.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="誰[だれ]かの家[いえ]に入[はい]る時[とき]、「お邪魔[じゃま]します」と言[い]います。" romaji="Dareka no ie ni hairu toki, 'ojama shimasu' to iimasu." english="When entering someone's house, you say 'ojama shimasu.'" />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="家[いえ]に帰[かえ]る時[とき]、「ただいま」と言[い]います。" romaji="Ie ni kaeru toki, 'tadaima' to iimasu." english="When you get home, you say 'tadaima.'" />

<Hint>入る時 uses the dictionary form even though the sentence describes a habitual, repeated situation, "whenever you enter." 時 doesn't care whether the action already happened, is happening, or hasn't happened yet, the form right before it just reflects that clause's own internal timing.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="寂[さび]しい時[とき]に家族[かぞく]に電話[でんわ]します。" romaji="Sabishii toki ni kazoku ni denwa shimasu." english="When I feel lonely, I call my family." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="暇[ひま]な時[とき]に本[ほん]を読[よ]みます。" romaji="Hima na toki ni hon o yomimasu." english="When I have free time, I read." />

<Hint>い-adjectives attach directly, 寂しい時. な-adjectives and nouns insert な, 暇な時, 休みの時 uses の instead since 休み is a noun, the same pattern you've now seen across many grammar points, ので, のに, はず, ように.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="旅行[りょこう]に行[い]く時[とき]は、ホテルを予約[よやく]します。" romaji="Ryokou ni iku toki wa, hoteru o yoyaku shimasu." english="When I go on a trip, I book a hotel." />

<Hint>時は, adding は, sets the whole time clause up as a topic, emphasizing a contrast or a general rule more strongly than 時 alone, "as for when I travel, [this is what I do]."</Hint>
