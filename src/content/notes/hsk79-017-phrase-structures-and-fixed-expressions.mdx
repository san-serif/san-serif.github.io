The weekend is nearly here: 巴不得周末马上到来 (“I can’t wait for the weekend”). 巴不得 expresses an eager wish; 别提了 shuts a topic down with “don’t even mention it,” while 除此之外 adds “apart from that.” The surrounding conversation gives each phrase its tone.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我巴不得现在就回家。" traditional="我巴不得現在就回家。" pinyin="wǒ bābudé xiànzài jiù huíjiā." jyutping="ngo5 baa1 bat1 dak1 jin6 zoi6 zau6 wui4 gaa1" english="I cannot wait to go home now." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="除此之外，别无办法。" traditional="除此之外，別無辦法。" pinyin="chúcǐzhīwài, bié wú bànfǎ." jyutping="ceoi4 ci2 zi1 ngoi6, bit6 mou4 baan6 faat3" english="Apart from this, there is no other option." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="别提了，昨天真倒霉。" traditional="別提了，昨天真倒楣。" pinyin="bié tí le, zuótiān zhēn dǎoméi." jyutping="bit6 tai4 liu5, zok6 tin1 zan1 dou2 mui4" english="Don’t ask; yesterday was a really bad day." />
