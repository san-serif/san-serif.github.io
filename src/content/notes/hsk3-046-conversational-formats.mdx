Your friends are still deciding where to eat, and the restaurant is about to close. These spoken patterns help you react to the moment: 都……了 points out what’s already true, X就X吧 accepts a trade-off, and X什么啊 challenges the reason for doing something.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="都九点了，我们快找个地方吃饭吧。" traditional="都九點了，我們快找個地方吃飯吧。" pinyin="dōu jiǔ diǎn le, wǒmen kuài zhǎo ge dìfang chīfàn ba." jyutping="dou1 gau2 dim2 liu5, ngo5 mun4 faai3 zaau2 go3 dei6 fong1 hek3 faan6 baa6." english="It’s already nine; let’s hurry and find somewhere to eat." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="远就远一点儿吧，风景好就值得。" traditional="遠就遠一點兒吧，風景好就值得。" pinyin="yuǎn jiù yuǎn yìdiǎnr ba, fēngjǐng hǎo jiù zhíde." jyutping="jyun5 zau6 jyun5 jat1 dim2 ji4 baa6, fung1 ging2 hou2 zau6 zik6 dak1." english="It may be a little far, but the view makes it worth it." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你急什么啊？火车还有半个小时才开。" traditional="你急什麼啊？火車還有半個小時才開。" pinyin="nǐ jí shénme a? huǒchē hái yǒu bàn ge xiǎoshí cái kāi." jyutping="nei5 gap1 sam6 mo1 aa3? fo2 ce1 waan4 jau5 bun3 go3 siu2 si4 coi4 hoi1." english="What are you rushing for? The train doesn’t leave for another half hour." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="等就等吧，我先去买杯咖啡。" traditional="等就等吧，我先去買杯咖啡。" pinyin="děng jiù děng ba, wǒ xiān qù mǎi bēi kāfēi." jyutping="dang2 zau6 dang2 baa6, ngo5 sin1 heoi3 maai5 bui1 gaa3 fe1." english="Well, we’ll wait then. I’ll go get a coffee first." />

<Hint type="hint">Repeating the same word in X就X吧 can sound resigned or accepting: “If we have to wait, we’ll wait.” X什么啊 is a spoken challenge, so use it only when the relationship and tone make that natural.</Hint>
