Your friend has a coupon that expires tonight. “Use it if you have it,” you say. Chinese has a few casual frames for making use of an opportunity, accepting a small amount at a time, or telling someone there’s nothing to worry about.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这张优惠券今天就过期，不用白不用，咱们去买点儿水果吧。" traditional="這張優惠券今天就過期，不用白不用，咱們去買點兒水果吧。" pinyin="zhè zhāng yōuhuìquàn jīntiān jiù guòqī, búyòng bái búyòng, zánmen qù mǎi diǎnr shuǐguǒ ba." jyutping="ze5 zoeng1 jau1 wai6 gyun3 gam1 tin1 zau6 gwo3 kei4, bat1 jung6 baak6 bat1 jung6, zaam2 mun4 heoi3 maai5 dim2 ji4 seoi2 gwo2 baa6." english="This coupon expires today, so we might as well use it and buy some fruit." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="能省一点是一点，我们坐地铁去吧。" traditional="能省一點是一點，我們坐地鐵去吧。" pinyin="néng shěng yìdiǎn shì yìdiǎn, wǒmen zuò dìtiě qù ba." jyutping="nang4 saang2 jat1 dim2 si6 jat1 dim2, ngo5 mun4 co5 dei6 tit3 heoi3 baa6." english="Every little bit we can save helps; let’s take the subway." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="没什么好担心的，医生说休息两天就会好。" traditional="沒什麼好擔心的，醫生說休息兩天就會好。" pinyin="méi shénme hǎo dānxīn de, yīshēng shuō xiūxi liǎng tiān jiù huì hǎo." jyutping="mut6 sam6 mo1 hou2 daam1 sam1 dik1, ji1 sang1 syut3 jau1 sik1 loeng5 tin1 zau6 wui5 hou2." english="There’s nothing to worry about; the doctor said two days of rest should do it." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="能看一眼是一眼，展览明天就结束了。" traditional="能看一眼是一眼，展覽明天就結束了。" pinyin="néng kàn yì yǎn shì yì yǎn, zhǎnlǎn míngtiān jiù jiéshù le." jyutping="nang4 hon3 jat1 ngaan5 si6 jat1 ngaan5, zin2 laam5 ming4 tin1 zau6 git3 cuk1 liu5." english="Let’s take a look while we can; the exhibition ends tomorrow." />

<Hint type="hint">不X白不X means “if you don’t X, the chance goes to waste.” V一V是一V has a more resigned “take what you can get” feeling. 没什么好X的 tells someone there’s no reason to X.</Hint>
