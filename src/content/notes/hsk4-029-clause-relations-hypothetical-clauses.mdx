“Take an umbrella in case it rains; otherwise, you’ll be soaked.” 假如 opens a possible situation. 万一 does too, often with a hint of concern; 否则 names what may happen if no action is taken.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="立刻起床，否则你上学会迟到。" traditional="立刻起床，否則你上學會遲到。" pinyin="lìkè qǐchuáng， fǒuzé nǐ shàngxué huì chídào。" jyutping="laap6 hak1 hei2 cong4， fau2 zak1 nei5 soeng5 hok6 wui2 ci4 dou3。" english="Get up at once, or you’ll be late for school." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="假如你认真听，就能听懂。" traditional="假如你認真聽，就能聽懂。" pinyin="jiǎrú nǐ rènzhēn tīng， jiù néng tīng dǒng。" jyutping="gaa2 jyu4 nei5 jan6 zan1 teng1， zau6 nang4 teng1 dung2。" english="If you listen carefully, you’ll understand." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="万一来不了，我会先给你打电话。" traditional="萬一來不了，我會先給你打電話。" pinyin="wànyī lái bùle， wǒ huì xiān gěi nǐ dǎdiànhuà。" jyutping="maan6 jat1 loi4 bat1 liu5， ngo5 wui2 sin1 kap1 nei5 daa2 din6 waa2。" english="In case I can't come, I'll give you a call beforehand." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="“我明天再做。” “这话你昨天就说过了！”" traditional="“我明天再做。” “這話你昨天就說過了！”" pinyin="“wǒ míngtiān zài zuò。” “zhè huà nǐ zuótiān jiù shuō guò le！”" jyutping="“ngo5 ming4 tin1 zoi3 zou6。” “ze5 waa6 nei5 zok6 tin1 zau6 seoi3 gwo3 liu5！”" english="“I’ll do it again tomorrow.” “You said that just yesterday!”" />
