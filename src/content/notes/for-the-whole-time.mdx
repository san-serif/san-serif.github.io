You have two days in Busan, and your friend wants to know what you will do while you are there. 동안 gives a stretch of time boundaries: for two days, during the holiday, or while something is happening.

### Name the Length

Put 동안 after a duration or a period: 사흘 동안, 방학 동안. There is no 에 between the time expression and 동안. The verb at the end tells you whether the period is past, present, or future.

<SentenceBlock formal="부산에서 사흘 동안 지냈습니다." formalRom="busaneseo saheul dongan jinaetseumnida." polite="부산에서 사흘 동안 지냈어요." politeRom="busaneseo saheul dongan jinaesseoyo." casual="부산에서 사흘 동안 지냈어." casualRom="busaneseo saheul dongan jinaesseo." english="I spent three days in Busan." />

<SentenceBlock formal="방학 동안 한국어를 공부할 것입니다." formalRom="banghak dongan hangugeoreul gongbuhal geosimnida." polite="방학 동안 한국어를 공부할 거예요." politeRom="banghak dongan hangugeoreul gongbuhal geoyeyo." casual="방학 동안 한국어를 공부할 거야." casualRom="banghak dongan hangugeoreul gongbuhal geoya." english="I will study Korean during the holiday." />

### Use an Action as the Time Window

Verb stem + -는 동안 means while that action is in progress: 기다리는 동안, 한국에 사는 동안. Unlike [-(으)면서](/notes/while), the two clauses do not need to describe the same person doing both actions.

<SentenceBlock formal="친구를 기다리는 동안 책을 읽었습니다." formalRom="chingureul gidarineun dongan chaegeul ilgeotseumnida." polite="친구를 기다리는 동안 책을 읽었어요." politeRom="chingureul gidarineun dongan chaegeul ilgeosseoyo." casual="친구를 기다리는 동안 책을 읽었어." casualRom="chingureul gidarineun dongan chaegeul ilgeosseo." english="I read a book while waiting for my friend." />

<SentenceBlock formal="제가 요리하는 동안 동생이 청소했습니다." formalRom="jega yorihaneun dongan dongsaengi cheongsohaetseumnida." polite="제가 요리하는 동안 동생이 청소했어요." politeRom="jega yorihaneun dongan dongsaengi cheongsohaesseoyo." casual="내가 요리하는 동안 동생이 청소했어." casualRom="naega yorihaneun dongan dongsaengi cheongsohaesseo." english="My younger sibling cleaned while I cooked." />

<Hint type="hint">An hour spent doing something is 한 시간 동안. An event scheduled for one o’clock is 한 시에. Duration and clock time answer different questions.</Hint>
