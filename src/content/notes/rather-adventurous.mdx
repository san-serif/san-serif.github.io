You enjoy hiking, but you would hesitate to call yourself an athlete. -는 편이다 describes a tendency with some room around it. When you make arrangements so that an activity is possible or safe, -게 gives the purpose or intended result.

### A Tendency Rather Than an Absolute

편 means a side or category here. Action verbs take -는 편이다: 자주 걷는 편이에요. Adjectives take -(으)ㄴ 편이다: 조용한 편이에요, 작은 편이에요. It places someone roughly toward that side of a comparison.

<SentenceBlock formal="저는 주말에 자주 걷는 편입니다." formalRom="jeoneun jumare jaju geonneun pyeonimnida." polite="저는 주말에 자주 걷는 편이에요." politeRom="jeoneun jumare jaju geonneun pyeonieyo." casual="나는 주말에 자주 걷는 편이야." casualRom="naneun jumare jaju geonneun pyeoniya." english="I tend to walk often on weekends." />

<SentenceBlock formal="이 길은 조용한 편입니다." formalRom="i gireun joyonghan pyeonimnida." polite="이 길은 조용한 편이에요." politeRom="i gireun joyonghan pyeonieyo." casual="이 길은 조용한 편이야." casualRom="i gireun joyonghan pyeoniya." english="This road is relatively quiet." />

### Arrange Things So That It Can Happen

Verb or adjective stem + -게 can introduce the result you want the next action to make possible: 아이들도 먹게, 춥지 않게, 먹을 수 있게. The action after -게 is what you do to bring that result about. You will also meet adjective + -게 as an adverb of manner, such as 안전하게 타요, “ride safely”; here we focus on the purpose/result use.

<SentenceBlock formal="아이들도 먹을 수 있게 맵지 않게 만들었습니다." formalRom="aideuldo meogeul su itge maepji anke mandeureotseumnida." polite="아이들도 먹을 수 있게 맵지 않게 만들었어요." politeRom="aideuldo meogeul su itge maepji anke mandeureosseoyo." casual="아이들도 먹을 수 있게 맵지 않게 만들었어." casualRom="aideuldo meogeul su itge maepji anke mandeureosseo." english="I made it mild so that children could eat it too." />

<SentenceBlock formal="약속을 잊어버리지 않게 메모했습니다." formalRom="yaksogeul ijeobeoriji anke memohaetseumnida." polite="약속을 잊어버리지 않게 메모했어요." politeRom="yaksogeul ijeobeoriji anke memohaesseoyo." casual="약속을 잊어버리지 않게 메모했어." casualRom="yaksogeul ijeobeoriji anke memohaesseo." english="I made a note so that I wouldn’t forget the appointment." />

<Hint type="hint">먹을 수 있게 만들었어요 tells us the intended result: make it so someone can eat it. A shorter request can use the action directly: 집에 가져가게 포장해 주세요, “Please wrap it so I can take it home.”</Hint>
