A friend asks whether you have ever missed a flight. You are counting experiences in your life, rather than describing what you did yesterday. -(으)ㄴ 적이 있다 says that an occasion like this has happened before.

### One Occasion Is Enough

Attach -ㄴ 적이 있다 after a vowel and -은 적이 있다 after most consonants: 가다 → 간 적이 있다, 먹다 → 먹은 적이 있다. A ㄹ stem drops ㄹ before ㄴ: 살다 → 산 적이 있다. 적 is a dependent noun meaning an occasion; leave a space before it.

<SentenceBlock formal="한국에서 산 적이 있습니다." formalRom="hangugeseo san jeogi itseumnida." polite="한국에서 산 적이 있어요." politeRom="hangugeseo san jeogi isseoyo." casual="한국에서 산 적이 있어." casualRom="hangugeseo san jeogi isseo." english="I have lived in Korea before." />

<SentenceBlock formal="비행기를 놓친 적이 있습니까?" formalRom="bihaenggireul nochin jeogi itseumnikka?" polite="비행기를 놓친 적이 있어요?" politeRom="bihaenggireul nochin jeogi isseoyo?" casual="비행기를 놓친 적이 있어?" casualRom="bihaenggireul nochin jeogi isseo?" english="Have you ever missed a flight?" />

### When It Has Never Happened

Replace 있다 with 없다 for an experience you have never had. [먹어 봤어요](/notes/try-it-and-see) often asks about a similar experience, but -아/어 보다 can also invite someone to try something now. -(으)ㄴ 적이 있다 only looks back at whether an occasion occurred.

<SentenceBlock formal="혼자 여행한 적이 없습니다." formalRom="honja yeohaenghan jeogi eopseumnida." polite="혼자 여행한 적이 없어요." politeRom="honja yeohaenghan jeogi eopseoyo." casual="혼자 여행한 적이 없어." casualRom="honja yeohaenghan jeogi eopseo." english="I have never travelled alone." />

<SentenceBlock formal="이런 음식을 먹은 적이 없습니다." formalRom="ireon eumsigeul meogeun jeogi eopseumnida." polite="이런 음식을 먹은 적이 없어요." politeRom="ireon eumsigeul meogeun jeogi eopseoyo." casual="이런 음식을 먹은 적이 없어." casualRom="ireon eumsigeul meogeun jeogi eopseo." english="I have never eaten food like this." />

<Hint type="hint">Use the completed-action modifier, 간 적, rather than the present modifier 가는 적 when asking about life experience. The ending 있어요 can stay present because the experience belongs to your life now.</Hint>
