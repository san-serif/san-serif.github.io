A doctor recommends an earlier bedtime. The advice is concrete, and so is the condition behind it: you need enough rest to recover. -도록 하다 gives a direction; -아/어야 sets a necessary condition.

### Give Advice or Set an Intention

Verb stem + -도록 하세요 asks someone to make sure they do something. It suits advice, instructions, and organised settings. A speaker can also commit with -도록 할게요: “I’ll make sure to…”.

<SentenceBlock formal="오늘은 일찍 쉬도록 하십시오." formalRom="oneureun iljjik swidorok hasipsio." polite="오늘은 일찍 쉬도록 하세요." politeRom="oneureun iljjik swidorok haseyo." casual="오늘은 일찍 쉬도록 해." casualRom="oneureun iljjik swidorok hae." english="Make sure you rest early today." />

<SentenceBlock formal="앞으로 물을 자주 마시도록 하겠습니다." formalRom="apeuro mureul jaju masidorok hagetseumnida." polite="앞으로 물을 자주 마시도록 할게요." politeRom="apeuro mureul jaju masidorok halgeyo." casual="앞으로 물을 자주 마시도록 할게." casualRom="apeuro mureul jaju masidorok halge." english="I’ll make sure to drink water often from now on." />

### A Condition That Must Be Met

Use the -아/어 form plus 야 before the result: 쉬어야 낫다, 일찍 자야 일어날 수 있다. Unlike a general if-clause, this presents the first condition as necessary for the second to be possible.

<SentenceBlock formal="푹 쉬어야 빨리 낫습니다." formalRom="puk swieoya ppalli natseumnida." polite="푹 쉬어야 빨리 나아요." politeRom="puk swieoya ppalli naayo." casual="푹 쉬어야 빨리 나아." casualRom="puk swieoya ppalli naa." english="You need plenty of rest to get better quickly." />

<SentenceBlock formal="영수증이 있어야 환불받을 수 있습니다." formalRom="yeongsujeungi isseoya hwanbulbadeul su itseumnida." polite="영수증이 있어야 환불받을 수 있어요." politeRom="yeongsujeungi isseoya hwanbulbadeul su isseoyo." casual="영수증이 있어야 환불받을 수 있어." casualRom="yeongsujeungi isseoya hwanbulbadeul su isseo." english="You need a receipt to be able to get a refund." />

<Hint type="hint">낫다 loses ㅅ before a vowel: 나아요, 나아야. It keeps ㅅ before the consonant ending in 낫습니다. A necessary condition also need not be sufficient: having a receipt may be one of several refund requirements.</Hint>
