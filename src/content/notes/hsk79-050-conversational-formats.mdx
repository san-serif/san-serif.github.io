You ask the manager to give your friend another chance: 看在我的面子上. Someone describes a price as fair, and you push back: 哪有这么贵的东西. 什么X的Y的 can wave away a category or list as unimportant. These frames make the speaker’s stance clear—and quite direct.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我觉得很难用一天就做完这么多工作。" traditional="我覺得很難用一天就做完這麼多工作。" pinyin="wǒ juéde hěn nán yòng yì tiān jiù zuòwán zhème duō gōngzuò。" jyutping="ngo5 gok3 dak1 han2 naan4 jung6 jat1 tin1 zau6 zou6 jyun4 ze2 mo1 do1 gung1 zok3。" english="I don’t think it’s possible to finish so much work in one day." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你这样做不对。" traditional="你這樣做不對。" pinyin="nǐ zhèyàng zuò bú duì。" jyutping="nei5 ze2 joeng6 zou6 bat1 deoi3。" english="It’s wrong to do it this way." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="那是什么？" traditional="那是什麼？" pinyin="nà shì shénme？" jyutping="naa5 si6 sam6 mo1？" english="What is that?" />
