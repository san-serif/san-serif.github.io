ようが～まいが says that the outcome stays the same whether an action happens or not. であれ～であれ and かれ～かれ likewise cover alternatives. なり～なり offers examples or choices, while ともなく・ともなしに describes doing something without a particular intention. なり can also mark an immediate sequence, “as soon as.” ともなると describes what applies once a situation reaches a certain stage. These are compact, mostly formal patterns; use context to distinguish their separate jobs.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="雨[あめ]が降[ふ]ろうが降[ふ]るまいが、予定[よてい]どおり開催[かいさい]します。" romaji="Ame ga furou ga furu mai ga, yotei doori kaisai shimasu." english="Whether it rains or not, we'll hold it as planned." />

Volitional + が～まいが places two alternatives side by side and states that neither changes the result. であれ～であれ is a formal “whether A or B.” Use かれ～かれ with paired adjectives, as in 多かれ少なかれ, “to a greater or lesser extent.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="困[こま]ったときは、電話[でんわ]なりメールなりで連絡[れんらく]してください。" romaji="Komatta toki wa, denwa nari meeru nari de renraku shite kudasai." english="When you have a problem, contact me by phone or email, for example." />

なり～なり suggests examples or acceptable options without claiming the list is complete. ともなく・ともなしに means an action happened without deliberate focus: 何となくテレビを見る, “watch TV absentmindedly.” The related なり can mark immediacy: 帰るなり眠った, “as soon as I got home, I slept.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="社会人[しゃかいじん]ともなると、自分[じぶん]で判断[はんだん]する場面[ばめん]が増[ふ]えます。" romaji="Shakaijin tomo naru to, jibun de handan suru bamen ga fuemasu." english="Once you become a working adult, situations where you decide for yourself increase." />

ともなると describes what becomes expected at a particular stage. 如何 (いかん) indicates that something depends on a condition, commonly in 如何によって or 如何にかかわらず.

<Hint type="insight">In an announcement, 雨が降ろうが降るまいが is firm and decisive. For a softer choice, なり～なり simply suggests ways to get in touch. ともなく・ともなしに describes lack of intention, not a deliberate method.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">No matter whether: ようが～まいが. Formal alternatives: であれ～であれ. Examples: なり～なり. Unnoticed action: ともなく. Immediate next event: verb + なり. A stage with its expectations: ともなると.</Hint>
