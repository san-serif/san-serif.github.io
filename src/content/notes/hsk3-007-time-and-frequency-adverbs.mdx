You’ve known about the meeting for ages, but the taxi is waiting now. 早已 and 已 look back to a point by which something was true; 赶紧、赶快, and 立刻 press for action now. 通常、往往, and 总是 describe a habit or pattern instead of a one-off event.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他早已来了。" traditional="他早已來了。" pinyin="tā zǎoyǐ lái le。" jyutping="taa1 zou2 ji5 loi4 liu5。" english="He arrived long ago." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我本来想给她打电话的，结果忘了。" traditional="我本來想給她打電話的，結果忘了。" pinyin="wǒ běnlái xiǎng gěi tā dǎ diànhuà de， jiéguǒ wàng le。" jyutping="ngo5 bun2 loi4 soeng2 kap1 taa1 daa2 din6 waa2 dik1， git3 gwo2 mong4 liu5。" english="I meant to call her, but I forgot." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="看来他曾经是个有钱人。" traditional="看來他曾經是個有錢人。" pinyin="kànlái tā céngjīng shì ge yǒuqiánrén。" jyutping="hon3 loi4 taa1 cang4 ging1 si6 go3 jau5 cin2 jan4。" english="It seems he was once a rich man." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我周日从来都不在家。" traditional="我週日從來都不在家。" pinyin="wǒ Zhōurì cónglái dōu bú zài jiā。" jyutping="ngo5 zau1 jat6 cung4 loi4 dou1 bat1 zoi6 gaa1。" english="I’m never at home on Sundays." />
