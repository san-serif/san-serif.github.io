The more you practice, the less you have to think about every word. Use 越……越…… when one change goes along with another. For a vivid comparison, 像……一样 means “like…” or “as…as.”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你越练习，说中文就越自然。" traditional="你越練習，說中文就越自然。" pinyin="nǐ yuè liànxí, shuō Zhōngwén jiù yuè zìrán." jyutping="nei5 jyut6 lin6 zaap6, syut3 zung1 man4 zau6 jyut6 zi6 jin4." english="The more you practice, the more naturally you speak Chinese." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="天气越冷，我越想喝热茶。" traditional="天氣越冷，我越想喝熱茶。" pinyin="tiānqì yuè lěng, wǒ yuè xiǎng hē rè chá." jyutping="tin1 hei3 jyut6 laang5, ngo5 jyut6 soeng2 hot3 jit6 caa4." english="The colder the weather gets, the more I want hot tea." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他跑得像兔子一样快。" traditional="他跑得像兔子一樣快。" pinyin="tā pǎo de xiàng tùzi yíyàng kuài." jyutping="taa1 paau2 dak1 zoeng6 tou3 zi2 jat1 joeng6 faai3." english="He runs as fast as a rabbit." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这个孩子像爸爸一样爱看书。" traditional="這個孩子像爸爸一樣愛看書。" pinyin="zhè ge háizi xiàng bàba yíyàng ài kàn shū." jyutping="ze5 go3 haai4 zi2 zoeng6 baa4 baa1 jat1 joeng6 oi3 hon3 syu1." english="This child loves reading just like his dad." />

<Hint type="hint">越……越…… links two changes (“the more…, the more…”). 像……一样 compares one thing with another. If the comparison is about how an action is done, place the quality before 一样: 跑得像兔子一样快.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">Keep 越来越 and 越……越…… apart: 天气越来越冷 says the weather keeps getting colder; 天气越冷，我越想喝热茶 links two changes—the colder it gets, the more I want tea.</Hint>
