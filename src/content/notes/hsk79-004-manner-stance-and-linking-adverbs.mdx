On your way home, you text 我顺便买点面包 (“I’ll pick up some bread while I’m at it”). You’re sure your colleague will arrive: 他必定会来. And if a friend is stuck, 不妨试试 is a gentle “why not give it a try?” Each word frames what comes next in a different way.

### Add a Small Errand on the Way

顺便 puts a convenient extra action beside the main plan. It often comes before the extra verb: you are already going somewhere, so you do one more thing while you are there.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="顺便问一下，餐厅晚上几点钟开始营业？" traditional="順便問一下，餐廳晚上幾點鍾開始營業？" pinyin="shùnbiàn wèn yīxià， cāntīng wǎnshang jǐdiǎn zhōng kāishǐ yíngyè？" jyutping="seon6 bin2 man6 jat1 haa6 ， caan1 teng1 maan5 soeng6 gei2 dim2 zung1 hoi1 ci2 jing4 jip6？" english="By the way, may I ask when in the evening your restaurant opens?" />

<Hint type="hint">If the second action is the main reason for the trip, leave 顺便 out; it means the action is incidental.</Hint>

### Make a Confident Prediction

必定 says the speaker is very sure an outcome will happen. It often appears before 能 or 会, or directly before the result.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他准备得这么认真，必定能通过考试。" traditional="他準備得這麼認真，必定能通過考試。" pinyin="tā zhǔnbèi de zhème rènzhēn, bìdìng néng tōngguò kǎoshì." jyutping="taa1 zeon2 bei6 dak1 ze3 mo1 jing6 zan1, bit1 ding6 nang4 tung1 gwo3 haau2 si3" english="He prepared so carefully that he is sure to pass the exam." />

<Hint type="hint">必定 expresses confidence, not an obligation. For “must do this,” use an expression of necessity.</Hint>

### Make a Gentle Suggestion

不妨 proposes an action as reasonable and worth trying. It softens advice: “you could…” or “why not…”.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你不妨告诉我一切。" traditional="你不妨告訴我一切。" pinyin="nǐ bùfáng gàosu wǒ yíqiè." jyutping="nei5 bat1 fong4 gou3 sou3 ngo5 jat1 cai3" english="You might as well tell me everything." />

<Hint type="hint">不妨 is a suggestion, not a command. It commonly comes before the recommended action.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">顺便 often attaches a quick errand to a plan already in motion. 不妨 softens a suggestion, while 必定 makes a prediction sound confident.</Hint>
