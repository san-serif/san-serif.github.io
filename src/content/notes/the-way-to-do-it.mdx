方 attaches to a verb's stem, the part right before ます, turning the verb into a noun that names the method or manner of doing it, "the way to X."

<JSentenceBlock furigana="メールの書[か]き方[かた]" romaji="Meeru no kakikata" english="how to write an email" />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="敬語[けいご]での話[はな]し方[かた]" romaji="Keigo de no hanashikata" english="how to speak using polite language" />

<Hint>書く becomes 書き方, 話す becomes 話し方, the same stem-attachment mechanism you already used for はじめる、おわる、and たい. Once turned into a noun, 方 can take の to connect to another noun, ちらしの作り方, "how to make a flyer."</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="本[ほん]の探[さが]し方[かた]を教[おし]えていただけませんか。" romaji="Hon no sagashikata o oshiete itadakemasen ka." english="Could you teach me how to find the book?" />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="DVDプレーヤーの使[つか]い方[かた]を教[おし]えていただけませんか。" romaji="DVD pureeyaa no tsukaikata o oshiete itadakemasen ka." english="Could you teach me how to use the DVD player?" />

<Hint>教えていただけませんか, a very polite request built on いただく, pairs naturally with 方 whenever you're asking someone to show you how something works, a common combination in real conversation.</Hint>
