-기로 하다 means "to decide to." It's built from pieces you already have: 기, the same ending that turns a verb into its "-ing" form in [기 전에](/notes/before-that), plus 로, the "by way of" particle in [-로 가요](/notes/this-way), plus 하다. 만나기로 하다 literally reads as "do it by way of meeting," which is exactly what deciding on a plan means.

<SentenceBlock formal="5시에 회사 앞에서 만나기로 했습니다." formalRom="daseot sie hoesa apeseo mannagiro haetseumnida." polite="5시에 회사 앞에서 만나기로 했어요." politeRom="daseot sie hoesa apeseo mannagiro haesseoyo." casual="5시에 회사 앞에서 만나기로 했어." casualRom="daseot sie hoesa apeseo mannagiro haesseo." english="I decided to meet in front of the office at 5." />

<SentenceBlock formal="내일 헬스클럽에 가기로 했습니다." formalRom="naeil helseukeulleobe gagiro haetseumnida." polite="내일 헬스클럽에 가기로 했어요." politeRom="naeil helseukeulleobe gagiro haesseoyo." casual="내일 헬스클럽에 가기로 했어." casualRom="naeil helseukeulleobe gagiro haesseo." english="I decided to go to the gym tomorrow." />

<Hint>-기로 했어요 (past) is overwhelmingly the form you'll actually use, since deciding is usually something that already happened by the time you're describing the plan. -기로 해요 (present) exists for a standing habit of decision-making, much rarer in everyday speech.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="장 보러 가기로 했습니다." formalRom="jang boreo gagiro haetseumnida." polite="장 보러 가기로 했어요." politeRom="jang boreo gagiro haesseoyo." casual="장 보러 가기로 했어." casualRom="jang boreo gagiro haesseo." english="I decided to go grocery shopping." />

<SentenceBlock formal="운동화가 필요해서 쇼핑하기로 했습니다." formalRom="undonghwaga piryohaeseo syopinghagiro haetseumnida." polite="운동화가 필요해서 쇼핑하기로 했어요." politeRom="undonghwaga piryohaeseo syopinghagiro haesseoyo." casual="운동화가 필요해서 쇼핑하기로 했어." casualRom="undonghwaga piryohaeseo syopinghagiro haesseo." english="I needed sneakers, so I decided to go shopping." />

<SentenceBlock formal="가족하고 같이 블루마운틴에 가기로 했습니다." formalRom="gajokhago gachi beullumauntine gagiro haetseumnida." polite="가족하고 같이 블루마운틴에 가기로 했어요." politeRom="gajokhago gachi beullumauntine gagiro haesseoyo." casual="가족하고 같이 블루마운틴에 가기로 했어." casualRom="gajokhago gachi beullumauntine gagiro haesseo." english="I decided to go to the Blue Mountains with my family." />

<Hint>가족하고 leans on [하고](/notes/with-a-friend), doing the "with" job on a family member instead of a friend. The particle doesn't care who's on the other side of it, family, friend, or coworker, the grammar stays identical.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="다음 주에 생일 파티를 하기로 했습니다." formalRom="daeum jue saengil patireul hagiro haetseumnida." polite="다음 주에 생일 파티를 하기로 했어요." politeRom="daeum jue saengil patireul hagiro haesseoyo." casual="다음 주에 생일 파티를 하기로 했어." casualRom="daeum jue saengil patireul hagiro haesseo." english="I decided to have a birthday party next week." />
