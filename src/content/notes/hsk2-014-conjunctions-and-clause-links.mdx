You’re planning a picnic and checking the weather: if it rains, you’ll stay in; if it clears, you’ll go; and if your friend brings snacks, even better. Chinese linkers show whether the next clause gives a condition, a contrast, or an added point. The examples let you hear those relationships in ordinary plans.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="如果下雨，我们就在家看电影。" traditional="如果下雨，我們就在家看電影。" pinyin="rúguǒ xiàyǔ, wǒmen jiù zài jiā kàn diànyǐng." jyutping="jyu4 gwo2 haa6 jyu5, ngo5 mun4 zau6 zoi6 gaa1 hon3 din6 jing2." english="If it rains, we’ll watch a film at home." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="虽然今天很忙，但是我会早点回来。" traditional="雖然今天很忙，但是我會早點回來。" pinyin="suīrán jīntiān hěn máng, dànshì wǒ huì zǎodiǎn huílai." jyutping="seoi1 jin4 gam1 tin1 han2 mong4, daan6 si6 ngo5 wui5 zou2 dim2 wui4 loi4." english="Although I’m busy today, I’ll come back early." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家店不但便宜，而且离我家很近。" traditional="這家店不但便宜，而且離我家很近。" pinyin="zhè jiā diàn búdàn piányi, érqiě lí wǒ jiā hěn jìn." jyutping="ze5 gaa1 dim3 bat1 daan6 pin4 ji4, ji4 ce2 lei4 ngo5 gaa1 han2 gan6." english="This shop is not only cheap, but also close to my home." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我们坐地铁或者坐公交都可以。" traditional="我們坐地鐵或者坐公交都可以。" pinyin="wǒmen zuò dìtiě huòzhě zuò gōngjiāo dōu kěyǐ." jyutping="ngo5 mun4 co5 dei6 tit3 waak6 ze2 co5 gung1 gaau1 dou1 ho2 ji5." english="We can take either the subway or the bus." />

<Hint type="hint">如果 often introduces a condition and 就 marks its result. 虽然 sets up a contrast, while 而且 adds another point. 或者 normally offers alternatives in a statement; questions offering a choice usually use 还是.</Hint>
