-면서 means "while," two actions happening at the same moment. You now have three ways to connect two actions, and they're not interchangeable.

<SentenceBlock formal="커피를 마시면서 음악을 듣습니다." formalRom="keopireul masimyeonseo eumageul deutseumnida." polite="커피를 마시면서 음악을 들어요." politeRom="keopireul masimyeonseo eumageul deureoyo." casual="커피를 마시면서 음악을 들어." casualRom="keopireul masimyeonseo eumageul deureo." english="I drink coffee while listening to music." />

<Hint>-고 (How's the Weather?) just lists two actions, no relationship implied. -아서/어서 (And Then) means the first action enables the second, done in sequence. -면서 means both are happening at the exact same instant: 마시면서 들어요 is sipping coffee and listening to music simultaneously, not one after the other.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="대학교에 다니면서 아르바이트를 합니다." formalRom="daehakgyoe danimyeonseo areubaiteureul hamnida." polite="대학교에 다니면서 아르바이트를 해요." politeRom="daehakgyoe danimyeonseo areubaiteureul haeyo." casual="대학교에 다니면서 아르바이트를 해." casualRom="daehakgyoe danimyeonseo areubaiteureul hae." english="I work part-time while attending university." />

<Hint>-면서 requires the same subject doing both actions. 제가 커피를 마시면서 친구가 이야기해요 ("I drink coffee while my friend talks") doesn't work, the two halves need one shared subject doing both things.</Hint>
