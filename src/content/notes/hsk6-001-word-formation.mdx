Chinese often builds vocabulary by attaching a short element to a familiar base. A prefix comes first: 无糖 means “without sugar,” while 超市 has the sense of something beyond an ordinary market. A suffix comes last: 现代化 means “modernisation,” and 可能性 means “possibility.” Learn the whole word, then use the shared element to make new words easier to recognise.
