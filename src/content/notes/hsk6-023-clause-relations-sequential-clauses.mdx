The moment she heard the news, she hurried over: 一听见消息便赶来了. 便 links the first event to the next step, much like “then” or “as soon as.” It sounds more formal and compact than 就.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他一来便走了。" traditional="他一來便走了。" pinyin="tā yī lái biàn zǒu le." jyutping="taa1 jat1 loi4 bin6 zau2 liu5." english="As soon as he came, he left." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这便是原因。" traditional="這便是原因。" pinyin="zhè biànshì yuányīn." jyutping="ze2 bin6 si6 jyun4 jan1." english="This is precisely the reason." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这不便。" traditional="這不便。" pinyin="zhè bùbiàn." jyutping="ze2 bat1 bin6." english="This is inconvenient." />
