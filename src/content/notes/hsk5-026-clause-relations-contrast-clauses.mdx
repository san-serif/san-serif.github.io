The event went ahead despite the rain. 尽管……但是…… grants a fact, then brings in the contrast. It often suits discussion or reports and can sound more formal than 虽然……但是…….

<CSentenceBlock simplified="尽管他学习的时间不多，但是成绩总比我高。" traditional="儘管他學習的時間不多，但是成績總比我高。" pinyin="jǐnguǎn tā xuéxí de shíjiān bù duō， dànshì chéngjì zǒng bǐ wǒ gāo。" jyutping="zeon6 gun2 taa1 hok6 zaap6 dik1 si4 gaan3 bat1 do1， daan6 si6 sing4 zik1 zung2 bei2 ngo5 gou1。" english="Even though he doesn’t study much, his grades are always higher than mine." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="尽管下着大雨，比赛还是照常举行了。" traditional="儘管下著大雨，比賽還是照常舉行了。" pinyin="jǐnguǎn xiàzhe dàyǔ, bǐsài háishi zhàocháng jǔxíng le." jyutping="zeon6 gun2 haa6 zoek6 daai6 jyu5, bei2 coi3 waan4 si6 ziu3 soeng4 geoi2 hang4 liu5." english="Although it was raining heavily, the match went ahead as usual." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="尽管路上很堵，但是我们还是准时到了。" traditional="儘管路上很堵，但是我們還是準時到了。" pinyin="jǐnguǎn lùshang hěn dǔ, dànshì wǒmen háishi zhǔnshí dào le." jyutping="zeon6 gun2 lou6 soeng6 han2 duk1, daan6 si6 ngo5 mun4 waan4 si6 zeon2 si4 dou3 liu5." english="Although traffic was heavy, we still arrived on time." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="尽管这家店不大，可是菜做得很地道。" traditional="儘管這家店不大，可是菜做得很地道。" pinyin="jǐnguǎn zhè jiā diàn bú dà, kěshì cài zuò de hěn dìdao." jyutping="zeon6 gun2 ze5 gaa1 dim3 bat1 daai6, ho2 si6 coi3 zou6 dak1 han2 dei6 dou6." english="Although this restaurant is small, the food is very authentic." />
