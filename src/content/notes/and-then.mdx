-아서/어서 is back, same spelling, same vowel-harmony rule as Because, but here it chains two actions where the first makes the second possible: go somewhere and then do something there, buy something and then eat it, wake up and then exercise.

<SentenceBlock formal="공원에 가서 산책을 할 겁니다." formalRom="gongwone gaseo sanchaegeul hal geomnida." polite="공원에 가서 산책을 할 거예요." politeRom="gongwone gaseo sanchaegeul hal geoyeyo." casual="공원에 가서 산책을 할 거야." casualRom="gongwone gaseo sanchaegeul hal geoya." english="I will go to the park and take a walk." />

<Hint>The test: can you swap it for "and then" without losing meaning? 가서 산책해요 means "go, and then walk," not "walk because I went." [-아서/어서](/notes/because) for a reason attaches to states and feelings (아파서, 좋아서). This version attaches almost exclusively to 가다/오다/사다/만나다/일어나다/씻다, verbs where the first action physically enables the second.</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="친구를 만나서 영화를 봤습니다." formalRom="chingureul mannaseo yeonghwareul bwatseumnida." polite="친구를 만나서 영화를 봤어요." politeRom="chingureul mannaseo yeonghwareul bwasseoyo." casual="친구를 만나서 영화를 봤어." casualRom="chingureul mannaseo yeonghwareul bwasseo." english="I met a friend and watched a movie." />

<SentenceBlock formal="김밥을 사서 먹었습니다." formalRom="gimbabeul saseo meogeotseumnida." polite="김밥을 사서 먹었어요." politeRom="gimbabeul saseo meogeosseoyo." casual="김밥을 사서 먹었어." casualRom="gimbabeul saseo meogeosseo." english="I bought gimbap and ate it." />

<SentenceBlock formal="일찍 일어나서 운동을 합니다." formalRom="iljjik ireonaseo undongeul hamnida." polite="일찍 일어나서 운동을 해요." politeRom="iljjik ireonaseo undongeul haeyo." casual="일찍 일어나서 운동을 해." casualRom="iljjik ireonaseo undongeul hae." english="I wake up early and exercise." />

<SentenceBlock formal="사과를 씻어서 먹습니다." formalRom="sagwareul ssiseoseo meokseumnida." polite="사과를 씻어서 먹어요." politeRom="sagwareul ssiseoseo meogeoyo." casual="사과를 씻어서 먹어." casualRom="sagwareul ssiseoseo meogeo." english="I wash the apple and eat it." />

<SentenceBlock formal="도서관에 가서 책을 읽었습니다." formalRom="doseogwane gaseo chaegeul ilgeotseumnida." polite="도서관에 가서 책을 읽었어요." politeRom="doseogwane gaseo chaegeul ilgeosseoyo." casual="도서관에 가서 책을 읽었어." casualRom="doseogwane gaseo chaegeul ilgeosseo." english="I went to the library and read a book." />

<Hint>[-고](/notes/hows-the-weather) just lists two actions with no relationship implied: 책을 읽고 텔레비전을 봐요, unrelated things done in sequence. -아서/어서 implies the first action put you in position for the second: 책을 사서 읽어요 specifically means you bought the book in order to read it, not two disconnected events.</Hint>
