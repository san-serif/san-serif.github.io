Someone asks how your day is going. You can answer with an action (我工作), a description (我很忙), or a quick comment (下雨了). Chinese predicates take several shapes, and an adjective usually describes the subject directly: there’s no 是 between them. 很 often makes that description sound neutral rather than emphatically “very.”

### Examples

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我每天工作。" traditional="我每天工作。" pinyin="Wǒ měitiān gōngzuò." jyutping="ngo5 mui5 tin1 gung1 zok3." english="I work every day." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他很高兴。" traditional="他很高興。" pinyin="Tā hěn gāoxìng." jyutping="taa1 han2 gou1 hing3." english="He is happy." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="下雨了。" traditional="下雨了。" pinyin="Xià yǔ le." jyutping="haa6 jyu5 laa3." english="It’s raining now." />

<Hint type="hint">Do not insert 是 before an adjective: 他很高兴 is the ordinary pattern, not 他是很高兴 in a basic neutral statement.</Hint>
