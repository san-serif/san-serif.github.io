You arrive at a quiet performance space and realise how many people are already seated. You can react to what you notice with -네요, then ask someone to stop an action with -지 마세요.

### React to What You Notice

Add -네요 to a verb or adjective stem: 많네요, 시작하네요. Drop ㄹ before ㄴ: 멀다 → 머네요. With a noun use -(이)네요: 학생이네요, 의사네요. It presents the information as something you are noticing now.

<SentenceBlock polite="사람이 정말 많네요." politeRom="sarami jeongmal manneyo." casual="사람이 정말 많네." casualRom="sarami jeongmal manne." english="There are so many people!" />

<SentenceBlock polite="공연이 벌써 시작했네요." politeRom="gongyeoni beolsseo sijakhaenneyo." casual="공연이 벌써 시작했네." casualRom="gongyeoni beolsseo sijakhaenne." english="The performance has already started!" />

### Ask Someone Not to Do It

Verb stem + -지 말다 gives a negative instruction. The usual polite request is -지 마세요; a casual request is -지 마. The instruction concerns an action someone can control, such as talking or taking photos.

<SentenceBlock polite="공연 중에는 사진을 찍지 마세요." politeRom="gongyeon jungeneun sajineul jjikji maseyo." casual="공연 중에는 사진을 찍지 마." casualRom="gongyeon jungeneun sajineul jjikji ma." english="Please don’t take photos during the performance." />

<SentenceBlock polite="여기에서 큰 소리로 이야기하지 마세요." politeRom="yeogieseo keun soriro iyagihaji maseyo." casual="여기에서 큰 소리로 이야기하지 마." casualRom="yeogieseo keun soriro iyagihaji ma." english="Please don’t speak loudly here." />

<Hint type="hint">말다 is the negative instruction verb. 먹지 마세요 means “please don’t eat”; 먹지 않아요 simply says someone does not eat. A polite ending can still carry a firm instruction.</Hint>
