A film review uses a different voice from a conversation. “The story moves quickly” becomes a plain written statement. If the reviewer recommends that you watch it, you need a quotation form for an instruction.

### The Plain Written Voice

Present action verbs take -ㄴ다 after a vowel, -는다 after a consonant: 본다, 먹는다. Drop ㄹ before ㄴ: 살다 → 산다. Adjectives keep -다: 좋다. Nouns take -(이)다. Past forms end in -았/었다; a future can use -(으)ㄹ 것이다.

<SentenceBlock polite="이 영화는 가족 이야기를 다뤄요." politeRom="i yeonghwaneun gajok iyagireul darwoyo." casual="이 영화는 가족 이야기를 다룬다." casualRom="i yeonghwaneun gajok iyagireul darunda." english="This film deals with a family story." />

<SentenceBlock polite="배우들의 연기가 자연스러워요." politeRom="baeudeurui yeongiga jayeonseureowoyo." casual="배우들의 연기가 자연스럽다." casualRom="baeudeurui yeongiga jayeonseureopda." english="The actors’ performances are natural." />

### Pass On an Instruction

Use -라고 하다 after a vowel or ㄹ and -으라고 하다 after another consonant: 보라고, 만들라고, 읽으라고. Negative instructions use -지 말라고 하다. A request for something to be done for the requester uses -아/어 달라고 하다.

<SentenceBlock polite="친구가 이 영화를 꼭 보라고 했어요." politeRom="chinguga i yeonghwareul kkok borago haesseoyo." casual="친구가 이 영화를 꼭 보라고 했어." casualRom="chinguga i yeonghwareul kkok borago haesseo." english="My friend told me to make sure I watched this film." />

<SentenceBlock polite="선생님이 늦지 말라고 하셨어요." politeRom="seonsaengnimi neutji mallago hasyeosseoyo." casual="선생님이 늦지 말라고 하셨어." casualRom="seonsaengnimi neutji mallago hasyeosseo." english="The teacher told us not to be late." />

<SentenceBlock polite="친구가 책을 빌려 달라고 했어요." politeRom="chinguga chaegeul billyeo dallago haesseoyo." casual="친구가 책을 빌려 달라고 했어." casualRom="chinguga chaegeul billyeo dallago haesseo." english="My friend asked me to lend them a book." />

<Hint type="hint">The Casual control in the first two examples shows the plain written form being taught. In an article, -다 is a writing convention rather than a choice about intimacy with a listener. In conversation, the same style can sound blunt.</Hint>
