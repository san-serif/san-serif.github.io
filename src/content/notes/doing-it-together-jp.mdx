Add 合[あ]う to a verb stem to describe people doing the action to or with one another. 同士[どうし] attaches to nouns for people or things in the same relationship, group, or situation.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="困[こま]ったときは、友達[ともだち]と助[たす]け合[あ]います。" romaji="Komatta toki wa, tomodachi to tasukeaimasu." english="When we're in trouble, my friends and I help each other." />

Common compounds include 話し合う, “discuss with one another,” 支え合う, “support each other,” and 分かち合う, “share with one another.” The action is reciprocal, so it normally involves two or more participants.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="二人[ふたり]は同[おな]じ会社[かいしゃ]の同僚[どうりょう]同士[どうし]です。" romaji="Futari wa onaji kaisha no douryou doushi desu." english="The two of them are coworkers at the same company." />

同士 labels the relationship between members: 友達同士, “friends with each other”; 会社同士, “between companies.” Unlike 合う, it doesn't form a verb or say what action they take.

<Hint type="insight">At work, 話し合う is a natural way to propose discussing a problem together: まずチームで話し合いましょう, “Let's discuss it as a team first.” 同士 is useful when describing a relationship, such as 学生同士で情報を交換する, “students exchange information with one another.”</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">合う is part of a verb and adds reciprocity; 同士 follows a noun and names the relationship. Compare 助け合う, “help each other,” with 友達同士, “as friends / between friends.”</Hint>
