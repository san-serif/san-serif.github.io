Your friend points out that the restaurant is expensive. You agree, then explain why you still like it: “Expensive it is, but the food is excellent.” Repeating the word with 是 accepts the fact before the speaker adds a qualification. Related conversational frames say a task has to be done either way, or that one simple action is enough.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="贵是贵，不过这家店的菜确实好吃。" traditional="貴是貴，不過這家店的菜確實好吃。" pinyin="guì shì guì, búguò zhè jiā diàn de cài quèshí hǎochī." jyutping="gwai3 si6 gwai3, bat1 gwo3 ze5 gaa1 dim3 dik1 coi3 koek3 sat6 hou2 hek3." english="It is expensive, but the food here really is good." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="去也得去，不去也得去，大家都等着我呢。" traditional="去也得去，不去也得去，大家都等著我呢。" pinyin="qù yě děi qù, bú qù yě děi qù, dàjiā dōu děngzhe wǒ ne." jyutping="heoi3 jaa5 dak1 heoi3, bat1 heoi3 jaa5 dak1 heoi3, daai6 gaa1 dou1 dang2 zoek6 ngo5 ne1." english="I have to go either way; everyone is waiting for me." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="有意见就直接说出来就是了。" traditional="有意見就直接說出來就是了。" pinyin="yǒu yìjiàn jiù zhíjiē shuō chūlai jiù shì le." jyutping="jau5 ji3 gin3 zau6 zik6 zip3 syut3 ceot1 loi4 zau6 si6 liu5." english="If you have an opinion, just say it directly." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="孩子小是小，可是做事特别认真。" traditional="孩子小是小，可是做事特別認真。" pinyin="háizi xiǎo shì xiǎo, kěshì zuòshì tèbié rènzhēn." jyutping="haai4 zi2 siu2 si6 siu2, ho2 si6 zou6 si6 dak6 bit6 jing6 zan1." english="The child is young, but very serious about what they do." />

<Hint type="hint">X是X often concedes the first point before 可是 or 不过 adds a qualification. X也得X，不X也得X says there is no way around it; X就是了 softens a direct instruction to “just do X.”</Hint>
