ずつ distributes an amount evenly among people or items, or describes a small amount repeated over time. Attach it after the number or amount: 一人ずつ, “one person at a time”; 少しずつ, “little by little.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="一人[ひとり]ずつ自己紹介[じこしょうかい]してください。" romaji="Hitori zutsu jikoshoukai shite kudasai." english="Please introduce yourself one person at a time." />

With a counter, ずつ means each person or item gets the same amount. It often follows the counter phrase directly: 一人に二枚ずつ, “two sheets each.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="毎日[まいにち]少[すこ]しずつ漢字[かんじ]を覚[おぼ]えています。" romaji="Mainichi sukoshi zutsu kanji o oboete imasu." english="I'm learning kanji little by little every day." />

With 少し or a repeated amount, ずつ highlights gradual progress through small increments. The action accumulates over time rather than happening all at once.

<Hint type="insight">For classroom instructions, ずつ keeps a group organized: 二人ずつ並んでください, “Please line up in pairs.” For steady progress, 毎日少しずつ続けています sounds natural for study, exercise, or saving money.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">A counter + ずつ divides an amount evenly; 少しずつ describes gradual change. The common idea is an even step or share, not a large one-off amount.</Hint>

当[あ]たり expresses a rate “per unit,” often for costs, quantities, or statistics: 一人当たり, “per person.” ずつ divides an amount among each person or item; 当たり gives a rate calculated for each unit.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="参加費[さんかひ]は一人[ひとり]当[あ]たり二千円[にせんえん]です。" romaji="Sankahi wa hitori atari nisen en desu." english="The participation fee is 2,000 yen per person." />

<Hint type="insight">Use 当たり for a per-person price or per-day figure in schedules and announcements. Use ずつ when telling people how many items each person receives: 一人に二枚ずつ渡します, “I'll give each person two sheets.”</Hint>
