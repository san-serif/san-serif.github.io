-기(가) 좋다 means "good to do" or "nice for doing," and -기 안 좋다 is the opposite. It's commonly used to talk about weather, places, books, and clothes, describing whether something is pleasant or suitable for an activity.

<SentenceBlock formal="이 도시는 살기가 좋습니다." formalRom="i dosineun salgiga joseumnida." polite="이 도시는 살기가 좋아요." politeRom="i dosineun salgiga joayo." casual="이 도시는 살기가 좋아." casualRom="i dosineun salgiga joa." english="This city is nice to live in." />

<Hint>기 doesn't just build a gerund for 전에 and 로 to attach to, it turns the whole verb into an actual noun, which is why 가 (the subject marker from [은/는 vs 이/가](/notes/old-news-new-news)) can attach right after it. 살기가 좋아요 literally parses as "[the act of] living is good."</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="이 책은 쉬워서 읽기가 좋습니다." formalRom="i chaegeun swiwoseo ilkgiga joseumnida." polite="이 책은 쉬워서 읽기가 좋아요." politeRom="i chaegeun swiwoseo ilkgiga joayo." casual="이 책은 쉬워서 읽기가 좋아." casualRom="i chaegeun swiwoseo ilkgiga joa." english="This book is easy, so it's good to read." />

<SentenceBlock formal="봄에는 날씨가 따뜻해서 산책하기가 좋습니다." formalRom="bomeneun nalssiga ttatteuthaeseo sanchaekhagiga joseumnida." polite="봄에는 날씨가 따뜻해서 산책하기가 좋아요." politeRom="bomeneun nalssiga ttatteuthaeseo sanchaekhagiga joayo." casual="봄에는 날씨가 따뜻해서 산책하기가 좋아." casualRom="bomeneun nalssiga ttatteuthaeseo sanchaekhagiga joa." english="In spring, the weather is warm, so it's good for walking." />

<SentenceBlock formal="도서관이 조용해서 책을 읽기가 좋았습니다." formalRom="doseogwani joyonghaeseo chaegeul ilkgiga joatseumnida." polite="도서관이 조용해서 책을 읽기가 좋았어요." politeRom="doseogwani joyonghaeseo chaegeul ilkgiga joasseoyo." casual="도서관이 조용해서 책을 읽기가 좋았어." casualRom="doseogwani joyonghaeseo chaegeul ilkgiga joasseo." english="The library was quiet, so it was good for reading." />

<SentenceBlock formal="근처에 공원이 없어서 운동하기 안 좋습니다." formalRom="geuncheoe gongwoni eopseoseo undonghagi an joseumnida." polite="근처에 공원이 없어서 운동하기 안 좋아요." politeRom="geuncheoe gongwoni eopseoseo undonghagi an joayo." casual="근처에 공원이 없어서 운동하기 안 좋아." casualRom="geuncheoe gongwoni eopseoseo undonghagi an joa." english="There's no park nearby, so it's not good for exercising." />

<Hint>Notice that last sentence dropped 가: 운동하기 안 좋아요, not 운동하기가 안 좋아요. Both are correct, and you'll hear the shorter version constantly in casual speech, the same optional-particle-dropping pattern from [아니에요](/notes/its-not-that) and [을/를 해요](/notes/what-are-you-doing).</Hint>

<SentenceBlock formal="이 스웨터는 추운 날씨에 입기가 좋습니다." formalRom="i seuweteoneun chuun nalssie ipgiga joseumnida." polite="이 스웨터는 추운 날씨에 입기가 좋아요." politeRom="i seuweteoneun chuun nalssie ipgiga joayo." casual="이 스웨터는 추운 날씨에 입기가 좋아." casualRom="i seuweteoneun chuun nalssie ipgiga joa." english="This sweater is good to wear in cold weather." />

<SentenceBlock formal="커피는 자기 전에 마시기 안 좋습니다." formalRom="keopineun jagi jeone masigi an joseumnida." polite="커피는 자기 전에 마시기 안 좋아요." politeRom="keopineun jagi jeone masigi an joayo." casual="커피는 자기 전에 마시기 안 좋아." casualRom="keopineun jagi jeone masigi an joa." english="Coffee is not good to drink before sleeping." />
