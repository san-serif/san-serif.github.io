上がる and 上げる can mark a quantity or degree going up; the first is intransitive (“rise”), while the second takes an object (“raise”).

って is a casual quotation and topic marker, common in conversation. である is a formal written copula used in reports and essays. と言っても qualifies a description: “even if we say X,” the reality may be more limited or nuanced than the label suggests. いわゆる means “so-called” and introduces a commonly recognized label. These forms let you adjust register and avoid overstatement.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="田中[たなか]さんって、来週[らいしゅう]引[ひ]っ越[こ]すんですか。" romaji="Tanaka-san tte, raishuu hikkosu n desu ka." english="I heard Tanaka is moving next week—is that right?" />

って often introduces a topic or repeats something heard, especially in casual speech. In formal contexts, use という or と聞きました instead. Its meaning is clear from whether it introduces a subject or a quoted statement.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="この報告書[ほうこくしょ]の目的[もくてき]は、現状[げんじょう]を明[あき]らかにすることである。" romaji="Kono houkokusho no mokuteki wa, genjou o akiraka ni suru koto de aru." english="The purpose of this report is to clarify the current situation." />

である is a formal alternative to です/だ in written explanations. It attaches to nouns and な-adjectives as である; the modifying form is である + noun, though であること is also common.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="古[ふる]いと言[い]っても、手入[てい]れが行[い]き届[とど]いています。" romaji="Furui to itte mo, teire ga ikitodoite imasu." english="Even though it's old, it has been very well maintained." />

と言っても accepts a label but limits what should be inferred from it. The continuation often gives the contrast or qualification: 古いと言っても、まだ十分使える, “though it's old, it's still usable.”

<Hint type="insight">って is natural with friends, but its informality can sound out of place in an official presentation. である appears in academic and formal writing. と言っても helps keep claims accurate by clarifying what a word like “old,” “famous,” or “experienced” means in context.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Casual topic or quotation: って. Formal written “is”: である. “Even if we call it X, actually…”: と言っても.</Hint>
