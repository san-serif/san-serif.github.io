“吃不吃都无所谓”—whether we eat or not is up to you. If a friend takes your joke the wrong way, 想到哪儿去了？ pushes back. And 要什么有什么 reassures them that everything they need is available. Each reply suits a different kind of conversational snag.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你想坐火车还是坐飞机，我都无所谓，按你方便的来。" traditional="你想坐火車還是坐飛機，我都無所謂，按你方便的來。" pinyin="nǐ xiǎng zuò huǒchē háishi zuò fēijī, wǒ dōu wúsuǒwèi, àn nǐ fāngbiàn de lái." jyutping="nei5 soeng2 zo6 fo2 ce1 waan4 si6 zo6 fei1 gei1, ngo5 dou1 mou4 so2 wai6, on3 nei5 fong1 bin6 dik1 loi4." english="I don’t mind whether we take the train or fly; let’s do whatever’s convenient for you." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我说的是去看电影，你想到哪儿去了？" traditional="我說的是去看電影，你想到哪兒去了？" pinyin="wǒ shuō de shì qù kàn diànyǐng, nǐ xiǎng dào nǎr qù le?" jyutping="ngo5 syut3 dik1 si6 heoi3 hon3 din6 jing2, nei5 soeng2 dou3 naa5 ji4 heoi3 liu5?" english="I meant going to see a movie—what did you think I meant?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="别担心，店里要什么有什么，咱们到那儿再买。" traditional="別擔心，店裡要什麼有什麼，咱們到那兒再買。" pinyin="bié dānxīn, diàn lǐ yào shénme yǒu shénme, zánmen dào nàr zài mǎi." jyutping="bit6 daam1 sam1, dim3 leoi5 jiu3 sam6 mo1 jau5 sam6 mo1, zaam1 mun4 dou3 naa5 ji4 zoi3 maai5." english="Don’t worry, the shop has everything we need; we can buy it when we get there." />

<Hint type="hint">In 无所谓X不X, X appears twice with 不 between the copies: 去不去, 做不做. 想到哪儿去了 is a fixed challenge to someone’s assumption. 要什么有什么 means “whatever you need, we have it.”</Hint>
