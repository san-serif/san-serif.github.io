The small café is crowded even on weekdays, let alone on a rainy Saturday. 何况 introduces that stronger supporting point. 进而 does something else: it carries the previous idea forward into a next step or further development.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="宝宝连走都不会，更何况是跑。" traditional="寶寶連走都不會，更何況是跑。" pinyin="bǎobao lián zǒu dōu bùhuì， gèng hékuàng shì pǎo。" jyutping="bou2 bou2 lin4 zau2 dou1 bat1 wui5， gang3 ho4 fong3 si6 paau2。" english="The baby can't walk, much less run." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他先是犹豫，进而拒绝了。" traditional="他先是猶豫，進而拒絕了。" pinyin="tā xiānshì yóuyù, jìn'ér jùjué le." jyutping="taa1 sin1 si6 jau4 jyu6, zeon3 ji4 keoi5 zyut6 liu5." english="He hesitated at first, and then refused." />
