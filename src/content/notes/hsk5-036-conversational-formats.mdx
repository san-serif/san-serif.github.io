“You’re already waiting here; let’s order while we do.” X着也是X着 treats an activity as already underway. X归X，Y归Y keeps two issues in separate boxes: agreeing with one point doesn’t settle the other.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我会在这里等着他回来。" traditional="我會在這裡等著他回來。" pinyin="wǒ huì zài zhèlǐ děng zhe tā huílái。" jyutping="ngo5 wui2 zoi6 ze5 leoi5 dang2 zoek3 taa1 wui4 loi4。" english="I’ll wait here for him to come back." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="它现在归我了。" traditional="它現在歸我了。" pinyin="tā xiànzài guī wǒ le。" jyutping="taa1 jin6 zoi6 gwai1 ngo5 liu5。" english="It belongs to me now." />
