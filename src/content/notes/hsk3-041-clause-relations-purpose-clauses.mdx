Your first train leaves soon, and you still need to get to the station. Put 为了 before the goal you have in mind, then say what you do to reach it: 为了赶上车, “to catch the train.”







<CSentenceBlock simplified="为了赶第一班火车，我起了个大早。" traditional="為了趕第一班火車，我起了個大早。" pinyin="wèile gǎn dìyī bān huǒchē， wǒ qǐ le ge dà zǎo。" jyutping="wai6 liu5 gon2 dai6 jat1 baan1 fo2 ce1， ngo5 hei2 liu5 go3 daai6 zou2。" english="I got up early to catch the first train." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="为了找个好位子，我很早就出门了。" traditional="為了找個好位子，我很早就出門了。" pinyin="wèile zhǎo yí ge hǎo wèizi， wǒ hěn zǎo jiù chūmén le。" jyutping="wai6 liu5 zaau2 jat1 go3 hou2 wai2 zi2， ngo5 han2 zou2 zau6 ceot1 mun4 liu5。" english="I left home early to get a good seat." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="为了避免迟到，我们提前在网上买好了车票。" traditional="為了避免遲到，我們提前在網上買好了車票。" pinyin="wèile bìmiǎn chídào, wǒmen tíqián zài wǎngshàng mǎi hǎo le chēpiào." jyutping="wai6 liu5 bei6 min5 ci4 dou3, ngo5 mun4 tai4 cin4 zoi6 mong5 soeng6 maai5 hou2 liu5 ce1 piu3." english="To avoid being late, we bought the tickets online in advance." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="为了省时间，他带了午饭，吃完就出发。" traditional="為了省時間，他帶了午飯，吃完就出發。" pinyin="wèile shěng shíjiān, tā dài le wǔfàn, chī wán jiù chūfā." jyutping="wai6 liu5 saang2 si4 gaan3, taa1 daai3 liu5 ng5 faan6, hek3 jyun4 zau6 ceot1 faat3." english="To save time, he brought lunch and set off as soon as he finished eating." />
