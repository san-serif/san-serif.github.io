第一组提前完成了，而第二组还没开始. 而 can set two developments against each other, with 则 sharpening the second side. 倒/反倒 marks a result that went the opposite way from what you expected.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我帮了他，他反倒怪我。" traditional="我幫了他，他反倒怪我。" pinyin="wǒ bāngle tā, tā fǎndào guài wǒ." jyutping="ngo5 bong1 liu5 taa1, taa1 faan2 dou2 gwaai3 ngo5." english="I helped him, but he blamed me instead." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你也许不会记得，而我永远不会忘记。" traditional="你也許不會記得，而我永遠不會忘記。" pinyin="nǐ yěxǔ bù huì jìde， ér wǒ yǒngyuǎn bù huì wàngjì。" jyutping="nei5 jaa5 heoi2 bat1 wui5 gei3 dak1， ji4 ngo5 wing5 jyun5 bat1 wui5 mong4 gei3。" english="You may not remember, but I’ll never forget." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="动物需要食物和饮料，而植物则需要雨水和阳光。" traditional="動物需要食物和飲料，而植物則需要雨水和陽光。" pinyin="dòngwù xūyào shíwù hé yǐnliào， ér zhíwù zé xūyào yǔshuǐ hé yángguāng。" jyutping="dung6 mat6 seoi1 jiu3 sik6 mat6 wo4 jam2 liu6， ji4 zik6 mat6 zak1 seoi1 jiu3 jyu5 seoi2 wo4 joeng4 gwong1。" english="As food and drink is to animals, so are rain and sunshine to plants." />
