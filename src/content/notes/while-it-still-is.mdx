うちに attaches after the plain form and means "while it's still X," urging an action before some window of opportunity closes. Unlike 間に, which simply reports two things overlapping, うちに carries a sense of urgency, do this before it's too late.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="拓也[たくや]さんは日本[にほん]にいるうちに、北海道[ほっかいどう]に行[い]きたいと思[おも]っています。" romaji="Takuya-san wa nihon ni iru uchi ni, Hokkaidou ni ikitai to omotte imasu." english="Takuya wants to go to Hokkaido while he's still in Japan." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="由紀[ゆき]さんは大学生[だいがくせい]のうちに、いろいろなアルバイトをしたいと思[おも]っています。" romaji="Yuki-san wa daigakusei no uchi ni, iroiro na arubaito o shitai to omotte imasu." english="Yuki wants to try all sorts of part-time jobs while she's still a university student." />

<Hint>いるうちに, 学生のうちに, both frame a temporary status as a limited-time opportunity, motivating the action before it inevitably ends, the trip in Japan will end, student life will end, so act now.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="友達[ともだち]が来[こ]ないうちに、部屋[へや]の掃除[そうじ]をしておきます。" romaji="Tomodachi ga konai uchi ni, heya no souji o shite okimasu." english="I'll clean the room before my friend arrives." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="外[そと]が暗[くら]くならないうちに、車[くるま]を洗[あら]っておきます。" romaji="Soto ga kurakunaranai uchi ni, kuruma o aratte okimasu." english="I'll wash the car before it gets dark outside." />

<Hint>Xないうちに means "before X happens," using the negative form to mark the deadline, 来ないうちに, "before they come," 暗くならないうちに, "before it gets dark," a pattern distinct from 前に but overlapping closely in meaning, with うちに adding more urgency.</Hint>
