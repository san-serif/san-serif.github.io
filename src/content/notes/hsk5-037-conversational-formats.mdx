These replies comment on a person’s actions rather than asking for information. 看你急的! teases someone for getting worked up; 真有你的! can praise a clever move or point out cheekiness; X什么X pushes back against an action, as in 急什么急. The relationship and tone decide whether the line is playful or sharp.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="看你急的，火车还有二十分钟才开呢。" traditional="看你急的，火車還有二十分鐘才開呢。" pinyin="kàn nǐ jí de, huǒchē hái yǒu èrshí fēnzhōng cái kāi ne." jyutping="hon3 nei5 gap1 dik1, fo2 ce1 waan4 jau5 ji6 sap6 fan1 zung1 coi4 hoi1 ne1." english="Look how worked up you are; the train doesn’t leave for another twenty minutes." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="真有你的，一个晚上就把这么难的电脑修好了！" traditional="真有你的，一個晚上就把這麼難的電腦修好了！" pinyin="zhēn yǒu nǐ de, yí ge wǎnshang jiù bǎ zhème nán de diànnǎo xiū hǎo le!" jyutping="zan1 jau5 nei5 dik1, jat1 go3 maan5 soeng6 zau6 baa2 ze5 mo1 naan4 dik1 din6 nou5 sau1 hou2 liu5!" english="You really did it—you fixed such a complicated computer in one evening!" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="急什么急？材料还没准备好，催也没用。" traditional="急什麼急？材料還沒準備好，催也沒用。" pinyin="jí shénme jí? cáiliào hái méi zhǔnbèi hǎo, cuī yě méi yòng." jyutping="gap1 sam6 mo1 gap1? coi4 liu6 waan4 mei6 zeon2 bei6 hou2, ceoi1 jaa5 mut6 jung6." english="What’s the rush? The materials aren’t ready yet, so pushing won’t help." />

<Hint type="hint">看你X的 often reacts to what the listener is doing; 真有你的 is a set reaction to someone’s behaviour. X什么X repeats the action to challenge or brush it off. Use these with people you know well, since they can sound critical.</Hint>
