Your friend says “I’m going,” and you answer with a surprised 啊 or a gentle 好吧. Later, you make a plan “if it rains”: 下雨的话. These small words can change the feel of a spoken line or link one idea to another; 等 can also leave a list open with “and so on.”

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我要是你的话，我就不去。" traditional="我要是你的話，我就不去。" pinyin="wǒ yàoshi nǐ dehuà， wǒ jiù bú qù。" jyutping="ngo5 jiu3 si6 nei5 dik1 waa6， ngo5 zau6 bat1 heoi3。" english="If I were you, I wouldn’t go." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="啊，我回来了！" traditional="啊，我回來了！" pinyin="ā， wǒ huílái le！" jyutping="aa1， ngo5 wui4 loi4 liu5！" english="Ah, I'm back!" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="不是吧！" traditional="不是吧！" pinyin="bùshì ba！" jyutping="bat1 si6 baa6！" english="No way!" />
