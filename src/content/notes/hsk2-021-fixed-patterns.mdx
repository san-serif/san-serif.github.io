You’re deciding how to get across town, describing someone you admire, then explaining your evening routine. These handy patterns let you recommend one choice, pair two qualities, and show when one event happens in relation to another.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="太晚了，我们还是坐出租车吧。" traditional="太晚了，我們還是坐出租車吧。" pinyin="tài wǎn le, wǒmen háishi zuò chūzūchē ba." jyutping="taai3 maan5 liu5, ngo5 mun4 waan4 si6 co5 ceot1 zou1 ce1 baa6." english="It’s late; we’d better take a taxi." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="她又聪明又热心，大家都喜欢她。" traditional="她又聰明又熱心，大家都喜歡她。" pinyin="tā yòu cōngming yòu rèxīn, dàjiā dōu xǐhuan tā." jyutping="taa1 jau6 cung1 ming4 jau6 jit6 sam1, daai6 gaa1 dou1 hei2 fun1 taa1." english="She’s both clever and warm-hearted, so everyone likes her." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="吃饭以前，先洗洗手。" traditional="吃飯以前，先洗洗手。" pinyin="chīfàn yǐqián, xiān xǐxi shǒu." jyutping="hek3 faan6 ji5 cin4, sin1 sai2 sai2 sau2." english="Wash your hands before eating." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="下课以后，我去超市买东西。" traditional="下課以後，我去超市買東西。" pinyin="xiàkè yǐhòu, wǒ qù chāoshì mǎi dōngxi." jyutping="haa6 fo3 ji5 hau6, ngo5 heoi3 ciu1 si5 maai5 dung1 sai1." english="After class, I’m going to the supermarket to buy a few things." />

<Hint type="hint">还是……吧 softens a recommendation. 又……又…… links two qualities that are both true. 以前 and 以后 can go before a noun or a short clause to mean “before” and “after.”</Hint>
