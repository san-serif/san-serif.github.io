The room is too bright, the music slightly too loud, and especially crowded near the door. 过于 marks excess; 稍/稍微 lower the degree; 尤其 singles out a standout item, while 大都 means most of a group. Listen for whether the word changes intensity or scope.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你的看法过于乐观。" traditional="你的看法過於樂觀。" pinyin="nǐ de kànfǎ guòyú lèguān。" jyutping="nei5 dik1 hon3 faat3 gwo3 jyu1 lok6 gun1。" english="Your view is overly optimistic." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你能稍微说一下自己的情况吗？" traditional="你能稍微說一下自己的情況嗎？" pinyin="nǐ néng shāowēi shuō yíxià zìjǐ de qíngkuàng ma？" jyutping="nei5 nang4 saau2 mei4 syut3 jat1 haa5 zi6 gei2 dik1 cing4 fong3 maa3？" english="Could you tell me a little about yourself?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他尤其忙。" traditional="他尤其忙。" pinyin="tā yóuqí máng." jyutping="taa1 jau4 kei4 mong4." english="He is especially busy." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他们大都很忙。" traditional="他們大都很忙。" pinyin="tāmen dàdū hěn máng." jyutping="taa1 mun4 daai6 dou1 han2 mong4." english="Most of them are busy." />
