At a busy café, the owner asks the server, Xiao Wang, to bring orders from the counter to the tables. In these sentences, phrases such as 从柜台 (from the counter) and 桌子上 (on the table) fit into a larger sentence, while 请服务员小王送来 (ask the server Xiao Wang to bring it) lets the same person receive an action and carry out the next one. Repeated measure phrases show how the work is divided, and 不慌不忙 describes the calm pace.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="服务员小王把订单一张一张放在桌子上。" traditional="服務員小王把訂單一張一張放在桌子上。" pinyin="fúwùyuán Xiǎo Wáng bǎ dìngdān yì zhāng yì zhāng fàng zài zhuōzi shàng." jyutping="fuk6 mou6 jyun4 siu2 wong4 baa2 ding6 daan1 jat1 zoeng1 jat1 zoeng1 fong3 zoi6 zoek3 zi2 soeng6." english="The server Xiao Wang places the orders on the table one by one." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="客人一个一个地进来，老板不慌不忙地做咖啡。" traditional="客人一個一個地進來，老闆不慌不忙地做咖啡。" pinyin="kèrén yí ge yí ge de jìnlai, lǎobǎn bùhuāng-bùmáng de zuò kāfēi." jyutping="haak3 jan4 jat1 go3 jat1 go3 dei6 zeon3 loi4, lou5 baan2 bat1 fong1 bat1 mong4 dei6 zou6 gaa3 fe1." english="The customers come in one by one, and the owner makes coffee without rushing." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="桌子不大不小，刚好放得下四杯咖啡。" traditional="桌子不大不小，剛好放得下四杯咖啡。" pinyin="zhuōzi bú dà bù xiǎo, gānghǎo fàng de xià sì bēi kāfēi." jyutping="zoek3 zi2 bat1 daai6 bat1 siu2, gong1 hou2 fong3 dak1 haa5 sei3 bui1 gaa3 fe1." english="The table is just the right size; it fits four cups of coffee." />

<Hint type="hint">Notice what each phrase does in the sentence: 从柜台 tells where the order comes from; 桌子上 tells where it goes; in 请服务员小王拿订单, 小王 is both the person being asked and the person who takes the orders. Repeating a number and measure word means “one at a time,” while 不A不B is a compact fixed pairing.</Hint>
