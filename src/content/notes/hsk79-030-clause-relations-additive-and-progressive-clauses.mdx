他别说跑马拉松了，连五公里也跑不动. This pattern builds from a difficult or unlikely case to an even stronger one. 况且 adds another supporting reason; 更不用说 introduces something even more obvious or extreme.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这本书连我都看不懂，别说孩子了。" traditional="這本書連我都看不懂，別說孩子了。" pinyin="zhè běn shū lián wǒ dōu kàn bù dǒng, bié shuō háizi le." jyutping="ze3 bun2 syu1 lin4 ngo5 dou1 hon3 bat1 dung2, bit6 syut3 haai4 zi2 liu5" english="Even I cannot understand this book, let alone a child." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="今天即使再忙，也要做完这些事情才走。" traditional="今天即使再忙，也要做完這些事情才走。" pinyin="jīntiān jíshǐ zài máng， yě yào zuòwán zhèxiē shìqing cái zǒu。" jyutping="gam1 tin1 zik1 si2 zoi3 mong4， jaa5 jiu3 zou6 jyun4 ze2 se1 si6 cing4 coi4 zau2。" english="Even if it’s very busy today, I have to finish these things before leaving." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="天黑了，况且还下着雨，最好别出门。" traditional="天黑了，況且還下著雨，最好別出門。" pinyin="tiān hēi le, kuàngqiě hái xiàzhe yǔ, zuìhǎo bié chūmén." jyutping="tin1 hak1 liu5, fong3 ce2 waan4 haa6 zoek6 jyu5, zeoi3 hou2 bit6 ceot1 mun4." english="It is dark, and besides, it is raining, so it is best not to go out." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="那是一个许多人连听也没有听过的小国。" traditional="那是一個許多人連聽也沒有聽過的小國。" pinyin="nà shì yí ge xǔduō rén lián tīng yě méiyǒu tīng guo de xiǎo guó。" jyutping="naa5 si6 jat1 go3 heoi2 do1 jan4 lin4 teng1 jaa5 mut6 jau5 teng1 gwo3 dik1 siu2 gwok3。" english="That’s a small country many people have never even heard of." />
