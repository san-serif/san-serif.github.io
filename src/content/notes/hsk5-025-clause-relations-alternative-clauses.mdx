或是……或是…… lays out alternatives without ranking them. Use it when either option remains open, then make clear whether the listener may choose freely or whether a later clause narrows the choice.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你或是他都可以。" traditional="你或是他都可以。" pinyin="nǐ huòshì tā dōu kěyǐ." jyutping="nei5 waak6 si6 taa1 dou1 ho2 ji5." english="Either you or him is fine." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这很过度。" traditional="這很過度。" pinyin="zhè hěn guòdù." jyutping="ze2 han2 gwo3 dou6." english="This is excessive." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他对这过敏。" traditional="他對這過敏。" pinyin="tā duì zhè guòmǐn." jyutping="taa1 deoi3 ze2 gwo3 man5." english="He's allergic to this." />
