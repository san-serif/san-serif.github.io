The plan looked simple, but 不料一场暴风雨打乱了安排. 而 can join related or contrasting descriptions; 同 and 与 bring people or items together in a more formal register; 若 opens a condition. Each linker gives the next part a different relationship to what came before.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你也许不会记得，而我永远不会忘记。" traditional="你也許不會記得，而我永遠不會忘記。" pinyin="nǐ yěxǔ bù huì jìde， ér wǒ yǒngyuǎn bù huì wàngjì。" jyutping="nei5 jaa5 heoi2 bat1 wui5 gei3 dak1， ji4 ngo5 wing5 jyun5 bat1 wui5 mong4 gei3。" english="You may not remember, but I’ll never forget." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="眼下我不得不和朋友同住一个房间。" traditional="眼下我不得不和朋友同住一個房間。" pinyin="yǎnxià wǒ bùdébù hé péngyou tóngzhù yí ge fángjiān." jyutping="ngaan5 haa6 ngo5 bat1 dak1 bat1 wo4 pang4 jau5 tung4 zyu6 jat1 go3 fong4 gaan1" english="For now, I have to share a room with a friend." />
