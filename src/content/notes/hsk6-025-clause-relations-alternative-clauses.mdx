The bus is late and the rain is getting heavier: 要么走路，要么叫车. 要么……要么…… lays out two alternatives, often the only choices on offer. Repeat 要么 to make both sides of the decision clear.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="要么去，要么不去。" traditional="要麼去，要麼不去。" pinyin="yàome qù, yàome bù qù." jyutping="jiu3 mo1 heoi3, jiu3 mo1 bat1 heoi3." english="Either go, or don't go." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他是高手。" traditional="他是高手。" pinyin="tā shì gāoshǒu." jyutping="taa1 si6 gou1 sau2." english="He's an expert." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这是稿子。" traditional="這是稿子。" pinyin="zhè shì gǎozi." jyutping="ze2 si6 gou2 zi2." english="This is a draft." />
