只要……就…… sets the minimum condition needed for the result: “as long as this happens, that follows.” Put the required condition first, then state the dependable outcome.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="只要你好好学中文，就能去中国读书。" traditional="只要你好好學中文，就能去中國讀書。" pinyin="zhǐyào nǐ hǎohāo xué Zhōngwén， jiù néng qù Zhōngguó dúshū。" jyutping="zi2 jiu3 nei5 hou2 hou2 hok6 zung1 man4， zau6 nang4 heoi3 zung1 gwok3 duk6 syu1。" english="As long as you study Chinese hard, you can go to school in China." />
