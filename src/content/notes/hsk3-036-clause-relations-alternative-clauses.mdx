You and your sister are both free to collect a parcel, but one of you has to do it. 不是……就是…… presents the two likely possibilities: either she’ll go, or you will. The speaker expects one of them to be true.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="不是我去拿快递，就是姐姐去。" traditional="不是我去拿快遞，就是姐姐去。" pinyin="bú shì wǒ qù ná kuàidì, jiù shì jiějie qù." jyutping="bat1 si6 ngo5 heoi3 naa4 faai3 dai6, zau6 si6 ze2 ze2 heoi3." english="Either I’ll pick up the parcel, or my older sister will." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他周末不是在家休息，就是去公园跑步。" traditional="他週末不是在家休息，就是去公園跑步。" pinyin="tā zhōumò bú shì zài jiā xiūxi, jiù shì qù gōngyuán pǎobù." jyutping="taa1 zau1 mut6 bat1 si6 zoi6 gaa1 jau1 sik1, zau6 si6 heoi3 gung1 jyun4 paau2 bou6." english="At the weekend, he either rests at home or goes running in the park." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这附近的餐馆不是太贵，就是已经关门了。" traditional="這附近的餐館不是太貴，就是已經關門了。" pinyin="zhè fùjìn de cānguǎn bú shì tài guì, jiù shì yǐjīng guānmén le." jyutping="ze5 fu6 kan5 dik1 caan1 gun2 bat1 si6 taai3 gwai3, zau6 si6 ji5 ging1 gwaan1 mun4 liu5." english="The restaurants nearby are either too expensive or already closed." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你不是忘了带钥匙，就是把它放在办公室了。" traditional="你不是忘了帶鑰匙，就是把它放在辦公室了。" pinyin="nǐ bú shì wàng le dài yàoshi, jiù shì bǎ tā fàng zài bàngōngshì le." jyutping="nei5 bat1 si6 mong4 liu5 daai3 joek6 si4, zau6 si6 baa2 taa1 fong3 zoi6 baan6 gung1 sat1 liu5." english="You either forgot to bring your keys or left them at the office." />

<Hint type="hint">Use 不是 before the first possibility and 就是 before the second. Unlike a neutral question with 还是, this pattern states what the speaker thinks the alternatives are.</Hint>
