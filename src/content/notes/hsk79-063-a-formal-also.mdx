When a report or essay has made one point, 亦 lets you add another: “also,” “likewise,” or “and … too.” It usually sits before the predicate, much like 也, but sounds more formal and is far more common in writing.

### Adding a Second Point

<CSentenceBlock simplified="他的成就令人敬佩，他的精神亦值得我们学习。" traditional="他的成就令人敬佩，他的精神亦值得我們學習。" pinyin="Tā de chéngjiù lìng rén jìngpèi, tā de jīngshén yì zhídé wǒmen xuéxí." jyutping="taa1 dik1 sing4 zau6 ling6 jan4 ging3 pui3, taa1 dik1 zing1 san4 jik6 zik6 dak1 ngo5 mun4 hok6 zaap6." english="His achievements are admirable, and his spirit is also worth learning from." />

The first clause introduces his achievements; 亦 adds a related quality. The word belongs before the new predicate, not at the end of the sentence.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这项政策影响深远，亦为未来发展奠定了基础。" traditional="這項政策影響深遠，亦為未來發展奠定了基礎。" pinyin="Zhè xiàng zhèngcè yǐngxiǎng shēnyuǎn, yì wèi wèilái fāzhǎn diàndìng le jīchǔ." jyutping="ze5 hong6 zing3 caak3 jing2 hoeng2 sam1 jyun5, jik6 wai6 mei6 loi4 faat3 zin2 din6 ding6 zo2 gei1 co2." english="This policy has a far-reaching effect and also lays a foundation for future development." />

亦 can add a consequence or a supporting point in formal prose. For a conversation with a friend, 也 is the natural everyday choice.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这种做法方便，亦能减少浪费。" traditional="這種做法方便，亦能減少浪費。" pinyin="Zhè zhǒng zuòfǎ fāngbiàn, yì néng jiǎnshǎo làngfèi." jyutping="ze5 zung2 zou6 faat3 fong1 bin6, jik6 nang4 gaam2 siu2 long6 fai3." english="This approach is convenient and can also reduce waste." />

<Hint type="hint">亦 adds information; it does not mean “but” or “therefore.” Use a different connector when the next clause contrasts with or follows as a result of the first.</Hint>

<Hint type="insight">In news reports and formal writing, 亦 can give two claims a balanced, parallel feel. In ordinary speech, speakers generally use 也 for the same job.</Hint>
