In a review, the food is 颇受欢迎—quite popular. 颇 sounds more formal than 很; 稍稍 lowers the degree to “slightly,” while 尤为 singles out the feature that stands out most.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这家报社在当地知名度颇高。" traditional="這家報社在當地知名度頗高。" pinyin="zhè jiā bàoshè zài dāngdì zhīmíngdù pō gāo。" jyutping="ze2 gaa1 bou3 se5 zoi6 dong1 dei6 zi1 ming4 dou6 po2 gou1。" english="This newspaper is well known in the local area." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="请稍稍等一下。" traditional="請稍稍等一下。" pinyin="qǐng shāoshāo děng yíxià." jyutping="cing2 saau1 saau1 dang2 jat1 haa6." english="Please wait a little bit." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="在所有科目中，他的数学成绩尤为突出，经常拿满分。" traditional="在所有科目中，他的數學成績尤為突出，經常拿滿分。" pinyin="zhè jiā diàn yóuwéi ānjìng。" jyutping="ze5 gaa1 dim3 jau4 wai4 on1 zing6。" english="Among all subjects, his math grades are particularly outstanding, often getting full marks." />
