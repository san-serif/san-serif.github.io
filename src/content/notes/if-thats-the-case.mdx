You hear that the meeting has moved online, so you decide to join from home. なら takes a situation someone has just mentioned and says what follows if that is the case.

なら attaches after a noun, adjective, or plain verb form and means "if that's the case, then." Unlike other conditionals, it almost always responds to something the other person just said, picking it up and building an answer around it.

それなら is the same conversational move in a sentence connector: “if so / in that case.” It explicitly takes up the other person's information before offering a response or next step.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="土曜日[どようび]なら、行[い]きます。" romaji="Doyoubi nara, ikimasu." english="If it's Saturday, I'll go." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="すみません、土曜日[どようび]はちょっと。。。日曜日[にちようび]ならいいですよ。" romaji="Sumimasen, doyoubi wa chotto... nichiyoubi nara ii desu yo." english="Sorry, Saturday's a bit... Sunday would work though." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="それなら、駅[えき]で待[ま]ち合[あ]わせましょう。" romaji="Sore nara, eki de machiawasemashou." english="In that case, let's meet at the station." />

<Hint type="hint">なら responds directly to a proposal already on the table, someone suggests Saturday, you counter with "if it's Sunday, that works." This makes it feel more like a live negotiation than a general hypothetical the way たら or ば can be.</Hint>

<JSentenceBlock furigana="お店[みせ]が遠[とお]いんなら、行[い]きません。" romaji="Omise ga tooin nara, ikimasen." english="If the shop turns out to be far, I won't go." />

<JSentenceBlock furigana="漢字[かんじ]が覚[おぼ]えられないんなら、フラッシュカードを使[つか]ってみたらどうですか。" romaji="Kanji ga oboerarenain nara, furasshukaado o tsukattemitara dou desu ka." english="If you can't remember kanji, why not try using flashcards?" />

<Hint type="insight">んなら, built on the explanatory ん you already know, is extremely common in casual speech, softening the condition into something closer to "given that's the situation." な-adjectives and nouns attach directly, no だ needed, 便利なら, 先生なら.</Hint>
