The surprise ending made everyone laugh; the announcement made the room go quiet. 叫、令、使, and 让 can name the person or thing that causes someone else to act or feel a certain way. The person after the causative verb carries out the next action.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="我叫他来的。" traditional="我叫他來的。" pinyin="wǒ jiào tā lái de。" jyutping="ngo5 giu3 taa1 loi4 dik1。" english="I told him to come." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这个消息令我很吃惊。" traditional="這個消息令我很吃驚。" pinyin="zhège xiāoxi lìng wǒ hěn chījīng。" jyutping="ze5 go3 siu1 sik1 ling6 ngo5 han2 hek3 ging1。" english="This news surprised me." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="这件事使他很高兴。" traditional="這件事使他很高興。" pinyin="zhè jiàn shì shǐ tā hěn gāoxìng。" jyutping="ze2 gin6 si6 sai2 taa1 han2 gou1 hing3。" english="This made him very happy." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="让我来帮你吧。你的包看起来很重。" traditional="讓我來幫你吧。你的包看起來很重。" pinyin="ràng wǒ lái bāng nǐ ba。 nǐ de bāo kànqǐlai hěn chóng。" jyutping="joeng6 ngo5 loi4 bong1 nei5 baa6。 nei5 dik1 baau1 hon1 hei2 loi4 han2 cung5。" english="Let me help you. Your bag looks very heavy." />
