Someone asks why you admire a friend. You can give a clear reason with -기 때문에, then add a conversational comment with -는데요. The first builds the explanation; the second leaves space for a response.

### Give the Reason Explicitly

Attach -기 때문에 to a verb or adjective stem: 도와주기 때문에, 친절하기 때문에. Nouns can take 때문에 directly, as in 비 때문에, “because of the rain.” A noun predicate uses -이기 때문에: 학생이기 때문에.

<SentenceBlock formal="약속을 잘 지키기 때문에 그 친구를 믿습니다." formalRom="yaksogeul jal jikigi ttaemune geu chingureul mitseumnida." polite="약속을 잘 지키기 때문에 그 친구를 믿어요." politeRom="yaksogeul jal jikigi ttaemune geu chingureul mideoyo." casual="약속을 잘 지키기 때문에 그 친구를 믿어." casualRom="yaksogeul jal jikigi ttaemune geu chingureul mideo." english="I trust that friend because he keeps his promises." />

<SentenceBlock formal="비 때문에 길이 막힙니다." formalRom="bi ttaemune giri makhimnida." polite="비 때문에 길이 막혀요." politeRom="bi ttaemune giri makhyeoyo." casual="비 때문에 길이 막혀." casualRom="bi ttaemune giri makhyeo." english="Traffic is heavy because of the rain." />

### Leave the Conversation Open

At the end of a turn, -는데요 can introduce background, react, or gently contrast with what was said. Use -는데요 for action verbs and 있다/없다, -(으)ㄴ데요 for adjectives, -인데요 for nouns. The meaning depends on the situation and intonation.

<SentenceBlock polite="저도 그런 친구를 만나고 싶은데요." politeRom="jeodo geureon chingureul mannago sipeundeyo." casual="나도 그런 친구를 만나고 싶은데." casualRom="nado geureon chingureul mannago sipeunde." english="I’d like to meet a friend like that too." />

<SentenceBlock polite="이 가방은 좀 비싼데요." politeRom="i gabangeun jom bissandeyo." casual="이 가방은 좀 비싼데." casualRom="i gabangeun jom bissande." english="This bag is a little expensive, though." />

<Hint type="hint">For a reason leading into a request or suggestion, [-(으)니까](/notes/since) is usually the easier choice: 비가 오니까 택시를 타세요. -기 때문에 is a useful way to explain a situation or judgement.</Hint>

### React With Admiration

A sentence ending in -는데요 can also react to something impressive or unexpected: 한국어를 정말 잘하는데요! The shape is the same as the background ending, but the turn-final intonation presents an appreciative observation rather than requiring an unfinished explanation.

<SentenceBlock polite="새로 산 옷이 정말 잘 어울리는데요!" politeRom="saero san osi jeongmal jal eoullineundeyo!" casual="새로 산 옷이 정말 잘 어울리는데!" casualRom="saero san osi jeongmal jal eoullineunde!" english="Those new clothes really suit you!" />
