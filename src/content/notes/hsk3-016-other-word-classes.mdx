Your friend sends a photo of their cat sitting inside a shopping bag. A quick 哈哈 tells them you found it funny. In texts and casual messages, this sound-word works like a short laugh; you can also put it before a comment to keep the tone friendly.

<CSentenceBlock simplified="哈哈，这只猫怎么跑到袋子里了？" traditional="哈哈，這隻貓怎麼跑到袋子裡了？" pinyin="hāhā, zhè zhī māo zěnme pǎo dào dàizi lǐ le?" jyutping="haa1 haa1, ze5 zek3 maau1 zam2 mo1 paau2 dou3 doi2 zi2 leoi5 liu5?" english="Haha, how did this cat get into the bag?" />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="你也忘了带伞？哈哈，我昨天也忘了。" traditional="你也忘了帶傘？哈哈，我昨天也忘了。" pinyin="nǐ yě wàng le dài sǎn? hāhā, wǒ zuótiān yě wàng le." jyutping="nei5 jaa5 mong4 liu5 daai3 saan3? haa1 haa1, ngo5 zok3 tin1 jaa5 mong4 liu5." english="You forgot your umbrella too? Ha, I forgot mine yesterday." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="哈哈，原来我们坐错车了。" traditional="哈哈，原來我們坐錯車了。" pinyin="hāhā, yuánlái wǒmen zuò cuò chē le." jyutping="haa1 haa1, jyun4 loi4 ngo5 mun4 co5 co3 ce1 liu5." english="Ha, turns out we got on the wrong bus." />

<CSentenceBlock simplified="听完这个笑话，大家都哈哈大笑。" traditional="聽完這個笑話，大家都哈哈大笑。" pinyin="tīng wán zhè ge xiàohua, dàjiā dōu hāhā dàxiào." jyutping="teng1 jyun4 ze5 go3 siu3 waa6, daai6 gaa1 dou1 haa1 haa1 daai6 siu3." english="After hearing the joke, everyone burst out laughing." />

<Hint type="hint">哈哈 can stand alone as a short written laugh or lead into a light comment. In speech, it doesn’t replace a spoken laugh; it’s especially common in texts and online chat.</Hint>
