Japanese can describe a change that causes another change, or a trend that keeps going. ば〜ほど links two degrees (“the more X, the more Y”); につれて describes two changes unfolding together; 一方だ says a trend continues in one direction. ますます is a common adverb that strengthens the sense of “increasingly.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="日本語[にほんご]は勉強[べんきょう]すればするほど、おもしろくなります。" romaji="Nihongo wa benkyou sureba suru hodo, omoshiroku narimasu." english="The more I study Japanese, the more interesting it gets." />

ば〜ほど repeats the same verb or adjective around ば and ほど. The first half names the change; the second half says how the result changes along with it. It often translates as “the more…the more,” but can also mean “as X increases, Y does too.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="年齢[ねんれい]を重[かさ]ねるにつれて、考[かんが]え方[かた]が変[か]わってきました。" romaji="Nenrei o kasaneru ni tsurete, kangaekata ga kawatte kimashita." english="As I've gotten older, my way of thinking has changed." />

につれて describes two developments progressing together over time. It often follows a verb in dictionary form or a noun, and suits gradual changes better than a sudden one-off event.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="この町[まち]では、人口[じんこう]が減[へ]る一方[いっぽう]です。" romaji="Kono machi de wa, jinkou ga heru ippou desu." english="The population in this town just keeps declining." />

一方だ attaches after a dictionary-form verb and says that a trend keeps moving in one direction, with no sign of stopping or reversing. It often appears with changes the speaker sees as concerning, such as costs rising or services shrinking.

<JSentenceBlock furigana="オンライン授業[じゅぎょう]を利用[りよう]する学生[がくせい]がますます増[ふ]えています。" romaji="Onrain jugyou o riyou suru gakusei ga masumasu fuete imasu." english="More and more students are using online classes." />

ますます means “increasingly” or “more and more.” It intensifies a change that is already happening, and works with both favorable and unfavorable trends.

と並[なら]んで places one thing alongside another, often to say that it belongs in the same group or is comparable in standing: 同時代の作家と並んで, “alongside writers of the same era.”

<JSentenceBlock furigana="この作品[さくひん]は、同時代[どうじだい]の名作[めいさく]と並[なら]んで高[たか]く評価[ひょうか]されています。" romaji="Kono sakuhin wa, doujidai no meisaku to narande takaku hyouka sarete imasu." english="This work is highly regarded alongside the masterpieces of its era." />

<Hint type="insight">In real life, these forms are useful when talking about a trend in work, study, or the news: 物価が上がるにつれて、外食する人が減っています, “As prices rise, fewer people are eating out.” Use につれて for two linked changes, and 一方だ when you want to stress that one trend keeps going without reversing.</Hint>

<Hint type="hint">Compare the shapes: 勉強すればするほど links the degree of two results; 年齢を重ねるにつれて follows parallel changes over time; 人口が減る一方だ emphasizes a one-direction trend; と並んで adds a peer or comparison point.</Hint>
